    The Bloodline Saga

    Acknowledge him — the greatest long-term storyline of the modern era.

    The Bloodline is widely considered the greatest long-term storyline of the modern era. After returning in 2020, Roman Reigns embraced a "Tribal Chief" persona, demanding his family "acknowledge" him with Paul Heyman as his "Wise Man." The multi-year family drama — built on the real Anoa'i lineage — ended when Cody Rhodes "finished the story" at WrestleMania XL.

    Timeline

    1. 2020

      Reigns returns as a ruthless heel, aligns with Paul Heyman and his cousins The Usos, and begins one of the longest world title reigns in modern WWE history.

    2. Jan 2023

      At the Royal Rumble, honorary member Sami Zayn turns on Roman; the Montreal crowd's reaction weeks later at Elimination Chamber becomes an all-time emotional peak.

    3. Apr 2023

      Cody Rhodes, chasing the title to "finish the story" his father Dusty never could, comes up short against Reigns at WrestleMania 39.

    4. 2023–2024

      Internal family fractures deepen — Jey and Jimmy Uso, Solo Sikoa and Sami Zayn all wrestle with loyalty and betrayal.

    5. Apr 2024

      At WrestleMania XL Night 2, Cody Rhodes defeats Roman Reigns for the Undisputed WWE Universal Title, ending a reign of over 1,300 days.

    The Arc

    What set The Bloodline apart was patience. Where most wrestling stories run in weeks, this one ran in years, treating its audience like viewers of prestige television. Reigns' cold demand to be "acknowledged," Heyman's whispered manipulations, and the slow erosion of family loyalty gave the saga a novelistic weight. The Usos, Solo Sikoa and honorary member Sami Zayn each became characters with real interior lives, their allegiances shifting under the Tribal Chief's thumb. Sami's betrayal of Roman in front of his hometown Montreal crowd remains one of the most emotionally charged moments WWE has ever produced.

    The genius of the payoff was that it drew on real lineage. The story leaned on the actual Anoa'i family and framed Cody Rhodes' quest around finishing the championship story his late father, Dusty Rhodes, never completed. When Cody finally dethroned Reigns at WrestleMania XL, it delivered a genuinely cathartic release earned over years — the rarest thing in wrestling, a long build that actually pays off.

    The Wrestlers

    At the center stands Roman Reigns, the Tribal Chief, against Cody Rhodes, the son of Dusty Rhodes chasing his father's unfinished story. The wider faction — Paul Heyman, The Usos (Jimmy and Jey Uso), Solo Sikoa and Sami Zayn — drove the internal family drama, drawing on the real Anoa'i family tree that also connects to The Rock.

    FAQ

    What is The Bloodline storyline?
    After returning in 2020, Roman Reigns embraced a "Tribal Chief" persona, demanding his family "acknowledge" him with Paul Heyman as his "Wise Man." It became a long-running family drama of loyalty and betrayal built on the real Anoa'i family.
    How did The Bloodline saga end?
    Cody Rhodes pursued Roman Reigns to "finish the story" his late father Dusty never could. After losing at WrestleMania 39, Cody defeated Reigns at WrestleMania XL in April 2024 to win the Undisputed WWE Universal Title, ending a reign of over 1,300 days.
    Why is The Bloodline considered the greatest modern storyline?
    It masterfully blended the real Anoa'i family lineage, faction intrigue and a multi-year build, rewarded with a genuinely cathartic WrestleMania payoff — the standard for long-term storytelling in the modern era.

    Eddie Guerrero vs Rey Mysterio

    Two best friends, one custody battle — shock booking meets genuine artistry.

    Eddie vs Rey is a lightning-rod example of shock booking colliding with genuine wrestling quality. Longtime friends Eddie Guerrero and Rey Mysterio feuded in 2005 after Eddie claimed to be the "real father" of Rey's son Dominik, escalating into an over-the-top custody battle contested in the ring. It carries added poignancy given Eddie's death months later.

    Timeline

    1. Early 2005

      The friends and tag partners begin to fracture, their competitive edge curdling into resentment.

    2. Apr 2005

      They meet in a widely praised singles match at WrestleMania 21.

    3. Mid 2005

      The angle turns infamous as Eddie threatens to reveal a "secret," ultimately claiming to be Dominik's biological father.

    4. Aug 2005

      At SummerSlam, they fight a Ladder Match with custody of Dominik as the prize hanging above the ring.

    5. Nov 13, 2005

      Eddie Guerrero dies suddenly, ending one of his final major programs and recasting the entire feud in a somber light.

    The Arc

    Few WWE stories are as hard to separate from their real-life context as this one. On paper, the custody angle was tasteless — a soap-opera premise culminating in a man's parentage dangling in a briefcase above the ring — and it was widely criticized as such. Yet it worked precisely because Eddie and Rey were genuine best friends and two of the finest in-ring performers alive; their real bond gave even the most lurid beats an undercurrent of authentic emotion, and their matches were consistently excellent.

    Eddie played the descent brilliantly, a man consumed by envy of his friend's family, and Rey answered with the sympathetic fire that had made him a star since his lucha days. It was one of Eddie's last major programs before his sudden death in November 2005, and that tragedy transformed how the feud is remembered — a garish story now shadowed by real grief, a final chapter between two friends who genuinely loved each other. Years later, the WCW classic that first paired them remains the purer testament to their chemistry.

    The Wrestlers

    The feud belonged to two real-life friends: Eddie Guerrero, the lovable liar-and-cheater of the Guerrero dynasty, and Rey Mysterio, the masked lucha icon. Rey's real son, Dominik, was written into the story as the stakes and is noted here in plain text.

    FAQ

    What was the Eddie vs Rey custody angle?
    In 2005, longtime friends Eddie Guerrero and Rey Mysterio feuded after Eddie claimed to be the "real father" of Rey's son Dominik, escalating into a custody battle contested in the ring — including a ladder match where custody papers hung above the ring.
    Were Eddie Guerrero and Rey Mysterio real-life friends?
    Yes. They were close real-life friends, which made the 2005 custody angle emotionally charged and, in hindsight, poignant given Eddie's death in November 2005.
    Did Eddie and Rey wrestle before 2005?
    Yes. Years earlier they had one of the greatest cruiserweight matches in American history — a Title vs Mask bout at WCW Halloween Havoc 1997 that helped make Rey a star.

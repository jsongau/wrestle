The TLC Tag Wars

Three teams, tables, ladders and chairs — a whole match genre, invented.

The TLC tag wars revitalized tag team wrestling and created an entire match genre. The Hardy Boyz, the Dudley Boyz and Edge & Christian — including MAT roster members Edge, Bubba Ray Dudley (Bully Ray) and Lita — pushed tag wrestling to violent, high-flying extremes across a series of ladder and Tables, Ladders & Chairs matches that WWE still runs today.

Timeline

  1. 1999

    The three teams begin trading the tag titles, each bringing a distinct identity — the daredevil Hardys, the table-obsessed Dudleys, the cocky Edge & Christian.

  2. Apr 2000

    The Triangle Ladder Match at WrestleMania 2000 raises the bar and hints at the carnage to come.

  3. Aug 2000

    The first-ever TLC match at SummerSlam 2000 adds tables and chairs to the ladder formula.

  4. Apr 2001

    TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven — with Rhyno, Spike Dudley and Lita adding chaos — becomes the definitive spot-fest, won by Edge & Christian.

The Arc

What made the trilogy special was contrast. The Hardys were fearless high-flyers who'd throw their bodies off anything; the Dudleys were the hardcore tacticians obsessed with putting people through tables; Edge & Christian were the smirking opportunists always ready to steal a win. Three clear identities meant that even amid the chaos, every spot told a story about who these teams were.

Across the Triangle Ladder Match, the first TLC, and the climactic TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven, they escalated the violence into something genuinely new — Edge's mid-air spear on a dangling Jeff Hardy, the Swanton bombs from the top of ladders, bodies crashing through stacked tables. It was car-crash choreography elevated to art, and it single-handedly made tag team wrestling appointment viewing again. The TLC match they invented became a permanent fixture of the WWE calendar, and every modern multi-man ladder match owes a debt to these three teams.

FAQ

Which teams were in the TLC tag wars?
Three teams: The Hardy Boyz (Matt & Jeff), The Dudley Boyz (Bubba Ray & D-Von) and Edge & Christian — each with a distinct identity, who pushed tag wrestling to violent, high-flying extremes across a series of ladder and TLC matches.
Did these teams invent the TLC match?
Yes. Their rivalry effectively invented the Tables, Ladders & Chairs match concept that WWE still uses today, evolving from the Triangle Ladder Match at WrestleMania 2000 through TLC at SummerSlam 2000 and TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven.
Why did the TLC tag wars matter?
They revitalized tag team wrestling and created an entire match genre, turning car-crash choreography into art with iconic spots like Jeff Hardy's Swanton off ladders and spears through tables.

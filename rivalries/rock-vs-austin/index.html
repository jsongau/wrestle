The Rock vs Stone Cold Steve Austin

Three WrestleManias, one golden age — arguably the greatest 'Mania rivalry ever.

The Rock vs Steve Austin is arguably the greatest WrestleMania rivalry of all time. The two biggest stars of the Attitude Era headlined three WrestleManias against each other — The Rock and Stone Cold Steve Austin — each with a different dynamic, pairing the two men most responsible for wrestling's turn-of-the-century boom.

Timeline

  1. Mar 1999

    WrestleMania XV: babyface Austin beats Corporate-champion Rock, the first chapter of the trilogy.

  2. Apr 2001

    WrestleMania X-Seven, in Austin's home state of Texas: Austin shockingly turns heel, aligns with Mr. McMahon and batters Rock with a chair to win the WWF Title — one of the most debated finishes in company history.

  3. Mar 2003

    WrestleMania XIX: The Rock finally beats Austin in the emotional rubber match, which doubled as a send-off for Austin's in-ring career.

The Arc

What made the rivalry timeless was that it never repeated itself. In 1999 it was the plucky anti-hero toppling the smug Corporate champion. In 2001, on the grandest stage of the boom, it inverted everything: Austin, the people's rebel, sold his soul to his archenemy Vince McMahon to finally put Rock away — a brutal, blood-soaked heel turn that closed the Attitude Era's golden age on a shocking note. By 2003, with Austin's body failing him, the rubber match became a genuine farewell, The Rock finally scoring the win he'd never had over his greatest rival.

Off screen the two shared genuine mutual respect, a friendly rivalry that pushed both to elevate the other. Their contrasting styles — Rock's electric charisma and comic timing against Austin's snarling everyman intensity — and their unmatched microphone work turned every encounter into event television.

The Wrestlers

The rivalry belonged to two men and two men only: The Rock, the most electrifying man in sports entertainment, and Stone Cold Steve Austin, the beer-swilling Texas Rattlesnake. Their WrestleMania X-Seven war also folded in Mr. McMahon, whose alliance with Austin gave the 2001 match its unforgettable twist.

FAQ

How many times did The Rock and Steve Austin wrestle at WrestleMania?
Three times, each with a different dynamic: WrestleMania XV (1999), WrestleMania X-Seven (2001) and WrestleMania XIX (2003).
Who won the Rock vs Austin WrestleMania matches?
Austin won at WrestleMania XV and again at WrestleMania X-Seven (turning heel and aligning with McMahon), while The Rock won the emotional rubber match at WrestleMania XIX, which served as a send-off for Austin's in-ring career.
Why is Rock vs Austin considered the greatest WrestleMania rivalry?
It paired the two biggest stars of the Attitude Era at the absolute peak of the boom. Their chemistry, contrasting styles and mic work made it the defining rivalry of a golden age.

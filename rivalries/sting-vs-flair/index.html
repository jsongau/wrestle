    Sting vs Ric Flair

    The rivalry that made a star — and bookended WCW itself.

    Sting vs Ric Flair is the cornerstone rivalry of NWA and WCW. A young Sting battled NWA World Champion Ric Flair to a legendary 45-minute time-limit draw at the first Clash of the Champions in 1988 — the match that made Sting a national star. Their rivalry spanned more than a decade, from Sting's first world title win in 1990 to the main event of the very last Nitro in 2001.

    Timeline

    1. Mar 27, 1988

      At the first Clash of the Champions, the up-and-coming Sting fights NWA World Champion Ric Flair to a 45-minute time-limit draw, instantly elevating Sting to main-event status.

    2. Jul 1990

      Sting defeats Flair at the Great American Bash to win his first NWA/WCW World Heavyweight Championship.

    3. 1990s

      The two trade matches and allegiances across NWA and WCW as Sting evolves from bright-eyed babyface to WCW's franchise hero.

    4. Late 1990s

      Sting reinvents himself as the silent "Crow" during the nWo era, but Flair remains a recurring measuring stick throughout WCW's history.

    5. Mar 26, 2001

      Sting vs Ric Flair headlines the final episode of WCW Monday Nitro — a fitting last chapter for two men who defined the company.

    The Arc

    If the nWo invasion is the story WCW is most famous for, Sting vs Flair is the rivalry the company was built on. Their 1988 Clash of the Champions draw is a foundational text: an established "Nature Boy" carrying a hungry newcomer through 45 flawless minutes and, in doing so, anointing a new star. From that night forward, Sting's career was measured against Flair's, and the contrast — Flair's cocky, decadent veteran against Sting's earnest, colorful hero — never stopped drawing.

    Over the following years Sting climbed to the top by going through Flair, capturing his first world title from him in 1990 and trading the championship and top-babyface mantle with him repeatedly. Even as Sting transformed into the brooding "Crow" to stand against the nWo, Flair endured as the constant against whom his legacy was framed. That the two shared the ring in the main event of the final Nitro in March 2001 was no accident — it was the only fitting way for WCW to close the book. Both men are now WWE Hall of Famers, and their rivalry remains a touchstone for what made Southern-rooted WCW special.

    The Wrestlers

    The rivalry belonged to two WCW icons: Sting, the franchise player who spent his whole career loyal to WCW, and Ric Flair, the 16-time world champion "Nature Boy" and figurehead of the Four Horsemen. Both are enshrined in the WWE Hall of Fame.

    FAQ

    When did Sting and Ric Flair first main-event together?
    At the first Clash of the Champions on March 27, 1988, when a young Sting battled NWA World Champion Ric Flair to a 45-minute time-limit draw — the match that made Sting a national star.
    Did Sting ever beat Ric Flair for the world title?
    Yes. Sting won his first NWA/WCW World Heavyweight Championship from Ric Flair at the Great American Bash in July 1990, one of the signature moments of their long rivalry.
    Was Sting vs Flair the last match on WCW Nitro?
    Yes. Sting vs Ric Flair was the main event of the final episode of WCW Monday Nitro on March 26, 2001, a fitting bookend for two men who defined the company.

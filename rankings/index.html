    How we rate

    The Wrestle Lore rating is an editorial score out of five stars. It weighs the two most cited critical yardsticks — Dave Meltzer's Wrestling Observer star ratings and the Cagematch community's out-of-ten scores — against a match's historical significance and lasting influence. A five-star Wrestle Lore match is one that is both critically elite and era-defining. Read the full methodology →

    The WrestleMania Streak

    No storyline shapes this ranking more than The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak. From 1991 onward, The Undertaker won every WrestleMania match he wrestled, building a mystique that reached 21–0 before Brock Lesnar shockingly ended it — 21–1 — at WrestleMania XXX in 2014. Three of the club's entries are Streak defences: the back-to-back masterpieces with Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania XXV and XXVI, and the "End of an Era" Hell in a Cell with Triple H at WrestleMania XXVIII. The Streak turned every one of his April matches into an event unto itself — which is why the Deadman appears at the top of the list.

    Frequently Asked

    What is the Wrestle Lore Five-Star Club?
    It is our ranking of every match that earns a perfect five-star Wrestle Lore rating — twelve matches across WWE, WCW, TNA, ROH and NXT. The Wrestle Lore rating blends Meltzer's Wrestling Observer stars, Cagematch community scores and historical weight. See how we rate →
    What is the greatest wrestling match of the modern era?
    By Wrestle Lore's ranking, The Undertaker vs Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania XXV (2009) tops the list — routinely voted the greatest WrestleMania match ever, with a Cagematch score around 9.6. Angle vs Benoit and Punk vs Cena round out the top three.
    What was The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak?
    The Undertaker won his first 21 WrestleMania matches — "The Streak" — before Brock Lesnar ended it 21–1 at WrestleMania XXX in 2014. Three club entries are Streak defences: WM25 and WM26 vs Shawn Michaels, and WM28 vs Triple H.
    Are rankings ever updated?
    Yes. Wrestle Lore ratings reflect critical consensus as of mid-2026 and can be revised as historians re-evaluate matches (Meltzer himself has revised several over the years). Wrestle Lore Insiders can also add their own ratings on each match page.

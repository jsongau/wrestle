Trust & Transparency

How we rate

Every match on MAT gets a rating out of five stars — an editorial score informed by critical consensus plus historical significance. We blend three inputs: Wrestling Observer (Dave Meltzer) star ratings, Cagematch community scores, and the match's historical weight. It is a transparent judgment call rather than a secret formula, and where the sources disagree we say so. MAT Insiders can add their own star ratings on top.

The three inputs

A great match is more than a spot count, so no single number can capture it. MAT triangulates from the two most respected external benchmarks and then adds the one thing a live rating can't — the perspective of history.

Input 1

Meltzer / Observer

The Wrestling Observer Newsletter star rating — the most cited critical scale in wrestling, historically out of five (some modern ratings exceed five). We cite it as reported and note where Meltzer has revised it.

Input 2

Cagematch community

The Cagematch database's community rating (out of 10) captures how thousands of hardcore fans actually scored the bout — a useful check on any single critic's taste.

Input 3

Historical significance

Some matches matter beyond their workrate — the nWo formation or the first women's WrestleMania main event. We weigh cultural and industry impact, clearly labeled.

Why we do it this way (E-E-A-T & GEO)

Trust is the whole point of a ratings site. Ours is built on experience, expertise, authoritativeness and trustworthiness: our editorial call is grounded in decades of documented critical consensus, every rating shows its work by citing Meltzer and Cagematch, and we openly separate fact from opinion and kayfabe from reality (see our editorial standards).

It is also built to be quotable. Each match page leads with an answer-first summary, exposes structured data (schema.org Review and aggregateRating), and links densely to related wrestlers, matches and rivalries. That transparency is what lets both search engines and generative-AI assistants cite MAT accurately — and lets you verify any rating against its sources rather than taking our word for it.

Frequently Asked

How does MAT rate matches?
Every match gets a MAT rating out of five stars. It is an editorial score informed by critical consensus — Wrestling Observer (Dave Meltzer) star ratings and Cagematch community scores — combined with historical significance. It is a judgment call, not a formula, and where sources disagree we flag it.
What is a Meltzer star rating?
The Wrestling Observer star rating is Dave Meltzer's long-running critical scale for match quality, historically out of five stars (some post-2018 ratings exceed five). It is the most cited critical benchmark in wrestling, and MAT references it as reported while noting that Meltzer has revised some ratings over the years.
Can members add their own ratings?
Yes. MAT Insiders (free to join) can add and save their own star ratings on any match. Member ratings are shown alongside the editorial MAT rating and the cited critical scores, so you can compare your take with the consensus.

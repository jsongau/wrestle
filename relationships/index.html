    Frequently Asked

    Which wrestlers are related in the Anoa'i family?
    Roman Reigns and The Rock are cousins; Roman is the son of Sika of The Wild Samoans and the storyline Tribal Chief of the Bloodline alongside real cousins The Usos and Solo Sikoa. The extended family includes Rikishi, Yokozuna, Umaga, Afa & Sika, Nia Jax and Tamina. Note that Samoa Joe, though Samoan, is not an Anoa'i.
    Which wrestlers are married to each other?
    Real-life couples include Triple H and Stephanie McMahon, Edge and Beth Phoenix, Becky Lynch and Seth Rollins, Daniel Bryan and Brie Bella, CM Punk and AJ Lee, and Jon Moxley and Renee Paquette.
    Who were the members of The Shield?
    The Shield were Roman Reigns, Seth Rollins and Dean Ambrose (now Jon Moxley). They debuted in 2012 and split in 2014 when Rollins betrayed his brothers — read the full Shield rise and betrayal.
    Is Charlotte Flair related to Ric Flair?
    Yes — Charlotte Flair is the daughter of 16-time world champion Ric Flair, and honours him with the Figure-Eight leglock and his signature robes. She is also a founding member of the Four Horsewomen.

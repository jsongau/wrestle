    Media & Creators

    The interviewers, podcasters, and journalists who cover pro wrestling. These are people whose main job is telling the story, not performing it. For the in-ring roster, see the wrestlers directory.

    This tab lists media figures only. Active in-ring performers who also appear on camera stay on the athlete grid at /wrestlers/. Two dual-role figures below, Bully Ray and Jim Cornette, are broadcasters now and are cross-linked to any wrestler page that exists.

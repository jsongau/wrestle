    Chris Van Vliet

    Emmy-winning host who runs the top long-form wrestler-interview show in the space, Insight with Chris Van Vliet.

    Who he is

    Chris Van Vliet is an interviewer and podcast host whose primary work is sitting down with wrestlers and entertainers, not performing in the ring. His show, Insight with Chris Van Vliet, has become the leading long-form destination for wrestler interviews, built on unhurried conversations rather than quick soundbites.

    He is a media figure, so he does not appear on the athlete grid. For the in-ring roster he covers, see the wrestlers directory.

    The platform

    Insight with Chris Van Vliet runs as a podcast and across two YouTube channels, with a combined audience of around two million subscribers. On November 6, 2025, Van Vliet joined the Cumulus Podcast Network, which now distributes the show.

    The format is the differentiator: full-length interviews that let guests talk through their careers, rather than short press hits. That is why so many wrestlers use the show to tell longer versions of their stories.

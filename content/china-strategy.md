# MAT China Go-to-Market Strategy

MAT's China strategy is a growth wedge modeling how a wrestling membership product would enter the largest untapped fan market. Because YouTube and most Western platforms are blocked in China, the plan localizes content into Simplified Chinese, distributes clips on Chinese platforms (Bilibili, Douyin, Weibo, Youku, WeChat), builds acquisition around QR-code and WeChat funnels, and maps membership payments to WeChat Pay and Alipay — while honestly flagging ICP-license and data-localization realities.

## Why does MAT target China?

WWE has repeatedly identified China as the largest untapped wrestling market, and MAT models the entry as a demonstration of membership-growth thinking. The China play is one of three top-of-funnel acquisition engines alongside SEO and GEO. The Simplified-Chinese entry lives at /zh/.

## How is content distributed in China?

Because YouTube is blocked in China, wrestling clips live on Chinese platforms: Bilibili, Douyin (the China version of TikTok), Weibo, Youku and WeChat Channels. MAT's China pages therefore embed or link Youku and Bilibili rather than YouTube, matching where Chinese fans actually watch.

## How is MAT localized for China?

MAT provides a /zh/ Simplified-Chinese entry with hreflang tags (zh-Hans) and culturally tuned hero content. It uses Chinese ring names familiar to local fans — for example, 送葬者 for The Undertaker and "巨石"强森 for The Rock — so the entity pages bind to how Chinese audiences already refer to the stars.

## How does MAT acquire Chinese fans?

The China acquisition funnel is built for local UX norms: a WeChat Mini-Program / Official Account funnel concept, QR-code-first capture (the standard entry point in China), and a Xiaohongshu (RED) content strategy aimed at younger fans. These replace the email/web-form patterns used in Western markets.

## How does membership and payment work in China?

MAT maps its tiered membership to China's payment rails — WeChat Pay and Alipay — and to how Chinese fans already pay for fandom, such as super-chats and digital collectibles. This makes the paid Ringside tier convertible within the local ecosystem rather than depending on Western card processing.

## What compliance realities does MAT acknowledge?

MAT flags the ICP-license requirement (needed to legally host a site served in mainland China) and data-localization rules honestly, treating them as real constraints rather than glossing over them. Acknowledging these signals maturity about what a genuine China launch requires. See the China entry at /zh/ and how member data is handled at /membership/.

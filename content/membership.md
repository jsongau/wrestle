# MAT Insider Membership

MAT Insider is MAT's membership program, built as a three-tier funnel that turns free crawlable content into a paid membership base. The core database is free forever; the free Insider tier unlocks full ratings, the classic-match archive and the weekly newsletter; and the paid Ringside tier ($6/month, illustrative) adds the premium archive, live watch-alongs, community and collectible drops.

## What are the MAT membership tiers?

MAT has three membership tiers: Fan, Insider and Ringside.

### Fan tier ($0 / forever)

The Fan tier is the full crawlable database, free. It includes all wrestler profiles, all match pages, and the rivalries and relationship map. It does NOT include full star-ratings, the classic-match archive or the weekly newsletter.

### Insider tier ($0 / free to join — most popular)

The Insider tier is free to join and includes everything in Fan plus: full MAT star-ratings, the ability to rate and save your own matches, classic-match archive access, the weekly "Rewind" newsletter, and a founding-member badge. It does NOT include watch-alongs and community (those are Ringside).

### Ringside tier ($6 / month)

The Ringside tier is the paid, die-hard tier. It includes everything in Insider plus: the premium archive and deep-dives, live PLE (Premium Live Event) watch-alongs, a members-only community, an ad-free experience with early features, and quarterly collectible drops.

## Can I cancel MAT membership anytime?

Yes. Paid tiers are month-to-month and can be cancelled anytime; members keep access until the end of the billing period.

## How is member data handled?

MAT collects the minimum data needed to run the membership and never sells member data. For China members, payments and data follow local rails and localization rules (see the 中文 page at /zh/).

## How does the MAT membership funnel work?

The MAT funnel is a six-stage lifecycle that moves a broad fan audience and a waitlist into paid membership:

1. Acquire — SEO, GEO and China distribution. Crawlable content, AI-citation and Chinese-platform distribution pull free, high-intent traffic to entity pages.
2. Engage — Ratings and the graph. Interlinked wrestlers, matches and relationships create session depth, the signal that predicts conversion.
3. Capture — Waitlist and gated value. Gated full-ratings and the archive convert anonymous readers into known email/SMS contacts.
4. Convert — Lifecycle to paid. Segmented welcome, onboarding and win-back journeys move Insiders to the paid Ringside tier.
5. Retain — Drops and events. Event-tied pushes, benefit drops and watch-alongs drive renewals and reduce churn.
6. Measure — Weekly readout. Waitlist growth, email CTR, landing-page conversion, checkout completion, paid conversion and retention are tracked in a weekly readout.

## What is the MAT waitlist?

The waitlist is the top of MAT's membership funnel. Fans join with an email (and optional first name, favorite promotion and mobile number for SMS drops) to get first access at launch and a founding-member badge. Every signup enters a lifecycle of four emails: welcome → onboarding → first-value → conversion, and is segmented by favorite promotion for tailored drops. As of the prototype, 12,840 fans are on the waitlist, the target waitlist-to-member conversion is 38%, and the welcome journey runs four lifecycle emails.

## Why does the membership model matter?

MAT is designed as a working demonstration of membership-growth thinking: an acquisition surface (SEO/GEO-driven traffic) feeds an engagement loop (ratings, interlinking), which drives waitlist capture, tiered paid membership, and lifecycle retention.

# MAT Rivalries Index

This is the index of the 15 landmark rivalries and storylines documented on MAT, spanning WWF/WWE, WCW, ECW and NXT (1996–2024). Each entry gives a one-line summary and its page link path. Full feud pages are at /rivalries/<slug>/.

## The 15 rivalries

- Stone Cold Steve Austin vs. Mr. McMahon — The boss-vs-employee war (1997–1999) that weaponized working-class resentment, defined the Attitude Era and became the template for every authority-figure feud since. — /rivalries/austin-vs-mcmahon/
- The Rock vs. Steve Austin — The two biggest Attitude Era stars headlined a WrestleMania trilogy (XV, X-Seven, XIX), including Austin's shocking 2001 heel turn — arguably the greatest WrestleMania rivalry ever. — /rivalries/rock-vs-austin/
- The Undertaker vs. Kane (Brothers of Destruction) — Kane debuted at Badd Blood 1997 to attack his kayfabe brother; the family premise (Undertaker started the fire that scarred Kane) gave their monster matches real narrative stakes. — /rivalries/undertaker-vs-kane/
- The Bloodline Saga — Roman Reigns' "Tribal Chief" era (2020–2024), a multi-year Anoa'i-family drama that peaked with Sami Zayn's Montreal turn and Cody Rhodes "finishing the story" at WrestleMania XL. — /rivalries/the-bloodline/
- Johnny Gargano vs. Tommaso Ciampa (#DIY) — NXT's best long-form feud (2016–2019): beloved tag partners torn apart by Ciampa's betrayal, settled across brutal, emotionally charged grudge matches. — /rivalries/gargano-vs-ciampa/
- The nWo Invasion of WCW — Hall, Nash and a heel-turned Hogan formed the New World Order in 1996, an invading-army angle that drove Nitro to 83 straight weeks of ratings wins over Raw. — /rivalries/nwo-invasion/
- Bret Hart vs. Shawn Michaels (Montreal Screwjob) — The 1997 reality-shattering double-cross in which McMahon and Michaels took the title from Hart against his wishes, birthing the "Mr. McMahon" character and years of real heat. — /rivalries/bret-vs-hbk-montreal/
- The Shield: Rise and Betrayal — Roman Reigns, Seth Rollins and Dean Ambrose debuted at Survivor Series 2012, dominated as a unit, then split in June 2014 when Rollins turned — launching three main-event singles careers. — /rivalries/shield-rise-and-betrayal/
- Daniel Bryan and the "Yes Movement" — An organic fan groundswell (2013–2014) forced WWE to rebook WrestleMania XXX around Bryan, who won two matches in one night to capture the WWE World Heavyweight Title. — /rivalries/yes-movement/
- CM Punk's "Pipebomb" / Summer of Punk — Punk's 2011 worked-shoot promo and Money in the Bank win in his native Chicago briefly restored Attitude-Era unpredictability to the PG era. — /rivalries/cm-punk-pipebomb/
- Eddie Guerrero vs. Rey Mysterio (Custody Angle) — The 2005 SmackDown feud in which longtime friends fought over the "custody" of Rey's son Dominik — over-the-top booking married to excellent in-ring work, made poignant by Eddie's death that November. — /rivalries/eddie-vs-rey/
- Hardy Boyz vs. Dudley Boyz vs. Edge & Christian (TLC Tag Wars) — Three teams (1999–2001) pushed tag wrestling to violent, high-flying extremes and effectively invented the Tables, Ladders & Chairs match. — /rivalries/tlc-tag-wars/
- The Invasion Angle (WWF vs. WCW/ECW Alliance) — After WWF bought WCW, the 2001 dream-war underdelivered because top WCW stars were unavailable; it's the biggest missed-opportunity storyline in history. — /rivalries/attitude-era-invasion/
- Sting vs. Ric Flair — A career-long WCW rivalry beginning at the first Clash of the Champions in 1988, pitting WCW's homegrown "franchise" against the flamboyant "Nature Boy." — /rivalries/sting-vs-flair/
- D-Generation X vs. The Nation of Domination — The 1998 faction war that, through raunchy comedy and one-upmanship (including DX's mocking parody), simultaneously elevated Triple H and The Rock into main-event stars. — /rivalries/dx-vs-nation/

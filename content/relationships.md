# Real-Life Relationships in Pro Wrestling (Modern Era)

The MAT relationship web maps the real-life connections between wrestlers — marriages and romances, wrestling families and bloodlines, factions and tag teams, and trainer lineages. This density of real-world ties is what makes the modern era's roster a single interconnected story. Scope: WWE/WWF, WCW, ECW, TNA/Impact and NXT.

## Which wrestlers are real-life couples?

Many top wrestlers are married to or have dated other wrestlers:

- Triple H (Paul Levesque) is married to Stephanie McMahon (wed 2003; three daughters; both are WWE/TKO executives, Triple H as Chief Content Officer).
- Edge (Adam Copeland) is married to Beth Phoenix (two Hall of Famers; married 2016; both later in AEW). Edge also had an earlier real-life romance with Lita (Amy Dumas) around 2005 that was written into WWE TV.
- Seth Rollins (Colby Lopez) is married to Becky Lynch (Rebecca Quin), wed 2021, with a daughter, Roux.
- CM Punk (Phil Brooks) is married to AJ Lee (AJ Mendez), a former WWE Divas Champion.
- Daniel Bryan (Bryan Danielson) is married to Brie Bella (Brianna Garcia), wed 2014, part of the Bella Twins.
- John Cena had a high-profile relationship (circa 2012–2018) with Nikki Bella (Brie's twin), later split.
- Rey Mysterio (Óscar Gutiérrez) is married to Angie Gutiérrez, parents of Dominik and Aalyah.
- Charlotte Flair (Ashley Fliehr) was married to Andrade (2022), later reported to have marital difficulties.
- The Miz (Mike Mizanin) is married to Maryse Ouellet; Miro (Rusev) is married to CJ Perry (Lana); Naomi (Trinity Fatu) is married to Jimmy Uso, marrying into the Anoa'i family.
- Triple H also had an earlier real relationship with Chyna (Joanie Laurer) that ended around the time he began dating Stephanie McMahon.

## What is the Anoa'i / Samoan family (Bloodline)?

The Anoa'i family is the most influential dynasty in wrestling and the real basis for the on-screen Bloodline. Roman Reigns (Joe Anoa'i) is the son of Sika (one half of The Wild Samoans). The Usos, Jimmy and Jey (Jonathan and Joshua Fatu), and their younger brother Solo Sikoa (Joseph Fatu) are sons of Rikishi (Solofa Fatu) and first cousins of Roman Reigns. The Rock (Dwayne Johnson) is the grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia and son of Rocky Johnson, tied into the family through his mother Ata. Umaga (Eddie Fatu), a brother of Rikishi, and Yokozuna (Rodney Anoa'i) are family members; patriarchs Afa & Sika (The Wild Samoans) are foundational trainers. Nia Jax and Tamina (daughter of Jimmy "Superfly" Snuka) are also in the extended family. Note: Samoa Joe is NOT a blood member of the Anoa'i family — a common misconception he has publicly clarified.

## What is the Hart family?

The Hart family descends from Stu Hart, founder of Stampede Wrestling and the legendary "Hart Dungeon" training school. Bret "Hitman" Hart is one of 12 Hart siblings; Owen Hart was his youngest brother (died in a 1999 in-ring accident). British Bulldog (Davey Boy Smith) and Jim "The Anvil" Neidhart married into the family as Bret's brothers-in-law. Natalya, Neidhart's daughter and Stu's granddaughter, is a third-generation Hart married to Tyson Kidd (TJ Wilson).

## What is the Guerrero family?

The Guerrero family is a Mexican lucha dynasty founded by Gory Guerrero. Eddie Guerrero was his youngest wrestling son; Chavo Guerrero Jr. is Eddie's nephew (son of Chavo Guerrero Sr., "Chavo Classic"). Vickie Guerrero is Eddie's widow, later an on-screen authority figure.

## What is the Rhodes family?

The Rhodes family is led by "The American Dream" Dusty Rhodes, a patriarch and NXT trainer. Cody Rhodes is his son, and Goldust (Dustin Rhodes) is Cody's half-brother; both are Dusty's sons. The Bloodline saga was framed around Cody finishing the championship story his late father never completed.

## What is the Flair family?

Ric Flair, the 16-time "Nature Boy" world champion, is the father of Charlotte Flair, a record 14-time women's world champion who echoes his Figure Four and robes. His sons Reid Flair (died 2013) and David Flair (wrestled in WCW) also pursued the ring.

## What is the Mysterio family?

Rey Mysterio is the real father of Dominik Mysterio (Dominik Gutiérrez); the two feuded on-screen after Dominik's Judgment Day heel turn (2022–2023). Rey took his ring name from his uncle, Rey Misterio Sr. Rey's daughter Aalyah also appeared in the family storyline.

## What other wrestling families are notable?

Randy Orton is a third-generation star, son of "Cowboy" Bob Orton and grandson of Bob Orton Sr. Bray Wyatt (Windham Rotunda, deceased 2023) was the son of Mike Rotunda (IRS) and grandson of Blackjack Mulligan, and brother of Bo Dallas (who played "Uncle Howdy"). Curtis Axel is the son of Curt Hennig ("Mr. Perfect") and grandson of Larry "The Axe" Hennig. Ted DiBiase Jr. is the son of the "Million Dollar Man."

## What are the major factions and tag teams?

- The Shield — Roman Reigns, Seth Rollins and Dean Ambrose (Jon Moxley).
- Evolution — Triple H, Ric Flair, Randy Orton and Batista.
- D-Generation X (DX) — Triple H and Shawn Michaels (also Chyna, X-Pac, the New Age Outlaws).
- The Hardy Boyz — Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy; Edge and Christian; The Dudley Boyz / Team 3D — Bully Ray and D-Von Dudley.
- The Rockers — Shawn Michaels and Marty Jannetty.
- nWo (New World Order) — led by Hollywood Hogan (WCW).
- Bullet Club — founded by Finn Balor (as Prince Devitt), later led by AJ Styles (NJPW).
- The Addiction / Bad Influence — Christopher Daniels and Frankie Kazarian.
- The Four Horsewomen (WWE) — Charlotte Flair, Becky Lynch, Sasha Banks (Mercedes Mone) and Bayley.
- The Bloodline — Roman Reigns, The Usos, Solo Sikoa and Jey Uso (the Anoa'i storyline faction).

## Who trained whom (trainer lineages)?

- Stu Hart trained Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Chris Benoit, Chris Jericho, British Bulldog, Natalya and many more in the Hart Dungeon.
- Afa & Sika (The Wild Samoans) trained much of the Anoa'i family and others at the Wild Samoan Training Center.
- Shawn Michaels is head of NXT creative and a mentor to Daniel Bryan and the current NXT generation.
- Triple H is the creative architect and mentor of the NXT generation (Balor, Nakamura, Owens, Charlotte, Becky, Bayley, Sasha).
- Dusty Rhodes was an NXT trainer/creative mentor to Bayley, Sasha Banks and the Four Horsewomen era.
- Paul Heyman is the advocate/manager for Brock Lesnar, CM Punk and Roman Reigns, and the ECW founder who developed RVD, the Dudleys and others.
- Killer Kowalski was Triple H's original trainer.

## What are notable real-life friendships and backstage heat?

- Sami Zayn (El Generico) and Kevin Owens (Kevin Steen) are best friends since the indies; their real bond fuels their on-screen feud/alliance cycle.
- The Kliq — Shawn Michaels, Kevin Nash, Scott Hall, Triple H and X-Pac — was the 1990s backstage power group behind the 1996 MSG "Curtain Call."
- Bret Hart carried years of real heat with Shawn Michaels and Vince McMahon after the 1997 Montreal Screwjob (later reconciled with Michaels).
- Eddie Guerrero and Rey Mysterio were genuine close friends, which made their 2005 custody angle emotionally charged.
- Steve Austin and The Rock were real-life friendly rivals who elevated each other.
- Cody Rhodes co-founded AEW (2019) with the Young Bucks and Kenny Omega before returning to WWE in 2022.

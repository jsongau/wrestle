MAT China Strategy

How MAT reaches Chinese wrestling fans — a content-first, platform-native plan with an honest read on the Great Firewall, ICP, payments and PIPL.

See the 中文 edition → 中文 membership →

Reaching Chinese wrestling fans is a content-first, not a site-first, problem. Because YouTube, Google and the major Western social networks are blocked and a foreign-hosted site loads slowly (10–30s) behind the Great Firewall, MAT wins by publishing native content on Bilibili, Douyin, Weibo, Youku and Xiaohongshu, and by running an owned WeChat funnel (Official Account → Mini-Program → WeChat Pay/Alipay). ICP filing and onshore hosting come later — only when paid membership hits real scale — because an ICP is a legal/hosting decision, not a speed switch.

2 · The content-first playbook

Step 1 · Repurpose

Native video, no ICP

Cut MAT's best highlights, five-star breakdowns and data explainers into native Bilibili + Douyin + Weibo accounts. This needs no ICP and dodges the Great Firewall entirely.

Step 2 · Own

WeChat as CRM

Stand up a WeChat Official Account as the owned retention channel — weekly classic-match rewinds, event recaps, and the entry point to the membership funnel.

Step 3 · Localize

A survivable zh edition

Serve a lightweight zh-Hans site tuned to survive the GFW: no Google Fonts, no YouTube, no googleapis/gstatic, self-hosted assets, Chinese video embeds only.

Step 4 · Bridge

QR-first everything

Every share point gets a QR: follow the OA, open the Mini-Program, pay, or share a wrestler card. White quiet-zone, ≥160px, high contrast, "长按识别二维码" for in-app viewing.

Content mix

Stats travel

MAT's edge is data — five-star ratings, records, historical significance. Numbered lists and tale-of-the-tape graphics translate cleanly and are inherently shareable on RED and Weibo.

Cadence

Event-tied cycles

Anchor pushes to WWE PLEs and AEW events in China-friendly time slots: pre-event hype on Weibo, live clips on Douyin, full breakdowns on Bilibili the next day.

4 · Payments

Rails

WeChat Pay + Alipay

The two dominant methods; locals expect native Pay. Run memberships via a Service Account or Mini-Program (WeChat Pay) plus Alipay.

Cross-border

Foreign merchants

WeChat Pay/Alipay cross-border (or aggregators) exist for foreign merchants but need a real merchant entity + KYC and settle in your currency.

Tourists

Cards-in-app

Both apps now accept international credit cards linked inside the app for inbound visitors — a fallback, not the default expectation for locals.

5 · ICP & PIPL — the honest reality

ItemWhat it actually means
ICP filing (ICP备案 / "beian")Required to legally host a site on mainland-China servers for informational content. Provincial MIIT approval; needs a China business entity/legal presence.
ICP commercial license (ICP证)Additionally required if you sell/charge (paid membership, e-commerce, ads-as-service). Must hold the filing first.
Hosting outside ChinaNo ICP required — but you're behind the GFW: slow, throttled or intermittently blocked. An ICP does not fix speed by itself; it's a legal/hosting decision, not a performance switch.
Legal and fast in ChinaGenerally needs a China entity (or partner/agent), an ICP filing, and onshore or "hybrid"/China-edge CDN hosting.
PIPL / CSL / DSL (data localization)Personal data of China users generally must be stored in China; cross-border transfer needs security assessment/standard contract/consent.

MAT's honest take: don't chase ICP first. Win on-platform (Bilibili/Douyin/Weibo/WeChat) where the audience and the fast infra already are; pursue ICP + onshore hosting only when there's a real paid-membership-at-scale business case. Because MAT uses no browser storage and minimal PII, it is already low-risk under PIPL — the China edition keeps it that way.

6 · Localization, hreflang & China-GEO

Localization + hreflang

  • Serve a real zh-Hans edition at /zh/ with <html lang="zh-Hans">; reciprocal hreflang pairs + x-default.
  • No Google Fonts / googleapis.com / gstatic.com — blocked/slow in China. Use the system CJK stack (Noto Sans SC fallback).
  • Professional human translation for wrestling terminology; provide alternateName in both scripts (e.g. 送葬者 = The Undertaker, 布雷特·哈特 = Bret Hart, 罗曼·雷恩斯 = Roman Reigns).
  • Chinese video embeds only (Bilibili/Youku) via the facade pattern; self-host poster images (more GFW-robust).

China-GEO

  • Baidu is the search engine (Google blocked). If hosting onshore, do Baidu SEO, add Baidu-specific meta, and submit to Baidu Search Console — Baidu weights on-page keywords and freshness more literally.
  • Allow Bytespider (ByteDance, powering Doubao) in robots.txt, alongside GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended and ClaudeBot.
  • Answer-first content + FAQ + hard statistics travel across engines — the same GEO tactics that win globally also win in Doubao / Kimi.
  • The llms.txt map and dense internal linking give Chinese AI crawlers a clean entity graph.

Frequently Asked

Do I need an ICP license to reach Chinese wrestling fans?
No — not to reach fans. An ICP filing is only required to legally host on mainland-China servers, and it doesn't by itself make a foreign-hosted site fast. Go content-first on Bilibili/Douyin/Weibo/WeChat, which needs no ICP; pursue ICP + onshore hosting only when paid membership reaches real scale.
Which platform should a wrestling site prioritize?
Bilibili is the primary video home (the YouTube substitute, embeddable). Douyin drives viral clips, Weibo carries real-time results and hype, and WeChat is the owned CRM/retention and payments channel.
How do Chinese fans pay for membership?
WeChat Pay and Alipay — the two dominant rails; credit cards are rare. Memberships run via a WeChat Service Account or Mini-Program with WeChat Pay, plus Alipay, with QR as the universal follow-and-pay bridge.
What about AI search in China (China-GEO)?
Baidu is the search engine (Google blocked); do Baidu SEO if onshore. For AI, allow Bytespider (ByteDance/Doubao) in robots.txt alongside the Western AI crawlers, and keep answer-first content with hard stats.

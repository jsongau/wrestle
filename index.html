    FAQ

    Frequently asked

    What is Wrestle Lore?
    Wrestle Lore is a crawlable database of modern professional wrestling from 1997 to today: 107 wrestler profiles, 30 five-star matches, 11 championship lineages, plus the rivalries, factions, tag teams and real-life relationships that connect them across WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA, NXT, NJPW and AEW.
    Where can I watch WWE premium live events?
    In the United States, WWE premium live events stream on ESPN. Internationally they stream on Netflix. For the weekly shows, Raw streams on Netflix, SmackDown airs on USA Network and NXT airs on The CW.
    How many times is Ric Flair in the WWE Hall of Fame?
    Twice. Ric Flair was inducted solo in 2008 and again in 2012 as a member of the Four Horsemen, which makes him the most decorated plaque on the wall.
    Which promotions does Wrestle Lore cover?
    Seven promotions with full hubs: WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA, NXT, NJPW and AEW. AAA is covered through its WWE partnership, which began in August 2025 and produced crossover cards like Worlds Collide.

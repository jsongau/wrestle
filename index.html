Frequently Asked

What is MAT?
MAT (Match · Athlete · Timeline) is a crawlable database of modern professional wrestling from 1997 to today, covering WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA/Impact and NXT with wrestler profiles, star-rated matches, rivalries and real-life relationships.
How does MAT rate matches?
Every match gets a MAT rating out of five stars — an editorial score informed by critical consensus (Wrestling Observer star ratings and Cagematch community scores) plus historical significance. MAT Insiders can add their own ratings on top.
Which promotions does MAT cover?
MAT covers the modern era across five major promotions: WWE (formerly WWF), WCW, ECW, TNA/Impact and WWE NXT — from the 1997 Monday Night Wars to the present.
Is MAT free?
Yes. The core database is free forever. MAT Insider (free to join) unlocks full ratings, the classic-match archive and the weekly newsletter; paid Ringside tier adds the premium archive, watch-alongs and community.

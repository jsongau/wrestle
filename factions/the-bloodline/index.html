    The Bloodline

    Formed 2020 WWE Anoa'i family

    The Bloodline formed in 2020 when Roman Reigns aligned with Paul Heyman and the Usos. Built on the real Anoa'i family ties, the group ran through several splits and reformations across the years that followed.

    Reporting through mid-2026 described the active lineup as in flux, with a Solo Sikoa group and a Roman Reigns reunion both in play. Confirm the current roster before treating it as settled.

    Why it mattered

    The Bloodline became the longest and most decorated modern WWE story. Roman Reigns held the Universal Championship past 1,300 days behind the Tribal Chief character and the "acknowledge me" narrative, and the real Anoa'i family history gave the angle weight.

    The 2022 and 2023 arc with Sami Zayn, an outsider adopted into the family before the fallout, drew some of the era's loudest crowd reactions.

    Bayley vs Sasha Banks

    NXT TakeOver: Brooklyn — August 22, 2015 · Barclays Center, Brooklyn, NY

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Bayley won the NXT Women's Championship, dethroning Sasha Banks in the match most credited with proving women's wrestling could steal the show on WWE's grandest stages. It played to a sold-out Barclays Center of more than 15,000 fans — a scale unimaginable for a women's match only a year earlier.

    The clash pitted opposites in perfect balance: the huggable, endlessly hopeful Bayley against the cold, calculating Boss. Banks controlled long stretches with vicious precision, targeting Bayley's injured hand, but the challenger kept clawing back, feeding off a crowd that treated the bout like a main-event world-title fight.

    The near-falls escalated into a genuine spectacle — a top-rope hurricanrana, a reversed Bank Statement, and finally Bayley's Bayley-to-Belly for the three-count. The victory, and its four-star-plus quality, forced the main roster to take the women's division seriously. It remains a cornerstone of the women's revolution and one of the greatest women's matches in company history.

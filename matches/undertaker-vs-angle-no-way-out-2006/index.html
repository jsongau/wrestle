    The Undertaker vs Kurt Angle

    WWE No Way Out — February 19, 2006 · 1st Mariner Arena, Baltimore, MD

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Kurt Angle retained the World Heavyweight Championship in what is frequently cited as one of the very best non-WrestleMania Undertaker matches. Away from the grand-stage spectacle, the two built a tense, counter-heavy near-fall symphony that proved just how far the Deadman's in-ring craft had come.

    The contrast drove the drama: Angle's relentless technical assault — ankle locks, German suplexes, a bottomless bag of counters — against Undertaker's size, reach and refusal to stay down. Every escape from a submission and every kick-out felt like a genuine turning point, the crowd rising with each exchange as the title hung in the balance.

    It elevated the SmackDown World Title picture and remains a favorite among hardcore fans, the kind who argue Undertaker's finest work happened away from WrestleMania. As a showcase of the "Big Evil"-to-Deadman transition years, it is an underrated gem in both men's catalogs.

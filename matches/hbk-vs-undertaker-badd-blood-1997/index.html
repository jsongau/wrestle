    Shawn Michaels vs The Undertaker

    WWF In Your House 18: Badd Blood — October 5, 1997 · Kiel Center, St. Louis, MO

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Shawn Michaels won after Kane made his debut, tore the cell door from its hinges, Tombstoned The Undertaker and let HBK steal the pin. It was the first-ever Hell in a Cell — the blueprint for the most iconic gimmick match WWE would ever create, executed with a violence and drama that later Cells rarely matched.

    The feud demanded a cage that could contain it. Michaels, the cocky centerpiece of the emerging D-Generation X, had spent weeks tormenting The Undertaker, and the giant roofed structure was built specifically to keep interference out. Instead it produced escalating brutality: Michaels bumping off the side of the cell through the announce table, both men bloodied, and the referee dragged out of the ring so the war could continue unchecked.

    The finish rewrote WWF history. The arena lights cut to red, pyro exploded, and Kane — Undertaker's storyline younger brother, appearing for the first time — ripped the cell door open, delivered a Tombstone to the Deadman, and walked away. A barely-conscious Michaels draped an arm across Undertaker for the three-count. The structure's psychology, the blade jobs and the escape spots all originate here, giving Hell in a Cell instant mythology and setting the Undertaker–Kane saga in motion.

    FAQ

    Who won Shawn Michaels vs The Undertaker at Badd Blood 1997?
    Shawn Michaels won. Kane made his debut, ripped the cell door off, and Tombstoned The Undertaker — allowing HBK to crawl over and steal the pin.
    Was this the first Hell in a Cell match?
    Yes. Badd Blood 1997 hosted the first-ever Hell in a Cell. Its psychology, blade jobs and escape spots became the template every later Cell match is measured against.
    What star rating did the match get?
    Dave Meltzer awarded roughly 4.5–4.75 stars and Cagematch community scores sit around 9.0/10. Wrestle Lore rates it 4.5 stars — genre-defining and the standard for every Cell match.

The Undertaker vs Shawn Michaels

WrestleMania XXV — April 5, 2009 · Reliant Stadium, Houston, TX

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

The Undertaker won, extending his WrestleMania streak to 17–0, in a match regularly voted the greatest in WrestleMania history. Two legends in their 40s built a near-perfect escalating drama of false finishes — including Undertaker's terrifying, near-catastrophic dive over the top rope — that permanently reset what a main-event could be.

There was no championship on the line. There didn't need to be. The story was simpler and bigger: the two most revered in-ring performers of their generation, the Deadman and the Showstopper, colliding for the first time on the grandest stage with the aura of the Streak at stake. Michaels spent the build taunting the supernatural — descending from the rafters in white, mocking the darkness — and in the ring he wrestled like a man convinced he could do the impossible.

The match crescendos through a sequence of near-falls so convincing that a live crowd of 72,000 repeatedly believed the Streak had ended. Undertaker's suicide dive to the outside went wrong — he nearly landed on his head on the cameraman — and the genuine danger only heightened the drama. The finish, a leaping Tombstone after Michaels missed a moonsault, felt earned rather than scripted. The two closed the show with a mutual respect that spawned an even more emotional rematch a year later (Streak vs Career, WM26).

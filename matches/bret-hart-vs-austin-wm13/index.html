Bret Hart vs Stone Cold Steve Austin

WWF WrestleMania 13 — March 23, 1997 · Allstate Arena (Rosemont Horizon), Rosemont, IL

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

Bret Hart won when Austin passed out in the Sharpshooter — yet Austin left as the hero. This is the gold standard of the wrestling double-turn: the villain walked out the biggest babyface in the industry, and the hero walked out a heel, all inside a single No-Disqualification Submission Match refereed by Ken Shamrock.

The genius is in the psychology. Austin entered as the sneering antagonist and Hart as the righteous veteran, but the match slowly inverted every expectation. The two brawled through the crowd and out to the timekeeper's area before Hart methodically targeted the leg to set up his signature Sharpshooter. When he locked it in, Austin — his face a crimson mask from blood loss — refused to tap, screaming in defiance until he blacked out. Shamrock called for the bell; Austin never submitted.

Then Hart kept attacking. He continued working the injured leg after the decision, and the Rosemont crowd's sympathy swung hard toward the man who wouldn't quit. The image of a bloodied Austin roaring rather than surrendering became the visual thesis of the entire Attitude Era. In one bout the company re-engineered both careers — launching Austin toward superstardom and repositioning Hart as the bitter anti-American heel who would headline the rest of 1997. It is still taught as the perfect example of story serving psychology.

FAQ

Who won Bret Hart vs Steve Austin at WrestleMania 13?
Bret Hart won. Austin passed out from blood loss while trapped in the Sharpshooter, so special referee Ken Shamrock stopped the match — but Austin never actually submitted.
What was the double-turn at WrestleMania 13?
During the match the heel Austin earned the crowd's respect by refusing to quit, while the babyface Hart kept attacking after the bell. Austin walked out the biggest babyface in wrestling and Hart turned heel — both in one match.

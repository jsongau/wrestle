Ronda Rousey vs Charlotte Flair vs Becky Lynch

WWE WrestleMania 35 — April 7, 2019 · MetLife Stadium, East Rutherford, NJ

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

Becky Lynch won both the Raw and SmackDown Women's Championships, pinning Ronda Rousey in a Winner Take All Triple Threat that became the first women's match ever to main-event WrestleMania — the symbolic summit of the women's revolution that began in NXT.

The build married three distinct forces: the crossover superstar in Rousey, the company's chosen ace in Charlotte Flair, and "The Man," Becky Lynch, riding one of the hottest fan-driven pushes in years. Lynch's rise had turned organic frustration into box-office heat, and by WrestleMania she was the audience's undeniable choice to stand tallest.

The match itself was hard-hitting and dramatic, if not spotless — its controversial pinfall finish, with Rousey's shoulders arguably up, sparked endless debate. But no argument could diminish the significance of women closing the biggest show of the year. It was the culmination of everything the division had built since Charlotte and the NXT women's revolution forced the main roster to catch up.

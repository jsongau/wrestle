    Rey Mysterio Jr vs Eddie Guerrero

    WCW Halloween Havoc — October 26, 1997 · MGM Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas, NV

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Rey Mysterio Jr won the WCW Cruiserweight Championship and kept his mask in a Title vs Mask match widely regarded as the greatest cruiserweight bout in American wrestling history — the match that proved US audiences would embrace lucha-style high flying.

    The stakes cut to the heart of lucha libre culture: Eddie Guerrero, the arrogant champion, had bullied Rey into risking the one thing a masked luchador never surrenders. The story wrote itself — every near-fall carried the double weight of the title and Rey's identity, and the two built a breathless escalation of counters, dives and reversals that never let the crowd exhale.

    The finish became one of the most replayed sequences of the decade. As Eddie went for a powerbomb, Rey twisted the momentum into a springboard hurricanrana that folded Guerrero up for the three-count. In a single night Rey secured the belt, saved his mask, and turned WCW's cruiserweight division into genuine must-see television. It remains the high-water mark for what two smaller wrestlers could achieve on America's biggest stage.

    FAQ

    Who won Rey Mysterio vs Eddie Guerrero at Halloween Havoc 1997?
    Rey Mysterio Jr won and captured the WCW Cruiserweight Championship. Because it was Title vs Mask, the victory also let Rey keep his mask.
    What was the stipulation of the match?
    It was a Title vs Mask match for the WCW Cruiserweight Championship. Eddie Guerrero had the title; if Rey lost he would have been unmasked, but he won both the belt and the right to stay masked.
    What star rating did the match get?
    Dave Meltzer awarded around 4.75 stars and Cagematch community scores sit near 9.0/10. Wrestle Lore rates it a full 5 stars — the high-flying benchmark of the era.

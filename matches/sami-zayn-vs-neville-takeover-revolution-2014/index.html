    Sami Zayn vs Adrian Neville

    NXT TakeOver: R Evolution — December 11, 2014 · Full Sail University, Winter Park, FL

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Sami Zayn won the NXT Championship — his first title in WWE — beating champion Adrian Neville in the emotional main event of TakeOver: R Evolution. It was the ultimate underdog payoff of the early TakeOver era, the beloved perennial nearly-man finally winning the big one.

    Zayn had built his entire NXT run on falling just short, and the match played on every ounce of that history. Neville, the high-flying champion, matched him move for move and near-fall for near-fall, forcing Zayn to dig deeper than he ever had. When Zayn finally landed the Helluva Kick to claim the belt, the release of years of frustration turned Full Sail into a cauldron of noise.

    The celebration became part of the legend: the entire NXT locker room emptied out to hoist Zayn on their shoulders, a genuine show of respect that felt like reality bleeding into storyline. It proved NXT could deliver long-form character storytelling that rivaled — or beat — the main roster.

    Then the knife twisted. Zayn's best friend Kevin Owens, freshly debuted, stormed the ring and brutally beat down the new champion, powerbombing him on the ring apron. In a single breath, the promotion crowned a hero and launched one of NXT's defining feuds.

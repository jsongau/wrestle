The Undertaker vs Triple H (End of an Era)

WrestleMania XXVIII — April 1, 2012 · Sun Life Stadium, Miami Gardens, FL

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

The Undertaker won inside Hell in a Cell, extending the Streak to 20–0, in the match billed and remembered as the "End of an Era." It served as the final chapter of the Undertaker, Triple H and Shawn Michaels trilogy — a three-way character drama disguised as a brutal brawl.

The masterstroke was the referee. With Michaels installed as special guest official, every count carried the weight of his own history with both men — his losses to the Deadman, his lifelong friendship with Triple H. As the punishment mounted, Michaels was forced to decide, over and over, whether to end his friend's suffering, turning the cage into a stage for guilt as much as violence.

The theme was aging warriors emptying the tank one last time, and the storytelling elevated a by-then physically limited Undertaker into something monumental. When it was over, the three men walked up the ramp together, embracing — a genuine curtain call that framed the night as a literal end of an era.

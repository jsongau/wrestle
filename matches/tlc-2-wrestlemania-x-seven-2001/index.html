    TLC II — Edge & Christian vs The Hardy Boyz vs The Dudley Boyz

    WWF WrestleMania X-Seven — April 1, 2001 · Reliant Astrodome, Houston, TX

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Edge & Christian won the WWF Tag Team Championship, outlasting the Hardy Boyz and the Dudley Boyz in the ladder-match arms race that redefined tag wrestling. It matters because it took car-crash choreography and turned it into art — the template every future Tables, Ladders & Chairs match would chase.

    These three teams had spent two years escalating their ladder-match rivalry, and TLC II was the point where they emptied the tank. The signature image is Edge launching himself off a ladder to spear a dangling Jeff Hardy clean out of the air, a freeze-frame that still headlines WrestleMania highlight reels. Jeff's high-wire Swanton and his suspended climb across the ropes gave the sprint its daredevil spine.

    The run-ins deepened the chaos rather than diluting it: Rhyno and Spike Dudley crashed the finish for their respective teams, and Lita got involved at ringside, keeping every fall in doubt. When Edge & Christian finally hauled down the belts, all six men had risked their bodies for one unforgettable, career-defining spectacle. It set the standard for the modern multi-man ladder match.

    FAQ

    Who won TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven?
    Edge & Christian won the WWF Tag Team Championship, defeating both the Hardy Boyz and the Dudley Boyz in the Tables, Ladders & Chairs match.
    What star rating did TLC II get?
    Dave Meltzer awarded roughly 4.25 stars; Cagematch community scores sit around 8.7/10. Wrestle Lore rates it 4.5 stars — the definitive spot-fest.
    What is the most famous spot in TLC II?
    Edge spearing a dangling Jeff Hardy out of mid-air, plus Jeff's Swanton off a giant ladder and the run-ins from Rhyno, Spike Dudley and Lita, are the match's signature moments.

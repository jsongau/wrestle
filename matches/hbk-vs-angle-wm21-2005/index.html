    Shawn Michaels vs Kurt Angle

    WrestleMania 21 — April 3, 2005 · Staples Center, Los Angeles, CA

    ▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

    The Story

    Kurt Angle won when Shawn Michaels submitted to the ankle lock, ending a long-awaited dream match that lived up to every ounce of anticipation. It paired the best pure wrestler of the era against its best all-around performer, and the result was a near-flawless blend of technique, tension and heart.

    For years fans had wondered what would happen if the Olympic gold medalist and the Showstopper were finally locked in the same ring. WrestleMania 21 gave them the answer with no title to muddy it — just pride and legacy. The two traded holds and momentum with a precision that made every reversal feel like a genuine fight for survival rather than a rehearsed exchange.

    The climax belonged to Michaels' selling. After a valiant struggle to reach the ropes, HBK finally collapsed into the ankle lock, tapping out in one of the most convincing defeats of his career. The bout spawned a celebrated rivalry that carried into a hard-hitting rematch at Vengeance 2005, and it stands today as a WrestleMania in-ring high point.

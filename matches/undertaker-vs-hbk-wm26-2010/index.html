The Undertaker vs Shawn Michaels (Streak vs Career)

WrestleMania XXVI — March 28, 2010 · University of Phoenix Stadium, Glendale, AZ

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

The Undertaker won, extending the Streak to 18–0 and forcing Shawn Michaels into retirement. A rare sequel that justified itself, it layered HBK's entire career onto the Streak's mystique and turned a rematch into a farewell.

Denied his rematch, Michaels had spent months provoking the Deadman until the only way to get it was to gamble everything — win, or retire. That desperation bled into every moment. Where the WM25 classic was a duel of pride, this was a man fighting the inevitable, hurling his body into moonsaults and kick-outs because there was nothing left to save it for.

The finish gave it real gravity. Michaels, beaten and defiant, mocked the Undertaker with the slit-throat taunt, daring him to finish it. Undertaker's response was a reluctant, almost mournful Tombstone — an execution he clearly did not want to perform. It sent one of the greatest of all time into retirement on the biggest stage, the emotional bookend to the WM25 masterpiece.

    Frequently Asked

    How many matches are in the Wrestle Lore database?
    Wrestle Lore catalogs 30 landmark matches spanning 1996 to 2021, across WWE/WWF, WCW, ECW, TNA/Impact, ROH and NXT. Each has a Wrestle Lore rating out of five stars, cited Wrestling Observer (Meltzer) and Cagematch scores, and a full breakdown of why it mattered.
    What is the highest-rated match on Wrestle Lore?
    Twelve matches earn a perfect five-star Wrestle Lore rating, including Kurt Angle vs Chris Benoit (Royal Rumble 2003), The Undertaker vs Shawn Michaels (WrestleMania 25), CM Punk vs John Cena (Money in the Bank 2011) and Styles vs Daniels vs Joe (TNA Unbreakable 2005). See the full ranking in The Wrestle Lore Five-Star Club.
    How does Wrestle Lore rate matches?
    The Wrestle Lore rating is an editorial score out of five stars, informed by critical consensus — Dave Meltzer's Wrestling Observer star ratings and Cagematch community scores — plus each match's historical significance. Read the full methodology.
    Which promotions are covered?
    The archive spans five headline promotions plus ROH: WWE/WWF (15 matches), WCW (4), ECW (1), TNA/Impact (1), NXT (5), and one ROH classic. Use the promotion filter above to narrow the list.

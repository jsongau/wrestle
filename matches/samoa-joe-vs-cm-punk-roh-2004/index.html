Samoa Joe vs CM Punk

ROH World Title Classic ("Joe vs Punk II") — October 16, 2004 · Frontier Fieldhouse, Chicago Ridge, IL

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

The match went the full 60-minute time limit to a draw, so Samoa Joe retained the ROH World Championship — and in doing so, "Joe vs Punk II" legitimized the American independent scene as a home for world-class wrestling. It is the best chapter of the Joe/Punk trilogy that defined early ROH.

What makes it a foundational text is the pacing. A full hour of wrestling should sag somewhere; this one never does. Joe, the immovable champion, and Punk, the ambitious challenger who framed the whole feud as a war for ROH's soul, built the drama in waves — mat exchanges giving way to strikes, near-falls stacking higher as the clock wound toward zero.

When time expired with the title still around Joe's waist, the draw felt like a victory for both men and for the promotion itself. Dave Meltzer awarding it five stars — ROH's first ever — validated the entire indie movement, and the match became a blueprint for the boom that would later feed WWE, NXT, and AEW. Punk carried that same fearlessness into his 2011 pipebomb summer.

FAQ

Who won Samoa Joe vs CM Punk (Joe vs Punk II)?
Neither — the match ended in a 60-minute time-limit draw. Samoa Joe retained the ROH World Championship because a draw meant no title change.
What star rating did Joe vs Punk II get?
Dave Meltzer awarded it a full 5 stars — ROH's first ever five-star match — and Cagematch scores sit near 9.3/10. MAT rates it a full 5 stars.
Why is this match a foundational classic?
It legitimized the American independent scene as a home for world-class wrestling. A full hour of escalating drama that never sagged, it made both men stars and validated the indie boom that later fed WWE, NXT, and AEW.

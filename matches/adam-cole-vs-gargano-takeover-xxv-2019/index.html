Adam Cole vs Johnny Gargano

NXT TakeOver: XXV — June 1, 2019 · Webster Bank Arena, Bridgeport, CT

▶ Opens the official WWE match search. Verified embed IDs (YouTube & Bilibili for China) drop straight into this player.

The Story

Adam Cole won the NXT Championship two falls to one, outlasting Johnny Gargano in a near-40-minute epic that many consider the greatest NXT match ever. It crowned Cole as the standard-bearer of the Black-and-Gold era at its creative peak.

The two-out-of-three-falls structure was the genius of it. The first fall came by pinfall, the second by submission, and the decisive third was contested under no-DQ rules — a design that let both men reset, escalate, and layer new drama on top of exhaustion without ever losing the crowd.

Gargano, NXT's emotional heartbeat, chased the title with everything he had, while Cole matched him counter for counter and kick-out for kick-out. By the final stretch the Bridgeport crowd was hanging on every near-fall. When Cole finally landed the Last Shot to claim the belt, it felt like a coronation — the flagship match of the TakeOver brand at its absolute summit.

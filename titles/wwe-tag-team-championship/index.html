    WWE Tag Team Championship

    Current: Damian Priest & R-Truth Won March 20, 2026 As of July 26, 2026

    Damian Priest and R-Truth are the current WWE Tag Team Champions, having won the SmackDown brand titles on March 20, 2026. WWE runs two men's tag team titles in 2026: this one on SmackDown, and the World Tag Team Championship on Raw, held by Bron Breakker and Austin Theory.

    FAQ

    Who are the current WWE Tag Team Champions?

    Damian Priest and R-Truth, as of July 26, 2026. They won the titles on SmackDown on March 20, 2026.

    How many men's tag team titles does WWE have?

    Two in 2026: the WWE Tag Team Championship on SmackDown and the World Tag Team Championship on Raw, held by Bron Breakker and Austin Theory.

    Which team had the longest recent reign?

    The Usos, who held the SmackDown tag titles for 622 days from 2021 to 2023.

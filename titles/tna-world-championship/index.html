    TNA World Championship

    Current: Nic Nemeth TNA Since 2007

    The TNA World Championship is the top title of TNA Wrestling. Its lineage begins with the NWA World Heavyweight Championship contested in the promotion from 2002 to 2007, moves to the TNA World Heavyweight Championship created in 2007, and was renamed the TNA World Championship with the company's 2024 rebrand.

    Kurt Angle opened the promotion's own world title, while AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Sting defined its main-event scene across the 2000s and 2010s.

    FAQ

    Who is the current TNA World Champion?
    Nic Nemeth is the current TNA World Champion, winning the title on June 28, 2026 at Slammiversary 2026.
    Who was the first TNA World Heavyweight Champion?
    Kurt Angle is recognized as the inaugural TNA World Heavyweight Champion in May 2007, the first holder of TNA's own world title.
    Why is it now called the TNA World Championship?
    The title was renamed the TNA World Championship as part of the promotion's 2024 rebrand. It descends from the earlier TNA World Heavyweight Championship and the NWA World title contested in TNA.

    ECW World Heavyweight Championship

    Final champion: Rhyno (Jan 2001) Retired ECW

    The ECW World Heavyweight Championship was the top title of Extreme Championship Wrestling, the Philadelphia-based promotion that made hardcore and technical wrestling its identity in the 1990s. The original lineage ran until ECW folded in early 2001, with Rhyno recognized as the final champion.

    One distinction matters for this belt. WWE ran a separate ECW Championship during its 2006 to 2010 ECW revival, which is a different title from the original world championship covered here. Bobby Lashley held that revived belt in 2007.

    Reigns that defined the title

    The SandmanMultiple reigns, 1990sA multi-time champion who became a face of the hardcore-era product (as reported)

    FAQ

    Who was the last original ECW World Heavyweight Champion?
    Rhyno held the ECW World Heavyweight Championship when the original ECW closed on January 12, 2001, making him the final champion of that lineage.
    Is the WWE ECW Championship the same title?
    No. WWE ran a separate ECW Championship during its 2006 to 2010 ECW revival. It is a distinct title from the original ECW World Heavyweight Championship, though it shares the ECW name.
    How did the ECW World title start?
    As reported in ECW lore, Shane Douglas threw down the NWA World Heavyweight title on television in 1994 and declared himself ECW World Champion, marking the storyline birth of the belt.

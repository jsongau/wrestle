    Intercontinental Championship

    Current: Penta Won March 2, 2026 As of July 26, 2026

    Penta is the current Intercontinental Champion, having won the belt on Raw on March 2, 2026 for his first WWE singles title. Created in 1979, the Intercontinental Championship is the workhorse title that made careers, from Randy Savage at WrestleMania III to Gunther's record run four decades later.

    Notable Reigns and Moments

    Randy Savage vs Ricky SteamboatWrestleMania III, Mar 29, 1987Widely cited as the greatest Intercontinental Championship match ever worked

    FAQ

    Who is the current Intercontinental Champion?

    Penta holds the title as of July 26, 2026. He won it on Raw on March 2, 2026.

    Who had the longest Intercontinental Championship reign?

    Gunther, with a 666-day reign that began on June 10, 2022.

    Who has held the title the most times?

    Chris Jericho, a record nine-time Intercontinental Champion.

    What is the most famous Intercontinental Championship match?

    Randy Savage versus Ricky Steamboat at WrestleMania III on March 29, 1987.

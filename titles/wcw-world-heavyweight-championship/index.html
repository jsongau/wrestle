    WCW World Heavyweight Championship

    Final champion: Booker T (2001) Retired WCW

    The WCW World Heavyweight Championship was the top prize of World Championship Wrestling from 1991 until the company folded in 2001. It carried the Big Gold Belt design for most of its run and sat at the center of the Monday Night War against WWE. When WWE bought WCW in 2001, the title crossed over during the Invasion angle and was later retired.

    Its reign chart reads like a map of 1990s wrestling: Ric Flair opened the lineage, Hulk Hogan anchored the nWo boom, and Goldberg rode a long unbeaten run to the belt on Nitro.

    FAQ

    Who was the last WCW World Heavyweight Champion?
    Booker T held the WCW World Heavyweight Championship when WCW closed in 2001. He carried the title into WWE, where it featured in the Invasion storyline before the lineage was retired.
    Who was the first WCW World Heavyweight Champion?
    Ric Flair was recognized as the inaugural WCW World Heavyweight Champion on January 11, 1991, bridging his NWA run into WCW's own banner.
    Is the WCW World Heavyweight Championship still active?
    No. The title was retired after WWE purchased WCW in 2001. Its history is preserved as a legacy world championship rather than an active belt.

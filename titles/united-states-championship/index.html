    United States Championship

    Current: Trick Williams Won April 19, 2026 As of July 26, 2026

    Trick Williams is the current United States Champion, having won the belt at WrestleMania 42 on April 19, 2026. The title began in NWA Mid-Atlantic in 1975, carried through WCW under holders like Ric Flair and Lex Luger, and was revived by WWE in 2003.

    Harley Race1975Recognized as the first NWA United States Champion of the lineage

    FAQ

    Who is the current United States Champion?

    Trick Williams holds the title as of July 26, 2026. He won it at WrestleMania 42 on April 19, 2026.

    Where did the United States Championship come from?

    It originated in NWA Mid-Atlantic in 1975, carried through WCW, and was revived by WWE in 2003.

    What was John Cena's US Open Challenge?

    During his 2015 reign, John Cena defended the title against a rotating field of opponents, revitalizing its prestige.

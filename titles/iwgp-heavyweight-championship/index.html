    IWGP Heavyweight Championship

    Current: Yota Tsuji NJPW Reactivated Jan 2026

    The IWGP Heavyweight Championship is New Japan Pro-Wrestling's flagship singles title, created in 1987. Its arc runs through three phases. It headlined Wrestle Kingdom cards for decades, was retired in 2021 when NJPW merged it into the IWGP World Heavyweight Championship, and then, as reported by NJPW, was reactivated in January 2026 with the three prior lineages folded back into it.

    Its reign chart tracks New Japan's rise as a global promotion, from home-grown aces to the Bullet Club and gaijin champions who carried the belt abroad. AJ Styles, Kenny Omega and Jon Moxley all held New Japan gold during that expansion.

    FAQ

    Who is the current IWGP Heavyweight Champion?
    Yota Tsuji is the reigning IWGP Heavyweight Champion. He won the title on June 14, 2026 at Dominion 6.14 by defeating Callum Newman, and is recognized as the 87th champion following the reactivation.
    Why was the IWGP Heavyweight Championship reactivated?
    As reported by NJPW, the promotion reactivated the IWGP Heavyweight Championship in January 2026 and folded the Heavyweight, Intercontinental and World Heavyweight lineages back into a single flagship title.
    When was the IWGP Heavyweight Championship created?
    The title was created in 1987 as New Japan Pro-Wrestling's top singles championship. It was retired in 2021, then reactivated in January 2026.

        05 — Pricing & trust

        Know the full price before you leave this page

        Three things worth understanding before you click buy, so the number at checkout is the number you expected, and leaving for the seller feels like a handoff instead of a gamble.

        06.1

        All-in pricing

        The price you see should be the price you pay. All-in means every required fee, service, facility, and processing, is folded into the number up front instead of appearing at checkout. The federal TICKET Act now pushes every seller this way, and a few, like TickPick, have shown the all-in total for years. When you compare links, compare all-in totals, not face values.

        06.2

        Resale vs. face value

        Face value is the price the promoter set. Resale is whatever another fan will take today, so it floats above face for a hot main-event city and below face when a building is slow to fill. A fair resale price sits close to face plus normal fees. If one listing is wildly under everything around it, read the section and row twice before you trust it.

        06.3

        Where your money goes

        A ticket is rarely one number. Most break down as face value (the seat) plus a service fee (the seller) plus a facility charge (the building), sometimes plus delivery. All-in pricing just shows you that sum before you commit rather than after.

    Professional Wrestling Events

    The complete guide to professional wrestling events across every major promotion — from WrestleMania 1 in 1985 to the present, covering WWE, WCW, ECW, AEW, NJPW, TNA, ROH, AAA, NWA, and the global indie scene. Every flagship event series, every era, fully documented for the historical record.

    Flagship Events

    WWE

    WrestleMania

    1985 – Present

    The Showcase of the Immortals. WWE's annual spring flagship since 1985. 41 editions across four decades, from MSG to the NFL's biggest stadiums. The most commercially successful event in wrestling history.

    Explore series →     WWE

    Royal Rumble

    1988 – Present

    30 superstars enter one at a time. One earns a WrestleMania world title shot. The winner-take-all January tradition since 1988. Steve Austin holds the record with three victories (1997, 1998, 2001).

    Explore series →     WWE

    SummerSlam

    1988 – Present

    WWE's second-biggest annual event. The Biggest Party of the Summer, held each August since 1988. High-profile WrestleMania rematches and summer blockbuster matches define its legacy.

    Explore series →     WWE

    Survivor Series

    1987 – Present

    Traditional elimination tag team warfare since 1987. Site of the Montreal Screwjob (1997) and decades of brand-vs-brand warfare. WarGames was added to the event beginning in 2022.

    Explore series →     AEW

    AEW Double or Nothing

    2019 – Present

    The inaugural AEW pay-per-view event (May 25, 2019, Las Vegas). The launch of All Elite Wrestling as a genuine WWE alternative. Chris Jericho defeated Kenny Omega in the first-ever AEW main event.

    Explore AEW →     AEW

    AEW All In

    2023 – Present

    AEW's international supercard at Wembley Stadium (Aug 27, 2023 — 81,035 fans). The largest outdoor wrestling event in UK history. Established AEW as a global brand capable of selling out stadium shows.

    Explore AEW →     WCW

    WCW Starrcade

    1983 – 2000

    The granddaddy of wrestling supercards — predating WrestleMania by two years. WCW's annual flagship from 1983–2000. NWA and WCW's most prestigious night, featuring Ric Flair and Sting throughout the era.

    Explore WCW →     WCW

    Bash at the Beach 1996

    July 7, 1996

    The night Hulk Hogan turned heel and the New World Order (nWo) was born. Considered one of the most consequential moments in wrestling history — it shifted the Monday Night Wars decisively toward WCW.

    Explore WCW →     ECW

    ECW Barely Legal

    April 13, 1997

    The first-ever ECW pay-per-view event. The Taz vs. Sabu match and Funk vs. Raven for the ECW title headlined the show. The hardcore alternative was legitimized in one night — ECW ran 21 PPVs through 2001.

    Explore ECW →     TNA

    TNA Bound for Glory

    2005 – Present

    TNA/Impact's WrestleMania. 21+ editions from 2005 through 2026. AJ Styles' 2009 win is considered the defining moment of TNA's original roster era — the homegrown champion crowned at the biggest show.

    Explore TNA →     NJPW

    NJPW Wrestle Kingdom

    2007 – Present

    NJPW's annual January 4 Tokyo Dome supercard — "1.4" in wrestling shorthand. Home to Okada vs. Omega and the modern strong style era. Wrestle Kingdom 20 (2026) marks a major milestone for the promotion.

    Explore NJPW →     NJPW

    NJPW G1 Climax

    1991 – Present

    The world's most prestigious wrestling tournament. Round-robin across 20 wrestlers over three weeks, producing the highest concentration of 5-star matches in any annual event. Kenny Omega was the first non-Japanese winner (2016).

    Explore NJPW →     ROH

    ROH Final Battle

    2003 – Present

    Ring of Honor's annual year-end flagship — the most critically acclaimed indie promotion of the 2000s. ROH launched CM Punk, Daniel Bryan, and Seth Rollins. Now under AEW/HonorClub ownership.

    Explore ROH → AAA

    AAA TripleManía

    1993 – Present

    The WrestleMania of Mexican lucha libre. 32+ editions since 1993. Known for the mask vs. mask (apuesta) tradition — losing your mask in lucha libre is one of the highest-stakes moments in the genre. AEW co-promoted beginning 2019.

    Explore AAA →     NWA

    NWA Starrcade

    1948 – Present

    The National Wrestling Alliance was the dominant North American wrestling body from 1948 through the 1980s. Every major promotion traces to the NWA. The NWA World Heavyweight Championship is the oldest world title lineage in wrestling, running since 1948.

    NWA history →     PWG

    Battle of Los Angeles

    2005 – Present

    The most prestigious independent wrestling tournament in North America. PWG's BOLA alumni include Seth Rollins, Kevin Owens, Sami Zayn, Kenny Omega, Adam Cole, and Jay White.

    WWE Event Series

    WWE Elimination Chamber 2010 – Present Six superstars. One inescapable steel structure. February title-match gauntlet en route to WrestleMania. WWE Money in the Bank 2010 – Present Briefcase ladder match · guaranteed championship shot, cashable any time, anywhere. WWE Hell in a Cell 1997 – 2022 Undertaker–Mankind 1998 (KOTR/HiaC) is the most replayed moment in WWE history. Annual PPV 2009–2022. WWE King of the Ring 1993 – Present (intermittent) Single-elimination tournament crowning a "King." Stone Cold 1996, Triple H 1997, Brock Lesnar 2002 are the defining reigns. WWE In Your House 1995 – 1999 27 mid-card PPVs across the New Generation and early Attitude eras. The original "B-show" PPV format. WWE TLC: Tables, Ladders & Chairs 2009 – 2021 December multi-stipulation PPV inspired by the Hardy Boyz / E&C / Dudley Boyz TLC matches of the early 2000s. WWE Extreme Rules 2009 – Present The annual "no rules" PPV — chairs, tables, ladders, and street fight matches across the card. WWE Saturday Night's Main Event 1985 – 2024 NBC prime-time specials of the Golden Era (1985–1992); revived on Peacock (2006, 2024). Stone Cold vs. McMahon were cornerstones of the original run. WWE Crown Jewel / Clash at the Castle 2018 – Present WWE's international Premium Live Event expansion: Saudi Arabia (Crown Jewel), UK (Clash at the Castle), France (Backlash 2023), Puerto Rico (Backlash 2024).

    WCW Event Series

    WCW Great American Bash 1985 – 2000 WCW's summer showpiece — originally a NWA stadium tour across the South before becoming an annual PPV. WCW Halloween Havoc 1989 – 2000 Rey Mysterio vs. Eddie Guerrero (1997) ★★★★½ — the match that defined WCW's cruiserweight division at its peak. WCW SuperBrawl 1991 – 2001 WCW's February flagship — Ric Flair and Sting programs defined the era. WCW Clash of Champions 1988 – 1997 35 free TV specials. The inaugural Clash (1988) featured Flair vs. Sting in one of NWA's most iconic matches. WCW Fall Brawl (WarGames) 1992 – 1999 WCW's annual WarGames match — origin of the double-ring cage format invented by Dusty Rhodes (NWA, 1987). WCW WCW Monday Nitro 1995 – 2001 The first serious Monday Night Wars opponent to WWF Raw. Launched Sept 4, 1995 — Lex Luger's surprise debut set the tone.

CM Punk's 2021 return happened at All Out. AEW AEW Full Gear 2019 – Present Fall supercard — part of the Big Four. Major title changes and blow-off matches each year. AEW Forbidden Door (AEW × NJPW) 2022 – Present Annual AEW/NJPW co-promotion supercard — the definitive inter-promotional event of the modern era. AEW WrestleDream 2023 – Present AEW's October supercard — held in Seattle, WA. Named as tribute to the dream-match appeal of AEW's roster. AEW AEW Dynamite (weekly) 2019 – Present Premiered Oct 2, 2019 on TNT. Launched the Wednesday Night Wars vs. NXT. Now airs on TBS.

    NJPW NJPW Dominion 2009 – Present Annual June Osaka-jo Hall supercard — Okada vs. Omega (Dominion 2018) rated ★★★★★★★ by Dave Meltzer ⚔ NJPW Best of the Super Juniors 1988 – Present Junior heavyweight round-robin tournament each May. Hiromu Takahashi, Will Ospreay, El Desperado, and Jushin Liger are modern greats of the tournament. NJPW King of Pro-Wrestling 2012 – Present NJPW's major October supercard at Ryogoku Kokugikan in Tokyo. Title defenses and stipulation matches. NJPW Wrestle Dynasty (AEW × NJPW) 2025 – Present New AEW/NJPW co-promotion supercard held January 5 in the Tokyo Dome — the day after Wrestle Kingdom.

    ROH ROH G1 Supercard April 6, 2019 Madison Square Garden — co-promoted with NJPW — the largest ROH event in history, setting a precedent for the Forbidden Door concept. ROH ROH Supercard of Honor WM Weekend — Annual WrestleMania weekend tradition. Eddie Kingston won the ROH World Championship at the 2022 edition under AEW's new ownership era. ROH ROH Best in the World 2012 – Present ROH's summer flagship — now under AEW/Tony Khan ownership. Streaming on HonorClub. ROH ROH Death Before Dishonor 2004 – Present The quintessential ROH summer event — CM Punk's 2004 debut match era, then revival under AEW.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    When was the first WrestleMania?

    The first WrestleMania was held on March 31, 1985, at Madison Square Garden in New York City. It drew 19,121 fans in attendance, with approximately 1 million pay-per-view buys. The main event was Hulk Hogan and Mr. T defeating Roddy Piper and Paul Orndorff. WrestleMania established the annual spring supercard format that all major promotions would eventually emulate. Full WrestleMania history →

    What was the first AEW pay-per-view event?

    The first AEW pay-per-view event was AEW Double or Nothing, held May 25, 2019, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada. Chris Jericho defeated Kenny Omega in the main event to win the first-ever AEW World Championship. The show launched All Elite Wrestling as a formal promotion and is considered the beginning of the modern alternative wrestling era.

    Which promotion invented WarGames?

    WarGames was invented by Jim Crockett Promotions under the National Wrestling Alliance (NWA) banner. The first WarGames match took place on July 4, 1987, at the Great American Bash in Atlanta, Georgia — The Four Horsemen vs. Dusty Rhodes, The Road Warriors, Nikita Koloff, and Paul Ellering. The concept (two rings, one cage, teams entering one at a time) was created by Dusty Rhodes. WCW continued WarGames through 1998 at Fall Brawl. WWE revived it in NXT (2017) and on the main roster at Survivor Series (2022).

    What happened to WCW? When did WCW go out of business?

    WCW (World Championship Wrestling) ceased operations on March 26, 2001. The final WCW Monday Nitro aired that date on TNT, simulcast with WWF Raw in a historic dual broadcast. WWE (then WWF) purchased WCW's tape library, intellectual property, and contracted talent from AOL Time Warner. The final WCW pay-per-view event was Greed (March 18, 2001). WCW's decline accelerated from 2000 onward due to creative issues, guaranteed contracts for aging talent, and parent company Time Warner's decision to remove wrestling from TNT and TBS.

    How many ECW pay-per-view events were there?

    Extreme Championship Wrestling produced 21 official pay-per-view events from April 13, 1997 (Barely Legal — the first ECW PPV) through January 7, 2001 (Guilty as Charged 2001, the final ECW PPV before the promotion declared bankruptcy). WWE later produced two reunion shows: ECW One Night Stand 2005 (June 12, 2005) and ECW One Night Stand 2006 (June 11, 2006), followed by the WWE ECW brand that ran from 2006–2010.

    What is the biggest event in lucha libre?

    AAA TripleManía is the biggest annual event in lucha libre. First held on March 4, 1993, in Mexico City, it is the WrestleMania of Mexican professional wrestling. Organized by Lucha Libre AAA Worldwide (AAA), TripleManía is known for the mask vs. mask (apuesta) tradition — when a wrestler loses their mask, it is considered one of the most significant career moments in lucha libre. CMLL's Aniversario (September) is the other major lucha libre supercard, representing the world's oldest continuously operating wrestling promotion (founded 1933).

    Who won the most Royal Rumble matches?

    Steve Austin has won the most Royal Rumble matches with three victories (1997, 1998, 2001). John Cena and Triple H have each won two Royal Rumble matches. Shawn Michaels won the 1996 Rumble from the #1 entry position, the only competitor to ever win the match from that spot. The first women's Royal Rumble was held in 2018, won by Asuka.

    What is the biggest professional wrestling event by attendance?

    WrestleMania 32 (2016), held at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, is officially reported at 101,763 in attendance ⚔ (some estimates suggest a portion were complimentary tickets), making it the highest-attended professional wrestling event in history by reported figure. WrestleMania 3 (1987) at the Pontiac Silverdome is reported at 93,173 ⚔. Outside WWE, AEW All In at Wembley Stadium (2023) drew 81,035, the largest outdoor wrestling event in UK history.

    What is NJPW's G1 Climax?

    The G1 Climax is NJPW's annual summer tournament, held since 1991. Twenty wrestlers are split into two blocks and compete in round-robin matches over approximately three weeks. The block winners meet in a final, with the winner earning an IWGP World Heavyweight Championship match at Wrestle Kingdom (January 4, Tokyo Dome). The G1 Climax produces the highest concentration of highly-rated matches of any annual wrestling event — including Kazuchika Okada vs. Kenny Omega (G1 2017, ★★★★★★ by Dave Meltzer ⚔, the first-ever 6-star rating).

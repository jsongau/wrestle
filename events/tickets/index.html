    07 Answer first

    Questions, answered

    When and where is WWE's next Premium Live Event?

    SummerSlam runs August 1 and 2, 2026 at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis. Tickets are on sale now through Ticketmaster.

    Where do I buy WWE tickets?

    In the United States, Ticketmaster is WWE's primary seller and On Location handles premium seats and travel. The seller changes by country. Saudi Arabia sells through webook, Australia through Ticketek, Europe through the local Ticketmaster, and AEG venues through AXS.

    What is a WWE presale, and do I need a code?

    A presale opens a short window to buy before the public on-sale. WWE's free presale runs on email registration, so register before the window closes for the earliest shot. We tell you when and where to register. We don't hand out codes, and nobody should.

    How much do WWE tickets cost?

    Prices are set by the seller and move with demand, so we don't quote a number here. Open the live listing on Ticketmaster for the current range.

    What is On Location?

    On Location is WWE's official hospitality partner and shares WWE's parent company, TKO. Its WWE Priority Pass packages bundle official tickets, premium seats, pre-show hospitality, and Superstar meet-and-greets. Package contents vary by event.

    Where is WrestleMania 43?

    WrestleMania 43 is heading to Riyadh, Saudi Arabia in 2027, the first WrestleMania held outside North America. The venue, dates, and travel packages have not been announced yet. Leave your email and we'll tell you the day travel goes on sale.

    A show I want is sold out. What can I do?

    Leave your email and we'll tell you if verified resale drops or more seats release. For early access next time, Club WWE is WWE's official route. Here's how it works on WWE.com.

    Is Wrestle Lore affiliated with WWE?

    No. Wrestle Lore is an independent fan guide, and we don't sell tickets. We link to official and authorized sellers including Ticketmaster, On Location, AXS, webook, and Ticketek. We're not affiliated with or endorsed by WWE or TKO.

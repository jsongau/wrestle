    Royal Rumble

    WWE’s annual January battle royal, where 30 superstars fight for one prize — the main event of WrestleMania.

    First Held1988Jan 24 · Hamilton, Ontario
    The Field30Over-the-top-rope battle royal
    Editions39Through 2026
    Winner’s PrizeWrestleManiaWorld-title main event

    How the match works

    One match, one rule set, elimination by the top rope. Here’s the format that has held since 1988:

    1

    Two start

    Entrants #1 and #2 open the match alone in the ring.

    2

    Every ~90 sec

    A new entrant arrives on a timed interval until the field reaches 30.

    3

    Over the top

    You’re out only when thrown over the top rope with both feet on the floor.

    4

    Last one wins

    The final superstar standing takes the match — and the title shot.

    It debuted as a 20-man match on a USA Network television special on January 24, 1988 in Hamilton, Ontario, and expanded to its signature 30-man format the following year.

    What’s at stake

    Since 1993, the winner earns a world-championship match at that year’s WrestleMania — making the Rumble the official starting gun for WWE’s Road to WrestleMania. That single stipulation turned a novelty bout into the second-biggest night on the wrestling calendar.

    Record-book moments

    Most winsSteve Austin — three (1997, 1998 and 2001).
    Wire-to-wire from #1Shawn Michaels (1995), Chris Benoit (2004) and Edge (2021) each lasted the whole match from the first spot.
    Won from #30The Undertaker (2007) and Triple H (2016) — the only men to win from the final entry.
    Vacant title from #3Ric Flair won the vacant WWF Championship from the #3 spot in 1992.

    The women’s Rumble & going global

    A women’s Royal Rumble was added in 2018, won by Asuka, and now runs on the same card with the same WrestleMania stakes. The event has also gone stadium-sized and global: 2025 was the first Rumble held outside January, and the 2026 edition in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — won by Roman Reigns and Liv Morgan — was the first ever staged outside North America. This page catalogues every Royal Rumble from 1988 to 2026.

    Why the Royal Rumble matters

    The Royal Rumble is the structural keystone of WWE's storytelling year. Every January it converts an entire roster into a single number-one contender, launching the roughly two-and-a-half-month build to WrestleMania. Because one match can elevate almost anyone — a returning veteran, a surprise entrant, a newly minted main-eventer — the Rumble is WWE's most efficient star-making machine, and its result sets the direction of the entire spring.

    Commercially it anchors the first quarter of the year: a marquee event that drives subscriptions, ticket sales and host-city tourism, and increasingly a global tentpole (the 2026 edition was staged in Saudi Arabia). For the performers, a Rumble win or a memorable iron-man run is a permanent career marker — which is why this hub links back to nearly every main-event profile on Wrestle Lore.

    Records & superlatives

    Most Rumble wins

    Steve Austin — 3 (1997, 1998, 2001), the only three-time winner of the men's Royal Rumble.

    Most career eliminations

    Kane — 46 across roughly 20 Royal Rumble appearances, the most in the match's history.

    Most eliminations, one match

    13 — shared by Brock Lesnar (2020) and Braun Strowman in the 50-man 2018 Greatest Royal Rumble.

    Longest single performance

    Rey Mysterio — 1:02:12 to win the 2006 Rumble from #2; Daniel Bryan lasted 1:16:05 in the oversized 2018 Greatest Royal Rumble.

    Fastest elimination

    Santino Marella — 1.9 seconds (2009), the quickest exit on record.

    Winning from #30

    The Undertaker (2007) and Triple H (2016) are the only men to win entering last, at number 30.

    First women's Rumble

    Asuka won the inaugural women's Royal Rumble in 2018; it now carries the same WrestleMania stakes as the men's.

    FAQ

    What do you win at the Royal Rumble?
    Since 1993, winning the men's or women's Royal Rumble match earns a world-championship match of the winner's choosing at that year's WrestleMania.
    How many wrestlers are in a Royal Rumble match?
    A standard Royal Rumble match features 30 entrants, who enter one at a time at timed intervals (traditionally every 90 seconds). A wrestler is eliminated by being thrown over the top rope with both feet touching the floor, and the last one remaining wins.
    Who won the first Royal Rumble?
    "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan won the first Royal Rumble on January 24, 1988 in Hamilton, Ontario. It was a 20-man match aired as a special on the USA Network before the event moved to pay-per-view in 1989.
    Who has won the most Royal Rumble matches?
    Steve Austin holds the record with three wins (1997, 1998 and 2001). Hulk Hogan, Shawn Michaels, Triple H, Batista, John Cena, Randy Orton, Edge and Cody Rhodes have each won twice.
    When did the women's Royal Rumble start?
    The first women's Royal Rumble was held in 2018 and won by Asuka. It now takes place on the same card as the men's match with the same WrestleMania stakes.
    Has anyone won the Royal Rumble from number 1?
    Yes, though it is one of the rarest feats in the match. Shawn Michaels (1995), Chris Benoit (2004) and Edge (2021) all won the men's Rumble after entering at number 1, and Rhea Ripley did it in the 2023 women's match.
    Who won the most recent Royal Rumble?
    At the 2026 Royal Rumble (January 31, 2026 in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, the first held outside North America), Roman Reigns won the men's match after entering at number 26, and Liv Morgan won the women's match. Both earned world-title shots at WrestleMania 42.

    WrestleMania

    WWE’s flagship show and the biggest night in wrestling — the two-night spring spectacle careers are built to reach.

    First Held1985Mar 31 · Madison Square Garden
    Editions42Through 2026
    Format2 NightsStadium era since 2020
    PromotionWWEIts flagship event

    From one arena to two nights

    WrestleMania grew from a one-night gamble into the biggest annual event in wrestling. The arc in three beats:

    1

    1985 · The gamble

    WWE chairman Vince McMahon reportedly bet the company on the first WrestleMania, staged at Madison Square Garden.

    2

    The flagship

    It became WWE’s marquee annual show — the night the entire storyline year is built to reach.

    3

    2020 → now

    Two nights since WrestleMania 36, drawing 100,000-plus across stadiums like AT&T, SoFi and Allegiant.

    Landmark editions

    WrestleMania III · 1987Hulk Hogan bodyslammed André the Giant before a crowd WWE announced at 93,173.
    X-Seven · 2001Widely rated the greatest single card in company history.
    WrestleMania XXX · 2014Brock Lesnar ended The Undertaker’s 21-match undefeated Streak.

    Moments that defined careers

    The StreakThe Undertaker went 21–0 at WrestleMania from 1991 to 2013.
    First women’s main eventBecky Lynch headlined WrestleMania 35 in 2019 — the first woman to main-event the show.
    Finished the storyCody Rhodes beat Roman Reigns at WrestleMania XL in 2024, ending the longest world-title reign of the modern era.

    This page catalogues every WrestleMania from 1985 to 2026.

    Why WrestleMania matters

    WrestleMania is the anchor of the professional-wrestling calendar. WWE builds its entire storyline year toward it: the Royal Rumble in January exists to crown a number-one contender for the WrestleMania main event, and championships are routinely held, delayed or switched to maximise their WrestleMania payoff. It is also WWE's single largest commercial engine, driving subscription sign-ups, ticket and merchandise revenue, and host-city tourism.

    For the performers, a WrestleMania moment is the closest thing wrestling has to a permanent record. Debuts, retirements, title coronations and career-defining upsets are staged here by design. That is why the event's history doubles as a history of the industry's biggest stars — and why nearly every profile on Wrestle Lore connects back to a WrestleMania match.

    Records & superlatives

    The Streak

    The Undertaker, 21–0 at WrestleMania (1991–2013) before Brock Lesnar ended it at WrestleMania XXX (2014). Final record 21–1.

    Record attendance

    WWE announced 101,763 ⚑ for WrestleMania 32 (2016, AT&T Stadium); independent estimates put it near 80,000. Two-night totals have since topped 160,000.

    Most appearances

    The Undertaker (25+), followed by Triple H, Shawn Michaels and John Cena.

    Highest-rated match

    The Undertaker def. Shawn Michaels, WrestleMania XXV (2009) — a rare five-star classic (Wrestling Observer).

    First women's main event

    Becky Lynch beat Ronda Rousey & Charlotte Flair to close WrestleMania 35 (2019) — the first women's match to headline.

    Two-night era

    Began at WrestleMania 36 (2020, empty arena) and has been the permanent format ever since.

    Shortest main-card match

    ~6 seconds — The Rock pinned Erick Rowan at WrestleMania 32 (2016).

    FAQ

    When did WrestleMania start?
    WrestleMania began on March 31, 1985 at Madison Square Garden in New York City, headlined by Hulk Hogan and Mr. T defeating Roddy Piper and Paul Orndorff.
    Where is WrestleMania held?
    WrestleMania rotates among major North American stadiums and arenas. Recent hosts include SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles (2023), Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia (2024), and Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas (2025 and 2026).
    Why is WrestleMania two nights now?
    WWE first split WrestleMania across two nights at WrestleMania 36 (2020) during the pandemic and made the two-night format permanent from WrestleMania 37 (2021) onward to fit a larger roster and longer card.
    What is The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak?
    The Undertaker won his first 21 WrestleMania matches from 1991 to 2013, a run known as The Streak. Brock Lesnar ended it at WrestleMania XXX (2014), making the final record 21–1.
    Which WrestleMania is considered the best?
    WrestleMania X-Seven (2001) at the Houston Astrodome is widely regarded as the greatest WrestleMania card. The Undertaker versus Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania XXV (2009) is the most critically acclaimed single match, earning a rare five-star rating.
    What is the most famous WrestleMania moment?
    Hulk Hogan bodyslamming André the Giant at WrestleMania III (1987) in the Pontiac Silverdome is widely considered wrestling's most iconic single image.
    Who won the most recent WrestleMania?
    At WrestleMania 42 (April 18–19, 2026, Allegiant Stadium, Las Vegas), Cody Rhodes defeated Randy Orton to retain the Undisputed WWE Championship on Night 1, and Roman Reigns defeated CM Punk to win the World Heavyweight Championship on Night 2.

Backlash

Backlash 2026

Date: May 9, 2026 Venue: Benchmark International Arena Location: Tampa, Florida

Backlash 2026 was WWE's first post-WrestleMania blow-off show of the year, held at Benchmark International Arena in Tampa. Roman Reigns made his first World Heavyweight Championship defense since winning it at WrestleMania 42, and John Cena used the show to announce a new cross-brand tournament, the John Cena Classic.

What is the John Cena Classic?

The John Cena Classic is a tournament announced by John Cena at Backlash 2026, featuring stars from both the main roster and NXT competing for a new championship voted on by fans.

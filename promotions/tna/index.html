TNA / Impact

TNA (Total Nonstop Action), later Impact Wrestling, was the leading alternative to WWE through the 2000s and the home of the innovative X-Division. Founded in 2002, TNA's modern-era significance is as a proving ground: its "no limits" X-Division made international stars of AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Christopher Daniels, and its main event drew Olympic gold medalist Kurt Angle and veterans Sting, Hulk Hogan and Bully Ray. Its 2005 Styles/Daniels/Joe triple threat earned TNA's first five-star rating.

A short history of TNA

Founded in 2002 by Jeff and Jerry Jarrett, Total Nonstop Action launched into a post-Monday-Night-Wars world where WWE stood alone. TNA's answer was to be different: a smaller, edgier, in-ring-focused product built around the X-Division — a weight-limit-free, high-flying, high-risk style summarized by the slogan "it's not about weight limits, it's about no limits." Its six-sided ring became an instant visual signature.

The X-Division's trifecta of AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Christopher Daniels produced some of the decade's finest matches, culminating in the 2005 Unbreakable triple threat — the first bout under a TNA banner to earn five stars. The main event drew credibility from Kurt Angle, whose arrival from WWE in 2006 signaled TNA's ambitions, and later from Bully Ray, who reinvented himself as a menacing TNA World Champion.

Through the late 2000s TNA leaned on nostalgia signings like Sting, Hulk Hogan and Mick Foley, with mixed results. The company rebranded as Impact Wrestling for much of the 2010s before reverting to the TNA name, and it endures today with talent-sharing partnerships across the industry. Its lasting legacy is as the launchpad that sent Styles, Joe and Angle on to WWE and helped mainstream the athletic, indie-influenced style now standard everywhere.

Frequently Asked

What is TNA?
TNA (Total Nonstop Action), later renamed Impact Wrestling and then TNA again, is the U.S. promotion founded in 2002 that became the leading alternative to WWE in the 2000s. It is best known for its innovative X-Division and for showcasing AJ Styles, Samoa Joe, Christopher Daniels and Kurt Angle.
What is the X-Division?
The X-Division is TNA's signature style — a fast, high-flying, high-risk division built around athleticism rather than weight limits, summed up by the slogan "it's not about weight limits, it's about no limits." Its greatest bout, the 2005 Styles vs Daniels vs Joe triple threat, earned TNA's first five-star rating.
Is TNA still around?
Yes. The company operated as Impact Wrestling for much of the late 2010s and early 2020s before reverting to the TNA name, and it continues today with talent-sharing ties to other promotions. Stars like AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Kurt Angle used TNA as a launchpad to WWE.

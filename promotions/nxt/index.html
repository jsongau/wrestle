    NXT

    WWE NXT is WWE's developmental brand that grew, under Triple H's creative leadership, into a critically acclaimed "third brand." Based at the Performance Center in Orlando, its "Black-and-Gold" TakeOver era (2014–2021) routinely outshone the main roster, producing multiple five-star matches and driving both the women's revolution and the indie-talent boom into WWE. NXT made or elevated Finn Balor, Shinsuke Nakamura, Kevin Owens, Seth Rollins and the Four Horsewomen before they defined the modern era.

    A short history of NXT

    NXT began around 2010 as a game-show-style competition before relaunching in 2012 as WWE's developmental brand at the Orlando Performance Center. Under the creative direction of Triple H, it quickly became something no developmental territory had ever been: appointment viewing. Seth Rollins was crowned the first-ever NXT Champion in 2014, and the special-event TakeOver series turned the brand into a critical darling.

    The "Black-and-Gold" era (2014–2021) was NXT's golden age. Triple H signed indie standouts and international icons — Finn Balor (from NJPW's Bullet Club), Shinsuke Nakamura, Kevin Owens and Samoa Joe — and let them wrestle a harder-hitting, story-first style. TakeOver events produced multiple five-star matches, including Andrade Almas vs Johnny Gargano and Adam Cole vs Gargano, and the long-form Gargano-Ciampa saga became a modern storytelling benchmark.

    NXT was also the launchpad of the women's revolution. The Four Horsewomen — Charlotte Flair, Becky Lynch, Sasha Banks (Mercedes Mone) and Bayley — proved women could headline, with the 2015 Bayley vs Banks TakeOver: Brooklyn classic changing how WWE booked its women's division. In 2021 WWE rebranded the brand as "NXT 2.0"; the Black-and-Gold years remain its creative high point and WWE's most important talent pipeline.

    Frequently Asked

    What is WWE NXT?
    NXT is WWE's developmental brand, based at the Performance Center in Orlando. Under Triple H's creative leadership it evolved from a developmental territory into a critically acclaimed "third brand" whose Black-and-Gold TakeOver events regularly outshone the main roster.
    What was the TakeOver era?
    NXT TakeOver was the brand's series of special events from 2014 to 2021, the heart of its "Black-and-Gold" golden age. TakeOvers delivered multiple five-star matches — including Almas vs Gargano and Adam Cole vs Gargano — and drove the women's revolution and the indie-talent boom into WWE.
    Who built NXT?
    Triple H (Paul Levesque) was the creative architect of the Black-and-Gold NXT, signing and elevating indie and international talent such as Finn Balor, Shinsuke Nakamura, Kevin Owens, Samoa Joe and the Four Horsewomen before it became WWE's head-of-creative pipeline.

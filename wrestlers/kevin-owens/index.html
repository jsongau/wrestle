Kevin Owens

Kevin Owens (Kevin Steen) is a former Universal and NXT Champion who announced himself by pinning John Cena in his 2015 main-roster debut. An elite promo and versatile "Prizefighter," his decades-long real-life bond with Sami Zayn produced some of WWE's most resonant storytelling.

  • Real name Kevin Yanick Steen
  • From Marieville, Quebec, Canada
  • Debut 2000 (WWE 2014)
  • Promotions ROH, PWG, WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames KO, The Prizefighter
  • Finishers Pop-up Powerbomb, Stunner, Swanton Bomb

Career

An independent standout in ROH and PWG, Kevin Steen signed with WWE in 2014 and quickly won the NXT Championship in 2015. Called up to the main roster the same year, he shocked the audience by defeating John Cena for the United States Championship in his debut feud — an instant credibility-maker.

Owens went on to hold the Universal Championship in 2016, plus multiple Intercontinental and United States reigns. His "Festival of Friendship" betrayal of Chris Jericho and his emotional arc through the Bloodline saga showcased his range, but the throughline of his career remains the lifelong friendship and rivalry with Sami Zayn.

FAQ

What is Kevin Owens's real name?
Kevin Yanick Steen, from Quebec, Canada. He wrestled as Kevin Steen on the indies before joining WWE in 2014.
Did he beat John Cena in his debut?
Yes — he pinned Cena to win the United States Championship in his 2015 main-roster debut feud.
What is the Owens–Sami Zayn story?
They are lifelong best friends and on-off rivals who came up together on the independents, fueling a landmark WWE saga.

Becky Lynch

Becky Lynch (Rebecca Quin) is "The Man" — the star whose 2018 rise led to the first women's WrestleMania main event. As "Becky Two Belts" she won both women's world titles at WrestleMania 35, cementing her as a cornerstone of WWE's women's evolution and an original Four Horsewoman.

  • Real name Rebecca Quin
  • From Dublin, Ireland
  • Debut 2002 (WWE 2013)
  • Promotions WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames The Man, Becky Two Belts, Big Time Becks
  • Finishers Dis-arm-her, Manhandle Slam

Career

An original member of the Four Horsewomen alongside Charlotte Flair, Sasha Banks and Bayley, Becky Lynch was the inaugural SmackDown Women's Champion in 2016. After a slower start, her organic 2018 turn into the defiant "The Man" made her the most popular performer in the company.

That momentum carried her to WrestleMania 35 in 2019, where she beat Charlotte Flair and Ronda Rousey in a "Winner Takes All" triple threat to claim both the Raw and SmackDown Women's Championships — the first women's match to main-event WrestleMania. A multi-time Raw Women's Champion, her career-long rivalry with Charlotte Flair spans from NXT to the main event.

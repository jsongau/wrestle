Finn Balor

Finn Balor (Fergal Devitt) founded the Bullet Club and became the first-ever WWE Universal Champion. His demonic "Demon King" alter ego and technical excellence carried him from New Japan stardom to multiple NXT Championships and a run with Judgment Day.

  • Real name Fergal Devitt
  • From County Wicklow, Ireland
  • Debut 2000 (WWE 2014)
  • Promotions NJPW, WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames The Prince, The Demon King
  • Finishers Coup de Grâce, 1916 (Bloody Sunday DDT)

Career

As Prince Devitt, Fergal Devitt became a top junior heavyweight in New Japan and founded the Bullet Club — a faction later led by AJ Styles that grew into one of wrestling's most influential brands. He joined WWE in 2014 and captured the NXT Championship, unveiling his supernatural "Demon" persona in NXT's biggest matches, including classics with Samoa Joe.

At SummerSlam 2016 he defeated Seth Rollins to become the first-ever WWE Universal Champion — only to vacate the title the next night due to a shoulder injury sustained in the bout. He rebuilt to win multiple NXT and Intercontinental titles and later anchored the Judgment Day faction.

FAQ

What is Finn Balor's real name?
Fergal Devitt, from County Wicklow, Ireland. He wrestled as Prince Devitt in New Japan before joining WWE in 2014.
Was he the first Universal Champion?
Yes — he beat Seth Rollins at SummerSlam 2016, but a shoulder injury in the match forced him to vacate the title the next night.
Did he found the Bullet Club?
Yes, as Prince Devitt in New Japan. The faction was later led by AJ Styles and became hugely influential.

    Penta

    Luchador · WWE Intercontinental Champion

    Penta, half of the Lucha Brothers with brother Rey Fenix, beat Dominik Mysterio for the WWE Intercontinental Championship on Raw on March 2, 2026.

    Biography

    Penta is a masked lucha libre standout who reached WWE's singles-title picture within his first full year on the main roster. On the March 2, 2026 episode of Raw he beat Dominik Mysterio for the WWE Intercontinental Championship, the workhorse title created in 1979, taking his first singles championship in the company.

    He is best known as one half of the Lucha Brothers alongside his real-life brother Rey Fenix. The pair built their reputation as a tag team before Penta's singles run in WWE put the Intercontinental Championship around his waist.

    The Intercontinental Championship Penta now holds carries heavy modern history. Gunther set the record for the longest reign in the title's history at 666 days, a benchmark that frames what Penta is chasing as champion.

    FAQ

    What title does Penta hold in WWE?

    The WWE Intercontinental Championship. Penta won it from Dominik Mysterio on Raw on March 2, 2026, his first singles title in WWE.

    Who is Penta's tag team partner?

    His brother Rey Fenix. Together they wrestle as the Lucha Brothers.

    Who has been chasing Penta for the Intercontinental Championship?

    Rey Fenix has been reported as pursuing the Intercontinental Championship. That program was still developing as of mid-2026.

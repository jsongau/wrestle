Edge

Edge (Adam Copeland), "The Rated-R Superstar," is an 11-time world champion and a ladder-match innovator who defined the opportunist archetype in WWE. He won the first Money in the Bank, cashed it in for his first WWE title, and after a 2011 retirement staged a stunning 2020 comeback before joining AEW.

  • Real name Adam Joseph Copeland
  • Born October 30, 1973 · Orangeville, ON
  • Debut 1992 (WWF 1998)
  • Promotions WWF/WWE, AEW
  • Nicknames The Rated-R Superstar, Ultimate Opportunist
  • Finishers Spear, Edgecution/Impaler DDT

Career

Edge first made his name as one half of the tag team Edge and Christian, whose TLC (Tables, Ladders and Chairs) wars with the Hardy Boyz and the Dudley Boyz revolutionized tag team wrestling around the turn of the millennium. He won the inaugural Money in the Bank ladder match at WrestleMania 21 (2005).

In January 2006 he cashed in that briefcase to beat John Cena for his first WWE Championship — the first successful cash-in ever — and reinvented himself as the scheming "Rated-R Superstar." He piled up 11 world titles and battled The Undertaker, Randy Orton and others across the era.

A neck injury forced Edge to retire in 2011, but a shocking return at the 2020 Royal Rumble revived his career. He later signed with AEW as Adam Copeland, adding new chapters to a Hall of Fame legacy.

FAQ

What is Edge's real name?
Adam Joseph Copeland, born October 30, 1973, in Orangeville, Ontario. He wrestles in AEW as Adam Copeland.
Did Edge win the first Money in the Bank?
Yes — he won the first-ever MITB ladder match in 2005 and performed the first successful cash-in to beat John Cena in 2006.
How many world titles did Edge win?
11 — seven World Heavyweight Championships and four WWE Championships — plus a record 14 tag title reigns.

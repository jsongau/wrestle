    01

    Overview

    Edge — Adam Copeland, and in AEW simply Cope — has the strangest shape of any great career: superstardom, a spinal diagnosis that ended it at 37, nine years of retirement, and then a second career longer and stranger than most wrestlers’ first. 11world championship reigns in WWE — four WWE Championships and a record seven World Heavyweight ChampionshipsHe won eleven world championships in WWE — four WWE Championships and a record seven World Heavyweight Championships — among 31 total WWE titles, the most of anyone in that company’s history. He won the first Money in the Bank match ever held and executed the first cash-in. He won Royal Rumbles eleven years apart. And as of August 31, 2026 he is an AEW World Tag Team Champion at 52, alongside the same partner he won his first title with at 26: Christian.

    Setting one thing straight

    One number needs untangling, because even the sources trip on it. Edge is frequently credited with winning the tag titles “a record 12 times with Christian.” The 12 is real but it is not theirs together: Edge holds 12 World Tag Team Championship reigns in total — seven with Christian, plus reigns with Chris Benoit, Hulk Hogan, Randy Orton and Chris Jericho — and two WWE Tag Team Championship reigns (with Rey Mysterio in 2002, and with Jericho in 2009) on top. Fourteen WWE tag reigns, seven of them with Christian. The arithmetic that gets him to 31 championships: 4 WWE titles + 7 World Heavyweight + 5 Intercontinental + 1 United States + 12 World Tag + 2 WWE Tag. The AEW titles — two TNT Championships and the current World Tag Team Championship — sit outside that count.

    The first career ran June 22, 1998 (his televised WWF debut) to April 3, 2011: the ladder and TLC wars with Christian, the Hardys and the Dudleys; King of the Ring 2001; the Rated-R Superstar heel run that started with the live sex celebration and ended with him as the best villain of his era; the WrestleMania XXIV main event against The Undertaker. 3,285days between the WrestleMania XXVII farewell and the Royal Rumble 2020 return — a retirement diagnosed as permanentThen an MRI found cervical spinal stenosis, and on April 11, 2011 he retired on Raw as reigning World Heavyweight Champion, days after beating Alberto Del Rio at WrestleMania XXVII. Hall of Fame, 2012, inducted by Christian. The comeback nobody planned for came at the Royal Rumble on January 26, 2020; he beat Randy Orton in a Last Man Standing match at WrestleMania 36, won the 2021 Rumble from the No. 1 spot, main-evented WrestleMania 37 against Roman Reigns and Daniel Bryan, and founded The Judgment Day — the faction that promptly threw him out and became WWE’s top act without him.

    The AEW chapter began at WrestleDream on October 1, 2023, six weeks after his final WWE match — a win over Sheamus in Toronto on August 18, 2023. Since then: two TNT Championships, both involving Christian Cage on the other side; a fractured tibia from the top of a barbed-wire cage at Double or Nothing 2024 that cost him the belt and eight months; a world title challenge against Jon Moxley at Revolution 2025 that Christian’s cash-in turned into a three-way; and FTR’s betrayal at Dynasty 2025, which finally pushed the two old rivals onto the same side. Cage & Cope beat FTR in an I Quit match at Double or Nothing on May 24, 2026 — Beth Phoenix, Copeland’s wife, punching Dax Harwood down the ramp on the way — to win the AEW World Tag Team Championship, and retained against the Young Bucks at All In: London on August 30, 2026, where the Motor City Machine Guns, FTR and New Level all appeared for the staredown that maps out what comes next.

    05

    Factions

    Four units across four eras — and the two that mattered most both had Christian in them.

    WWF · 1998–1999

    The Brood

    Gangrel, Edge, Christian

    The vampire-adjacent trio that introduced both Canadians: rising through the ring in a circle of fire, dumping 'bloodbaths' on enemies. A gimmick with a shelf life, but the entrance remains one of the most-referenced of the era, and it welded Edge and Christian together on screen as they already were off it.

    WWF/WWE · 1998–2001

    Edge & Christian

    Edge, Christian

    Seven tag title reigns, the five-second pose, kazoos, and the ladder/TLC trilogy with the Hardys and Dudleys that redefined what tag wrestling could draw. They split in 2001 when Edge won King of the Ring and Christian turned on him — the first of a quarter-century of breakups and reunions.

    WWE · 2022

    The Judgment Day

    Edge (founder), Damian Priest, Rhea Ripley

    Founded by Edge in April 2022 as a cult-of-personality heel stable; by June the members had voted him out, with Finn Balor taking his place. The faction became WWE's dominant act for years — built on a foundation he laid and was evicted from.

    AEW · 2025–present

    Cage & Cope

    Christian Cage, Cope, with Beth Phoenix in their corner

    The reunion nobody in the story wanted until FTR forced it: after the Dynasty 2025 betrayal, Christian — Copeland's tormentor through 2023-24 — became his partner again at Forbidden Door on August 24, 2025. AEW World Tag Team Champions since Double or Nothing 2026, with Copeland's wife Beth Phoenix as the equaliser at ringside.

    06

    Personas

    One man, three billing names and a quarter century of recalibration: Sexton Hardcastle (independents) → Edge (1998–2023) → Adam Copeland / Cope (AEW, 2023–present). WWE owns the name Edge; he took his own to AEW.

    SHIndependents · 1992–1998

    Sexton Hardcastle

    The Ontario independent scene name, alongside Christian as 'Suicide Blondes.' He broke in by winning a Toronto newspaper essay contest for free training with Sweet Daddy Siki and Ron Hutchison at 17.

    BRWWF · 1998–2000

    The brooding loner

    Debuted June 22, 1998 as a mute, trench-coated mystery who entered through the crowd — then found his actual gift, comedy, when the Brood dissolved into Edge & Christian's five-second poses.

    RRWWE · 2004–2011

    The Rated-R Superstar

    The defining persona: an opportunistic, gleefully hateable heel built on the live sex celebration with Lita, the first Money in the Bank cash-in, and a genius for winning titles in ways that felt like theft. 'The Ultimate Opportunist' was an insult he adopted as a title.

    GMWWE · 2020–2023

    The grizzled comeback

    The 2020-23 version wrestled as a man borrowing time: Last Man Standing wars with Orton, the 2021 Rumble from No. 1, the Judgment Day founding and eviction, and a farewell lap that ended where he started, in Toronto.

    COAEW · 2023–present

    Cope

    Billed under his real name — then just Cope — since WrestleDream 2023. The Cope Open challenge series, two TNT titles, a broken tibia off a cage top at 50, and now one half of Cage & Cope. The persona is mostly just Adam Copeland: a lifer who cannot stop.

    07

    Career Timeline

    An essay contest in Toronto to a Wembley title defence 34 years later.

    1992

    Debut

    First match in Toronto in 1992, after winning free training from Sweet Daddy Siki and Ron Hutchison in a newspaper essay contest at 17.

    1998

    WWF debut

    Televised debut June 22, 1998 on Raw against Jose Estrada Jr.; the Brood, then Edge & Christian follow.

    2000

    The ladder era begins

    First tag titles in the WrestleMania 2000 triangle ladder match on April 2; TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven a year later.

    2001

    King of the Ring, and the split

    Wins the 2001 tournament; Christian turns on him, and the singles career starts.

    2005

    Mr. Money in the Bank

    Wins the first Money in the Bank ladder match at WrestleMania 21 on April 3, 2005 — then holds the case for nine months.

    2006

    The first cash-in

    Cashes in on a bloodied John Cena at New Year's Revolution on January 8, 2006 for his first WWE Championship, inventing the move every holder since has copied.

    2008

    Champion era

    La Familia, three world title reigns in a year, and the WrestleMania XXIV main event against The Undertaker's streak on March 30.

    2011

    Retired by his own spine

    Retains over Del Rio at WrestleMania XXVII on April 3; an MRI finds cervical spinal stenosis; he announces retirement on the April 11 Raw and vacates the World Heavyweight Championship. Hall of Fame, 2012.

    2020

    The comeback

    Returns at the Royal Rumble on January 26, 2020, nine years after doctors said never; beats Orton in a Last Man Standing match at WrestleMania 36.

    2021

    Rumble to main event

    Wins the Royal Rumble from No. 1 on January 31; main-events WrestleMania 37 against Reigns and Bryan.

    2023

    Toronto goodbye, AEW hello

    Final WWE match August 18 in Toronto, beating Sheamus; debuts in AEW at WrestleDream on October 1.

    2024

    TNT gold and a broken leg

    Beats Christian in the I Quit match on March 20 in Toronto; retains against Malakai Black in the barbed-wire cage at Double or Nothing on May 26 but fractures his tibia coming off the top of it, and is stripped of the title.

    2025

    Moxley, betrayal, reunion

    Loses the three-way world title match at Revolution on March 9; FTR turn on him at Dynasty; he and Christian Cage reunite at Forbidden Door on August 24 and beat FTR at All Out in Toronto on September 20.

    2026

    Tag champions again, 26 years on

    Cage & Cope take the AEW World Tag Team Championship from FTR in the Double or Nothing I Quit match on May 24 and retain against the Young Bucks at All In: London on August 30.

    08

    Rivalries

    Who he went to war with.

    Christian Cage

    Childhood best friend, seven-time tag partner, 2001 betrayer, 2012 Hall of Fame inductor, 2023-24 AEW tormentor, and current tag partner again. Their three TNT title matches — Worlds End 2023 twice in one night, then the Toronto I Quit match — were the meanest wrestling either has done, which is exactly why the 2025 reunion landed. No relationship in wrestling has been mined this long or this well.

    Randy Orton

    Rated-RKO partners turned career-long opposites. Orton was the opponent for the 2020 comeback — the WrestleMania 36 Last Man Standing match and the vicious 'greatest wrestling match ever' Backlash follow-up — and the man Edge last eliminated to win the 2021 Rumble... the 2010 one having ended the same way.

    John Cena

    The definitive Edge victim: the first cash-in at New Year's Revolution 2006, the TLC wars, the Unforgiven 2006 title change in Toronto. Edge's heel peak exists in opposition to Cena's face peak.

    The Undertaker

    The 2008 program — champion versus streak at WrestleMania XXIV, then the SummerSlam Hell in a Cell — was the elevation ceremony: Edge main-evented WrestleMania against the most protected act in the company and took the chokeslam through the ring for his trouble.

    FTR

    Allies against the Death Riders through early 2025 until they beat him down at Dynasty in April — the turn that put him on a stretcher and created Cage & Cope. Beaten at All Out 2025, beaten again in the I Quit title match at Double or Nothing 2026 where Dax Harwood threatened his family and Beth Phoenix answered personally — and back in the frame after the All In: London staredown.

    Mick Foley

    One match, permanent consequences: the WrestleMania 22 hardcore match, April 2, 2006, ending with Edge spearing Foley through a flaming table. Edge has said it legitimised him as a main-eventer; Foley designed it to do exactly that.

    Records & Feats

    The records, and the asterisks the retellings drop.

    31WWE championships
    7World Heavyweight reigns
    3,285Days retired
    ★

    31 championships in WWE — the most in company history

    4 WWE Championships + 7 World Heavyweight + 5 Intercontinental + 1 United States + 12 World Tag Team + 2 WWE Tag Team. The AEW titles sit outside this count.

    ★

    Record seven World Heavyweight Championship reigns

    No one else reached seven with that title before it was retired in 2013. The last reign was surrendered, not lost — vacated April 15, 2011 upon retirement.

    ★

    First Money in the Bank winner and first cash-in

    Won the inaugural ladder match at WrestleMania 21 on April 3, 2005; cashed in on John Cena at New Year's Revolution on January 8, 2006. Every cash-in since is quoting him.

    ★

    Two Royal Rumble wins, eleven years apart

    2010 and 2021 — the 2021 win from the No. 1 spot, at age 47, one year into a comeback from a nine-year retirement. Only a handful of men have won multiple Rumbles; none with that gap.

    Frequently Asked

    Why did Edge retire in 2011, and how did he come back?

    An MRI in the spring of 2011 found cervical spinal stenosis — a narrowing of the spinal canal — and doctors refused to clear him, citing risk of paralysis. He announced retirement on the April 11, 2011 Raw as reigning World Heavyweight Champion, days after beating Alberto Del Rio at WrestleMania XXVII, and vacated the title on April 15. Nine years of medical advances and re-evaluation later, he was cleared, and returned in the 2020 Royal Rumble on January 26 — 3,285 days after his last match. He has wrestled ever since, in WWE until 2023 and AEW from October 2023.

    How many world titles has Edge won — and does AEW change the number?

    Eleven, all in WWE: four WWE Championships and a record seven World Heavyweight Championships. AEW does not change it — his AEW titles are two TNT Championships and the current World Tag Team Championship, and his one AEW World Championship match (Revolution, March 9, 2025, against Jon Moxley, turned into a three-way by Christian Cage’s cash-in) was a loss. His overall WWE total is 31 championships, the most in that company’s history.

    What is Edge’s status right now?

    As of August 31, 2026 he is one half of the reigning AEW World Tag Team Champions with Christian Cage, as Cage & Cope. They took the titles from FTR in an I Quit match at Double or Nothing on May 24, 2026 — with Beth Phoenix intervening on her husband’s behalf — and retained against the Young Bucks at All In: London on August 30, 2026. After that defence the Motor City Machine Guns, FTR and New Level all confronted the champions, so the queue is formed. He turns 53 in October and wrestles as Cope, under his real surname’s shorthand, because WWE owns the name Edge.

    Why is he called Cope in AEW?

    Because WWE owns the trademark on “Edge.” He debuted in AEW at WrestleDream on October 1, 2023 under his real name, Adam Copeland, and the billing has since compressed to Cope — which also names his open-challenge series, the Cope Open. The Rated-R iconography travelled with him even though the name could not.

    What happened between Edge and FTR?

    They were allies first — FTR stood with him against Jon Moxley’s Death Riders through early 2025. After his world title loss at Revolution, FTR beat him down at Dynasty in April 2025, putting him out on a stretcher. The answer was the reunion with Christian Cage: wins over FTR at All Out in Toronto on September 20, 2025 and again — for FTR’s tag titles — in the Double or Nothing I Quit match on May 24, 2026, where Dax Harwood invoked Copeland’s daughters and Beth Phoenix personally delivered the receipts. FTR reappeared in the challengers’ staredown at All In: London, so it is not over.

    Will Ospreay

    NJPW-built high flyer · AEW headliner

    Will Ospreay built his name in NJPW as IWGP World Heavyweight and United States Champion. He now headlines AEW after winning the 2026 Owen Hart Cup and an AEW World Title shot.

    Biography

    Will Ospreay made his name in New Japan Pro-Wrestling, rising from the junior heavyweight division to the top of the card as IWGP World Heavyweight Champion and IWGP United States Champion.

    He now works the top of the card in All Elite Wrestling, where he featured at Forbidden Door 2026. He shares an NJPW-to-AEW path with names such as Jon Moxley and Kenny Omega.

    After returning from neck surgery in 2026, Ospreay won the Owen Hart Cup by beating Swerve Strickland in the final and earned an AEW World Championship shot against Kenny Omega at All In 2026.

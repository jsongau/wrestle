    Biography

    Goldberg's 1997-1998 undefeated streak in WCW — eventually reaching 173-0 — is the most successful monster-push in American wrestling history. WCW, desperate for a homegrown star to counter WWF's Austin-driven ratings surge, stumbled into Goldberg almost by accident: he was supposed to be one-note squash fodder, but audiences saw something different.

    The "Goldberg" chant that started appearing in arenas by mid-1998 was genuine, spontaneous, and unprecedented for a power-first performer with minimal psychology. He didn't need a character: the crowd wanted to count the wins, they wanted to chant "Who's Next", and they wanted to watch the Spear-Jackhammer sequence. The simplicity was the point.

    His WCW Championship win over Hollywood Hulk Hogan at WCW Monday Nitro (not PPV) — witnessed by 40,000 fans in the Georgia Dome in July 1998 — was arguably WCW's last genuine cultural moment. The streak ended at Starrcade 1998 when Kevin Nash defeated him in a taser-assisted finish that the crowd felt was a cheat, because it was.

    Goldberg's WWE tenure came in two phases: a 2003 run that suffered from McMahon's creative insistence on making him look beatable, and a 2016-2019 return that was handled much better. His Universal Championship reign (defeating Brock Lesnar at Survivor Series 2016 in under two minutes) was a viral moment that proved the Goldberg mystique still worked two decades later.

    Finishing Moves

    • Spear — One of the most impactful running spears in wrestling history — at full height and speed, the contact was visually devastating.
    • Jackhammer — A delayed vertical suplex into a powerslam — the signature finisher that closed every squash match. Nearly impossible to execute at his size.

    Career Timeline

    1. 1997

      WCW debut

      Hired by WCW after an NFL career (Rams, Falcons) ended by injury — has minimal in-ring training but extraordinary physical presence.

    2. 1997–98

      The streak begins

      Begins accumulating wins in WCW squash matches — crowd starts chanting and counting; WCW leans in.

    3. 1998

      173-0 — WCW Champion

      Defeats Hollywood Hulk Hogan at WCW Monday Nitro in the Georgia Dome in front of 40,000 fans — WCW's last genuine cultural moment.

    4. 1998

      Starrcade — streak ends

      Kevin Nash defeats Goldberg in a taser-assisted finish — the ending the crowd found unsatisfying, and rightly so.

    5. 2003

    FAQ

    What was Goldberg's undefeated streak?

    Goldberg's WCW winning streak reached 173 consecutive victories before Kevin Nash defeated him at WCW Starrcade 1998 with taser assistance.

    Did Goldberg win the WCW World Heavyweight Championship?

    Yes — Goldberg defeated Hollywood Hulk Hogan for the WCW World Heavyweight Championship on July 6, 1998 at WCW Monday Nitro in front of 40,000 fans in the Georgia Dome.

    Did Goldberg win the WWE Universal Championship?

    Yes — Goldberg won the WWE Universal Championship in 2017 by defeating Kevin Owens, and later held the WWE Championship in 2020 by defeating The Fiend Bray Wyatt.

    Signature Matches

    ★★★

    Goldberg vs. Hollywood Hulk Hogan

    WCW Monday Nitro — Jul 6, 1998

    WCW Championship — not a great match technically but one of wrestling's most significant moments: 40,000 fans in the Georgia Dome; Goldberg's peak; WCW's last genuine pop-culture milestone.

    ★★

    Goldberg vs. Kevin Nash

    WCW Starrcade — Dec 27, 1998

    The streak ends — Nash wins with taser assistance. The finish infuriated the crowd and damaged the streak's mythology.

    ★★★½

    Goldberg vs. Brock Lesnar

    Survivor Series — Nov 20, 2016

    Under-90-second squash — Goldberg destroys Lesnar and sends the internet into meltdown. The most effective use of the Goldberg character in WWE.

Goldberg

Goldberg (Bill Goldberg) is a two-time WCW World Champion and two-time WWE Universal Champion whose famous undefeated streak made him one of the era's biggest attractions. A former NFL player, he exploded in 1998 WCW with a signature Spear-and-Jackhammer squash formula and later returned to WWE to capture the Universal Championship in his 50s.

  • Real name William Scott Goldberg
  • From Oklahoma, USA
  • Debut / era 1997 · WCW 1997–2001 · WWE 2003–04, 2016–2022
  • Promotions WCW, WWE
  • Nicknames Da Man, "The Streak"
  • Finishers Spear, Jackhammer

Career

A former NFL defensive lineman, Goldberg debuted in WCW in 1997 and became an overnight phenomenon. His billed 173–0 undefeated streak, built on rapid-fire Spear-and-Jackhammer squashes, made him the hottest act of the late Monday Night Wars.

The apex came in July 1998 when he pinned Hollywood Hogan to win the WCW World Championship in front of 40,000 fans at the Georgia Dome — a famous moment given away free on Nitro. The nWo's Kevin Nash later ended his streak at Starrcade 1998.

His WWE returns bookended a feud with Brock Lesnar and a later program with Roman Reigns, and he twice captured the Universal Championship in high-impact short matches.

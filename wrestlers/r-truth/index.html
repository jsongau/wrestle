    01

    Overview

    R-Truth has the strangest resume on the WWE roster. Before he was the man who chased a 24/7 belt across parking lots, golf courses and a wedding, he was Ron “The Truth” Killings, two-time NWA World Heavyweight Champion, beating Ken Shamrock for the title on August 7, 2002 in NWA-TNA’s first year. 54reigns as WWE 24/7 Champion, 2019–2022. The next-best count is 15The rest of the ledger is long and real: two Hardcore reigns in 2001 as K-Kwik, two United States Championships, tag titles with Kofi Kingston in 2012, The Miz at WrestleMania XL in 2024 and Damian Priest in 2026, and a WWE Championship match against John Cena at Capitol Punishment in 2011.

    Setting one thing straight

    Two things people get wrong about him. First, he was not released in June 2025 in the usual sense. He posted “I just got released from WWE” on June 1, but Fightful reported that WWE was letting his contract lapse rather than cutting it. Six days later he was back, costing Cena and Logan Paul at Money in the Bank. Second, the 24/7 count depends on who is counting. WWE.com’s profile says 54. Wikipedia’s champions list says 54 overall and 53 recognized by WWE. Either number is a record no one is close to, and the title was retired in November 2022, so no one ever will be.

    He was born Ronnie Aaron Killings in Charlotte, North Carolina on January 19, 1972. He turned down college scholarships to chase music, sold drugs to pay for it, was arrested four times and served 13 months. Wrestling came after. He trained under George South and others, won the NWA Wildside TV title from AJ Styles in 1999, and reached the WWF as K-Kwik, Road Dogg’s rapping partner, in November 2000. 2002the year he won the NWA World Heavyweight Championship, a title he calls proof he was the first Black man to hold itReleased in 2001, he became a main-event act in NWA-TNA: two NWA world titles, the 3 Live Kru with Konnan and B.G. James, and TNA tag gold with NFL cornerback Adam “Pacman” Jones in 2007. WWE brought him back as R-Truth on August 29, 2008.

    The second WWE run has lasted 18 years and three characters. The rapping babyface of 2008–10. The paranoid “Little Jimmy” heel of 2011, who got a world title match and a Survivor Series main event. Then the comedy act that took over the back half of his career and made him one of WWE’s most popular performers. 147days as WWE Tag Team Champion with Damian Priest in 2026, won at 542025 was the wildest year of it: a feud with his childhood hero Cena, the non-renewal, the return, a haircut on Raw that announced “Ron Killings,” and a quiet slide back to R-Truth within two months. In 2026 he and Priest won the WWE Tag Team Championship on March 20 and held it 147 days. Priest turned on him on September 11. Their singles match on October 16 was stopped when Truth was hurt on a top-rope dropkick. As of November 9, 2026 he has not wrestled since, and says he will be back.

    04

    Factions

    A career of partnerships, most of which ended with somebody turning on him.

    NWA-TNA · 2003–05

    3 Live Kru

    Ron Killings, Konnan, B.G. James

    Formed in July 2003. Won the vacant NWA World Tag Team titles on November 26, 2003 and defended them under the Freebird rule. It ended when Konnan turned on the others in December 2005.

    NWA-TNA · 2007

    Team Pacman

    Ron Killings, Adam "Pacman" Jones

    The NFL cornerback and the former world champion beat Kurt Angle and Sting for the TNA tag titles at No Surrender 2007. Jones could not always appear, so Consequences Creed defended in his place and lost them at Bound for Glory.

    WWE · 2011, 2024

    Awesome Truth

    R-Truth, The Miz

    Debuted as heels on August 22, 2011, got fired by Triple H, rehired, and main-evented Survivor Series against Cena and The Rock. Miz broke it up the next night. The 2024 reunion won the Raw tag titles at WrestleMania XL, and Miz walked out on him again on September 30, 2024.

    WWE · 2016–17

    Golden Truth

    R-Truth, Goldust

    A comedy tag team built on two veterans who should not have worked together and did. Beat Breezango at Money in the Bank 2016, and had its own remix of "What's Up?".

    WWE · 2023

    The Judgment Day (self-appointed)

    R-Truth, uninvited

    After his Survivor Series return he decided he was a member. The group attacked him on December 11, 2023, and a week later he beat JD McDonagh in a Loser Leaves Judgment Day street fight.

    WWE · 2026

    Wepa Up

    R-Truth, Damian Priest

    Named on WWE Shop merch for Priest's "Wepa" and Truth's "What's up?". Won the WWE Tag Team Championship on March 20, 2026 and held it 147 days. Priest ended it with South of Heaven on September 11, 2026.

    05

    Personas

    Six names in 27 years. Every one of them raps.

    KKWWF · 2000–01

    K-Kwik

    Road Dogg's rapping partner, debuting on Raw is War on November 13, 2000. Two Hardcore title reigns in February 2001, then released that August.

    TRNWA-TNA · 2002–07

    Ron "The Truth" Killings

    The serious version. Two NWA World Heavyweight titles, the 3 Live Kru, and "Toma" by Pitbull as his walk-out. This is the run his own song "Legacy" is about.

    RTWWE · 2008–10

    R-Truth

    Returned on SmackDown on August 29, 2008, rapping "What's Up?" to the ring. A popular babyface who won the US title in 2010.

    LJWWE · 2011

    The paranoid heel

    Convinced the world was lying to him, talking to the invisible "Little Jimmy". Got a WWE Championship match against Cena and a Survivor Series main event. Many fans still rate it his best character work.

    24WWE · 2015–2025

    The comedy legend

    The confused, joyful R-Truth who thinks he is someone else and never stops trying. Carried the 24/7 Championship for three years and became one of WWE's most popular acts.

    RKWWE · June 2025

    Ron Killings

    Back from the non-renewal, he cut off his hair on Raw on June 9, 2025 and said the Truth had set Ron Killings free. Within two months he was R-Truth again. He later called Ron Killings "the cavalry" and "a selfish move".

    06

    Career Timeline

    Prison, music, the WWF, a world title in TNA, and an 18-year second act.

    1999

    K-Krush, and a first title

    Wins the NWA Wildside Television Championship from AJ Styles on November 6, and signs a WWF developmental deal.

    2000

    K-Kwik reaches Raw

    Twice MCW Southern Heavyweight Champion in Memphis, then debuts on Raw is War on November 13 alongside Road Dogg.

    2001

    Hardcore, and released

    Wins the Hardcore title from Raven on February 3 and 4, loses it to Crash Holly both times within hours, and is released in August.

    2002

    NWA World Heavyweight Champion

    Signs with NWA-TNA in June and beats Ken Shamrock for the NWA world title on August 7 in Nashville. Jeff Jarrett takes it on November 20.

    2003

    The 3 Live Kru

    Forms the Kru with Konnan and B.G. James in July. They win the vacant NWA World Tag Team titles on November 26.

    2004

    Second world title

    Wins a four-way over AJ Styles, Chris Harris and Raven on May 19. Jarrett regains it in a King of the Mountain match on June 2.

    2007

    Team Pacman, then out

    Wins the TNA tag titles with Adam "Pacman" Jones at No Surrender on September 9, loses them October 14, and leaves TNA in December.

    2008

    R-Truth arrives

    Returns to WWE on SmackDown on August 29 and beats Kenny Dykstra.

    2010

    First US title

    Loses with John Morrison at WrestleMania XXVI, then wins the vacant US title from The Miz on May 24.

    2011

    The heel turn

    Turns on John Morrison, challenges Cena for the WWE Championship at Capitol Punishment on June 19, and main-events Survivor Series with The Miz.

    2012

    Tag champion with Kofi

    Wins the WWE Tag Team titles with Kofi Kingston on April 30 and holds them until Night of Champions on September 16.

    2019

    The 24/7 era begins

    Wins a second US title on January 29, then the 24/7 title on May 20. Over the next three years he wins it 54 times.

    2023

    Judgment Day, uninvited

    Returns at Survivor Series on November 25 and declares himself a member. Beats JD McDonagh in a Loser Leaves Judgment Day match on December 18.

    2024

    WrestleMania gold at 52

    Wins the Raw tag titles with The Miz in the WrestleMania XL ladder match on April 6. Loses them June 24. Miz walks out on him September 30.

    2025

    Cena, the release, and Ron Killings

    Loses to John Cena at Saturday Night's Main Event on May 24. Announces his release on June 1, returns at Money in the Bank on June 7, and cuts his hair on Raw as Ron Killings on June 9. Back to R-Truth by late summer. Releases The White Album, a No. 10 Billboard country record.

    2026

    Wepa Up, then betrayal

    Wins the WWE Tag Team Championship with Damian Priest on March 20 and loses it August 14. Priest turns on him September 11. Their October 16 match is stopped when Truth is hurt. He says he will be back.

    07

    Rivalries

    Who he went to war with, and who turned on him.

    John Cena

    Two feuds 14 years apart. In 2011 the paranoid heel challenged Cena for the WWE Championship at Capitol Punishment and lost. In 2025 Truth called Cena his childhood hero, got put through a table at the Backlash press conference, lost at Saturday Night's Main Event on May 24, and returned from his non-renewal to cost Cena at Money in the Bank.

    Damian Priest

    Partner, then attacker. They won the WWE Tag Team Championship on March 20, 2026 and held it 147 days. After losing an AAA tag title shot on September 11, Priest hit him with South of Heaven. Their October 16 match ended in a referee stoppage, and Truth says Priest "tried to kill me".

    The Miz

    The partner who keeps leaving. Awesome Truth main-evented Survivor Series 2011 and split the next night. The 2024 reunion won tag gold at WrestleMania XL, and Miz walked out on him again that September.

    Jeff Jarrett

    Frequently Asked

    Is R-Truth still in WWE?

    Yes. He is on SmackDown but out injured as of November 9, 2026. His match with Damian Priest on October 16, 2026 was stopped by the referee after he was hurt on a top-rope dropkick. On October 19 he posted from hospital: “I AM OK” and “I’ll be back.” No return date has been announced.

    Did WWE release R-Truth in 2025?

    Not in the usual sense. On June 1, 2025 he posted “I just got released from WWE,” but Fightful reported WWE was letting his contract expire rather than cutting it. After a week of backlash from fans and wrestlers he returned at Money in the Bank on June 7, 2025, costing John Cena and Logan Paul their tag match against Cody Rhodes and Jey Uso.

    Why did R-Truth change his name to Ron Killings?

    After his return he cut off his hair on Raw on June 9, 2025 and said the Truth had set Ron Killings free. Dave Meltzer reported he asked for the name in his new deal so he could keep using it after WWE. Fightful reported he wanted a more serious run. Within two months he was back to the R-Truth character. He later told CBS Sports Ron Killings was “the cavalry” and “a selfish move.”

    How many times did R-Truth win the 24/7 Championship?

    54, per WWE.com. Wikipedia’s champions list counts 54 overall with 53 recognized by WWE. The first was May 20, 2019, pinning Robert Roode in a parking lot. The last was June 20, 2022. Nobody else reached 16. The title was retired in November 2022.

    Was R-Truth a world champion?

    Yes, in NWA-TNA. As Ron “The Truth” Killings he won the NWA World Heavyweight Championship twice: from Ken Shamrock on August 7, 2002, and in a four-way over AJ Styles, Chris Harris and Raven on May 19, 2004. Jeff Jarrett ended both reigns. In WWE his only world title match was against John Cena at Capitol Punishment in 2011.

    Who did R-Truth win tag team titles with?

    Five partners across three promotions: the 3 Live Kru (Konnan and B.G. James) for the NWA tag titles in 2003, Adam “Pacman” Jones for the TNA tag titles in 2007, Kofi Kingston in 2012, The Miz at WrestleMania XL in 2024, and Damian Priest from March 20 to August 14, 2026.

    Why did Damian Priest turn on R-Truth?

    On the September 11, 2026 SmackDown they lost an AAA World Tag Team Championship match to the War Raiders. Priest hugged Truth, put a sombrero on him, then hit him with a clothesline and South of Heaven. On the October 30 SmackDown Priest said Truth had been dragging him down.

    What is R-Truth's real name and how old is he?

    Ronnie Aaron Killings, born January 19, 1972 in Charlotte, North Carolina. He is 54. One wrestling database gives his birthplace as Williamsburg, South Carolina; Wikipedia says Charlotte, which WWE also bills as his hometown.

    What is R-Truth's entrance theme?

    “What’s Up?”, which he performs himself. It has been his WWE theme since 2008, apart from short swaps in 2010 and 2011, and appears on Voices: WWE The Music, Vol. 9. In June 2025 he declined Metro Boomin’s offer of a new theme: “What’s up ain’t broken, I wanna keep it.”

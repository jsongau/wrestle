    01

    Overview

    Stacy Keibler got into wrestling through a dance contest. In November 1999 she won WCW's fan-voted Nitro Girl Search out of 300 entrants and joined the troupe as Skye. Within months WCW turned her into Miss Hancock, a business-suit heel valet who danced on tables. When the WWF bought WCW in 2001 it bought her contract too, and she spent the next five years as one of the most visible women on Raw and SmackDown. 2023her WWE Hall of Fame class, inducted by Mick Foley and Torrie WilsonShe managed the Dudley Boyz as the Duchess of Dudleyville, worked as Mr. McMahon's assistant, managed Test and then Scott Steiner, and wrestled at WrestleMania XX. In 2006 she finished third on Dancing with the Stars, left WWE that July and built a TV career. WWE inducted her into its Hall of Fame on March 31, 2023.

    Setting one thing straight

    Two things get repeated about her that the record does not support. First, she never held a championship. She challenged for the Women's title in November 2001, at Judgment Day 2002, on Raw in October 2004 and in the Taboo Tuesday 2004 battle royal, and Trish Stratus beat her every time. Second, she did not win Dancing with the Stars. She finished third in season 2, behind Drew Lachey and Jerry Rice, though she earned the season's first perfect score. Her Hall of Fame case was never about titles. WWE's own announcement rests it on her time as a manager and a fan favorite who crossed into mainstream TV.

    She was born Stacy Ann-Marie Keibler on October 14, 1979 in Rosedale, Maryland, took dance from age three and cheered for the Baltimore Ravens at 18. 2001the first tag team bra and panties match, Invasion, July 22, with Torrie WilsonHer WCW year was all angles: a feud with Daffney over David Flair, WCW's first Wedding Gown match at Bash at the Beach 2000, and a false pregnancy story that ended with her off TV. In the WWF she started as an Alliance heel alongside Torrie Wilson. The two lost the first tag team bra and panties match to Lita and Trish Stratus at Invasion, then split, and Wilson beat her in the first lingerie match at No Mercy 2001.

    Her role kept changing. She held the cage key for the Dudleys at Survivor Series 2001 and went through a table for them in March 2002. Test and Steiner fought over her contract at Bad Blood and Unforgiven 2003. 3rdher finish on Dancing with the Stars season 2, 2006Randy Orton dated her on screen and then hit her with an RKO in March 2005 to turn heel. Her last run was as Super Stacy, sidekick to The Hurricane and Rosey, and her last match was a loss to Jillian Hall on Velocity in October 2005. After Dancing with the Stars she acted in What About Brian, Chuck, Psych and How I Met Your Mother. She returned to WWE to induct Torrie Wilson in 2019, was inducted herself in 2023, and is playable in WWE 2K26.

    04

    Before WWE

    WCW, 1999 to 2001. Three roles in under two years.

    Baltimore · 1997–99

    Ravens cheerleader

    Stacy Keibler

    Dance training from age three and a spot on the Baltimore Ravens cheer squad at 18. That background won her the Nitro Girl Search and gave her the dance routines she used for years.

    WCW · 1999–2000

    Skye, Nitro Girl

    The Nitro Girls troupe

    Won WCW's nationwide fan-vote contest on Nitro on November 8, 1999, out of 300 entrants, and danced weekly as Skye until the troupe wound down in early 2000.

    WCW · 2000

    Miss Hancock

    Standards and Practices; David Flair

    A heel valet in a business suit who stripped down to table-dance. She first managed the tag team Standards and Practices, then took David Flair from Daffney. That feud gave her WCW's first Wedding Gown match at Bash at the Beach 2000 and a mud match loss to Major Gunns at New Blood Rising.

    WCW · 2001

    Stacy Keibler, manager

    Shawn Stasiak

    After the false pregnancy angle took her off TV, she came back under her own name and managed Shawn Stasiak against Bam Bam Bigelow. When WCW was sold in 2001, the WWF bought her contract.

    05

    Personas

    Five characters in six years. Most were someone's manager.

    MHWCW · 2000

    Miss Hancock

    The table-dancing heel valet. WWE.com and the Bash at the Beach card spell it Ms. Hancock; Cagematch spells it Miss. Same character.

    ALWWF · 2001

    Alliance mean girl

    Debuted on SmackDown on June 14, 2001, then joined The Alliance with Torrie Wilson as a heel pair who mocked everyone.

    DDWWF · 2001–02

    Duchess of Dudleyville

    Manager of the Dudley Boyz through the tag title unification at Survivor Series 2001. It ended when they powerbombed her through a table on Raw in March 2002.

    MGWWE · 2002–03

    Manager for hire

    Mr. McMahon's assistant on SmackDown, then Test's manager on Raw, then the prize in a Test and Scott Steiner feud. Mick Foley fired both men on December 1, 2003.

    SSWWE · 2005

    Super Stacy

    Superhero sidekick to The Hurricane and Rosey after Randy Orton's RKO ended their on-screen romance. Her last character before she left wrestling.

    06

    Career Timeline

    Dance contest to Hall of Fame in twenty-four years, with the wrestling packed into six.

    1999

    Wins the Nitro Girl Search

    Announced on Nitro on November 8, 1999. She dances for WCW as Skye.

    2000

    Miss Hancock

    Becomes a heel valet, feuds with Daffney over David Flair, and loses WCW's first Wedding Gown match at Bash at the Beach on July 9.

    2001

    WWF debut and Invasion

    Debuts on SmackDown on June 14 after the WCW sale. Loses the first tag team bra and panties match at Invasion on July 22 and the first lingerie match at No Mercy on October 21. Takes over as the Dudleys' manager in October.

    2002

    WrestleMania X8 and Judgment Day

    WrestleMania debut at ringside for the Dudleys, then a Women's Championship loss to Trish Stratus at Judgment Day on May 19. Works as Mr. McMahon's assistant, moves to Raw on August 12 and pairs with Test.

    2003

    Test against Steiner

    Scott Steiner wins her services at Bad Blood on June 15; Test wins them back at Unforgiven on September 21. Mick Foley fires both men on December 1.

    2004

    WrestleMania XX and a title shot

    Loses the Playboy Evening Gown match at Madison Square Garden on March 14. Loses a Women's Championship match to Trish Stratus in Manchester on October 11 and the Taboo Tuesday battle royal eight days later.

    2005

    The RKO and Super Stacy

    Randy Orton hits her with an RKO on Raw on March 21. She joins The Hurricane and Rosey as Super Stacy, moves to SmackDown in August, and wrestles her last match on Velocity in October.

    2006

    Dancing with the Stars, then out

    Finishes third on season 2 with Tony Dovolani on February 26. Announces on July 21 that her WWE contract has ended and that she has a Touchstone TV development deal.

    2007

    Acting

    What About Brian and George Lopez in 2007, then October Road and Samurai Girl, followed by Chuck, Psych and How I Met Your Mother from 2010.

    2013

    Host

    Hosts Lifetime's Supermarket Superstar. Marries Jared Pobre on March 8, 2014.

    2019

    Inducts Torrie Wilson

    Returns to WWE on April 6 to induct her old partner and rival into the Hall of Fame.

    2023

    Hall of Fame

    Inducted on March 31 in Los Angeles by Mick Foley and Torrie Wilson.

    2026

    WWE 2K26

    Playable in WWE 2K26, with the Kid Rock cover of Legs back as her entrance music.

    07

    Rivalries

    Partners, rivals and the women who kept beating her for the title.

    Torrie Wilson

    Alliance partner in 2001, then her main rival after Wilson's Tajiri romance made Wilson a fan favorite. Wilson won the No Mercy lingerie match and most of their 2002 bra and panties series. They stayed close friends, roomed together in Los Angeles, and each inducted the other into the Hall of Fame, in 2019 and 2023.

    Trish Stratus

    The champion in every Women's Championship match Stacy had: SmackDown and Raw in November 2001, Judgment Day 2002, Raw on October 11, 2004, and Taboo Tuesday 2004. Trish won them all.

    Lita

    Across the ring at Invasion 2001, the first big women's match Stacy had in the WWF, and a regular singles and tag opponent through 2001 and 2002.

    Daffney

    Her WCW rival over David Flair in 2000. Daffney beat her in WCW's first Wedding Gown match at Bash at the Beach.

    Molly Holly

    The 2004 Raw opponent she beat most often, including a singles win at Madison Square Garden on October 4, a week before her last title shot. Holly also eliminated her from the Taboo Tuesday battle royal.

    Frequently Asked

    Is Stacy Keibler in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Yes. She went in with the Class of 2023, inducted by Mick Foley and Torrie Wilson on March 31, 2023 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. Rey Mysterio, The Great Muta and Andy Kaufman were in the same class.

    Did Stacy Keibler ever win a championship?

    No. She never held a title in WCW or WWE. She challenged Trish Stratus for the Women's Championship in November 2001, at Judgment Day on May 19, 2002, on Raw on October 11, 2004, and in the Taboo Tuesday battle royal on October 19, 2004, and lost each time.

    What was Stacy Keibler's name in WCW?

    Two names. She joined as Skye after winning the Nitro Girl Search on November 8, 1999, then became Miss Hancock in 2000, a heel valet for Standards and Practices and later David Flair. In 2001 she worked under her own name as Shawn Stasiak's manager.

    Who did Stacy Keibler manage in WWE?

    The Dudley Boyz as the Duchess of Dudleyville from October 2001, then Test from late 2002, then Scott Steiner after he won her services at Bad Blood 2003. Test won her back at Unforgiven 2003. In 2005 she was Super Stacy alongside The Hurricane and Rosey.

    How did Stacy Keibler do on Dancing with the Stars?

    She finished third in season 2 with Tony Dovolani, behind Drew Lachey and Jerry Rice, in the finale on February 26, 2006. Her week 5 samba got a perfect 30, which WWE.com calls the season's first perfect score.

    When did Stacy Keibler leave WWE?

    In July 2006. She took time off for Dancing with the Stars, and on July 21, 2006 she posted that her WWE contract “has come to an end” and that she had signed a development deal with Touchstone TV.

    What was Stacy Keibler's last match?

    A loss to Jillian Hall on Velocity, which Cagematch dates to October 25, 2005. Her last match on a pay-per-view was the Taboo Tuesday battle royal on October 19, 2004.

    What was Stacy Keibler's entrance theme?

    Legs, Kid Rock's cover of the ZZ Top song, released on the WWF Forceable Entry album on March 26, 2002. WWE 2K26 brought it back as her entrance music.

    Who is Stacy Keibler married to?

    Jared Pobre. They married in Mexico on March 8, 2014, have three children, and co-founded the men's skincare brand Caldera + Lab. Before that she dated actor George Clooney from 2011 to 2013.

    Is Stacy Keibler coming back to WWE?

    Nothing is announced. She has not wrestled since 2005. Her WWE appearances since leaving were Tough Enough in 2011, inducting Torrie Wilson in 2019 and her own Hall of Fame induction in 2023. As of November 9, 2026 her current WWE presence is as a playable legend in WWE 2K26.

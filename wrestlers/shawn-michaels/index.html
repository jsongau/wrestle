Shawn Michaels

Shawn Michaels — "The Heartbreak Kid" — is widely regarded as one of the greatest in-ring performers ever and the man they call Mr. WrestleMania. He pioneered the ladder match, delivered a run of five-star classics, co-founded D-Generation X, and sat at the center of the 1997 Montreal Screwjob before becoming a beloved elder statesman and NXT trainer.

  • Real name Michael Shawn Hickenbottom
  • From Texas, USA
  • Debut / era 1984 · WWF star 1988–1998 · comeback 2002–2010
  • Promotions WWF/WWE · head of NXT creative
  • Nicknames HBK, The Showstopper, Mr. WrestleMania, The Icon
  • Finisher Sweet Chin Music (superkick)

Career

Breaking out of the tag team The Rockers with Marty Jannetty, Michaels reinvented himself as the cocky, dazzling "Heartbreak Kid." He pioneered the WWF's first-ever Ladder Match against Razor Ramon at WrestleMania X (1994) and elevated the entire main-event style, becoming a two-time Royal Rumble winner and multi-time world champion.

His real-life and on-screen rivalry with Bret Hart culminated in the infamous 1997 Montreal Screwjob, and he co-founded D-Generation X alongside Triple H. After a four-year retirement due to a back injury, he returned in 2002 and produced arguably the finest late-career run in history — including the dream match with Kurt Angle at WrestleMania 21 and the two Undertaker epics at WrestleMania 25 and 26.

FAQ

What is Shawn Michaels's real name?
Michael Shawn Hickenbottom, known as HBK, "The Heartbreak Kid" and "Mr. WrestleMania."
What is Sweet Chin Music?
His superkick finisher — one of the most recognizable finishing moves in wrestling.
Why is he called Mr. WrestleMania?
For a career of WrestleMania classics: the first ladder match, the Iron Man Match with Bret Hart, and his acclaimed WrestleMania 25 and 26 matches with The Undertaker.

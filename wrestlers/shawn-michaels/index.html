Shawn Michaels

Shawn Michaels — HBK, the Heartbreak Kid, Mr. WrestleMania — is the greatest in-ring performer of the 1990s and arguably of all time. He pioneered the ladder match, built the standard for WrestleMania main events, and capped a legendary comeback with the two greatest matches in WrestleMania history against The Undertaker. His career ended at WrestleMania 26 in a match he could never have deserved more.

How they win

Sweet Chin Music — the superkick heard around the world

HBK built to the superkick — tuning up the band in the corner, waiting for the opponent to rise, then unloading. The sweet spot of his career combined elite in-ring psychology with the most dramatic finishing sequence in the industry.

WM 25 & 26

Two consecutive WrestleMania matches with The Undertaker rated ★★★★★ by virtually every observer — the high-water mark of the event's history.

Iron Man Match

The WrestleMania XII Iron Man match with Bret Hart — 60 minutes, 0–0, resolved in overtime — remains the most demanding match in WM history.

Comeback

Retired in 1998 with a severe back injury; came back in 2002 after four years and produced arguably the finest late-career run in history.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1984–1988

    The Rockers — learning the craft

    Started as half of The Rockers with Marty Jannetty, one of the WWF's best tag teams. Chemistry, timing, and athletic style defined the partnership.

  2. 1991–1993

    Going solo & IC gold

    The infamous Barbershop window segment ended The Rockers; Michaels relaunched as the cocky Heartbreak Kid and established himself as a world-class singles performer.

  3. 1994–1997

    Ladder, Iron Man & DX

    The first-ever ladder match with Razor Ramon at WM X; the 60-minute Iron Man with Bret Hart; co-founding D-Generation X with Triple H. The Montreal Screwjob ended his first run.

  4. 1998

    The match that ended Act One

    Lost the WWF title to Steve Austin at WrestleMania XIV. A back injury sustained in the Royal Rumble 1998 Royal Rumble forced retirement.

  5. 2002–2007

    The comeback — Act Two

    Returned at SummerSlam 2002 in an unsanctioned match with Triple H; won his third and fourth world titles; the Kurt Angle WM 21 classic.

  6. 2008–2010

    The Undertaker saga

    WrestleMania 25 and 26 vs The Undertaker — two of the finest matches ever contested. His career ended at WM 26 as stipulated. Hall of Fame 2011.

FAQ

Why is he called Mr. WrestleMania?
For a career of WrestleMania masterpieces — the ladder match, the Iron Man, WM 25 and 26 with The Undertaker.
What is Sweet Chin Music?
HBK's superkick finisher — he tunes up the band in the corner and unloads a kick to the chin. One of wrestling's most iconic finishers.
When did HBK retire?
Officially at WrestleMania XXVI (2010) when The Undertaker pinned him in a Streak vs Career match. He had a one-off return at Crown Jewel 2018 (a tag match) but has not returned to singles competition.

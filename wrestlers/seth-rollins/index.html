Seth Rollins

Seth Rollins (Colby Lopez) is a multi-time WWE world champion, a founding member of The Shield and the man who stole the show at WrestleMania 31 — cashing in Money in the Bank mid-main-event to win the WWE Championship. The self-styled "Architect" became the inaugural WWE World Heavyweight Champion in 2023.

  • Real name Colby Daniel Lopez
  • From Davenport, Iowa, USA
  • Debut 2005 (WWE 2012)
  • Promotions ROH, WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames The Architect, The Visionary, The Kingslayer
  • Finishers Curb Stomp (The Stomp), Pedigree, Phoenix Splash

Career

Trained on the independents and in Ring of Honor, Colby Lopez signed with WWE in 2012 and was immediately placed in The Shield alongside Roman Reigns and Dean Ambrose (Jon Moxley). He had also become the first-ever NXT Champion in 2014, the same year he detonated the faction by turning on his brothers and aligning with The Authority.

His signature moment came at WrestleMania 31, where he cashed in Money in the Bank during the Lesnar–Reigns main event to capture the WWE Championship — one of the boldest booking swerves in company history. A relentless workhorse, Rollins collected two WWE Championships, two Universal Championships and, in 2023, became the inaugural WWE World Heavyweight Champion.

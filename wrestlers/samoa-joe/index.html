Samoa Joe

Samoa Joe, "The Samoan Submission Machine," is a world champion in TNA, ROH, WWE NXT and AEW and one of the most credible, hard-hitting performers of his era. He anchored ROH and TNA in the 2000s with a near-untouchable aura, headlining the classic trifecta with AJ Styles and Christopher Daniels.

  • Real name Nuufolau Joel Seanoa
  • Born March 17, 1979 · Orange County, CA
  • Debut 1999
  • Promotions ROH, TNA, WWE/NXT, AEW
  • Nicknames The Samoan Submission Machine, The Destroyer
  • Finishers Coquina Clutch, Muscle Buster

Career

Joe built an aura of dominance in Ring of Honor, where his long ROH World title reign and 2004 wars with CM Punk are considered independent-scene classics. He carried that credibility into TNA, becoming a centerpiece of its main event and X-Division.

His defining program was the TNA "trifecta" with AJ Styles and Christopher Daniels, capped by their acclaimed three-way at Unbreakable 2005, along with a memorable series against Kurt Angle. He won the TNA World Heavyweight Championship as the promotion's near-unstoppable force.

In WWE he became a two-time NXT Champion and feuded with Seth Rollins and Brock Lesnar, later serving as a commentator. He then reignited his in-ring career in AEW, winning the TNT and AEW World Championships.

FAQ

What is Samoa Joe's real name?

What is Samoa Joe's finishing move?
The Coquina Clutch (a rear naked choke) and the Muscle Buster.

Yes — TNA World Heavyweight, ROH World, WWE NXT (twice) and AEW World, plus multiple AEW TNT titles.

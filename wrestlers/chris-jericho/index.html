Chris Jericho

Chris Jericho (Y2J) is the first-ever Undisputed WWF Champion and the first-ever AEW World Champion — a perpetual reinventor who has headlined across five decades. A record nine-time Intercontinental Champion and rock frontman, he bridged generations from WCW to WWE to AEW while constantly evolving his character.

  • Real name Christopher Keith Irvine
  • From Born New York; raised Winnipeg (American-Canadian)
  • Debut / era 1990 · WCW 1996–99 · WWE 1999–2018 · AEW 2019–present
  • Promotions WCW, ECW, WWF/WWE, NJPW, AEW
  • Nicknames Y2J, Le Champion, The Demo God
  • Finishers Walls of Jericho (Liontamer), Codebreaker, Judas Effect

Career

After building his reputation in WCW's cruiserweight division and abroad, Jericho debuted in the WWF in 1999 by interrupting The Rock. In 2001 he made history at Vengeance, defeating both The Rock and Stone Cold Steve Austin in one night to become the first Undisputed WWF Champion.

Across the following decade he reinvented himself repeatedly — from flashy fan favorite to cerebral suit-wearing heel — delivering a landmark 2008 feud with Shawn Michaels and a memorable 2012 program with CM Punk. In 2019 he capped his career of firsts by becoming the inaugural AEW World Champion, helping legitimize the new promotion.

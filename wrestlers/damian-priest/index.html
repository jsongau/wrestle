    01

    Overview

    Damian Priest waited nineteen years for the twenty seconds that define him. A New Yorker raised in Vega Baja, Puerto Rico, he debuted on the East Coast independents in 2005, spent 2015 to 2018 in Ring of Honor as Punishment Martinez, and did not reach WWE's main roster until he was 38. 118days as World Heavyweight Champion in 2024, from a WrestleMania XL cash-in to the SummerSlam loss against GuntherThen it compounded fast: the United States Championship for 191 days in 2021–22, the Money in the Bank briefcase in 2023, and the cash-in at WrestleMania XL on April 7, 2024 — moments after Drew McIntyre had won the World Heavyweight Championship and stopped to taunt CM Punk — that made him champion for 118 days. He is also a three-time tag team champion across two partners who could not be less alike: two reigns with Finn Balor at the Judgment Day's peak, and one in 2026 with R-Truth.

    The Judgment Day is the spine of the middle of the career. He joined at WrestleMania 38 in April 2022 as Edge's first recruit, helped vote Edge out that June, and spent two years as the group's enforcer and tag champion — until the group expelled him in August 2024, days after Gunther took the world title off him at SummerSlam. 3tag team championship reigns — two with Finn Balor in the Judgment Day, one with R-Truth in 2026The SmackDown move followed on January 24, 2025, and with it the two long feuds of his post-Judgment Day life: Drew McIntyre — the Sin City Street Fight loss at WrestleMania 41 on April 20, 2025, answered with the steel cage win at Saturday Night's Main Event on May 24 — and Aleister Black, a half-year blood feud that ran from a Last Man Standing loss on October 10, 2025 to the ambulance-match win that ended it on January 2, 2026.

    Setting one thing straight

    One date in circulation needs setting straight. A widely mirrored summary of his career dates the end of the United States Championship reign to February 21, 2022 — while also calling it a 191-day reign. Those two facts cannot both be true: 191 days from the SummerSlam win on August 21, 2021 lands on February 28, 2022, and WWE's own video archive titles the footage “Damian Priest snaps after Finn Balor wins the United States Title: Raw, Feb. 28, 2022.” This page publishes February 28 and treats the arithmetic and the primary source as the citation. The “snaps” part matters too: that loss triggered the heel turn that eventually delivered him to the Judgment Day.

    The 2026 chapter is the odd couple. Paired with the 54-year-old R-Truth, he won the WWE Tag Team Championship from the MFT's Tama Tonga and JC Mateo on the March 20 SmackDown — R-Truth pinning Mateo with, of all things, John Cena's Attitude Adjustment — and held it 147 days through the MFT's collapse, including the June 19 defense Solo Sikoa's interference wrecked for the challengers. 147days as WWE Tag Team Champions with R-Truth in 2026, ended by the Tongas’ triple-threat win on August 14Tama and Talla Tonga took the titles back in a triple threat with the War Raiders on August 14, 2026 and beat Priest and R-Truth clean in the rematch a week later, hitting the Red Cross on Truth. Wrestling Inc.'s report of the rematch noted Priest was “noticeably unhappy with his partner” afterward — but still helped him up. As of August 31, 2026 he holds no championship, and whether the partnership survives September is the question his television time is being spent on.

    05

    Factions

    One faction, three phases: recruit, enforcer, expelled champion.

    WWE · 2022

    The Judgment Day — Edge's version

    Edge, Damian Priest, Rhea Ripley

    Priest was the founding recruit, joining Edge at WrestleMania 38 in April 2022. The recruitment pitch was rebirth for a stalled career, and it worked in the least expected way: within two months, Priest, Ripley and new arrival Finn Balor voted Edge out of his own group.

    WWE · 2022–2024

    The Judgment Day — the peak

    Damian Priest, Finn Balor, Rhea Ripley, Dominik Mysterio, JD McDonagh

    The group's imperial phase, with Priest as its heavy and half of its tag championship: two Undisputed WWE Tag Team title reigns with Balor from September 2023 to April 2024, plus the Money in the Bank briefcase he carried for nine months. The internal tension — Balor eyeing the briefcase, Priest eyeing the exits — was the best long-form character work of the group's run.

    WWE · 2024

    The expulsion

    Priest out; Balor, Mysterio, McDonagh, Morgan, Rodriguez in

    Days after Gunther beat him at SummerSlam 2024, the Judgment Day threw him out, with Finn Balor leading the coup — the exact mirror of what they had done to Edge. Priest left as a babyface, feuded with the group into the autumn, and moved to SmackDown on January 24, 2025.

    WWE · 2026–present

    With R-Truth

    Damian Priest, R-Truth

    Not a faction — a comedy-and-violence tag team that won real gold. Truth's Attitude Adjustment on JC Mateo won the WWE Tag Team Championship on March 20, 2026; the pair held it 147 days. Since losing the titles and the August 21 rematch, Priest's frustration with his partner has been the on-screen story, and WWE reporting has openly speculated about a heel turn.

    06

    Personas

    Three names in twenty-one years: Damian Martinez on the early independents → Punishment Martinez in Ring of Honor (2015–2018) → Damian Priest from NXT in 2018 onward.

    DMIndependents · 2005–2015

    Damian Martinez

    A decade of East Coast independents before anyone was watching — the long apprenticeship that makes him one of the latest bloomers among modern WWE champions.

    PMRing of Honor · 2015–2018

    Punishment Martinez

    The breakout: a 6'5" agile heavy in ROH, where he became World Television Champion. The silhouette of the WWE character — gothic, long-limbed, spin-kick-heavy — was built here.

    APWWE · 2018–present

    Damian Priest, the Archer of Infamy

    Signed in 2018, rebuilt in NXT, main roster from February 1, 2021 with a win over The Miz. The archer imagery, the “live forever” catchphrase and the rock-star aura carried him from Bad Bunny's WrestleMania partner to world champion inside four years.

    08

    Rivalries

    Who he went to war with.

    Gunther

    The defining championship rivalry: Priest beat him at Money in the Bank on July 6, 2024 in Toronto, and Gunther took the World Heavyweight Championship by technical submission at SummerSlam on August 3 — the loss that ended the 118-day reign and, within days, his Judgment Day membership.

    Drew McIntyre

    Two years of unfinished business in three stipulations. Priest's WrestleMania XL cash-in stole the title McIntyre had held for five minutes; McIntyre took the WrestleMania 41 Sin City Street Fight on April 20, 2025; Priest closed it inside a steel cage at Saturday Night's Main Event on May 24, 2025.

    Aleister Black

    The blood feud of his SmackDown run, from June 2025 to January 2026. Black took the Last Man Standing match on October 10, 2025 with Zelina Vega's help; Priest ended it by winning the ambulance match on the January 2, 2026 SmackDown.

    Finn Balor

    Partner and nemesis in alternating order: Balor took his US title on February 28, 2022 and triggered the heel turn; then they held the tag titles together twice; then Balor led the vote that expelled him in August 2024. No WWE relationship of his has produced more plot.

    The MFTs

    The 2026 tag rivalry. Priest and R-Truth beat Tama Tonga and JC Mateo for the titles on March 20, retained against the Tongas on June 19 when Solo Sikoa's interference backfired on his own men, then lost the titles in the August 14 triple threat and the August 21 rematch — with Haku at ringside and the Red Cross double powerbomb as the full stop.

    Frequently Asked

    How did Damian Priest win the World Heavyweight Championship?

    By cashing in Money in the Bank at WrestleMania XL on April 7, 2024. Drew McIntyre had just beaten Seth Rollins for the title and paused to taunt CM Punk at ringside; Punk attacked McIntyre, Priest's music hit, and the cash-in match was over in under a minute. He held the championship for 118 days, beating Gunther at Money in the Bank on July 6 before losing it to him at SummerSlam on August 3, 2024 by technical submission.

    Why was Damian Priest kicked out of the Judgment Day?

    Because the group ate its founder a second time. Priest was Edge's first recruit at WrestleMania 38, helped vote Edge out in June 2022, and became the stable's enforcer and tag champion. Days after Gunther beat him for the World Heavyweight Championship at SummerSlam 2024, the Judgment Day — with Finn Balor at the front — expelled him exactly as he had helped expel Edge. He left as a babyface and moved to SmackDown on January 24, 2025.

    Is Damian Priest still a tag team champion with R-Truth?

    No. The 147-day reign ended on the August 14, 2026 SmackDown, when the MFTs — Tama Tonga and Talla Tonga, with their father Haku at ringside — won a triple threat that also involved the War Raiders, pinning R-Truth after the Red Cross double powerbomb. Priest and Truth got the rematch a week later on August 21 and lost that too. Priest was visibly frustrated with his partner afterward, and the state of the team is the current story.

    When did Damian Priest lose the United States Championship — February 21 or 28, 2022?

    February 28, 2022, to Finn Balor on Raw. A widely mirrored career summary says February 21 while also calling it a 191-day reign — but 191 days from the August 21, 2021 SummerSlam win is February 28, and WWE's own video archive dates the footage of the loss, and of Priest snapping afterward, to February 28. The snap matters more than the date: it started the heel run that led to the Judgment Day.

    What is Damian Priest's real name and background?

    Luis Martinez, born September 26, 1982 in New York City and raised in Vega Baja, Puerto Rico. He debuted in 2005, wrestled Ring of Honor as Punishment Martinez from 2015 to 2018 and became ROH World Television Champion, then signed with WWE in 2018. The finisher, South of Heaven, is named after the Slayer album — the metal obsession is genuine, not gimmick.

Bully Ray

Bully Ray (Mark LoMonaco), better known as Bubba Ray Dudley of the Dudley Boyz / Team 3D, is one half of the most decorated tag team in wrestling history and a TNA World Champion. Famed for putting opponents through tables, he later reinvented himself as a menacing singles heel.

  • Real name Mark LoMonaco
  • Born July 14, 1971 · New York, NY
  • Debut 1991
  • Promotions ECW, WWF/WWE, TNA
  • Nicknames Bubba Ray Dudley, Brother Ray
  • Finishers Bubba Bomb, 3D (team)

Career

With kayfabe brother D-Von, Bully Ray formed the Dudley Boyz in ECW, where their table-smashing brawls made them hardcore icons. The team jumped to WWF/WWE and became a cornerstone of the tag division, most famously in the TLC (Tables, Ladders and Chairs) trilogy against Edge and Christian and the Hardy Boyz.

As Team 3D, the Dudleys collected an unmatched haul of tag team championships across ECW, WWE, WCW, TNA and NJPW — a record body of work that made them the most decorated tag team of all time. Their 3D (Dudley Death Drop) finisher and "Get the tables!" catchphrase became instantly recognizable.

In TNA, Bully Ray transformed into a hated singles main-eventer, winning the TNA World Heavyweight Championship in 2013 as the leader of the Aces & Eights storyline — proving his range far beyond tag team wrestling, including a program with AJ Styles.

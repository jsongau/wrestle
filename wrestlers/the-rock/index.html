    Overview

    A third-generation superstar and the son of Rocky Johnson and grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia, Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson was rejected by fans as the smiling ‘Rocky Maivia’ before a 1997 heel turn unlocked the most electric mic work the business had seen. Across the Attitude Era he won ten world titles, main-evented three WrestleManias with Steve Austin, and became The People’s Champion.

    Then he did what no wrestler had: he became the biggest movie star on Earth. From The Scorpion King to the Fast & Furious saga, Moana, Jumanji and the A24 drama The Smashing Machine, his films have grossed over $11 billion. Off screen he built an empire, Teremana, ZOA, Papatui, Project Rock, Seven Bucks and the UFL, while returning to WWE as the menacing Final Boss.

    Frequently Asked

    What is The Rock’s real name?

    Dwayne Douglas Johnson, born May 2, 1972 in Hayward, California.

    How many world titles did The Rock win?

    Ten world reigns: eight WWF/WWE Championships and two WCW/World Championships, plus two Intercontinental and five Tag Team reigns and the 2000 Royal Rumble.

    What is The Rock’s WrestleMania record?

    6 wins and 5 losses across 11 matches, from WrestleMania 13 to WrestleMania 32, including the ~6-second win over Erick Rowan.

    Is The Rock in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Not individually as of 2026. He inducted his father Rocky Johnson and grandfather Peter Maivia in 2008; his grandmother Lia Maivia was inducted in 2024.

    What movies is The Rock known for?

    The Fast & Furious saga (Luke Hobbs), Moana (Maui), the Jumanji reboot, Jungle Cruise, Red Notice, Black Adam, Red One, Moana 2, and The Smashing Machine (2025).

    What businesses does The Rock own?

    Teremana Tequila, ZOA Energy, the Papatui grooming brand, Project Rock with Under Armour, Seven Bucks Productions, and a co-ownership of the UFL.

    Is The Rock related to Roman Reigns?

    Yes. Both belong to the Anoa’i Samoan wrestling family through Peter Maivia; Reigns is a legitimate relative.

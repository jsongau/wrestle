The Rock

The Rock (Dwayne Johnson) is a multi-time world champion and the most electrifying man in sports entertainment — a third-generation Anoa'i star whose peerless microphone work carried the Attitude and Ruthless Aggression eras before he became a global Hollywood icon. His Rock Bottom and People's Elbow are among wrestling's most iconic finishers.

  • Real name Dwayne Douglas Johnson
  • From California / Hawaii (Samoan–Black Nova Scotian descent)
  • Debut / era 1996 · peak 1998–2004 · returns through 2024
  • Promotions WWF/WWE
  • Nicknames The Great One, The People's Champion, The Brahma Bull
  • Finishers Rock Bottom, People's Elbow

Career

A third-generation member of the Anoa'i family — son of Rocky Johnson and grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia — The Rock debuted in 1996 and quickly turned a rejected babyface start into the most charismatic heel in the company as leader of the Nation of Domination. His catchphrases and promos made him a phenomenon and the co-headline attraction of the Attitude Era.

He blended electrifying mic work with main-event drama, headlining against Stone Cold Steve Austin in a WrestleMania trilogy, feuding with Triple H and Mankind, and beating Hollywood Hogan in a generational showdown at WrestleMania X8. His 2012–13 program with John Cena and his 2024 WrestleMania return alongside Roman Reigns proved his drawing power spanned decades.

FAQ

What is The Rock's real name?
Dwayne Douglas Johnson, a third-generation Anoa'i-family wrestler and son of Rocky Johnson.
What are his finishing moves?
The Rock Bottom and the People's Elbow — the latter one of the most imitated finishers in wrestling.
Is he related to Roman Reigns?
Yes — they are cousins in the Anoa'i wrestling dynasty, which also includes The Usos, and The Rock is the grandson of High Chief Peter Maivia.

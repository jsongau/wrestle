The Rock

The Rock (Dwayne Johnson) is an 8-time world champion, a three-time WrestleMania main-eventer opposite Steve Austin, and the most electrifying man in sports-entertainment history. From Nation of Domination rookie to Hollywood's biggest action star, he defined the Attitude Era alongside Stone Cold and became the most crossover-successful pro wrestler of all time.

  • Real name Dwayne Douglas Johnson
  • Born May 2, 1972 · Hayward, CA
  • Debut 1996 (as Rocky Maivia)
  • Promotions WWF / WWE
  • Finisher Rock Bottom, People's Elbow

How they win

The People's Elbow closed the show

The Rock finished matches with the Rock Bottom slam setting up the theatrical People's Elbow — a sequence that became iconic for its showmanship. Against Austin he needed three Stunners back at WrestleMania XIX to finally keep him down, a testament to his selling and narrative craft.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1996–1997

    Rocky Maivia — the rejected rookie

    Debuted at Survivor Series 1996; fan backlash to the corporate push led to a 'Die Rocky Die' chant at Madison Square Garden. The rejection forged the character.

  2. 1997–1999

    Nation of Domination & the Corporation

    Joined and eventually led the Nation of Domination; reinvented as a charismatic heel who coined 'If you smell what the Rock is cooking.' Feuded intensely with Steve Austin and won his first world title.

  3. 1999–2001

    The People's Champion — Attitude Era peak

    Turned babyface to massive pops; the Austin vs Rock trilogy defined WrestleMania for a generation. Earned the People's Champion and The Great One monikers.

  4. 2002–2004

    'Hollywood' Rock & Hollywood

    A brief heel run as Hollywood Rock; feuded with Goldberg, transitioned out of full-time wrestling and into film. The Scorpion King (2002) launched a blockbuster career.

  5. 2011–2013

    The comeback

    Returned to a record Royal Rumble pop (2011); hosted WrestleMania XXVII; faced John Cena in two successive WrestleMania main events (WM 28 & 29) and captured the WWE title at Royal Rumble 2013.

FAQ

What is The Rock's real name?
Dwayne Douglas Johnson, born May 2, 1972 in Hayward, California.
How many WWE titles did The Rock win?
Eight world championships total — six WWF/WWE Championships and two WCW/World Championships.
Why is The Rock called The People's Champion?
Because of his explosive rapport with live crowds. Despite debuting as a face who got booed (Rocky Maivia), he earned the People's Champion nickname as a babyface in 1999–2001 when he became the company's top draw.
What is the People's Elbow?
The Rock's theatrical finisher — after laying an opponent out, he removes an elbow pad, runs the ropes twice, and lands a flying elbow drop. Mocked as the worst finisher by some commentators, but one of wrestling's most over signatures.

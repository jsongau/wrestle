Cody Rhodes

Cody Rhodes (Cody Runnels) is "The American Nightmare" — an AEW co-founder who returned to WWE and "finished the story" by beating Roman Reigns for the title at WrestleMania 40. The son of Dusty Rhodes, he proved a modern star could be built entirely outside WWE.

  • Real name Cody Garrett Runnels
  • From Marietta, Georgia, USA
  • Debut 2006 (WWE 2007)
  • Promotions WWE, ROH, NJPW, AEW
  • Nicknames The American Nightmare, The Prince of Wrestling
  • Finishers Cross Rhodes, Cody Cutter, Figure Four

Career

Son of the legendary "American Dream" Dusty Rhodes and brother of Dustin Rhodes (Goldust), Cody debuted in WWE in 2007 and won Intercontinental and tag gold. In 2016 he made the bold decision to leave, reinventing himself across the independents, ROH and NJPW before helping co-found AEW in 2019 — becoming a multi-time TNT Champion and an executive.

He returned to WWE in 2022 with an acclaimed trilogy against Seth Rollins (wrestling a Hell in a Cell match with a torn pec). His quest to win the title his father never did culminated at Roman Reigns' WrestleMania: after falling short at WrestleMania 39, he "finished the story" at WrestleMania 40 in 2024, dethroning Reigns for the Undisputed WWE Championship.

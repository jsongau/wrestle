    Solo Sikoa

    Bloodline leader · Anoa'i family

    Solo Sikoa leads his faction of the Bloodline in WWE, the younger brother of The Usos and a cousin of Roman Reigns in the Anoa'i wrestling family.

    Biography

    Solo Sikoa is a member of the Anoa'i wrestling family, a brother of The Usos and a cousin of Roman Reigns. Through 2026 he was the on-screen lead of a faction of the Bloodline, WWE's longest and most decorated modern storyline.

    His brother Jey Uso and the rest of the family have moved in and out of the group across several splits. By mid-2026 the active Bloodline lineup was shifting between Sikoa's group and a Roman Reigns reunion, so the exact roster changed week to week.

    On Raw on July 20, 2026, Sikoa was part of a Bloodline program that also involved LA Knight and Jacob Fatu, the WWE United States Champion of that period. Reporting through 2026 credited Sikoa with a championship run of his own, though the exact belt and dates are still being confirmed.

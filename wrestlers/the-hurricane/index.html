    01

    Overview

    The Hurricane is Gregory Shane Helms, a cruiserweight from Smithfield, North Carolina who got over in three different costumes. In WCW he was one third of 3 Count, a boy band parody, and then Sugar Shane, the company's last Cruiserweight Champion before the sale. In the WWF he put on a green mask and cape and became a comic-book superhero who won the European, Hardcore, Cruiserweight and World Tag Team titles. 385days as Cruiserweight Champion, 2006–07, the longest reign in that title’s historyOn March 10, 2003 he pinned The Rock on Raw. Then he took the mask off. As Gregory Helms he held the Cruiserweight Championship for 385 days, from the 2006 Royal Rumble to No Way Out 2007.

    He has spent more of the last seven years behind the curtain than in front of it. WWE hired him as a backstage producer in January 2019. He was furloughed in April 2020, worked one AEW appearance that November, and was back producing for WWE within weeks. He signed a Legends deal in March 2022. His last WWE match was a 30-second cameo in the 2021 Royal Rumble. As of November 2026 the most recent reports still describe him as a WWE producer.

    Setting one thing straight

    Three things on the old version of this page needed fixing. First, the 385-day reign is the record for the Cruiserweight title that started in WCW and was retired in 2007. The separate 2016–2022 Cruiserweight title had a longer reign, Jordan Devlin’s 439 days. Second, the belts he won with Kane in 2002 were Raw’s World Tag Team Championship. Third, he entered the 2021 Royal Rumble at No. 23, not No. 24. His debut date is also disputed: two sources say July 6, 1990, and one says 1991.

    He started in the Carolinas, where he and Mike Maverick won the OMEGA tag titles twice in the Hardys’ home promotion. WCW signed him with Shannon Moore in May 1999. 2001WCW Cruiserweight Champion at Greed, March 18, WCW’s last pay-per-view3 Count held the WCW Hardcore title for 20 days in 2000 and won a ladder match at Starrcade for a title shot. Helms beat Chavo Guerrero Jr. for the Cruiserweight title at Greed and carried it into the WWF, where he lost it to Billy Kidman in July 2001. The Hurricane debuted on August 27 and won the European title from Matt Hardy that night.

    The superhero years brought sidekicks. Mighty Molly turned on him at WrestleMania X8. Rosey, the Super Hero in Training, won the World Tag Team title with him at Backlash 2005, and Stacy Keibler joined them as Super Stacy. 2vertebrae broken in his neck, reported May 18, 2007A Kurt Angle beating in October 2005 turned him into Gregory Helms. A broken neck in 2007 cost him about 18 months. He came back in December 2008, revived the mask on ECW in 2009, and was released in February 2010. He worked the independents, then TNA as an agent and on-screen leader of The Helms Dynasty from 2015 to 2017, then ROH in 2018, before WWE brought him back as a producer.

    05

    Factions

    A boy band, a tag team in the Hardys' backyard, and a superhero's supporting cast.

    Carolina indies · 1997–98

    The Serial Thrillaz

    Shane Helms, Mike Maverick

    Two-time OMEGA Tag Team Champions in the Cameron, North Carolina promotion run by Matt and Jeff Hardy of The Hardy Boyz, plus the SCW tag titles.

    WCW · 1999–2001

    3 Count

    Shane Helms, Shannon Moore, Evan Karagias; Tank Abbott as enforcer

    Jimmy Hart's boy band parody. Debuted on Thunder on December 23, 1999, won the WCW Hardcore title as a trio, and won the Gold Record ladder match at New Blood Rising. Karagias was thrown out, and Helms and Moore split in February 2001.

    WWF · 2001–02

    The Hurricane & Mighty Molly

    The Hurricane, Molly Holly

    Molly became his superhero sidekick in September 2001. At WrestleMania X8 she hit him with a frying pan, took his Hardcore title and went solo.

    WWE · 2002

    Kane & The Hurricane

    Kane, The Hurricane

    The odd couple that beat Christian and Lance Storm for Raw's World Tag Team title on September 23, 2002. Chris Jericho and Christian took it three weeks later.

    WWE · 2003–05

    The Hurricane & Rosey

    The Hurricane, Rosey (Super Hero in Training); Super Stacy in 2005

    Rosey became his trainee hero in 2003. They won the World Tag Team title at Backlash 2005 and lost it at Unforgiven. Stacy Keibler joined as Super Stacy until her trade to SmackDown in August 2005.

    TNA · 2016–17

    The Helms Dynasty

    Gregory Shane Helms, Trevor Lee, Andrew Everett

    Formed after Helms helped Lee win the X Division title on February 2, 2016. Helms led it on screen while working as a TNA agent.

    06

    Personas

    Three main characters, one man, and a mask that kept coming back.

    SSWCW · 2000–01

    Sugar Shane

    The solo cruiserweight after 3 Count, with the Vertebreaker as his finisher. WCW Cruiserweight Champion when the company was sold.

    HGWWF · 2001

    Hollywood Gregory Helms

    His first WWF character, an Alliance member who debuted on SmackDown on July 5, 2001 and lost the WCW Cruiserweight title to Billy Kidman.

    THWWF/WWE · 2001–05, 2009–10

    The Hurricane

    Green mask, cape, the Hurri-Cycle and a chokeslam far too big for him. Won the European, Hardcore, Cruiserweight and tag titles and the 2001 Observer award for Best Gimmick. Revived on ECW in 2009.

    GHWWE · 2005–09

    Gregory Helms

    The serious heel who said he had been carrying Rosey. No cape, Shining Wizard and Nightmare on Helms Street, and the 385-day Cruiserweight reign.

    HH2010–present

    Hurricane Helms

    The name that joins both halves, used on the independents, in TNA as Gregory Shane Helms, at the 2018 and 2021 Royal Rumbles, and in WWE 2K22 onward.

    07

    Career Timeline

    Carolina indies to the producer's headset, with three costumes in between.

    1990

    Debut in North Carolina

    July 6, 1990 per Wikipedia's infobox and Cagematch; Wikipedia's body text says 1991. Wins the SWA Light Heavyweight title early.

    1997

    The Serial Thrillaz

    Wins the OMEGA tag titles twice with Mike Maverick in the Hardys' promotion.

    1999

    WCW and 3 Count

    Signs with WCW in May with Shannon Moore. 3 Count debuts on Thunder on December 23.

    2000

    Hardcore Champions

    3 Count wins the WCW Hardcore title on February 28 and loses it at Uncensored. Wins the Starrcade ladder match for a Cruiserweight title shot on December 17.

    2001

    Cruiserweight Champion, then The Hurricane

    Beats Chavo Guerrero Jr. at Greed on March 18. Loses the belt to Billy Kidman in the WWF in July. Debuts as The Hurricane on August 27 and wins the European title.

    2002

    Three more belts

    Hardcore title at WrestleMania X8, the Cruiserweight title on May 16, and the World Tag Team title with Kane on September 23.

    2003

    He pins The Rock

    Rolls up The Rock on Raw in Cleveland on March 10. Rosey becomes his Super Hero in Training.

    2005

    Tag champions, then the turn

    Wins the World Tag Team title with Rosey at Backlash, adds Super Stacy, loses the belts at Unforgiven, and turns heel as Gregory Helms in October.

    2006

    The 385-day reign begins

    Wins the Cruiserweight Open at the Royal Rumble on January 29 and holds the title through the year.

    2007

    Reign ends, neck breaks

    Loses the title in the No Way Out Cruiserweight Open on February 18. WWE reports two broken vertebrae on May 18.

    2008

    Comeback

    Returns on September 19 and beats MVP on SmackDown on December 5.

    2009

    The mask on ECW

    Drafted to ECW, revives The Hurricane, and beats Paul Burchill in a mask vs career match on November 17. Released February 26, 2010.

    2015

    TNA agent

    Hired by TNA in March. Debuts on screen at Bound for Glory and leads The Helms Dynasty from 2016. Leaves in June 2017.

    2018

    Rumble cameo and ROH

    Surprise Royal Rumble entrant No. 21 on January 28. Signs with ROH in June.

    2019

    WWE producer

    Returns to WWE as a backstage producer, reported January 28.

    2020

    Furlough and return

    Furloughed April 15. Appears at AEW Full Gear on November 7. Back producing for WWE by Survivor Series weekend.

    2021

    Last match

    Royal Rumble entrant No. 23 at the ThunderDome on January 31.

    2022

    Legends deal

    Signs a WWE Legends contract on March 24 while still producing.

    2026

    Still producing

    Fightful describes him as a WWE producer in June and July 2026. He is playable in WWE 2K26.

    08

    Rivalries

    The upset everybody remembers, and the men he traded belts with.

    The Rock

    The Hurricane pinned The Rock on Raw on March 10, 2003 in Cleveland after Steve Austin's arrival distracted him. Helms later called it a debt he can never repay.

    Chavo Guerrero Jr.

    Beat Helms at Sin in January 2001, lost the Cruiserweight title to him at Greed two months later, and won the Cruiserweight Open that ended the 385-day reign in 2007.

    Matt Hardy

    Old friend from OMEGA. The Hurricane took his European title on debut night in 2001, and Hardy beat Gregory Helms at No Mercy 2006 in Raleigh.

    Rosey

    Partner from 2003 and World Tag Team Champion with him in 2005, then the man Gregory Helms said he had been carrying. Helms beat him on Raw on November 7, 2005.

    Shannon Moore

    3 Count bandmate from the Carolinas to WCW. Moore turned on him in early 2001, and Helms won the six-way at SuperBrawl Revenge.

    Frequently Asked

    Who is The Hurricane?

    Gregory Shane Helms, a cruiserweight from Smithfield, North Carolina. He was Sugar Shane and one third of 3 Count in WCW, then The Hurricane, a masked superhero, in WWE from 2001. He also wrestled as Gregory Helms and Hurricane Helms.

    Is The Hurricane still with WWE?

    Yes, as a backstage producer. WWE hired him in January 2019, furloughed him in April 2020 and brought him back that November. He signed a Legends deal in March 2022, and Fightful still described him as a WWE producer in July 2026.

    Did The Hurricane really beat The Rock?

    Yes. On Raw on March 10, 2003 in Cleveland, Steve Austin's arrival distracted The Rock and The Hurricane rolled him up for the pin. Helms later called it a debt he can never repay.

    How long was Gregory Helms Cruiserweight Champion?

    385 days. He won the Cruiserweight Open at the Royal Rumble on January 29, 2006 and lost the title in the No Way Out Cruiserweight Open on February 18, 2007. WWE counts it as 384 days.

    Is that the longest Cruiserweight reign ever?

    For the original Cruiserweight title, the one that began in WCW and was retired in 2007, yes. It is the only reign of a year or more. The separate 2016–2022 Cruiserweight title had a longer one: Jordan Devlin held it for 439 days.

    What titles did The Hurricane win?

    Eight reigns in WCW and WWE: the Cruiserweight title three times, the World Tag Team title twice (with Kane and with Rosey), and the WWF European, WWF Hardcore and WCW Hardcore titles once each.

    What was 3 Count?

    A WCW boy band parody put together by Jimmy Hart: Shane Helms, Shannon Moore and Evan Karagias. They debuted on Thunder on December 23, 1999, held the WCW Hardcore title together for 20 days in 2000, and broke up in early 2001.

    Who was Super Stacy?

    Stacy Keibler, who joined The Hurricane and Rosey as a superhero sidekick after they won the World Tag Team title at Backlash in May 2005. It lasted until she was traded to SmackDown that August.

    Why did The Hurricane become Gregory Helms?

    Kurt Angle attacked him on Raw on October 17, 2005. He skipped a title match the next week, came back unmasked as Gregory Helms, said he had been carrying Rosey, and beat Rosey on November 7.

    Did The Hurricane wrestle in AEW or TNA?

    Briefly. He was a TNA agent from March 2015 to June 2017 and led The Helms Dynasty on screen. His only AEW appearance was in the Elite Deletion match at Full Gear on November 7, 2020, while WWE had him furloughed.

    What was The Hurricane's entrance theme?

    Eye of the Hurricane, written and performed by Jim Johnston. It appears on WWE Anthology, released November 12, 2002.

    01

    Overview

    Trick Williams came to WWE as somebody else's sidekick. He debuted on NXT on September 14, 2021 as Carmelo Hayes's friend and hype man, and five years later he is the WWE United States Champion on SmackDown. In between he won the NXT North American Championship, the NXT Championship twice, and the TNA World Championship, which no WWE-contracted wrestler had won before him. 2×NXT Championship reigns, 2024–25: 75 days, then 98The crowd built the character with him. Booker T started ad-libbing “Whoop that Trick” on commentary, a line from Al Kapone's song in the 2005 film Hustle & Flow. Fans picked it up and never stopped. Williams says it was organic, and it followed him to the main roster.

    Before wrestling he was a football player. He caught four passes as a wide receiver at Hampton University in 2012, then walked on at the University of South Carolina, where he caught 11 passes for 121 yards in 2015 and was named the team's Spring Outstanding Walk-on on offense. He went undrafted in 2017, tried out at a Philadelphia Eagles rookie minicamp in May 2018, took a WWE tryout that December, and trained at CZW in Philadelphia and KnokX in Los Angeles. WWE signed him on February 24, 2021. 140days as TNA World Champion, May 25 to October 12, 2025The TNA reign is the strangest line on the resume. He beat Joe Hendry for the TNA World Championship at NXT Battleground on May 25, 2025, defended it on NXT and on TNA shows including Slammiversary, and lost it to Mike Santana in the Bound for Glory main event on October 12, 2025, after 140 days.

    Setting one thing straight

    One record needs its counting rule. His WWE.com profile lists two NXT Championships, and fans often call him a three-time world champion. WWE does not class the NXT Championship as a world title, so this dossier counts one world title: TNA. A second correction concerns the night he and NXT Champion Oba Femi met in a Winner Take All match on September 23, 2025. It is sometimes listed as if it had a winner. It ended in a no contest after Williams slapped Mike Santana at ringside, and neither title moved.

    The main-roster run started with a walkout. He declared himself a free agent on SmackDown on December 26, 2025, signed with the brand a week later, and beat Rey Fenix in his debut on January 9, 2026. He entered the Royal Rumble at No. 10, reached the Elimination Chamber, and in April teamed up with rapper Lil Yachty and turned face. WM42his WrestleMania debut, April 19, 2026: beat Sami Zayn for the US titleAt WrestleMania 42 in Las Vegas on April 19, 2026, in his first WrestleMania match, he beat Sami Zayn for the United States Championship by countering the Helluva Kick with the Trick Shot. Baron Corbin took the title at SummerSlam on August 2 after 105 days. Williams won it back on September 6 at Sunday Night's Main Event. On October 30 he challenged Zayn, now the Undisputed WWE Champion, and lost after a low blow and a belt shot. As of November 9, 2026 he is still the US Champion.

    04

    Before WWE

    A wide receiver first. The wrestling started at 25.

    Hampton · 2012–13

    Hampton Pirates

    Wide receiver, scholarship player

    Six games and one start as a freshman in 2012, with four catches for 33 yards, including a 20-yard catch at Florida A&M. He was on the 2013 roster but did not play; Wikipedia attributes the lost season to a pulled hamstring.

    South Carolina · 2014–16

    South Carolina Gamecocks

    Walk-on wide receiver, No. 15

    Walked on in his hometown in spring 2014 and sat out the fall under transfer rules. In 2015 he played every game, started five, caught 11 passes for 121 yards and won the team's Spring Outstanding Walk-on award on offense. In 2016 he played nine games, mostly on special teams, and made the SEC Fall Academic Honor Roll. Graduated in December 2016.

    2017–18

    The NFL door

    Undrafted; Philadelphia Eagles tryout

    Undrafted in 2017. He attended a Philadelphia Eagles rookie minicamp on a tryout basis in May 2018. That December he took a WWE tryout instead.

    2019–21

    CZW and KnokX

    Training in Philadelphia, then Los Angeles

    Trained with CZW in Philadelphia, where his first recorded match was a loss to D. J. Hyde on January 31, 2020, then moved to KnokX in Los Angeles. He built a Dolemite-inspired character, Sweet Daddy Trick, named after his uncle's nickname. WWE signed him on February 24, 2021.

    05

    Personas

    One name the whole way. Four different jobs inside it.

    TMNXT · 2021–23

    The hype man

    Carmelo Hayes's friend and second in the Trick Melo Gang, introduced by attacking Duke Hudson on September 14, 2021. A heel act until Bron Breakker attacked them on April 4, 2023 and the pair turned face.

    WTNXT · 2023–25

    Whoop That Trick

    The solo run. An amicable split on August 1, 2023, the North American title, the Iron Survivor win, Hayes's betrayal at Vengeance Day 2024, and two NXT Championship reigns, with the crowd chanting Booker T's ad-lib at every entrance.

    TCNXT and TNA · 2025

    The crossover heel

    Insulted and attacked Joe Hendry at TNA Rebellion on April 27, 2025, took his TNA World Championship a month later, and defended it across both promotions with AJ Francis and KC Navarro in his corner.

    TYSmackDown · 2026–present

    The champion with a rapper

    Free agent in December 2025, SmackDown in January 2026, and a face turn in April alongside Lil Yachty as the Trick-Yachty Gang. He ended the weekly US title open challenges because he wants that belt treated as the top prize on the show.

    Career Timeline

    Walk-on, tryout, hype man, champion of three promotions' belts.

    2015

    South Carolina

    Plays every game for the Gamecocks as a walk-on wide receiver: 11 catches, 121 yards, five starts.

    2018

    2020

    First recorded match

    Loses to D. J. Hyde at CZW in Philadelphia on January 31.

    2021

    Signed, then on TV

    Signs with WWE on February 24 and debuts on NXT on September 14 as Carmelo Hayes's friend. The Trick Melo Gang begins.

    2023

    Out on his own

    Turns face with Hayes in April, splits amicably on August 1, wins the North American title from Dominik Mysterio at No Mercy on September 30 and loses it three days later, then wins the Iron Survivor Challenge on December 9.

    2024

    Betrayed, then champion twice

    Hayes hits him with a chair after the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic final on February 4. Williams beats him at Stand & Deliver on April 6 and in a cage on April 16, wins the NXT Championship from Ilja Dragunov on April 23, loses it to Ethan Page on July 7, and wins it back on October 1.

    2025

    The TNA World Champion

    Loses the NXT title to Oba Femi on January 7. Wins the TNA World Championship from Joe Hendry on May 25 and holds it 140 days until Mike Santana beats him at Bound for Glory on October 12. Declares himself a free agent on SmackDown on December 26.

    2026

    SmackDown and the US title

    Debuts with a win over Rey Fenix on January 9, beats Sami Zayn for the US title at WrestleMania 42 on April 19, marries Lash Legend on June 20, loses the belt to Baron Corbin at SummerSlam on August 2 and regains it on September 6. Falls short in the Money in the Bank ladder match on October 10 and against Zayn for the Undisputed WWE Championship on October 30.

    07

    Rivalries

    Who he went through.

    Carmelo Hayes

    Best friend, partner, then the man who hit him with a chair at Vengeance Day on February 4, 2024 and admitted two days later that he had also ordered the October 2023 backstage attack. Williams won the Stand & Deliver singles match on April 6 and the cage match ten days later. They crossed again on SmackDown in an Elimination Chamber qualifier in February 2026, which Williams won.

    Oba Femi

    The wall at the top of NXT. Femi took the NXT Championship from him in a triple threat on January 7, 2025, beat him again at Stand & Deliver that April, and met him champion against champion in a Winner Take All match on September 23, 2025 that ended in a no contest.

    Ethan Page

    The NXT title trade of 2024: Williams beat him at Battleground, lost the belt to him in a Heatwave four-way, took it back on the CW premiere with CM Punk refereeing, and retained in a Devil's Playground match at Halloween Havoc.

    Joe Hendry & Mike Santana

    The TNA chapter. Williams invaded TNA to attack Hendry, took his world title at NXT Battleground, and beat both men in the Slammiversary four-way. Santana kept coming, cost him an NXT No. 1 contender match in August, and ended the reign at Bound for Glory.

    Sami Zayn

    Williams took the US title from him at WrestleMania 42 and beat him again at Backlash. By October Zayn was the Undisputed WWE Champion, and he kept that title against Williams on October 30 with a low blow and a belt shot.

    Baron Corbin

    Back in WWE in July 2026, Corbin took the US title at SummerSlam with the belt and End of Days. Williams won it back from him at Sunday Night's Main Event five weeks later.

    Frequently Asked

    Is Trick Williams the current United States Champion?

    Yes, as of November 9, 2026. He won the title back from Baron Corbin at Sunday Night's Main Event on September 6, 2026, his second reign. His first ran 105 days, from WrestleMania 42 on April 19 to SummerSlam on August 2.

    How many times has Trick Williams been NXT Champion?

    Twice. He beat Ilja Dragunov on April 23, 2024 and lost to Ethan Page on July 7 (75 days). He beat Page on October 1, 2024 and lost to Oba Femi on January 7, 2025 (98 days).

    Was Trick Williams TNA World Champion?

    Yes. He beat Joe Hendry for the TNA World Championship at NXT Battleground on May 25, 2025, the first WWE-contracted wrestler to win a TNA title. He lost it to Mike Santana at Bound for Glory on October 12, 2025, after 140 days.

    Why did Trick Williams and Carmelo Hayes split up?

    Twice over. Williams ended the Trick Melo Gang amicably on August 1, 2023 to chase his own titles. They reunited for the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, and after losing the final at Vengeance Day on February 4, 2024, Hayes hit him with a chair. Two days later Hayes admitted he was behind the October 2023 backstage attack too. Williams beat him at Stand & Deliver on April 6, 2024.

    Did Trick Williams play college football?

    Yes, as a wide receiver. He played at Hampton in 2012, then walked on at South Carolina, where he caught 11 passes for 121 yards in 2015. He went undrafted in 2017 and had a Philadelphia Eagles minicamp tryout in May 2018.

    Where does the "Whoop That Trick" chant come from?

    From Al Kapone's song in the 2005 film Hustle & Flow, by way of Booker T, who started ad-libbing the line on NXT commentary. Fans took it from there. His actual entrance theme is an instrumental; the words come from the crowd.

    What happened in the Winner Take All match with Oba Femi?

    Nothing changed hands. On September 23, 2025, NXT Champion Oba Femi faced TNA World Champion Williams on NXT with both belts at stake. It ended in a no contest after Williams slapped Mike Santana at ringside and the TNA and NXT rosters brawled.

    When did Trick Williams join the main roster?

    He declared himself a free agent on SmackDown on December 26, 2025, announced on January 2, 2026 that he had signed with SmackDown, and beat Rey Fenix in his debut match on January 9, 2026.

    Who is Trick Williams married to?

    WWE wrestler Lash Legend. They married on June 20, 2026 in Port Antonio, Jamaica.

    Is Trick Williams a world champion?

    Once, by the strict count. The TNA World Championship is a world title. His two NXT Championships are not, because WWE does not class the NXT title as a world championship. He challenged Sami Zayn for the Undisputed WWE Championship on October 30, 2026 and lost.

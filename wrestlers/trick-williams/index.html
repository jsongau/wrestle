    Trick Williams

    United States Champion · NXT and TNA crossover

    Trick Williams is the WWE United States Champion, winning the title at WrestleMania 42 in April 2026, and a former TNA World Champion from his NXT run.

    Biography

    Trick Williams turned an NXT run into a main-roster title. At WrestleMania 42 on April 19, 2026 he won the WWE United States Championship, a title with roots in NWA Mid-Atlantic and WCW that WWE revived in 2003.

    Before that, Williams held the TNA World Championship while still on the NXT roster, one of the more unusual title crossovers between the two promotions. That reign has since ended, with the TNA World Championship moving on to a new holder in mid-2026.

    One of the defining matches of his rise came against Oba Femi in a Winner Take All match on September 23, 2025, part of the rivalry that shaped the top of NXT heading into 2026.

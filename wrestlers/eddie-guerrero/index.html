Eddie Guerrero

Eddie Guerrero — "Latino Heat" — was a charismatic technical marvel and lucha-influenced high-flyer who overcame personal demons to win the WWE Championship in 2004. His "Lie, Cheat and Steal" persona and Frog Splash made him one of the most beloved performers of his generation before his sudden death in 2005 devastated the industry.

  • Real name Eduardo Gory Guerrero Llanes
  • From El Paso, Texas (Mexican-American)
  • Debut / era 1987 · ECW/WCW 1995–2000 · WWE 2000–2005
  • Promotions ECW, WCW, WWF/WWE, AAA/CMLL
  • Nicknames Latino Heat, "Lie, Cheat and Steal"
  • Finishers Frog Splash, Lasso from El Paso

Career

A member of the legendary Guerrero wrestling dynasty, Eddie honed a lucha-influenced, technically brilliant style across Mexico, ECW and WCW — including his classic WCW Cruiserweight rivalry with Rey Mysterio at Halloween Havoc 1997, widely called the greatest cruiserweight match in American history.

In WWE his roguish "Lie, Cheat and Steal" charisma turned him into a top star. He captured the WWE Championship from Brock Lesnar at No Way Out 2004 — an emotional peak of the era — and later feuded with best friend Rey Mysterio in a 2005 custody storyline and matched up with Kurt Angle. His sudden passing in November 2005 remains one of wrestling's most mourned losses.

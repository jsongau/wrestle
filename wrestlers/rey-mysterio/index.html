Rey Mysterio

Rey Mysterio (Óscar Gutiérrez) is the most influential luchador in American wrestling — a World and WWE Champion who brought high-flying lucha libre to the mainstream. "The Master of the 619" became an unlikely world champion in 2006, dedicating his run to the late Eddie Guerrero, and his masked underdog persona has spanned four decades.

  • Real name Óscar Gutiérrez Rubio
  • From San Diego, California (Mexican-American)
  • Debut / era 1989 · ECW/WCW 1995–2001 · WWE 2002–present
  • Promotions AAA, ECW, WCW, WWF/WWE
  • Nicknames The Master of the 619, The Biggest Little Man, The Ultimate Underdog
  • Finishers 619, West Coast Pop / Frog Splash (tribute)

Career

Trained in Mexican lucha libre, Rey Mysterio broke through in WCW's cruiserweight division, where his 1997 classic against Eddie Guerrero at Halloween Havoc proved US audiences would embrace lucha-style high flying — legitimizing the whole division and making him a star.

In WWE his 619 and daredevil aerial offense turned the smaller man into a genuine main-eventer. He won the 2006 Royal Rumble and the World Heavyweight Championship at WrestleMania 22, dedicating the run to his late best friend Eddie Guerrero, then added the WWE Championship in 2011. His rivalries include a 2009 mask feud with Chris Jericho and a real-life storyline against his own son Dominik.

FAQ

What is Rey Mysterio's real name?
Óscar Gutiérrez Rubio, known as "The Master of the 619."
What is the 619?
His signature swinging kick through the ropes, named after the area code of his native San Diego.
When did he win the World title?
At WrestleMania 22 in 2006, dedicating the run to the late Eddie Guerrero. He won the WWE Championship in 2011.

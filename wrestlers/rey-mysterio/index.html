    Biography

    Rey Mysterio is the most important small man in the history of sports entertainment. At 5'6" and 175 pounds, he performed in a world built for men twice his size — and he not only competed, he elevated the art form. His influence on the lucha libre-meets-WWE style can be heard in how any aerial move is called today.

    Mysterio's WCW run in the mid-1990s (alongside Eddie Guerrero and Chris Jericho) produced some of the best cruiserweight matches in American television history. When WWE acquired WCW in 2001, Mysterio came with it, and his 2002 SmackDown debut immediately raised the in-ring quality of the entire brand.

    His 2006 Royal Rumble win — the shortest time-in-ring winner in Royal Rumble history before that moment — and subsequent WrestleMania 22 world title win (in an exhausted, emotional tribute to Eddie Guerrero) is one of wrestling's most powerful storyline payoffs. The crowd in Chicago had been building to that moment for months.

    Mysterio's career is marked by spectacular matches against opponents who outweigh him by 100+ pounds: Undertaker, Batista, Edge, JBL, Randy Orton, and Brock Lesnar. He makes every opponent look physically dominant while remaining impossible to root against. That's a technical achievement requiring extraordinary skill.

    Finishing Moves

    • 619 — Running leg scissors to the ropes — a move that shouldn't work but does, every single time, because of his positioning and timing.
    • West Coast Pop (Frog Splash variant) — Springboard seated senton or frog splash from the top — the finish after the 619 sets up the pin.
    • Hurricanrana — His signature throughout his career — used as a finish, a transition, and a counter. Flawlessly executed at every speed.

    Career Timeline

    1. 1989

      Pro debut at age 14

      Begins wrestling in Mexico under the tutelage of his uncle Rey Mysterio Sr. — one of wrestling's earliest prodigies.

    2. 1996

      WCW debut

      Arrives in WCW and immediately matches with Eddie Guerrero — the two produce career-defining cruiserweight classics.

    3. 1999

      WCW unmasks him

      Creative forces Mysterio to unmask — a storyline decision that dampened his character's mystique.

    4. 2002

      WWE SmackDown debut

      Debuts on SmackDown with the mask — immediately elevates the brand's in-ring quality.

    5. 2005

      Eddie Guerrero's death

      Close friend and storyline partner Eddie Guerrero dies in November — Mysterio dedicates his 2006 season to Eddie's memory.

    6. 2006

      Royal Rumble win & WM22 World title

      Wins the Royal Rumble (entering #2) and defeats Randy Orton and Kurt Angle in a triple-threat at WM22 — the Eddie tribute payoff.

    7. 2010

      Second World title reign

      Briefly holds the World Heavyweight title — a reign interrupted by an injured Batista cash-in.

    8. 2019

      Feud with Samoa Joe, IC title run

      Four-time Intercontinental Champion — continues performing at high quality into his mid-40s.

    9. 2023

      WWE Hall of Fame

      Inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame — one of the most universally beloved inductions in recent memory.

    FAQ

    Has Rey Mysterio won the WWE Championship?

    Yes — Rey Mysterio won the WWE Championship once in 2011, defeating The Miz.

    Is Rey Mysterio in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Yes — Mysterio was inducted in the WWE Hall of Fame Class of 2023.

    What is Rey Mysterio's finishing move?

    Rey Mysterio's signature finishing sequence is the 619 — a running leg scissors off the ropes — followed by the West Coast Pop (springboard seated senton) or a frog splash for the pin.

    Signature Matches

    ★★★★★

    Rey Mysterio vs. Eddie Guerrero

    WCW Halloween Havoc — Oct 26, 1997

    The best cruiserweight match in WCW history — Guerrero and Mysterio work a match that would pass muster in any era. The hurricanrana counter and the structure of the near-falls is textbook.

    ★★★★

    Rey Mysterio vs. Kurt Angle vs. Randy Orton

    WrestleMania 22 — Apr 2, 2006

    The Eddie tribute payoff — Mysterio wins the World Heavyweight Championship. Chicago erupts. A triple-threat that tells a complete story with Mysterio as the emotional center.

    ★★★★

    Rey Mysterio vs. Samoa Joe

    Backlash — May 5, 2019

    Late-career masterpiece — Mysterio and Joe produce one of 2019's best matches; Joe wins but both men are elevated.

Kurt Angle

Kurt Angle is the only man to win an Olympic gold medal and become a world professional wrestling champion — and he did both at the highest possible level. A 1996 Olympic freestyle wrestling champion who debuted in WWF in 1999, he became a six-time WWE/WWF Champion and produced WrestleMania's finest technical matches against Brock Lesnar and Shawn Michaels. It's true. It's damn true.

How they win

Angle Slam and the Ankle Lock — a two-move arsenal of destruction

Angle finishes matches with an Angle Slam (release overhead belly-to-back slam) or the Ankle Lock — a wrenching submission that he won't release even when opponents roll through. His amateur wrestling credentials make every hold look legitimate.

Olympic gold

1996 Atlanta Games — freestyle wrestling — completed the tournament with a broken neck. The only Olympic gold medalist to headline WrestleMania.

WM 21

The Angle vs HBK match at WrestleMania 21 is routinely cited as a top-five WrestleMania match of all time by historians and former wrestlers alike.

King of the Ring 2000

Won the 2000 KOTR tournament — used the Olympic Hero gimmick with milk, confetti and unironically patriotic promos.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1996

    Olympic gold — 'with a broken freaking neck'

    Won the freestyle wrestling gold at the 1996 Atlanta Games despite a broken neck suffered during the trials. The injury became wrestling legend when turned into kayfabe.

  2. 1999–2001

    WWF debut — from rookie to champion

    Debuted at Survivor Series 1999; won the WWF King of the Ring in 2000; rapidly became a multiple-time world champion during the peak Attitude Era.

  3. 2001–2004

    WWE Championship reigns & WrestleMania

    Title reign opposite Steve Austin; the Brock Lesnar WrestleMania XIX classic; consistent SmackDown main-eventer.

  4. 2005

    The WrestleMania 21 masterpiece

    vs Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania 21 — routinely rated the finest technical WrestleMania match ever contested.

  5. 2006–2016

    TNA — rebuilding on a different stage

    Left WWE for TNA/Impact Wrestling; four world title reigns and a sustained main-event run that proved his longevity.

  6. 2017–2019

    WWE return & Hall of Fame

    Returned to WWE for a final run; inducted into the Hall of Fame in 2017 — still technically active in occasional appearances.

FAQ

Did Kurt Angle really win an Olympic gold medal?
Yes — 1996 Atlanta Games, freestyle wrestling, with a broken neck. It's true. It's damn true.
What is the Ankle Lock?
A submission hold where Angle grabs an opponent's ankle, sits down, and twists and cranks. He also does a grapevine version (wrapping his legs around the trapped leg) that makes escaping nearly impossible.
How many world titles did Kurt Angle win?
Six WWF/WWE Championships plus four TNA World Heavyweight Championships — 10 world title reigns across his career.

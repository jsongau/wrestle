Kurt Angle

Kurt Angle is a genuine Olympic gold medalist who became a four-time WWE Champion and six-time TNA World Champion. Blending amateur credibility with comedic charisma, "The Olympic Hero" transitioned to pro wrestling with astonishing speed, and his technical classics with Brock Lesnar, Shawn Michaels and AJ Styles rank among the finest of the era.

  • Finishers Angle Slam, Ankle Lock

Career

A genuine 1996 Olympic freestyle wrestling gold medalist, Angle made his pro debut in 1998 and rose with astonishing speed, winning the WWE Championship within his first full year. His blend of amateur pedigree and cocky comedic charisma made him one of the most complete performers of the Ruthless Aggression era.

His technical showcases are legendary: a WWE Championship classic with Brock Lesnar at WrestleMania XIX, a pure clinic with Chris Benoit at Royal Rumble 2003, the dream match with Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania 21, and an underrated gem against The Undertaker at No Way Out 2006.

He later anchored TNA's main event alongside AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Sting, winning six TNA World titles before a 2017 WWE return and Hall of Fame induction.

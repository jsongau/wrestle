Daniel Bryan

Daniel Bryan (Bryan Danielson) is a multiple-time world champion and the grassroots hero whose "Yes Movement" forced WWE to make him the WrestleMania XXX main-event champion in 2014. A pure in-ring technician beloved by hardcore fans, he blended independent credibility with mainstream stardom across ROH, WWE and AEW.

  • Real name Bryan Lloyd Danielson
  • Born May 22, 1981 · Aberdeen, WA
  • Debut 1999 (WWE 2010)
  • Promotions ROH, WWE, AEW, NJPW
  • Nicknames The American Dragon, The Yes! Man
  • Finishers Running Knee, "Yes!" Lock

Career

Trained in part at the San Antonio school connected to Shawn Michaels, Bryan Danielson became "The American Dragon," a technical marvel and ROH World Champion revered on the independents. He arrived in WWE in 2010 and, despite management's doubts, became a phenomenon through sheer crowd connection and his "Yes!" chant.

The fan-driven "Yes Movement" peaked at WrestleMania XXX (2014), where he beat Triple H and then Randy Orton and Batista in the main event to win the world title. Along the way he formed the comedy tag team "Team Hell No" with Kane and delivered a SummerSlam 2013 classic with John Cena.

After neck and concussion issues threatened his career, Bryan returned to the ring and later signed with AEW as Bryan Danielson, adding a run of acclaimed technical classics to one of the most beloved careers of his generation.

FAQ

What is Daniel Bryan's real name?
Bryan Lloyd Danielson, born May 22, 1981, in Aberdeen, Washington. He now wrestles in AEW under his real name.
What was the Yes Movement?
The grassroots fan campaign that forced WWE to make Bryan the WrestleMania XXX main-event world champion in 2014.
Did Daniel Bryan win at WrestleMania XXX?
Yes — he beat Triple H, then won a triple threat over Randy Orton and Batista to take the WWE World Heavyweight Championship.

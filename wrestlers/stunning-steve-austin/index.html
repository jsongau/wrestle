    “Stunning” Steve Austin

    "Stunning" Steve Austin was the WCW identity of the future Stone Cold — a blond, arrogant technician who became one of WCW's best in-ring workers. He held the World Television and United States titles twice each, won the World Tag Team titles with Brian Pillman as The Hollywood Blonds, and had a career-making feud with Ricky Steamboat — before Eric Bischoff fired him via FedEx in 1995.

    • Real name Steven James Williams

    WCW's best-kept secret

    Weeks into his WCW run in 1991, "Stunning" Steve Austin beat Bobby Eaton for the World Television Championship. Over the next four years he became one of the company's most reliable in-ring hands: a two-time TV champion, a two-time United States champion (first won from Dustin Rhodes at Starrcade '93), and one half of The Hollywood Blonds with Brian Pillman — a cocky, hugely entertaining tag team that won the World Tag Team titles and feuded with Ric Flair and Arn Anderson.

    His feud with Ricky Steamboat produced a run of excellent matches and remains the in-ring highlight of the era. But a triceps tear on a 1995 tour changed everything: while he rehabbed, Eric Bischoff fired him via FedEx, calling him not marketable. It was the spark. Austin reinvented himself in ECW and then WWF — and became the biggest star the business had ever seen.

    FAQ

    Who was Stunning Steve Austin?
    The WCW ring name (1991–1995) of Steve Austin. A blond technical heel who won the WCW TV and US titles and the World Tag Team titles with Brian Pillman as The Hollywood Blonds.
    Why did he leave WCW?
    After a 1995 triceps injury, Eric Bischoff fired him — famously via FedEx — deeming him not marketable. He reinvented himself in ECW, then WWF as Stone Cold.

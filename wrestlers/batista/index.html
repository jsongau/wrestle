Batista

Batista (Dave Bautista), "The Animal," is a six-time world champion who broke out of the Evolution faction to become one of WWE's biggest draws of the late 2000s. His 2005 betrayal of Triple H launched a main-event run built on raw power and the Batista Bomb, before he transitioned into a major Hollywood career.

  • Real name David Michael Bautista Jr.
  • Born January 18, 1969 · Washington, D.C.
  • Debut 2000 (main event 2005)
  • Promotions WWE
  • Nicknames The Animal, The Leviathan
  • Finishers Batista Bomb, Spinebuster

Career

Batista rose to prominence as the powerhouse enforcer of Evolution alongside Triple H, Ric Flair and Randy Orton. After winning the 2005 Royal Rumble, he turned on Triple H and defeated him for the World Heavyweight Championship at WrestleMania 21 — one of the most successful babyface breakouts of the era.

For the rest of the decade "The Animal" was a fixture at the top of the card, trading the world title with the likes of The Undertaker, John Cena and Rey Mysterio. His hard-hitting, explosive style made him a dependable main-event attraction across multiple brands.

Bautista left to pursue acting and became a genuine Hollywood star, most famously as Drax in the Guardians of the Galaxy films. He returned for select runs — winning the 2014 Royal Rumble and facing Daniel Bryan — before a final program against Triple H in a retirement match at WrestleMania 35 (2019).

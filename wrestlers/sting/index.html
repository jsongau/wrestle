Sting

Sting (Steve Borden) is a six-time WCW World Champion and "The Icon" — the loyal franchise of WCW who never jumped to WWF during the Monday Night Wars. He reinvented himself as a silent, face-painted "Crow" vigilante for his epic feud with the nWo and Hollywood Hogan, then wrestled into his 60s before retiring undefeated in AEW in 2024.

  • Real name Steve Borden
  • From California, USA

  • Nicknames The Icon, The Stinger, The Vigilante
  • Finishers Scorpion Death Lock, Scorpion Death Drop

Career

Sting broke out in 1988 with a star-making draw against Ric Flair at the first Clash of the Champions, launching a career-long rivalry. As the loyal "franchise of WCW," he became a multiple-time World Champion and the promotion's most beloved hero.

His masterpiece was the 18-month "Crow" storyline: transforming into a silent, rafter-dwelling vigilante stalking the nWo, culminating in the most anticipated match in company history against Hollywood Hogan at Starrcade 1997. He was part of the roster betrayed at the nWo's formation in 1996.

He later anchored TNA's main event, had a WWE run, and closed his career in AEW — mentoring Darby Allin and going out on a win at Revolution 2024.

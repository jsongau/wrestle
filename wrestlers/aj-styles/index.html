    01

    Overview

    Trained by Rick Michaels and breaking in on the Georgia independents in 1998, AJ Styles became the original franchise player of NWA-TNA, defining its high-flying X-Division before graduating to the main event as the company’s first-ever Triple Crown and Grand Slam Champion. He is, by acclaim, one of the greatest in-ring performers of his generation.

    A 2014 move to New Japan reinvented him as the cocky figurehead of the Bullet Club and a two-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion, cementing his reputation as the best wrestler outside WWE. That reputation drove a phenomenal 2016 WWE debut: a two-time WWE Champion whose second reign ran 371 days, he headlined WrestleMania and proved an independent star with no developmental pedigree could rule the main event, before bowing out against Gunther at the 2026 Royal Rumble.

    Frequently Asked

    What is AJ Styles’ real name?

    Allen Neal Jones, born June 2, 1977. He is billed from Gainesville, Georgia.

    How many world titles has AJ Styles won?

    Nine world-title reigns: 3 NWA World Heavyweight, 2 TNA World Heavyweight, 2 IWGP Heavyweight and 2 WWE Championship reigns.

    Is AJ Styles a Grand Slam Champion?

    Yes, he was the first-ever TNA Triple Crown and Grand Slam champion, and is also a WWE Grand Slam champion.

    How long was his second WWE title reign?

    371 days, from beating Jinder Mahal in Manchester on November 7, 2017 to losing to Daniel Bryan on November 13, 2018, one of the longest of the modern era.

    Did AJ Styles lead the Bullet Club?

    He was the on-screen figurehead of Bullet Club in NJPW from 2014 to 2016, though he has said he never considered himself its literal leader.

    Is AJ Styles retired?

    Yes. He lost a career match to Gunther at the Royal Rumble on January 31, 2026 and enters the WWE Hall of Fame with the Class of 2026.

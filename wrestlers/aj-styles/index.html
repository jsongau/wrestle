AJ Styles

AJ Styles, "The Phenomenal One," is a multiple-time world champion in TNA, NJPW and WWE and one of the best in-ring performers of his generation. He built his legend as the "Face That Runs the Place" in TNA and a Bullet Club leader in Japan before a headline 2016 WWE arrival.

  • Real name Allen Neal Jones
  • Born June 2, 1977 · Jacksonville, NC
  • Debut 1998 (WWE 2016)
  • Promotions TNA, NJPW, WWE, ROH
  • Nicknames The Phenomenal One
  • Finishers Styles Clash, Phenomenal Forearm, Calf Crusher

Career

Styles became the cornerstone of TNA, dominating its X-Division and world title picture across the 2000s. His defining program was the "trifecta" with Samoa Joe and Christopher Daniels, capped by an acclaimed three-way at Unbreakable 2005, and he also waged classics with Kurt Angle.

After leaving TNA he reinvented himself in New Japan Pro-Wrestling, winning the IWGP Heavyweight Championship and leading the Bullet Club faction founded by Finn Balor (as Prince Devitt). His global reputation made his 2016 WWE debut a genuine event.

In WWE, Styles won the WWE Championship twice and delivered acclaimed matches with John Cena and a WrestleMania 34 showcase against Shinsuke Nakamura, cementing his status as a true world traveler and modern great.

FAQ

What is AJ Styles' real name?
Allen Neal Jones, born June 2, 1977 — nicknamed "The Phenomenal One."
What was the TNA trifecta?
The era-defining rivalry among AJ Styles, Samoa Joe and Christopher Daniels, headlined by their three-way at TNA Unbreakable 2005.
Did AJ Styles lead Bullet Club?
Yes — in NJPW he led the Bullet Club faction founded by Finn Balor (Prince Devitt) before joining WWE in 2016.

    Jey Uso

    Main-event babyface · The Usos · Bloodline

    Jey Uso is a Raw main-event babyface, a former World Heavyweight Champion, and one half of The Usos with his twin brother Jimmy Uso in the Bloodline.

    Biography

    Jey Uso spent years as one half of The Usos, the tag team he forms with his twin brother Jimmy Uso. Together they became multi-time tag champions, including a 622-day SmackDown Tag Team Championship reign that ran from 2021 to 2023, one of the longest tag reigns of the era.

    As part of Roman Reigns' Bloodline, Jey was central to the longest modern WWE storyline. He is a cousin of Reigns, a member of the Anoa'i family, and a brother of Solo Sikoa. His singles run broke him out of the group and made him a top babyface, and he went on to become a World Heavyweight Champion.

    He returned to WWE in mid-2026 and moved back into the main-event picture on Raw, chasing the Undisputed WWE Championship held by CM Punk after the reign of Sami Zayn. Zayn was himself an honorary Bloodline member during the group's 2022 to 2023 run.

    FAQ

    Is Jey Uso a former world champion?

    Yes. Jey Uso is a former World Heavyweight Champion and, with his twin brother Jimmy Uso as The Usos, a multi-time tag team champion, including a 622-day SmackDown Tag Team Championship reign from 2021 to 2023.

    How is Jey Uso related to Roman Reigns?

    Jey Uso is a cousin of Roman Reigns and a member of the Anoa'i wrestling family. He and Jimmy Uso form The Usos, and Solo Sikoa is his brother.

    What is Jey Uso doing in 2026?

    He returned to WWE as a top Raw babyface and has been chasing the Undisputed WWE Championship, a title held by CM Punk after Sami Zayn's reign.

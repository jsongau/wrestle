Charlotte Flair

Charlotte Flair (Ashley Fliehr) is "The Queen" — a record 14-time women's world champion and the daughter of Ric Flair. She translated a legendary wrestling pedigree into a run of headline "firsts" that defined WWE's modern women's era alongside the other Four Horsewomen.

  • Real name Ashley Elizabeth Fliehr
  • From Charlotte, North Carolina, USA
  • Debut 2012 (WWE 2013)
  • Promotions WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames The Queen, The Nature Girl
  • Finishers Figure-Eight Leglock, Natural Selection

Career

The daughter of 16-time world champion Ric Flair, Charlotte carried a wrestling bloodline into a record 14 women's world titles — more than anyone in WWE women's history. A founding Four Horsewoman with Becky Lynch, Sasha Banks and Bayley, she was central to a string of division milestones.

She contested the first women's Hell in a Cell against Sasha Banks in 2016 and helped headline WrestleMania 35 in 2019 — the first women's WrestleMania main event. Her career-long rivalry with Becky Lynch and her firsts with Sasha Banks shaped the era. She uses the Figure-Eight leglock and robes as a tribute to her father.

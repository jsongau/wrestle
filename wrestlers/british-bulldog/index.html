    01

    Overview

    Davey Boy Smith headlined the biggest crowd the WWF had drawn outside North America, and he did it at home. On August 29, 1992 at Wembley Stadium he beat his brother-in-law Bret Hart for the Intercontinental Championship, with his wife Diana, Bret's sister, at ringside. 78,927the cited Wembley attendance for SummerSlam 1992. The WWF claimed 80,355The rest of the record fits around that night. He won the WWF Tag Team Championship with his cousin Dynamite Kid at WrestleMania 2. He won it again with Owen Hart in 1996. He became the first WWF European Champion in Berlin in 1997 and held that belt for 206 days, the longest reign it ever had. WWE inducted him into the Hall of Fame as part of the Class of 2020.

    Setting one thing straight

    The old version of this page got several titles wrong, and the errors are common elsewhere too. He held the Intercontinental Championship once, not twice. He held the European Championship twice, not three times. He never held a WCW title of any kind, with Sting or anyone else. The Allied Powers team with Lex Luger lost its only tag title shot, at In Your House 2 in 1995. The Wembley finish was not a powerslam: Hart went for a sunset flip and Smith sat down on it for the pin. One more number needs care. The WWF announced 80,355 for that show, and the cited figure is 78,927. This page uses the cited figure and flags the claim.

    He was born David Smith on November 27, 1962 in Golborne, Lancashire. He was trained by Ted Betley and was on British television as Young David by 1978, at about 15. In 1981 he left for Calgary and Stu Hart's Stampede Wrestling. 294days as WWF Tag Team Champions with Dynamite Kid, from WrestleMania 2 to January 1987There he and Tom Billington, the Dynamite Kid, became The British Bulldogs. The team worked Stampede, New Japan and All Japan before the WWF signed them in 1984. They won the tag titles from Greg Valentine and Brutus Beefcake on April 7, 1986, with Ozzy Osbourne in their corner. Dynamite Kid hurt his back badly that December, and the belts went to Bret Hart and Jim Neidhart, the original Hart Foundation team, on January 26, 1987. He married Diana Hart in October 1984. That made him family to the men he would spend the next decade facing and joining.

    The singles career ran in pieces. He returned to the WWF in 1990, beat The Warlord at WrestleMania VII, and was released after losing the Intercontinental title to Shawn Michaels in late 1992. WCW had him for most of 1993. Back in the WWF from 1994, he reached the final two of the 1995 Royal Rumble and turned on Diesel that August. He then challenged for the WWF Championship against Diesel, Bret Hart and Michaels. 206days as the first WWF European Champion, the longest reign in the title’s historyIn 1997 he joined Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Jim Neidhart and Brian Pillman in the reunited Hart family stable. The group was cheered in Canada and Europe and booed in the United States. He left for WCW after the Montreal Survivor Series. A back injury on a ring trapdoor at Fall Brawl in September 1998 led to a spinal infection and about six months in hospital. He came back to the WWF in 1999, won the European and Hardcore titles, and left in May 2000.

    Davey Boy Smith died of a heart attack on May 18, 2002, while on vacation in British Columbia. He was 39. A week earlier he had teamed with his son Harry in a six-man match in Winnipeg. WWE announced his Hall of Fame induction on March 12, 2020. The pandemic delayed the ceremony, and Harry, his daughter Georgia and Diana Hart-Smith inducted him on the broadcast of April 6, 2021. As of November 9, 2026, Harry still wrestles under the family name as Davey Boy Smith Jr. In February 2026 he got back his father's Hall of Fame ring after it was stolen from a ring truck in Canada.

    Factions

    Nearly every team he joined was family by blood or by marriage.

    Stampede · Japan · WWF · 1983–1989

    The British Bulldogs

    Davey Boy Smith, Dynamite Kid (Tom Billington)

    Two cousins from the Wigan area who met again in Calgary. Their power and speed won the Wrestling Observer's Tag Team of the Year in 1985 and the WWF titles at WrestleMania 2, with Matilda the bulldog as the team mascot. They left the WWF after the 1988 Survivor Series and split in Stampede in May 1989. The relationship soured badly afterward, including a fight over who could use the British Bulldog name.

    WWF · 1995

    The Allied Powers

    Lex Luger, British Bulldog

    A short babyface pairing built on an American and a Briton. It won at WrestleMania XI and lost its one tag title match at In Your House 2. It ended in August 1995 when Smith attacked Diesel and went over to Jim Cornette.

    WWF · 1995–1997

    Camp Cornette / Owen Hart &amp; British Bulldog

    Owen Hart, British Bulldog, with Yokozuna and Vader in the wider camp

    The in-laws as a heel tag team. They debuted together at WrestleMania XII, took the tag titles from the Smoking Gunns in September 1996 and held them 246 days. The partnership cracked when Smith beat Owen for the European title in February 1997, and it was repaired on Raw that March.

    WWF · 1997

    The 1997 Hart family stable

    Bret Hart, Owen Hart, British Bulldog, Jim Neidhart, Brian Pillman

    The Hart Foundation began as the tag team of Bret Hart and Jim Neidhart, and in 1997 Bret rebuilt it as a five-man stable. The group was pro-Canada and anti-American, heroes in Calgary and Birmingham and villains in the US. It won the 10-man main event at Canadian Stampede, and its war with Steve Austin was the Observer's Feud of the Year. It ended with the Montreal Survivor Series in November 1997.

    06

    Personas

    One name for most of the career. The eras changed with the company around him.

    YDBritain · 1978–1981

    Young David

    A teenage television wrestler trained by Ted Betley. He was briefly a tag partner for Big Daddy, and in December 1979 he took two falls from welterweight champion Jim Breaks in a title match the officials disallowed.

    BDStampede · WWF · 1983–1988

    Davey Boy Smith of the Bulldogs

    The power half of the Dynamite Kid team: short hair, Union Jack gear, a bulldog named Matilda. The Observer's best move award in 1984 went to his power clean dropkick.

    UKWWF · 1990–1997

    The British Bulldog

    The singles star, billed at 260 lb from Manchester, finishing with the running powerslam. A babyface through Wembley, a heel in Camp Cornette, and a national hero again in the 1997 Hart family stable.

    WCWCW · 1993, 1997–1998

    Davey Boy Smith in WCW

    Two stints. The first, in 1993, ended with his release that December. The second, after Montreal, ended with the Fall Brawl trapdoor injury and a six-month hospital stay.

    99WWF · 1999–2000

    The jeans-era heel

    Back in September 1999 in denim, siding with Triple H at Unforgiven. He won the European title one more time and the Hardcore title in London, and left in May 2000.

    Career Timeline

    Lancashire to Calgary to Wembley, in twenty-four years.

    1978

    Young David

    Television debut at about 15, trained by Ted Betley.

    1981

    To Calgary

    A month in South Africa, then Stu Hart's Stampede Wrestling, where he trains in the Hart family Dungeon.

    1982

    First Stampede title

    Beats Dynamite Kid for the British Commonwealth Mid-Heavyweight Championship on July 9.

    1984

    The Bulldogs and the WWF

    The team wins its first Stampede International tag title in March and joins the WWF when Vince McMahon buys Stampede. Smith marries Diana Hart in October.

    1986

    Tag champions at WrestleMania 2

    Beat Greg Valentine and Brutus Beefcake on April 7 with Ozzy Osbourne in the corner. Dynamite Kid's back gives out in December.

    1987

    The belts go to the in-laws

    Lose the titles to Bret Hart and Jim Neidhart on January 26, then lose the WrestleMania III six-man on March 29.

    1988

    Out of the WWF

    The 1988 Survivor Series on November 24 is the team's last WWF pay-per-view. They leave after backstage trouble with the Rougeaus.

    1990

    A singles return

    The team's last match is January 28 in All Japan. Smith is back in the WWF by October.

    1992

    Wembley

    Enters the Royal Rumble first on January 19 and lasts 23:33. Beats Bret Hart at SummerSlam on August 29, loses the title to Shawn Michaels, and is released.

    1993

    WCW, briefly

    Debuts at SuperBrawl III on February 21 and wins by disqualification over Vader at Slamboree on May 23. Released in December.

    1994

    Home again

    Returns to the WWF at SummerSlam on August 29, in the middle of the Hart family feud.

    1995

    Final two, then a turn

    Runner-up in the Royal Rumble from No. 2, a WrestleMania XI win with Lex Luger, a heel turn on Diesel in August, and WWF title shots against Diesel and Bret Hart.

    1996

    Title shots and tag gold

    A draw and a loss against Shawn Michaels for the WWF Championship, then the tag titles with Owen Hart on September 22.

    1997

    The first European Champion

    Wins the Berlin final on February 26, rejoins Bret in the Hart family stable, loses the belt at One Night Only on September 20, survives the Montreal Survivor Series on November 9, and leaves for WCW.

    1998

    The trapdoor

    Hurt on a ring trapdoor at Fall Brawl on September 13. A spinal infection keeps him in hospital about six months, and WCW ends his contract.

    1999

    One more WWF run

    Returns in September, sides with Triple H at Unforgiven, beats X-Pac at Rebellion, and holds the European title from October 26 to December 12.

    2000

    Last WWF title

    Wins the Hardcore title in London on May 6 and leaves the company on May 15.

    2002

    Death at 39

    Teams with his son Harry in Winnipeg on May 11. Dies of a heart attack on May 18 while on vacation in British Columbia.

    2020

    Hall of Fame

    Announced on March 12. The ceremony, delayed by the pandemic, is broadcast April 6, 2021, with Harry, Georgia and Diana inducting him.

    2026

    The ring comes home

    Harry's gear bag is stolen from a ring truck in Canada in February. The Hall of Fame ring inside it is recovered after an attempted pawn.

    08

    Rivalries

    Most of his great opponents were also his partners at some point.

    Bret Hart

    His brother-in-law, and the opponent of his career. Bret's team took the tag titles from the Bulldogs in 1987. Smith took the Intercontinental title from Bret at Wembley in 1992, and Bret beat him for the WWF Championship at In Your House 5 in 1995. In 1997 they stood together in the Hart family stable. Bret has called the Wembley match his favorite.

    Shawn Michaels

    The man who ended his two best singles reigns. Michaels took the Intercontinental title in 1992 and the European title at One Night Only in 1997. In between came the 1995 Rumble final two, a 1996 draw, and the King of the Ring title match.

    Owen Hart

    Brother-in-law, tag partner and the first European final opponent. They won the tag titles in 1996, fought for the new belt in Berlin in 1997, brawled on Raw a month later, and were pulled back together by Bret.

    Dynamite Kid

    Cousin, partner and later bitter rival. They feuded in Stampede after the 1989 split, and they fought over the British Bulldog name for years. Tom Billington died on December 5, 2018, his 60th birthday.

    Frequently Asked

    How did the British Bulldog die?

    Davey Boy Smith died of a heart attack on May 18, 2002, while on vacation in British Columbia, Canada. He was 39. Reports name the town as Invermere or nearby Fairmont Hot Springs. A week earlier he had wrestled alongside his son Harry in Winnipeg.

    Is the British Bulldog in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Yes. WWE announced him for the Class of 2020 on March 12, 2020. The pandemic pushed the ceremony back, and he was inducted on the broadcast of April 6, 2021 by his son Harry, his daughter Georgia and Diana Hart-Smith.

    Who won at SummerSlam 1992, and how big was the crowd?

    The British Bulldog beat Bret Hart for the Intercontinental Championship at Wembley Stadium on August 29, 1992, countering a sunset flip for the pin after 25:40. The cited attendance is 78,927. The WWF claimed 80,355. Pro Wrestling Illustrated named it Match of the Year.

    Was the British Bulldog the first WWF European Champion?

    Yes. He beat Owen Hart in the tournament final in Berlin on February 26, 1997, which aired on Raw on March 3. He held it 206 days, the longest reign in the title's history, and lost it to Shawn Michaels at One Night Only in Birmingham. He won it a second time from D'Lo Brown in October 1999.

    Who was in The British Bulldogs tag team?

    Davey Boy Smith and his cousin Tom Billington, the Dynamite Kid. They formed in Stampede Wrestling, won the WWF Tag Team Championship at WrestleMania 2 on April 7, 1986, and held it 294 days. They split in 1989. Dynamite Kid died in 2018.

    How was the British Bulldog related to Bret Hart?

    He married Diana Hart, sister of Bret and Owen Hart, in October 1984. That made both men his brothers-in-law. He trained in Stu Hart's Dungeon in Calgary, and he joined Bret and Owen in the 1997 Hart family stable. The marriage ended in divorce in January 2000.

    How many championships did the British Bulldog win in WWF?

    Seven reigns across four titles: the World Tag Team Championship twice (with Dynamite Kid and Owen Hart), the Intercontinental Championship once, the European Championship twice, and the Hardcore Championship twice. He never won a world title. Claims of a second IC reign, a third European reign, or WCW tag titles with Sting are wrong.

    What was the British Bulldog's finishing move?

    The running powerslam. He won with it at WrestleMania VII against The Warlord and to take the tag titles at In Your House: Mind Games. His biggest win came another way: at Wembley he sat down on a Bret Hart sunset flip for the pin.

    Is Harry Smith the British Bulldog's son?

    Yes. Harry Smith, born August 2, 1985 in Calgary, wrestled in WWE as DH Smith and David Hart Smith, winning tag gold with Tyson Kidd in 2010, and later became Davey Boy Smith Jr. He held the IWGP tag titles three times and the AJPW Triple Crown in 2024. In 2015 he acquired the British Bulldog trademark.

    What was the British Bulldog's real name?

    David Smith. He was born November 27, 1962 in Golborne, Lancashire, worked as Young David as a teenager, and became Davey Boy Smith in Stampede. WWE bills him from Manchester.

British Bulldog

Davey Boy Smith — the British Bulldog — delivered one of WWF history's most celebrated performances at SummerSlam 1992 at Wembley Stadium, where 80,000 fans watched him win the Intercontinental title from Bret Hart in front of his home country. A legitimate powerhouse with surprising agility, he was a cornerstone of the Hart Foundation, a multi-time IC and tag champion, and one of the most beloved performers the UK ever produced.

How they win

Running Powerslam — Britain's finishing move

The Running Powerslam was Davey Boy Smith's calling card — he would scoop an opponent, run across the ring, and drive them into the mat. At Wembley 1992, it finished Bret Hart to the delight of 80,000 British fans. His strength was genuine and his powerslam was legitimately one of the most impactful-looking moves in the business.

Wembley 1992

SummerSlam 1992 at Wembley Stadium — 80,000 fans, the largest WWF crowd in UK history, all cheering for Davey Boy to beat Bret Hart. He did.

Hart family

Married Diana Hart (Bret's sister) — a genuine member of wrestling's most accomplished family dynasty.

British Bulldogs

As part of The British Bulldogs with Dynamite Kid, he was one of the WWF's best tag teams (1985–1988) — athletic, fast and powerful.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1983–1984

    Learning the craft — UK scene

    Trained by his uncle Tom Billington (Dynamite Kid) on the UK wrestling circuit before joining WWF.

  2. 1985–1988

    The British Bulldogs — tag gold

    With Dynamite Kid as The British Bulldogs — one of the finest tag teams in WWF history. WWF Tag Champions; work rate and athleticism that set a new standard.

  3. 1988–1992

    Singles push & Wembley

    Went singles; multiple IC title shots. The SummerSlam 1992 IC title win at Wembley before 80,000 fans remains his career peak.

  4. 1992–1997

    Hart Foundation — family values

    Multiple tag title reigns; core member of the 1997 Hart Foundation with Bret, Owen, Brian Pillman and Jim Neidhart. The European Championship win in the UK.

  5. 1998–2002

    Final years & WCW

    Brief WCW run (1998–1999); returned to the independents. Died on May 18, 2002, aged 39, from a heart attack.

FAQ

What is British Bulldog's most famous match?
SummerSlam 1992 at Wembley Stadium — IC title vs Bret Hart before 80,000 fans. He won with the Running Powerslam.
Was Bulldog part of the Hart family?
Yes — he married Diana Hart (Bret's sister) and was a legitimate Hart family member, becoming part of the legendary Hart wrestling dynasty.
How did Davey Boy Smith die?
He died on May 18, 2002, aged 39, from a heart attack while on vacation in Invermere, British Columbia, Canada.

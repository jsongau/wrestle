    Jacob Fatu

    The Samoan Werewolf · Bloodline enforcer

    Jacob Fatu, the Samoan Werewolf of the Bloodline, is one of WWE's most feared brawlers and traded blows with LA Knight over the United States Championship.

    Biography

    Jacob Fatu wrestles as the Samoan Werewolf, a nickname earned through a heavy, high-risk brawling style unusual for a man his size. He is part of the Bloodline, the Samoan family group tied to Roman Reigns, and one of the most physically feared members of that group on WWE television.

    Through 2026 Fatu was a central figure in the WWE United States Championship picture, trading blows with LA Knight in a program that ran on the July 20, 2026 episode of Raw. Reports credited Fatu with a United States Championship reign earlier in the year, though the exact holder was still being confirmed as the title picture shifted week to week.

    His work sits inside the wider Bloodline story that has driven WWE's main-event scene, alongside relatives including Solo Sikoa and the Usos.

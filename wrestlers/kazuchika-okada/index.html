    Kazuchika Okada

    The Rainmaker · NJPW and AEW headliner

    Kazuchika Okada, The Rainmaker, was the defining NJPW star of the 2010s and a multi-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion. He now headlines AEW as the AEW Unified Champion.

    Biography

    Kazuchika Okada, known as The Rainmaker, became the face of New Japan Pro-Wrestling through the 2010s. His run as a multi-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion put him at the top of NJPW's main-event scene for most of the decade.

    One of the defining chapters of that run came on May 3, 2014, when AJ Styles beat Okada for the IWGP Heavyweight Championship in Styles' first reign with the belt. Styles held it for roughly 163 days.

    Okada later left NJPW for All Elite Wrestling, where he won AEW singles championship gold and now holds the AEW Unified Championship, which he retained at All Out 2026 in a record-setting reign. He shares an NJPW-to-AEW lineage with peers such as Jon Moxley and Kenny Omega.

    Overview

    John Cena became the defining WWE star of his generation, transforming from a trash-talking rapper into a record 17-time world champion, a record-setting wish granter and a global entertainment star. Across a 26-year career he carried WWE through its most polarizing modern era, delivered landmark rivalries with Edge, Randy Orton, CM Punk, The Rock and AJ Styles, and closed his run in 2025.

    Before WWE, Cena was an NCAA Division III All-American center and Springfield College football captain who pursued competitive bodybuilding after graduation. He broke in as the semi-robotic Prototype, answered Kurt Angle’s open challenge with “ruthless aggression” in 2002, then found his voice as the Doctor of Thuganomics. By 2005 he was WWE’s franchise player and one of the most prolific big-match performers in company history.

    His peak was defined by extraordinary longevity at the top and by a genuinely split crowd. The “Super Cena” comeback formula was endlessly criticized by hardcore fans, even as his connection with younger audiences and the mainstream was undeniable. He headlined more than a decade of WrestleManias, held the WWE Championship for a modern-era 380 days, and, outside the ring, set the Make-A-Wish record with more than 650 wishes granted.

    In 2025 Cena ran a year-long farewell tour, shocked audiences with a heel turn, and defeated Cody Rhodes at WrestleMania 41 to win a record-breaking 17th world title. He completed the Grand Slam by winning the Intercontinental Championship in November, recorded his 100th premium-live-event victory, and wrestled his final match against Gunther on December 13, 2025.

    Frequently Asked

    How many world titles has John Cena won?

    A record 17 world championships: 14 in the WWE Championship lineage and 3 World Heavyweight Championship reigns. He broke the 16-time tie by beating Cody Rhodes at WrestleMania 41 in 2025.

    What is John Cena’s real name?

    John Felix Anthony Cena, born April 23, 1977 in West Newbury, Massachusetts, where he is also billed from.

    Did John Cena retire?

    Yes. After a year-long 2025 farewell tour he wrestled his final match on December 13, 2025, losing to Gunther by submission, ending a 26-year career.

    What are John Cena’s finishing moves?

    The Attitude Adjustment (originally the F-U) and the STF submission (originally the STFU).

    What did John Cena do before wrestling?

    He was a Division III All-American football center and tri-captain at Springfield College (Class of 1999), then pursued competitive bodybuilding in California before training at Ultimate Pro Wrestling.

    How many Make-A-Wish wishes has John Cena granted?

    More than 650, the most by any individual in Make-A-Wish history, as recognized by Guinness World Records and Make-A-Wish.

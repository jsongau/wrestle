Biography

John Cena is the most commercially successful WWE superstar of the post-Attitude Era. His 16 world title reigns — tying Ric Flair's all-time record — and more than a decade as the company's primary face make him the defining WWE performer of the 2000s and early 2010s.

Cena's career trajectory is one of wrestling's most studied: he arrived in 2000 as a standard powerhouse, found his voice with the Doctor of Thuganomics hip-hop character in 2002, and ascended to the top of the card with a relentlessness and work ethic that made him indispensable. His five-move-of-doom formula was endlessly criticized by hardcore fans, but his connection with younger audiences and mainstream media was undeniable.

The Cena era is defined by extraordinary longevity at the top. From 2005 to 2016 he was rarely outside the main event picture, headlining 13 WrestleManias and feuding with essentially every major WWE star of the period: Edge, Randy Orton, CM Punk, Daniel Bryan, Sheamus, The Rock, Brock Lesnar, AJ Styles, and more.

His record-breaking Make-A-Wish contributions (650+ wishes granted, the most of any public figure in the organization's history) and crossover into Hollywood (Fast & Furious, The Suicide Squad, Peacemaker) cement a legacy that extends far beyond the ring. He was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2025.

Finishing Moves

  • Attitude Adjustment (AA) — Fireman's carry into a toss slam — simple, impactful, and made him look strong executing on opponents of every size.
  • STF (Stepover Toehold Facelock) — Submission finisher — the STF earned genuine heat because critics found his application questionable, but it won many major matches.

Career Timeline

  1. 1999

    Pro debut

    Begins wrestling after a bodybuilding and college football background.

  2. 2002

    WWE debut & Doctor of Thuganomics

    Debuts in WWE, quickly pivoting to the rap-battle character that makes him a fan-favourite on the cusp of villainy.

  3. 2004

    United States Champion

    His first singles title — won from the Big Show, signals his ascent to the top tier.

  4. 2005

    First WWE Championship

    Defeats JBL at WrestleMania 21 — the beginning of the Cena era at the top of WWE.

  5. 2006

    New Year's Revolution cash-in

    Edge cashes in Money in the Bank after Cena survives the Elimination Chamber — Cena loses the title moments after winning it.

  6. 2007

    Torn pectoral — rapid return

    Suffers a torn pectoral muscle in October; returns in just five months at Royal Rumble 2008 in one of the decade's great surprise returns.

  7. 2011

    WrestleMania XXVII vs. The Rock announcement

    The Rock returns to confront Cena — their feud culminates in two straight WrestleMania main events.

  8. 2012

    WrestleMania XXVIII vs. The Rock

    Loses to The Rock in Miami in a historic celebrity-vs-superstar main event.

  9. 2013

    16th world title — ties Flair

    Wins the 16th world title, tying Ric Flair's record — a genuinely significant achievement regardless of title inflation debates.

  10. 2025

    WWE Hall of Fame & retirement

    Inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame and completes a retirement tour following a final full-time WWE run.

FAQ

How many world titles has John Cena won?

John Cena has won 16 world titles in WWE — 13 WWE Championships and 3 World Heavyweight Championships — tying Ric Flair's record.

Is John Cena in the WWE Hall of Fame?

Yes — Cena was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2025.

What is John Cena's best match?

Widely considered to be his WWE Championship match against CM Punk at Money in the Bank 2011 — a near-five-star match in Chicago where Punk won the title and left the company with it.

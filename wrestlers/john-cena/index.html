John Cena

John Cena is a record-tying 16-time world champion and the defining WWE star of the post-Attitude era. His rapping "Doctor of Thuganomics" gimmick evolved into a polarizing all-American hero whose "Never Give Up" mantra, Make-A-Wish record and mainstream crossover made him the face of WWE for over a decade.

  • Real name John Felix Anthony Cena Jr.
  • Born April 23, 1977 · West Newbury, MA
  • Debut 2000 (main roster 2002)
  • Promotions WWE
  • Nicknames The Champ, Cenation Leader
  • Finishers Attitude Adjustment (FU), STF

Career

Cena broke through with a brash "Doctor of Thuganomics" rapper persona and won his first WWE Championship from JBL at WrestleMania 21 (2005). He quickly became WWE's franchise player, a divisive hero cheered by kids and jeered by adults, headlining show after show against every top star of his generation.

His most acclaimed program came against CM Punk, whose 2011 "pipe bomb" set up an all-time classic at Money in the Bank in Punk's hometown of Chicago. Cena also waged a career-long war with Randy Orton, feuded with Edge and Brock Lesnar, and traded torch-passing WrestleMania main events with The Rock.

Across two decades Cena amassed 16 world titles — tying Ric Flair's record — while becoming a genuine Hollywood actor and global ambassador for WWE. He closed his in-ring career with a celebrated retirement tour in 2025.

FAQ

How many world titles has John Cena won?
16 — thirteen WWE Championships and three World Heavyweight Championships, tying Ric Flair's record.
What is John Cena's finishing move?
The Attitude Adjustment (originally the FU), plus the STF submission hold.
When did John Cena retire?
He wrestled a farewell retirement tour that closed out his in-ring career in 2025.

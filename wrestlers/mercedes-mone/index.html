Mercedes Mone

Mercedes Mone — "The Boss" Sasha Banks — was a driving force of WWE's women's revolution and is now a global champion. A founding Four Horsewoman who contributed to numerous division firsts, she rebranded after leaving WWE to win the IWGP Women's, AEW TBS and TNA Knockouts titles.

  • Real name Mercedes Justine Kaestner-Varnado
  • From California, USA
  • Debut 2010 (WWE 2012)
  • Promotions WWE / NXT, NJPW / STARDOM, AEW, TNA
  • Nicknames The Boss, The CEO, Legit Boss
  • Finishers Bank Statement, Frog Splash, Meteora

Career

As Sasha Banks, "The Boss" was a cornerstone of WWE's women's revolution and a founding Four Horsewoman with Charlotte Flair, Becky Lynch and Bayley. Her NXT rivalry with best friend Bayley — including the 30-minute Iron Man match at TakeOver: Respect 2015 — is a benchmark for women's wrestling.

She contested the first women's Hell in a Cell against Charlotte Flair in 2016 and co-main-evented WrestleMania 37 against Bianca Belair in 2021. After leaving WWE in 2022 she rebranded as Mercedes Mone, capturing the IWGP Women's Championship in Japan, the AEW TBS Championship in 2024 and the TNA Knockouts World Championship.

FAQ

Is Mercedes Mone the same as Sasha Banks?
Yes — she wrestled as Sasha Banks in WWE until 2022, then rebranded as Mercedes Mone in NJPW/STARDOM, AEW and TNA.
What is her real name?
Mercedes Justine Kaestner-Varnado, from California.
What titles has she won?
Multiple Raw and NXT Women's titles as Sasha Banks; the IWGP Women's, AEW TBS and TNA Knockouts titles as Mercedes Mone.

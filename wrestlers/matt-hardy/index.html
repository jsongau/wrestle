    01

    Overview

    Matt Hardy is 52, under contract to TNA, and still in a weekly storyline. On the October 29, 2026 iMPACT, syX beat the Hardys in a Slaughterhouse syX match and left Jeff written out with storyline head trauma. Matt answered by teaming with Moose, one of syX’s earlier victims, for a match at Destination X on November 15. 34years since his first match on October 15, 1992He started before the internet era and outlasted most of it. He booked his own backyard shows as a teenager, built a character out of online fandom, and turned a rural compound into a television set.

    Setting one thing straight

    A correction, because it is often out of date: the Hardys are not the current TNA World Tag Team Champions. Their fifth TNA reign began at Slammiversary in Boston on June 28, 2026, in a four-way ladder match, and ended 56 days later. The Nemeths won the steel cage match at Lockdown in Chicago on August 23, 2026, and beat the Hardys again in an October 8 rematch. In between, on September 10, Matt challenged Nic Nemeth for the TNA World Championship in a No DQ main event and lost to a wrench shot. As of November 9, 2026, Matt holds no title.

    The foundation is the tag team. Matt and Jeff ran their own promotion, OMEGA, in Cameron, North Carolina, then won the first tag team ladder match at No Mercy in 1999 and fought the TLC trilogy with Edge and Christian and the Dudley Boyz. 12WWE tag title reigns, six of them in the original World Tag lineageMatt has twelve WWE tag reigns, five TNA tag reigns, one ROH tag reign and one NXT tag reign. All but two came with Jeff. The exceptions were a WWE Tag reign with MVP in 2007 and a Raw Tag reign with Bray Wyatt in 2018.

    On his own he kept reinventing. Version 1 in 2002 made him a smug heel with Mattitude and a follower in Shannon Moore, and it won him the Cruiserweight title. After WWE released him in 2005 during the Edge and Lita affair, the fan reaction brought him back for a real-life feud on television. He won the United States title in April 2008 and the ECW title that September. In TNA in 2016 he created Broken Matt Hardy, and the Final Deletion on July 5 changed how wrestling shoots cinematic matches. Delete. The catchphrase that defined Broken Matt Hardy.He took the same character to WrestleMania 33, Raw, AEW and back to TNA in 2024.

    05

    Factions

    One team above everything, and the groups he led on his own.

    1997–present

    The Hardy Boyz / The Hardys

    Matt Hardy, Jeff Hardy

    Brothers from Cameron who founded OMEGA in 1997, signed full time with the WWF in 1998 and won tag gold in WWE, TNA, ROH and NXT. Their 2024-26 TNA run produced three more TNA tag reigns.

    WWF · 2000–2002

    Team Xtreme

    Matt Hardy, Jeff Hardy, Lita

    The Hardys with Lita at ringside, the trio at the center of the TLC era. The 2005 Edge and Lita affair later became Matt's most personal feud.

    WWE · 2003

    Mattitude Followers

    Matt Hardy, Shannon Moore

    The Version 1 cult of one follower. Matt disbanded it in November 2003 and went back to Raw.

    AEW · 2021–2024

    Hardy Family Office

    Matt Hardy, Private Party, and later Andrade El Idolo as co-owner

    Formed after Revolution 2021 as the Matt Hardy Empire, then renamed. Andrade bought a 51% stake in storyline. The Hardy Party ended on the January 5, 2024 Rampage.

    06

    Personas

    Five characters, one performer who keeps rewriting himself.

    HBWWF · 1998–2002

    Hardy Boyz daredevil

    The steadier half of the ladder team. Twist of Fate, Side Effect, and the first tag team ladder match at No Mercy 1999.

    V1WWE · 2002–2003

    Matt Hardy Version 1

    Heel turn on August 12, 2002, then SmackDown, Mattitude and a Cruiserweight title. A Best Gimmick winner from the Wrestling Observer.

    BRTNA · 2016–2017, 2024–

    Broken Matt Hardy

    Debuted May 17, 2016. The Final Deletion, Delete or Decay and the Great War followed. He brought it back for the 2024 TNA return.

    WKWWE · 2018

    Woken Matt Hardy

    The Broken character under a new name for WWE. The Ultimate Deletion with Bray Wyatt aired on Raw on March 19, 2018.

    BMAEW · 2020–2024

    Big Money Matt

    The cash-flashing manager persona behind Private Party and the Hardy Family Office.

    07

    Career Timeline

    From a backyard promotion in 1992 to a TNA storyline in November 2026.

    1992

    Debut

    First match on October 15, 1992. First WWF match on May 23, 1994, a loss to Nikolai Volkoff on Raw.

    1997

    OMEGA

    Founds the Organization of Modern Extreme Grappling Arts with Jeff. It closes in October 1999 after both sign with the WWF.

    1999

    First tag titles, first ladder match

    Wins the WWF Tag titles on July 5, then the No Mercy ladder match over Edge and Christian on October 17.

    2000

    The first TLC

    Loses the triangle ladder match at WrestleMania 2000 and the first TLC at SummerSlam on August 27.

    2001

    TLC II and singles gold

    TLC II at WrestleMania X-Seven on April 1, the European title that April, and the WCW and World tag titles in the fall.

    2002

    Version 1

    Turns on Jeff on the August 12 Raw and becomes Matt Hardy Version 1 on SmackDown.

    2003

    Cruiserweight Champion

    Beats Billy Kidman at No Way Out on February 23 and retains against Rey Mysterio at WrestleMania XIX.

    2005

    Released, then back

    Released on April 11 after the Edge and Lita affair went public. He returns in August and fights Edge at SummerSlam, Unforgiven and Raw Homecoming.

    2008

    US and ECW Champion

    Wins the United States title at Backlash on April 27 and the ECW title at Unforgiven on September 7.

    2009

    Brother against brother

    Costs Jeff the WWE title at the Royal Rumble on January 25, beats him at WrestleMania 25, and loses the Backlash I Quit match.

    2010

    WWE release

    Sent home from a European tour in September. WWE announces his release on October 15.

    2011

    TNA, arrest, release

    Debuts in TNA on January 9. He is released after an August 20 DUI arrest.

    2015

    TNA World Champion

    Wins the TNA tag titles in Ultimate X and the TNA World Heavyweight Championship at Bound for Glory on October 4.

    2016

    Broken

    Debuts Broken Matt Hardy on May 17, airs the Final Deletion on July 5, and wins the TNA tag titles at Bound for Glory on October 2.

    2017

    ROH, then WrestleMania

    Holds the ROH tag titles from March 4 to April 1, then returns to WWE at WrestleMania 33 the next night and wins the Raw Tag titles.

    2018

    Woken

    Beats Bray Wyatt in the Ultimate Deletion, wins the WrestleMania 34 battle royal, then wins the Raw Tag titles with Wyatt on April 27.

    2020

    AEW

    Debuts on the March 18 Dynamite, wins the Stadium Stampede on May 23, and beats Sammy Guevara at All Out on September 5.

    2022

    The Hardys in AEW

    Jeff arrives on March 9. They beat the Young Bucks at Double or Nothing on May 29.

    2024

    Home to TNA

    His AEW deal expires in April. He returns at Rebellion on April 20, and the Hardys win the TNA tag titles at Bound for Glory on October 26.

    2025

    Double champions

    Regains the TNA tag titles at Slammiversary on July 20, adds the NXT titles on October 7, and beats Team 3D in their retirement match on October 12.

    2026

    Gold, then syX

    Wins a fifth TNA tag reign on June 28 and loses it at Lockdown on August 23. Challenges Nic Nemeth for the TNA World title in a No DQ match on September 10, then loses to syX at Bound for Glory and in the October 29 Slaughterhouse match.

    08

    Rivalries

    Who he went to war with.

    Edge & Christian and The Dudley Boyz

    Frequently Asked

    Is Matt Hardy still wrestling in 2026?

    Yes. He is on the TNA roster at 52. After syX beat the Hardys in the October 29, 2026 Slaughterhouse syX match and Jeff was written out injured, Matt was booked to team with Moose against syX at Destination X on November 15, 2026.

    Are the Hardys the current TNA World Tag Team Champions?

    No. Their fifth TNA reign began at Slammiversary on June 28, 2026 and ended at Lockdown on August 23, 2026, when the Nemeths won a steel cage match. The Nemeths also beat them in an October 8 rematch.

    How many tag team titles has Matt Hardy won?

    Twelve WWE tag reigns (six World Tag, three WWE/Raw Tag, one SmackDown Tag, one WCW Tag and one NXT Tag), five TNA World Tag Team reigns and one ROH World Tag Team reign. All but two came with Jeff. The others were with MVP in 2007 and Bray Wyatt in 2018.

    Has Matt Hardy been a world champion?

    He won the TNA World Heavyweight Championship twice, at Bound for Glory on October 4, 2015 and in a last man standing match taped January 8, 2016. In WWE he held the ECW Championship from September 7, 2008 to January 13, 2009.

    What was Matt Hardy Version 1?

    His 2002–2003 heel character. He turned on Jeff on the August 12, 2002 Raw, moved to SmackDown, preached Mattitude with Shannon Moore as his follower, and won the Cruiserweight title from Billy Kidman at No Way Out 2003.

    What was the Final Deletion?

    A cinematic match that aired on Impact on July 5, 2016. Broken Matt Hardy beat his brother Jeff at the Hardy compound in Cameron, North Carolina, and took sole ownership of the Hardy brand in storyline. It was followed by Delete or Decay on September 8, 2016.

    Why did WWE release Matt Hardy in 2005?

    WWE released him on April 11, 2005, after his public online conduct following the affair between Lita, his girlfriend of six years, and Edge. Fan backlash followed, and WWE brought him back that summer for a televised feud with Edge.

    What did Matt Hardy do in AEW?

    He debuted on the March 18, 2020 Dynamite, won the Stadium Stampede on May 23, 2020, managed as Big Money Matt and led the Hardy Family Office. Jeff joined in 2022. He won no AEW championship, and his contract expired in April 2024.

    Is Matt Hardy in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    No. Matt has said he expects the Hardys to go in eventually but does not think they are ready while they are still wrestling.

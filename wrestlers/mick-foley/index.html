Mick Foley

Mick Foley is a three-time WWF Champion and the "Hardcore Legend" who redefined violence in wrestling across three personas — Cactus Jack, Mankind and Dude Love. His Hell in a Cell fall against The Undertaker is among the most replayed moments in the sport, and his sacrifices helped make stars of The Rock and Triple H.

  • Real name Michael Francis Foley
  • From New York, USA
  • Debut / era 1986 · WWF peak 1996–2000
  • Promotions WCW, ECW, WWF/WWE, TNA
  • Nicknames Cactus Jack, Mankind, Dude Love, The Hardcore Legend
  • Finishers Mandible Claw (Mr. Socko), Double-Arm DDT

Career

Forged in the hardcore lineage of ECW under Paul Heyman and a punishing WCW run, Foley became wrestling's ultimate everyman — a heavyset, self-sacrificing brawler who turned brutal bumps into art. In the WWF he split into the "Three Faces of Foley": the deranged Cactus Jack, the tortured Mankind, and the groovy Dude Love.

His defining moment came at King of the Ring 1998, when as Mankind he was thrown off and then chokeslammed through the roof of the Hell in a Cell by The Undertaker. His "I Quit" war with The Rock and the Street Fight with Triple H at Royal Rumble 2000 elevated both younger stars while cementing his own legend.

A three-time WWF Champion and later a TNA World Champion, Foley became a best-selling author and one of the most beloved figures in the business.

FAQ

What is Mick Foley's real name?
Michael Francis Foley. He wrestled as three personas: Cactus Jack, Mankind and Dude Love.
What are his three personas?
The deranged hardcore brawler Cactus Jack, the tortured Mankind (with sock puppet Mr. Socko), and the groovy Dude Love — the "Three Faces of Foley."
What happened in the Hell in a Cell fall?
At King of the Ring 1998, as Mankind, Foley was thrown off the top of the Cell and then chokeslammed through its roof by The Undertaker — a legendary, oft-replayed moment.

Kane

Kane — the Big Red Machine — is arguably the most consistently over monster character in WWE history. He debuted at Badd Blood 1997, delivered one of WrestleMania's great debut angles at WM XIV, and remained a legitimate main-event threat for two decades — winning a WWF Championship, ECW title, and a record 12 tag titles.

  • Real name Glen Thomas Jacobs
  • Born April 26, 1969 · Torrejón de Ardoz, Spain (raised USA)
  • Promotions WWF / WWE (1995–2019)
  • Finisher Chokeslam, Tombstone Piledriver
  • Alter egos Issac Yankem DDS (1995), Fake Diesel (1996)
  • Real world Mayor of Knox County, TN (2018–present)

How they win

Chokeslam and monster authority

Kane wins with the Chokeslam and Tombstone Piledriver (learned from his brother). As a monster heel he also wins via disqualification or count-out, protecting his aura of invincibility across 20+ years as a main-roster threat.

12 tag reigns

The most tag team championship reigns in WWE history — a record that reflects his versatility as both a powerhouse and a team threat.

Mayor of Knox Co.

Glen Jacobs was elected Mayor of Knox County, Tennessee in 2018 — perhaps wrestling's most successful pivot to politics.

Debut vs HBK/Taker

Few debut angles in wrestling history matched his 1997 HIAC appearance — he turned what was already a landmark match into a generational moment.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1995–1997

    Issac Yankem & Fake Diesel

    Debuted as Isaac Yankem DDS, Jerry Lawler's dentist heel. Later repackaged as a fake Diesel in 1996 — neither gimmick clicked. The character was quietly shelved.

  2. 1997

    Kane debuts at Badd Blood

    October 5, 1997 — he tore through the cell door during the first-ever Hell in a Cell. A debut angle that immediately elevated him to main-event credibility.

  3. 1997–2005

    The Big Red Machine era

    WrestleMania XIV match vs The Undertaker; WWF title win over Austin; 12 tag title reigns; the unmasking in 2003; a feud with Shane McMahon and an enduring main-roster presence.

  4. 2006–2019

    Corporate Kane & later career

    World Heavyweight title (2010); partnerships with Daniel Bryan as Team Hell No (2012–13, fan-favourite comedy); final active years as a comedy-serious hybrid character.

  5. 2018–present

    Mayor of Knox County

    Glen Jacobs won the Mayor of Knox County, Tennessee election in 2018 — one of sports entertainment's most unusual off-screen success stories.

FAQ

What is Kane's real name?
Glen Thomas Jacobs — and he's also the Mayor of Knox County, Tennessee.
When did Kane debut?
October 5, 1997 at Badd Blood: In Your House — he tore through the Hell in a Cell door during the Undertaker vs HBK match.
How many tag titles did Kane win?
12 — the most in WWE history, with partners including The Undertaker, Rob Van Dam, Big Show, and Daniel Bryan.

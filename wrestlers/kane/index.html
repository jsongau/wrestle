Kane

Kane (Glenn Jacobs), "The Big Red Machine," is a WWE and World Heavyweight Champion and one half of the Brothers of Destruction. Debuting in 1997 as the disfigured, masked brother of The Undertaker, he became one of WWE's most durable monsters across masked and unmasked eras — and later a real-life mayor in Tennessee.

  • Real name Glenn Thomas Jacobs
  • Born April 26, 1967 · Madrid, Spain
  • Debut Kane character 1997
  • Promotions WWF/WWE (with ECW title reign)
  • Nicknames The Big Red Machine, The Devil's Favorite Demon
  • Finishers Chokeslam, Tombstone Piledriver

Career

Kane debuted at Badd Blood 1997 by tearing the door off the first-ever Hell in a Cell to attack his "brother" The Undertaker — one of the most memorable entrances in WWE history. The supernatural masked monster became a fixture of the Attitude Era, feuding with Stone Cold Steve Austin and Triple H and briefly capturing the WWF Championship in 1998.

Across more than two decades Kane reinvented himself repeatedly — as a masked demon, an unmasked "corporate" enforcer, and a comedic figure. He won the World Heavyweight Championship by cashing in Money in the Bank in 2010 and set a WWE record with a dozen tag team title reigns, most iconically alongside The Undertaker as the Brothers of Destruction.

One of his most beloved runs came as one half of "Team Hell No" with Daniel Bryan, a mismatched comedy tag team that became WWE Tag Team Champions. In real life, Glenn Jacobs was elected Mayor of Knox County, Tennessee, in 2018 while continuing sporadic WWE appearances.

FAQ

What is Kane's real name?
Glenn Thomas Jacobs, born April 26, 1967, in Madrid, Spain, to American parents.
Who are the Brothers of Destruction?
Kane and his kayfabe brother The Undertaker — one of WWE's most dominant tag teams and a decades-long storyline pairing.
Is Kane really a politician?
Yes. Glenn Jacobs was elected Mayor of Knox County, Tennessee, in 2018 while still appearing for WWE.

Bayley

Bayley (Pamela Martinez) went from the lovable "Hugger" to the cynical "Role Model" and became WWE's first women's Grand Slam champion. Her NXT title classics with Sasha Banks helped legitimize women's main events, and she remains a founding Four Horsewoman.

  • Real name Pamela Rose Martinez
  • From San Jose, California, USA
  • Debut 2008 (WWE 2012)
  • Promotions WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames The Hugger, The Role Model
  • Finishers Bayley-to-Belly, Rose Plant

Career

Beloved as the earnest underdog "Hugger" in NXT, Bayley delivered a run of acclaimed title matches with best friend Sasha Banks — including their NXT TakeOver: Brooklyn 2015 classic and the 30-minute Iron Man match at TakeOver: Respect — that helped prove women could headline. She is a founding Four Horsewoman with Banks, Charlotte Flair and Becky Lynch.

On the main roster she reinvented herself as the calculating "Role Model" heel, anchoring a long 2019–2020 SmackDown Women's Championship reign and teaming with Banks as the "Golden Role Models" before their split. She became WWE's first women's Grand Slam champion, having held the NXT, Raw and SmackDown Women's and Women's Tag titles.

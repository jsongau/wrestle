    Dominik Mysterio

    WWE Raw heel · Former Intercontinental Champion

    Dominik Mysterio is a WWE Raw heel and the son of Rey Mysterio. A former Intercontinental Champion and a breakout of The Judgment Day, he lost the IC Title to Penta on the March 2, 2026 episode of Raw.

    Biography

    Dominik Mysterio is the son of Rey Mysterio and one of WWE's most booed heels. He turned his family name into heat, playing the ungrateful son opposite his father before establishing himself as a singles act.

    He became a breakout of The Judgment Day, WWE's dominant faction from 2023 to 2025, working alongside Finn Balor, Damian Priest, and Rhea Ripley. His pairing with Ripley drove one of the group's top merchandise lines.

    As a singles competitor he held the WWE Intercontinental Championship, the title that gave him his first sustained run with gold. He lost it to Penta on the March 2, 2026 episode of Raw.

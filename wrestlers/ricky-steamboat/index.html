    Ricky Steamboat

    Ring science

    How Ricky Steamboat won — and lost

    Steamboat's crossbody was among the most credible high-flying finishers of the territory era — a fast, fluid spot that could beat anyone convincingly. He rarely needed gimmick finishes; his in-ring story told the win.

    Chapter by chapter

    Career timeline

    1. 1976

      Territory debut

      Trained in the NWA system; debut across Southeast territories.

    2. 1985

      WWF arrival

      Brought in as a fan-favourite babyface; immediate push into the IC title picture.

    3. 1987

      WrestleMania III — the match

      Defeats Randy Savage for the Intercontinental title at WM III (93,000 fans, Silverdome). One of wrestling's greatest moments.

    4. 1989

      NWA World Title & the Flair Trilogy

      Three all-time-great matches with Ric Flair over the NWA title (Chi-Town Rumble, WrestleWar, Clash VI). The 1989 trilogy set the standard for match quality worldwide.

    5. 1991–94

      WCW second run

      Returns to WCW; feuds with Austin over the U.S. title. Still delivering ★★★★+ matches in his late 30s.

    6. 2009

      One-night return

      Competes at WWE Backlash 2009 in a Stretcher Match — receives a standing ovation from the live crowd and media.

    7. 2010

      WWE Hall of Fame

      Inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame Class of 2010, presented by Ric Flair.

    Quick answers

    Frequently asked questions

    Did Ricky Steamboat ever win the NWA World Heavyweight Championship?

    Yes. Steamboat defeated Ric Flair for the NWA World Heavyweight Championship at Chi-Town Rumble on February 20, 1989. He held the title for approximately two months before losing it to Flair at WrestleWar 1989 in a 2-out-of-3-falls match.

    What is Ricky Steamboat's best match?

    Most historians cite either the WrestleMania III match vs. Randy Savage (March 29, 1987) or the WrestleWar 1989 2/3-falls match vs. Ric Flair as Steamboat's best. Dave Meltzer awarded both ★★★★★. The 1989 Flair trilogy is widely considered the greatest series of matches in wrestling history.

    How is Ricky Steamboat connected to Steve Austin?

    Steve Austin and Brian Pillman (as The Hollywood Blonds) defeated Ricky Steamboat and Shane Douglas to win the NWA/WCW World Tag Team Championship in March 1993. Austin also feuded with Steamboat over the WCW United States Championship in 1994, with Austin winning that feud.

    When was Ricky Steamboat inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Ricky Steamboat was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2010, presented by his long-time rival and friend Ric Flair.

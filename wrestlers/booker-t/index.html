    01

    Overview

    Booker T held the WCW World Heavyweight Championship five times between July 2000 and August 2001, and he was the man holding it when WCW closed. 11WCW World Tag Team Championship reigns, the individual record; ten of them with Stevie Ray as Harlem HeatBefore that he was half of Harlem Heat with his older brother Stevie Ray. The team won the WCW World Tag Team Championship ten times, a team record, and Booker's 11 reigns with that title (the last with Test in 2001) are the individual record. He also holds the record for WCW World Television Championship reigns with six. In WWE he became King Booker, won the 2006 King of the Ring and the World Heavyweight Championship, and went into the WWE Hall of Fame twice: alone in 2013 and with Harlem Heat in 2019.

    Setting one thing straight

    Two claims on old pages, including the one this dossier replaces, need fixing. The first is that King Booker won the WWE Championship in 2006. He did not. He beat Rey Mysterio for the World Heavyweight Championship at The Great American Bash on July 23, 2006, and he never held the WWE Championship. The second is the count. WWE.com calls him a six-time world champion but lists only four WCW title reigns. Both are right by their own rules. He won the WCW title four times inside WCW, then a fifth time on Raw on July 30, 2001, after the WWF bought WCW, when the belt was billed as the WCW Championship. Add the 2006 World Heavyweight title and you get six.

    He was born Booker T. Huffman Jr. on March 1, 1965, in Plain Dealing, Louisiana, the youngest of eight children, and grew up in Houston after losing both parents. At 22 he pleaded guilty to two aggravated robberies of Wendy's restaurants and served 19 months in prison. He trained at Ivan Putski's school under Scott Casey and debuted in 1990 as G.I. Bro. 2000first WCW world title, beating Jeff Jarrett at Bash at the Beach on July 9WCW signed the brothers in 1993 as Harlem Heat, first as Kole and Kane, and their first tag title came in December 1994. Booker's singles run began with the Television title on December 29, 1997, and a best-of-seven series with Chris Benoit in 1998. His first world title came on the strangest night in WCW history: at Bash at the Beach on July 9, 2000, Vince Russo's angle with Hulk Hogan blew up, the title went back to Jeff Jarrett, and Booker beat him for it in an impromptu match. He traded it with Kevin Nash and Russo, lost it to Scott Steiner in a straitjacket cage at Mayhem on November 26, 2000, and took it back from Steiner in the opening match of the final Nitro.

    In the WWF he led the WCW side of the Invasion, pinned Kurt Angle in the Inaugural Brawl, and dropped the WCW title to The Rock at SummerSlam 2001. He lost a World Heavyweight title match to Triple H at WrestleMania XIX after a build that used his criminal record against him, a promo Michael Cole publicly questioned. 2006King of the Ring, then the World Heavyweight Championship as King Booker with Queen SharmellThen came King Booker: a 2006 King of the Ring win over Bobby Lashley, a crown, a faux British accent, his wife Sharmell as Queen, and a World Heavyweight Championship reign from July to November 2006. TNA followed from 2007 to 2009, where he co-founded the Main Event Mafia and won the TNA tag titles with Scott Steiner. Since 2011 he has been a broadcaster, a SmackDown general manager and a promoter. As of November 9, 2026, he is NXT's full-time color commentator alongside Vic Joseph, runs Reality of Wrestling in Houston as a WWE ID partner, and coaches on WWE LFG. His last match was a win over Zilla Fatu at a Reality of Wrestling show on January 11, 2025. Trick Williams has challenged him for SummerSlam 2027. Booker said he never says never. Nothing is booked.

    05

    Factions

    A brother, a comedy partner, an invading army and a mafia of former world champions.

    WCW · 1993–2000

    Harlem Heat

    Booker T, Stevie Ray; managed by Col. Robert Parker, Sister Sherri and later Jacqueline

    The Huffman brothers debuted in WCW in 1993 as Kole and Kane and went back to their own names in 1994. Ten WCW World Tag Team title reigns is the team record. They split in 1998, reunited in 1999 for reigns eight through ten, and broke up for good in 2000. WWE inducted them in 2019, and they reunited once more in 2015 to win Reality of Wrestling's tag titles.

    WWF · 2001

    The Alliance

    WCW and ECW talent; Shane McMahon in Booker's corner

    Booker was the WCW world champion who walked into the WWF at King of the Ring 2001 and attacked Steve Austin. He pinned Kurt Angle to win the Invasion main event for Team WCW/ECW and held the WCW title until The Rock took it at SummerSlam.

    WWE · 2002–03

    Booker T &amp; Goldust

    Booker T, Goldust

    The comedy tag team WWE.com still lists among his highlights. They won the World Tag Team Championship in a four-way elimination at Armageddon 2002, with Booker pinning Chris Jericho.

    WWE · 2006–07

    King Booker &amp; Queen Sharmell

    King Booker, Queen Sharmell; help from Finlay and William Regal

    William Regal crowned him on SmackDown after the 2006 King of the Ring. Finlay's shillelagh had helped win that final, and Chavo Guerrero's chair won him the World Heavyweight title. Sharmell's interference cost him the belt to Batista at Survivor Series.

    TNA · 2008–09

    Main Event Mafia

    Kurt Angle, Sting, Kevin Nash, Booker T; Scott Steiner joined a week later

    Formed on Impact on October 23, 2008, the same night Booker unveiled the Legends Championship. Booker and Scott Steiner won the TNA World Tag Team Championship from Beer Money at Victory Road 2009, and Steiner is half of the Steiner Brothers, the team Harlem Heat traded the WCW tag titles with in 1996.

    Four names, one Houston accent underneath all of them.

    GBWWA · 1990

    G.I. Bro

    A Gulf War-era military gimmick tied to the Sgt. Slaughter angle, eight weeks into his training at Ivan Putski's school.

    KOWCW · 1993–94

    Kole

    His first WCW name, with his brother billed as Kane, as Harlem Heat debuted under Col. Robert Parker. Both went back to their real first names in 1994.

    BTWCW · WWE · TNA · 1994–

    Booker T

    The Spinaroonie, the Scissors Kick, the Book End and &ldquo;Sucka!&rdquo; Tag specialist, record Television champion, five-time WCW world champion and, later, the voice of NXT.

    KBWWE · 2006–07

    King Booker

    Crowned on SmackDown after winning the 2006 King of the Ring, with a faux British accent and Sharmell as Queen. WWE.com says many fans rank the accent among wrestling's best. He won the World Heavyweight title in the role.

    07

    Career Timeline

    Prison to the last WCW championship, then a king, then the broadcast desk.

    1987

    Prison

    Pleads guilty in December 1987 to two aggravated robberies of Houston Wendy's restaurants. He serves 19 months and stays on parole until April 1992.

    1990

    Debut as G.I. Bro

    Debuts in the Western Wrestling Alliance after training under Scott Casey at Ivan Putski's school.

    1993

    Harlem Heat in WCW

    Signs with WCW alongside his brother Lash, now Stevie Ray. They debut as Kole and Kane, managed by Col. Robert Parker.

    1994

    First tag title

    Beats Stars and Stripes on December 8 for the WCW World Tag Team Championship, the first of ten Harlem Heat reigns.

    1997

    Singles breakout

    Wins the WCW World Television Championship from Disco Inferno on December 29. He will hold it six times, the record.

    1998

    The best-of-seven

    Beats Chris Benoit in Game 7 at The Great American Bash on June 14, then beats Fit Finlay for the TV title the same night.

    1999

    Reunion and the record

    Re-forms Harlem Heat, wins the tag titles at Road Wild, Fall Brawl and Halloween Havoc, and reaches ten team reigns.

    2000

    World champion

    Beats Jeff Jarrett at Bash at the Beach on July 9, trades the title with Kevin Nash and Vince Russo, wins it a third time in October, and loses it to Scott Steiner at Mayhem on November 26.

    Rivalries

    The men he traded titles and blows with.

    Scott Steiner

    Took the WCW title from him in a straitjacket cage at Mayhem 2000 with a chair and the Steiner Recliner, then lost it back to him in the opening match of the final Nitro. Eight years later they were TNA tag champions together in the Main Event Mafia.

    Jeff Jarrett

    The champion Booker beat for his first WCW world title at Bash at the Beach 2000, and again in a San Francisco 49ers match for the vacant title that October.

    Kevin Nash

    Took the WCW title from Booker on Nitro on August 28, 2000, and lost it back in a cage at Fall Brawl three weeks later. They met again in Booker's TNA debut in 2007.

    The Rock

    Won the WCW Championship from him at SummerSlam 2001, kipping up from the Spinaroonie to hit a Rock Bottom. The two used nearly the same side slam as a finisher: the Book End and the Rock Bottom.

    Stone Cold Steve Austin

    Booker's WWF arrival was an attack on Austin at King of the Ring 2001. Their feud produced the supermarket brawl, filmed in one take in a real California store and aired on SmackDown in late 2001.

    Triple H

    WrestleMania XIX, March 30, 2003. Triple H's build leaned on Booker's criminal record, and Michael Cole asked at the press conference whether it was racially insensitive. Triple H won with a Pedigree after working the knee.

    Batista

    Challenged King Booker at SummerSlam 2006 and won only by DQ, then took the World Heavyweight Championship at Survivor Series when Sharmell's interference backfired.

    Chris Benoit

    Two best-of-seven series eight years apart: the 1998 WCW series for a TV title shot, which Booker won in Game 7, and the 2005&ndash;06 WWE series over the US title, which Benoit won at No Way Out.

    Frequently Asked

    What is Booker T doing in 2026?

    He calls NXT. As of November 9, 2026, Booker T is NXT's full-time color commentator alongside Vic Joseph, a job he started on October 6, 2022. He also runs Reality of Wrestling in Houston, an official WWE ID partner, and coaches on WWE LFG. NXT held a Booker T Appreciation Night in Houston on March 17, 2026. His last match was a win over Zilla Fatu on January 11, 2025. Trick Williams has challenged him for SummerSlam 2027, and nothing is booked.

    How many world titles did Booker T win?

    Six, by WWE's count: five reigns with the WCW World Heavyweight Championship (2000–2001) and one with the World Heavyweight Championship as King Booker (2006). WWE.com lists “WCW Champion (4 times)” because his fifth WCW reign came on Raw on July 30, 2001, after the WWF bought WCW.

    Was Booker T ever WWE Champion?

    No. This gets repeated often, including on the page this dossier replaced. In 2006 King Booker beat Rey Mysterio for the World Heavyweight Championship, the SmackDown world title, at The Great American Bash on July 23. He never held the WWE Championship.

    How many tag team titles did Harlem Heat win?

    Ten WCW World Tag Team Championships between December 1994 and October 1999, the most of any team. Booker won the title an 11th time with Test in 2001, which makes his 11 the individual record. Booker also won three WWF/WWE World Tag Team titles, with Test, Goldust and Rob Van Dam, and the TNA tag titles with Scott Steiner.

    How did Booker T win his first WCW title?

    At Bash at the Beach on July 9, 2000, in Daytona Beach. Earlier that night Vince Russo had Jeff Jarrett lie down for Hulk Hogan, and the angle fell apart when Hogan left. Booker then beat Jarrett by pinfall in an impromptu match, 13:41, for the WCW World Heavyweight Championship.

    What happened between Booker T and Scott Steiner at Mayhem 2000?

    Steiner took the WCW World Heavyweight Championship from him in a Straitjacket Caged Heat match in Milwaukee on November 26, 2000, hitting him with a chair and knocking him out with the Steiner Recliner for a technical submission. Booker won it back from Steiner on the final Nitro on March 26, 2001. The two later teamed in TNA's Main Event Mafia and won the TNA World Tag Team titles in 2009.

    Who were King Booker and Queen Sharmell?

    Booker's 2006–07 WWE characters. He won the 2006 King of the Ring, beating Bobby Lashley in the final at Judgment Day on May 21. William Regal crowned him on SmackDown, and he named Sharmell, his real-life wife since 2005, his Queen. As King Booker he won the World Heavyweight Championship in July 2006.

    Why is WrestleMania XIX remembered for Booker T?

    Because of the build. On the March 3, 2003, Raw, Triple H suggested that Booker, a convicted criminal, could never win a world title in WWE. Michael Cole asked at the WrestleMania press conference whether the promo was racially insensitive. Triple H then beat Booker in Seattle on March 30, and the finish was widely disliked.

    Is Booker T in the Hall of Fame twice?

    Yes. Stevie Ray inducted him as a singles wrestler at Madison Square Garden on April 6, 2013, and he went in again with Harlem Heat at the Barclays Center on April 6, 2019.

    What is Reality of Wrestling?

    Booker T's own promotion in Houston, founded as Pro Wrestling Alliance in 2005 and renamed in 2012. It is now an official WWE ID partner. Harlem Heat won its tag titles in a 2015 reunion, Booker beat Zilla Fatu there in 2025, and in October 2026 he handed ROW's Josiah Jean a WWE ID contract.

    What is Booker T's real name, and where is he from?

    Booker T. Huffman Jr., born March 1, 1965, in Plain Dealing, Louisiana. He grew up in Houston, and WWE bills him from there. He has said the “T.” does not stand for anything.

Roman Reigns

Roman Reigns (Joe Anoa'i) is the "Tribal Chief" whose record 1,316-day Undisputed reign redefined the modern main event. A former member of The Shield and part of the storied Samoan Anoa'i dynasty, he ruled WWE at the head of The Bloodline until Cody Rhodes finally dethroned him at WrestleMania 40.

  • Real name Leati Joseph "Joe" Anoa'i
  • From Pensacola, Florida, USA
  • Debut 2010 (main roster 2012)
  • Promotions WWE
  • Nicknames The Tribal Chief, Head of the Table, The Big Dog
  • Finishers Spear, Superman Punch, Guillotine

Career

Roman Reigns arrived in 2012 as the powerhouse enforcer of The Shield with Seth Rollins and Dean Ambrose (Jon Moxley). He won the 2015 Royal Rumble and headlined multiple WrestleManias, but it was his 2020 reinvention as the menacing "Tribal Chief" that turned him into the defining star of the era.

Leading The Bloodline with the guidance of advocate Paul Heyman, Reigns held the Universal and Undisputed WWE Championship for a record 1,316 days. The saga's civil war with Jey Uso and its climactic feud with Cody Rhodes — who "finished the story" at WrestleMania 40 in 2024 — anchored WWE's biggest programs. He is a cousin of The Rock within the Anoa'i family.

FAQ

What is Roman Reigns's real name?
Leati Joseph "Joe" Anoa'i, a member of the Samoan Anoa'i wrestling dynasty.
How long was his Undisputed title reign?
1,316 days from 2020 to 2024 — the longest modern world-title reign — ended by Cody Rhodes at WrestleMania 40.
How is he related to The Rock?
They are cousins in the Anoa'i family, alongside The Usos, Rikishi, Umaga and Yokozuna.

    01

    Overview

    Scott Hall wrestled under several names, and two of them changed the business. As Razor Ramon, a Miami tough guy built on Tony Montana from Scarface, he flicked a toothpick at the camera, said “Hey, yo,” and became the first man to hold the WWF Intercontinental Championship four times. As plain Scott Hall, he walked through a Nitro crowd on May 27, 1996 and started the invasion that became the nWo. Born October 20, 1958 in St. Mary’s County, Maryland, he debuted in Florida in 1984, won the AWA World Tag Team Championship with Curt Hennig (Mr. Perfect) in 1986, and worked WCW as The Diamond Studd, managed by Diamond Dallas Page, before the WWF signed him in 1992.

    The Razor run lasted four years. He won the vacant Intercontinental title in a Raw battle royal in September 1993, and when Shawn Michaels claimed the belt was still his, WrestleMania X settled it: both belts hung above the ring at Madison Square Garden on March 20, 1994, and Razor climbed for both. 434days as Intercontinental Champion across four reigns, as WWE counts themDave Meltzer gave the match five stars, and both the Wrestling Observer and Pro Wrestling Illustrated named it the 1994 Match of the Year. He won the title back from Diesel at SummerSlam 1994, again from Jeff Jarrett in May 1995, and a fourth time from Dean Douglas in October 1995. WWE’s title history credits him with 434 days as champion. Backstage he ran with Michaels, Kevin Nash, Sean Waltman and Triple H as the Kliq, and the Madison Square Garden Curtain Call on May 19, 1996 was his WWF exit.

    Setting one thing straight

    One correction, because the old version of this page got it backwards: Razor Ramon won the WrestleMania X ladder match. Michaels tangled himself in the ropes going for the belts, and Razor retrieved both to stay the undisputed champion. Michaels won the rematch, a ladder match at SummerSlam on August 27, 1995. The old page's Triple H rows from April 1996 and WrestleMania XII had no source and are gone, and so is its 1-5-0 career record. Hall never held a world heavyweight title.

    In WCW, Hall and Nash were The Outsiders. At Bash at the Beach on July 7, 1996 their mystery partner turned out to be Hulk Hogan, and the match with Randy Savage, Sting and Lex Luger ended in a no contest and a new faction. The Outsiders won the WCW World Tag Team Championship six times, starting at Halloween Havoc on October 27, 1996, and Hall added a seventh reign with The Giant. He won the 1997 World War 3 battle royal, two WCW United States titles and the Television title. At Starrcade 1998 his stun gun set up the end of Goldberg’s streak.

    The later years were harder. He came back to the WWF with the nWo in 2002, lost to Steve Austin at WrestleMania X8, and was released that May. He worked TNA in 2002, 2004–2005 and 2010, and his last match was in Des Moines on June 17, 2016. His fight with addiction was public; he moved into Diamond Dallas Page’s Atlanta home during the recovery shown in The Resurrection of Jake the Snake. WWE inducted him into the Hall of Fame in 2014, Nash giving the speech, and Hall closed with “bad times don’t last, but bad guys do.” He went in again with the nWo in the 2020 class. He died on March 14, 2022, at 63, after complications from hip-replacement surgery, at WellStar Kennestone Hospital in Marietta, Georgia.

    05

    Factions

    Teams and groups across five promotions. The Outsiders and the nWo are the ones history kept.

    CWF & Crockett · c.1983–1985

    American Starship

    Coyote (Scott Hall), Eagle (Dan Spivey)

    His first act, a tag team in Championship Wrestling from Florida that also worked Jim Crockett Promotions and Central States. Not an AWA team.

    WCW · 1991–1992

    The Diamond Mine

    The Diamond Studd, Diamond Dallas Page (manager), Oz

    Page's stable, where Hall first teamed with the man then billed as Oz. That was Kevin Nash, five years before The Outsiders.

    WWF · 1994–1996

    The Kliq

    Scott Hall, Kevin Nash, Shawn Michaels, Sean Waltman, Triple H

    The backstage group of friends. The Curtain Call at Madison Square Garden on May 19, 1996 broke kayfabe in front of a live crowd, and only Triple H was punished, because Hall and Nash were already leaving.

    WCW · 1996–2000

    The Outsiders

    Scott Hall, Kevin Nash (Syxx as a stand-in in 1997)

    Six WCW World Tag Team Championship reigns and PWI Tag Team of the Year for 1997. The team split at Slamboree 1998 and reunited in 1999.

    WCW · 1996–1999 · WWF 2002

    nWo

    Hulk Hogan, Scott Hall, Kevin Nash, later many more

    Founded at Bash at the Beach on July 7, 1996. Hall sided with nWo Hollywood after Slamboree 1998, and the three founders brought the group to the WWF in 2002. Inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame with the 2020 class.

    TNA · 2004–2005 · 2010

    Kings of Wrestling and The Band

    Hall, Nash, Jeff Jarrett (2004); Hall, Nash, Waltman, Eric Young (2010)

    Two late TNA reunions. The Band held the TNA World Tag Team Championship in 2010 until it was stripped that June.

    06

    Personas

    One career, four names. The Diamond Studd built the toothpick and the finisher, Razor Ramon made them famous, and Scott Hall used his own name to start an invasion.

    SHFlorida · AWA · 1984–1989

    Starship Coyote / Big Scott Hall

    American Starship's Coyote in Florida, then Magnum and Big Scott Hall in the AWA, where he won the tag titles with Curt Hennig and challenged for the AWA world title. New Japan, Germany and Puerto Rico filled the gaps.

    DSWCW · 1991–1992

    The Diamond Studd

    The toothpick flick and Diamond Dallas Page as his manager. The crucifix powerbomb was already the finish. He left WCW in May 1992 and the WWF signed him that month.

    RRWWF · 1992–1996

    Razor Ramon

    A heel modeled on Scarface who was captaining the fan favorites by Survivor Series 1994. Four Intercontinental reigns, the WrestleMania X ladder match, and a crucifix powerbomb finish named for the character.

    HLWCW · 1996–2000 · WWF 2002

    Scott Hall

    His real name and no gimmick. He walked out of the Nitro crowd on May 27, 1996, co-founded the nWo, won seven WCW tag titles, and kept the crucifix powerbomb under a new name.

    07

    Career Timeline

    Florida to Madison Square Garden to Daytona Beach, and out through Des Moines in 2016.

    1984

    Debut in Florida

    Starts in Championship Wrestling from Florida as Coyote of American Starship with Dan Spivey. Wikipedia dates the debut October 2, 1984.

    1986

    AWA tag champion

    Wins the AWA World Tag Team Championship with Curt Hennig on January 18 in Albuquerque.

    1989

    First WCW run

    Debuts on WCW television on July 9, pinned by The Great Muta.

    1991

    The Diamond Studd

    Returns to WCW managed by Diamond Dallas Page, teaming with Oz, who is Kevin Nash.

    1992

    Razor Ramon arrives

    Signs with the WWF and wins his TV debut on Superstars on August 8 with his crucifix powerbomb.

    1993

    Intercontinental Champion

    Pins Bob Backlund at WrestleMania IX, then wins the vacant title in a Raw battle royal final over Rick Martel.

    1994

    The ladder match

    Beats Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania X on March 20 to unify the title. Loses it to Diesel, wins it back at SummerSlam, and is sole survivor at Survivor Series.

    1995

    Third and fourth reigns

    Loses to Jeff Jarrett at the Royal Rumble, regains the belt in Montreal in May, loses the SummerSlam ladder rematch to Michaels, and wins a fourth title in Winnipeg in October.

    1996

    The Outsider

    Drops the title to Goldust in January, takes part in the Curtain Call on May 19, debuts on Nitro on May 27, and forms the nWo with Nash and Hogan at Bash at the Beach on July 7.

    1997

    Tag champion, World War 3 winner

    The Outsiders hold the tag titles for 231 days in one stretch, Syxx filling in for Nash. Hall wins the World War 3 battle royal on November 23.

    1998

    Title shot and the split

    Loses to Sting for the WCW World Heavyweight Championship at Uncensored, turns on Nash at Slamboree, and stun-guns Goldberg at Starrcade.

    1999

    Three titles in one year

    Two United States title wins, the Television title at Mayhem, and a last tag reign with Nash in December.

    2002

    Back to the WWF

    Returns with the nWo at No Way Out, loses to Steve Austin at WrestleMania X8, and is released in May.

    2004

    TNA

    Teams with Nash and Jeff Jarrett as the Kings of Wrestling; loses to Jeff Hardy at Final Resolution in January 2005.

    2010

    One more tag title

    Wins the TNA World Tag Team Championship with Nash and Eric Young, then is stripped and released in June.

    2014

    Hall of Fame

    Inducted by Kevin Nash on April 5 in New Orleans.

    2016

    Final match

    Beats Chuck Taylor in Des Moines on June 17.

    2021

    Hall of Fame, again

    Inducted with the nWo as part of the 2020 class, in a ceremony delayed by the pandemic and aired April 6, 2021.

    2022

    Death

    Dies March 14, 2022, at 63, in Marietta, Georgia, after complications from hip-replacement surgery. WWE announces it at the start of that night's Raw.

    08

    Rivalries

    The men he fought, and the friend he fought most.

    Shawn Michaels

    Friends off camera, rivals on it. WrestleMania X put both belts above the ring and Razor got them. Michaels won the SummerSlam 1995 rematch.

    Kevin Nash

    Took the Intercontinental title from him as Diesel in April 1994 and lost it back at SummerSlam. Then the partner of his career, until Hall hit him with a belt at Slamboree 1998.

    Jeff Jarrett

    Three Intercontinental title changes in 1995, a WrestleMania XI win by disqualification, and TNA matches against each other in 2002.

    Goldust

    Ended Razor's fourth and last Intercontinental reign at the 1996 Royal Rumble in Fresno on January 21.

    The Steiner Brothers

    Traded the WCW World Tag Team Championship with The Outsiders through 1997 and 1998, until Scott Steiner turned on Rick at SuperBrawl VIII.

    Goldberg

    Hall's stun gun at Starrcade 1998 cost Goldberg the streak. Goldberg answered in a stun gun ladder match at Souled Out 1999.

    Frequently Asked

    How did Scott Hall die?

    He died on March 14, 2022, at 63, at WellStar Kennestone Hospital in Marietta, Georgia. He had broken his hip in a fall earlier that month. After hip-replacement surgery a blood clot came loose, he had three heart attacks on March 12, and his family took him off life support two days later. WWE announced his death at the start of that night's Raw.

    Who won the WrestleMania X ladder match?

    Razor Ramon. On March 20, 1994 at Madison Square Garden, both Intercontinental belts hung above the ring. Shawn Michaels got caught in the ropes and Razor climbed down with both, staying the undisputed champion. Michaels won their SummerSlam 1995 ladder rematch.

    How many times was Razor Ramon Intercontinental Champion?

    Four, and he was the first wrestler to reach both three and four reigns. WWE dates them September 27, 1993, August 29, 1994, May 19, 1995 and October 22, 1995, for 434 days in all.

    Was Scott Hall a founding member of the nWo?

    Yes. Hall and Kevin Nash arrived in WCW as The Outsiders in 1996, and at Bash at the Beach on July 7, 1996 Hulk Hogan was revealed as their third man. Those three are the founders. WWE inducted the nWo, with Hall, into the Hall of Fame as part of the 2020 class.

    How many WCW tag team titles did Scott Hall win?

    Seven WCW World Tag Team Championships. Six came with Kevin Nash as The Outsiders, starting at Halloween Havoc on October 27, 1996, and one with The Giant on July 20, 1998. During one 1997 reign Syxx defended in the injured Nash's place.

    What was the Razor’s Edge?

    His finisher, a crucifix powerbomb: he lifted the opponent onto his shoulders with the arms spread and threw them down back first. In WCW he kept the move and renamed it for The Outsiders.

    Was Razor Ramon really Cuban?

    No. The character was built on Tony Montana from Scarface, and he was billed from Miami. Scott Hall was born in St. Mary’s County, Maryland, on October 20, 1958.

    Is Scott Hall in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Twice. Kevin Nash inducted him individually on April 5, 2014 in New Orleans. He went in again with the nWo, alongside Hulk Hogan, Nash and Sean Waltman, in the 2020 class, whose ceremony was delayed by the pandemic and aired April 6, 2021.

    What was the Kliq and the Curtain Call?

    The Kliq was a backstage group of friends: Hall, Kevin Nash, Shawn Michaels, Sean Waltman and Triple H. On May 19, 1996, at the end of Hall and Nash's last WWF night at Madison Square Garden, four of them hugged in the ring in front of the crowd. Hall and Nash were already leaving for WCW, so Triple H took the punishment.

    Did Scott Hall ever win a world title?

    No. His best shot came from winning the World War 3 battle royal on November 23, 1997, which earned him a WCW World Heavyweight Championship match. Sting beat him at Uncensored on March 15, 1998.

    Who was The Diamond Studd?

    Scott Hall in WCW in 1991 and 1992, managed by Diamond Dallas Page in the Diamond Mine. The toothpick and the crucifix powerbomb started there. His partner Oz was Kevin Nash.

    What was Scott Hall’s last match?

    A win over Chuck Taylor at F1RST Wrestling in Des Moines, Iowa, on June 17, 2016. His last WWF match came in May 2002, and his last WCW match was SuperBrawl 2000 on February 20.

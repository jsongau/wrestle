    Frequently Asked

    How many wrestlers are in the Wrestle Lore database?
    Wrestle Lore profiles 41 of the most significant wrestlers of the modern era (1997–present), spanning WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA/Impact and NXT — from Attitude Era icons like Stone Cold Steve Austin and The Rock to modern main-eventers like Roman Reigns, Becky Lynch and Cody Rhodes.
    Can I filter the roster by promotion?
    Yes. Use the promotion buttons (WWE, WCW, ECW, TNA, NXT) to filter the roster, and use the search box to find a wrestler by name, nickname or promotion. Many stars appear under more than one promotion because they jumped companies during their careers — for example Chris Jericho (WCW → WWE) and AJ Styles (TNA → WWE).
    Who are the Four Horsewomen of WWE?
    The Four Horsewomen are Charlotte Flair, Becky Lynch, Sasha Banks (now Mercedes Mone) and Bayley — four NXT-bred stars who drove WWE's women's revolution, headlined by the first-ever women's WrestleMania main event. See how they connect on the relationships map.
    Which wrestlers competed in multiple promotions?
    Cross-promotion journeymen are a hallmark of the era. Mick Foley worked WCW, ECW, WWE and TNA; Hulk Hogan headlined WWE, WCW and TNA; and Ric Flair spanned NWA, WCW, WWE and TNA. Explore full career paths on each profile and trace feuds in the Rivalries hub.

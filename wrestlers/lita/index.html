Lita

Lita (Amy Dumas) is the high-flying "Queen of Extreme," a four-time Women's Champion who helped legitimize women's wrestling as athletic spectacle. With Trish Stratus she main-evented Raw in 2004, and her real-life relationships fueled one of the era's most notorious storylines.

  • Real name Amy Christine Dumas
  • From Fort Lauderdale, Florida, USA
  • Debut 1999 (retired 2006; periodic returns)
  • Promotions ECW (briefly), WWF / WWE
  • Nicknames The Queen of Extreme
  • Finishers Litasault (moonsault), DDT

Career

A punk-rock, high-flying counterpart to Trish Stratus, Lita broke through as a member of Team Xtreme alongside the Hardy Boyz and won four WWE Women's Championships. Her daredevil moonsaults and hurricanranas helped reframe women's wrestling as genuine athletic spectacle in the early 2000s.

In December 2004 she and Trish Stratus became the first women to main-event Monday Night Raw. Away from the ring, her real-life romances with Matt Hardy and then Edge ignited a bitter on-screen feud between the two men in 2005. She retired after a farewell against Trish at Unforgiven 2006 and later returned, winning the Women's Tag Team titles with Trish in 2023.

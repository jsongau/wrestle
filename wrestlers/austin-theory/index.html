    Austin Theory

    World Tag Team Champion · WWE Raw

    Austin Theory is a WWE Raw wrestler and one half of the World Tag Team Champions, a title he won alongside Bron Breakker on Raw on July 6, 2026.

    Biography

    Austin Theory wrestles on WWE's Raw brand, where in 2026 he formed a tag team with Bron Breakker. On Raw on July 6, 2026, the pair won the World Tag Team Championship, the Raw-lineage men's tag title.

    The title win put Theory and Breakker at the top of the Raw tag division for that period, one of two men's tag championships WWE ran in 2026 alongside the WWE Tag Team Championship held by Damian Priest and R-Truth.

Randy Orton

Randy Orton, "The Viper," is a 14-time world champion and the youngest World Heavyweight Champion in WWE history. A third-generation star, he evolved from the cocky "Legend Killer" of Evolution into a methodical, cold-blooded main-event fixture whose "out of nowhere" RKO became one of wrestling's most iconic finishers.

  • Real name Randal Keith Orton
  • Born April 1, 1980 · Knoxville, TN
  • Debut 2000 (WWE 2002)
  • Promotions WWE
  • Nicknames The Viper, The Legend Killer, Apex Predator
  • Finishers RKO, Punt Kick

Career

The son of "Cowboy" Bob Orton and grandson of Bob Orton Sr., Randy Orton is a genuine third-generation wrestler. He rose as the youngest member of Evolution alongside Triple H, Ric Flair and Batista, branding himself the "Legend Killer." At SummerSlam 2004 he became the youngest World Heavyweight Champion in history at 24.

Orton reinvented himself as the sinister "Viper," a patient predator who could strike with the RKO at any moment. He engaged in a career-long rivalry with John Cena, formed and feuded with Edge as Rated-RKO, and battled The Undertaker and Christian in memorable programs.

One of the most reliable main-eventers of his generation, Orton collected 14 world championships across more than two decades, later reinventing himself again as a tag team champion with Riddle (RK-Bro) while remaining a top-card fixture.

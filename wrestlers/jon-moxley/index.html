Jon Moxley

Jon Moxley (Jonathan Good) — WWE's "Lunatic Fringe" Dean Ambrose reborn as a brawling AEW icon. A founding member of The Shield and a 2016 WWE Champion, he left in 2019 to become a multi-time AEW World Champion and one of wrestling's foremost deathmatch performers.

  • Real name Jonathan David Good
  • From Cincinnati, Ohio, USA
  • Debut 2004 (WWE 2011; AEW 2019)
  • Promotions CZW, WWE / NXT, AEW, NJPW
  • Nicknames Mox, The Lunatic Fringe, The Purveyor of Violence
  • Finishers Death Rider / Paradigm Shift, Dirty Deeds, Bulldog Choke

Career

After forging a reputation on CZW's deathmatch scene, Jonathan Good joined WWE in 2011 and became Dean Ambrose, the unhinged "Lunatic Fringe" of The Shield with Roman Reigns and Seth Rollins. He won the WWE Championship via a Money in the Bank cash-in in 2016 along with Intercontinental and United States gold.

In 2019 he walked away from WWE and reclaimed the name Jon Moxley, immediately becoming a cornerstone of AEW. A multi-time AEW World Champion — including the inaugural interim reign — he blends main-event credibility with the raw, violent style that made his name, also holding the IWGP US and GCW World titles. He is married to broadcaster Renee Paquette.

FAQ

What is Jon Moxley's real name?
Jonathan David Good, from Cincinnati, Ohio. He wrestled as Dean Ambrose in WWE from 2011 to 2019.
Is Jon Moxley the same as Dean Ambrose?
Yes — Dean Ambrose was his WWE ring name. He reverted to his indie name Jon Moxley when he joined AEW in 2019.
How many times has he been AEW World Champion?
Multiple times, including the inaugural interim reign, making him one of AEW's most decorated champions.

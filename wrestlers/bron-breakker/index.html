    Bron Breakker

    World Tag Team Champion · Raw powerhouse

    Bron Breakker, son of Rick Steiner, is a reigning World Tag Team Champion with Austin Theory and a multi-time WWE Intercontinental Champion on Raw.

    Biography

    Bron Breakker is a second-generation powerhouse on Raw. He is the son of Rick Steiner and nephew of Scott Steiner, and he has built a résumé that already includes multiple WWE Intercontinental Championship reigns, the workhorse title created in 1979.

    On the July 6, 2026 episode of Raw he added the World Tag Team Championship, winning the belts with Austin Theory. He was also a central figure in the build to Survivor Series: WarGames 2026, one of the promotion's marquee autumn events.

    His Intercontinental Championship history connects him to the current holder, Penta, and to the record set by Gunther, whose 666-day reign is the longest in the title's history. On Raw he has shared the top of the card with names such as Seth Rollins.

    “Stone Cold” Steve Austin

    Stone Cold Steve Austin is the face of the Attitude Era — a 6-time WWF Champion, 3-time Royal Rumble winner, and one of the biggest draws in wrestling history. He built his craft as "Stunning" Steve Austin in WCW (World TV, US and tag champion), reinvented himself with legendary promos in ECW, then became the beer-swilling, boss-defying antihero whose war with Mr. McMahon powered WWF past WCW.

    Tag teams & partners

    Five reigns, five very different partners

    Austin's tag résumé is a study in range: a genuine best-friends team early with Brian Pillman, then four WWF reigns won mostly with rivals and mismatched partners — proof his presence made almost any pairing credible.

    WCW · 1993

    The Hollywood Blonds

    with Brian Pillman — WCW World Tag Champions. A cohesive, cocky, brilliantly-worked heel team; the real thing, not a forced pairing.

    WWF · 1997

    with Shawn Michaels

    WWF Tag Champions — two alpha egos and locker-room rivals reluctantly aligned against the Hart Foundation.

    WWF · 1997

    with Dude Love

    WWF Tag Champions — Foley's surprise mystery-partner reveal; an odd-couple pairing that worked.

    WWF · 1998

    with The Undertaker

    WWF Tag Champions — enemies forced to team while feuding over the WWF Title. A distrustful alliance.

    WWF · 2001

    Two-Man Power Trip

    with Triple H — a dominant super-heel duo who briefly held the WWF, Intercontinental and Tag titles at once.

    Career timeline

    From Stunning to Stone Cold

    1. 1991–1995

      "Stunning" Steve Austin (WCW)

      Blond, cocky heel; the Hollywood Blonds with Brian Pillman; TV, US and tag titles; a career-defining feud with Ricky Steamboat — then fired by Bischoff via FedEx.

    2. 1995–1996

      ECW — the reinvention

      A short run for Paul Heyman built on scorching anti-WCW promos that rebuilt his edge and caught WWF's eye.

    3. 1996

      The Ringmaster "Austin 3:16"

      Debuts as Ted DiBiase's Ringmaster, then wins the 1996 King of the Ring and delivers the "Austin 3:16" promo that launches a phenomenon.

    4. 1997–2003

      The Attitude Era icon

      The WM13 double-turn, six WWF Titles, the war with Mr. McMahon — the hottest act in wrestling, until a neck injury forces retirement in 2003.

    5. 2022

      One more Stunner

      Returns at WrestleMania 38 to beat Kane's stablemate Kevin Owens — his first match in 19 years.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked

    Did Stone Cold wrestle in WCW and ECW?
    Yes. He was "Stunning" Steve Austin in WCW (1991–1995), winning the World Television, United States and World Tag Team (with Brian Pillman) titles. After WCW fired him he had a brief, career-reinventing ECW run (1995–1996) built on promos; he held no ECW title.
    How many WWF/WWE Championships did he win?
    Six WWF/WWE Championships, plus two Intercontinental titles, four tag titles, the 1996 King of the Ring, and a record-tying three Royal Rumble wins (1997, 1998, 2001).
    What is his WrestleMania record?
    6–2 across eight matches. Both losses were to Bret Hart (WM13, 1997) and The Rock (WM XIX, 2003).
    What is his real name and finisher?
    Steven James Williams (born Steven James Anderson), from Austin, Texas. His finisher is the Stone Cold Stunner.

Stone Cold Steve Austin

Stone Cold Steve Austin is a six-time WWF Champion and the beer-swilling, boss-defying antihero who powered WWF past WCW in the Monday Night Wars. His 1996 "Austin 3:16" promo launched a cultural phenomenon, and his war with Mr. McMahon became the template for anti-authority storytelling. He is the defining face of the Attitude Era.

  • Real name Steven James Anderson (later Williams)
  • From Texas, USA
  • Debut / era 1989 · Attitude Era peak 1996–2003
  • Promotions WCW, ECW, WWF/WWE
  • Nicknames The Texas Rattlesnake, The Bionic Redneck
  • Finisher Stone Cold Stunner

Career

After breaking in through WCW and a star-making run in ECW, Austin arrived in the WWF in 1996 and immediately reinvented himself. His King of the Ring victory that June produced the "Austin 3:16" promo — "Austin 3:16 says I just whipped your ass" — a line that became merchandise, gospel and rallying cry for a generation of fans tired of cartoon heroes.

The 1996–97 program with Bret Hart transformed Austin from edgy heel to the biggest babyface in the industry, culminating in the legendary double-turn at WrestleMania 13. His anti-authority feud with Mr. McMahon then defined the Attitude Era, and his WrestleMania trilogy with The Rock gave the boom period its marquee rivalry. A stacked neck ultimately forced his early retirement after the peak of WrestleMania X-Seven in 2001.

FAQ

What is Stone Cold Steve Austin's real name?
Steven James Anderson, later Steven James Williams. He performed as Steve Austin, "The Texas Rattlesnake."
What is Austin 3:16?
The catchphrase Austin coined after winning the 1996 King of the Ring — "Austin 3:16 says I just whipped your ass." It became a merchandising and cultural phenomenon that helped launch the Attitude Era.
How many WWF Championships did he win?
Six WWF Championships (1998–2001), plus two Intercontinental reigns, four Tag Team titles and three Royal Rumble wins (1997, 1998, 2001).

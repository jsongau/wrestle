    Tiffany Stratton

    The Buff Barbie · SmackDown

    Tiffany Stratton, the Buff Barbie, is a former NXT Women's Champion who won Money in the Bank in 2024 and held the WWE Women's Championship for about 302 days.

    Biography

    Tiffany Stratton, billed as the Buff Barbie, came into WWE with a competitive gymnastics background and turned it into a fast rise through NXT. She held the NXT Women's Championship before moving to the main roster, where she won the 2024 Money in the Bank ladder match and cashed the contract in for the WWE Women's Championship.

    Her run with the WWE Women's Championship lasted roughly 302 days, one of the longer reigns of the mid-2020s division. She spent 2026 near the top of the SmackDown women's picture, moving into the WWE Women's United States Championship program and back into the world-title conversation.

    Her 2026 rivalry with Jade Cargill ran through a match at Night of Champions 2026, where Charlotte Flair inserted herself. Both Stratton and Cargill then qualified for the first-ever interim WWE Women's Championship ladder match at SummerSlam 2026, held while Rhea Ripley was out injured and the title was not vacated.

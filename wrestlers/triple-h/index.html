Triple H

Triple H (Paul Levesque) is a 14-time world champion, co-founder of D-Generation X, and one of the Attitude Era's defining performers. As a wrestler, The Cerebral Assassin engineered some of wrestling's most compelling long-term storylines. Today, as WWE's Head of Creative, he shapes the product from the boardroom.

  • Real name Paul Michael Levesque
  • Born July 27, 1969 · Nashua, NH
  • Promotions WWF / WWE (1995–2019)

How they win

The Pedigree as a political instrument

Triple H finished with the Pedigree — but his real signature was the cerebral build to get there. As a heel he held titles through management interference, contract clauses, and Evolution's muscle. As a face the Pedigree landed clean and the crowd popped huge.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1992–1995

    Terra Ryzing / Jean-Paul Lévesque

    Started as Terra Ryzing in WCWA and IWF; signed with WWF as Jean-Paul Lévesque, a French-Canadian blue-blood character.

  2. 1995–1997

    Connecticut Blueblood & DX founding

    Repackaged as Hunter Hearst Helmsley, a wealthy Connecticut socialite. Co-founded D-Generation X with Shawn Michaels and Chyna in 1997.

  3. 1999–2002

    The Game — Attitude Era peak

    Emerged as one of wrestling's top heels; multiple WWF title reigns, the Power Trip with Austin, and an in-ring career that set the standard for main-event storytelling.

  4. 2002–2008

    Evolution & long title runs

    Led Evolution (with Ric Flair, Randy Orton, Batista); held the World Heavyweight title for extended periods — both praised as dominant booking and criticized as overly political.

  5. 2009–2019

    Final chapter & WrestleMania epics

    WrestleMania classics vs The Undertaker (WM 27, 28); match vs Daniel Bryan at WM 30; last in-ring years.

  6. 2022–present

    EVP of Creative

    After Vince McMahon's retirement, Levesque became WWE's Head of Creative — reshaping the product, reviving NXT, and bringing back talent.

FAQ

What is Triple H's finishing move?
The Pedigree — a double underhook facebreaker where he drives the opponent's face into the mat. One of WWE's most iconic finishers.
How many world titles did Triple H win?
14 world titles across his career — one of the highest totals in WWE history, spanning WWF/WWE Championships and World Heavyweight Championships.
What was the Two-Man Power Trip?
A 2001 faction of Triple H and Steve Austin, who simultaneously held multiple titles until HHH tore his quad in May 2001 in a match against Benoit and Jericho.

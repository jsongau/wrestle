Triple H

Triple H (Paul Levesque) is a 14-time world champion, the dominant main-event heel known as "The Game," and now WWE's creative architect. He married into the McMahon family, co-founded D-Generation X, led Evolution, and later built the NXT generation before becoming WWE's Chief Content Officer.

  • Real name Paul Michael Levesque
  • From New Hampshire, USA
  • Debut / era 1992 · main-event run 1999–2013 · now WWE executive
  • Promotions WCW (early), WWF/WWE
  • Nicknames The Game, The Cerebral Assassin, The King of Kings
  • Finisher Pedigree

Career

After an early WCW stint, Levesque became "Hunter Hearst Helmsley" in the WWF before co-founding D-Generation X with Shawn Michaels. His breakout as a ruthless main-event heel came at the turn of the millennium, headlined by a brutal Street Fight with Cactus Jack (Mick Foley) at Royal Rumble 2000 that legitimized him as "The Game."

He married Stephanie McMahon and led the Evolution faction with Ric Flair, Randy Orton and Batista — a group whose betrayals fueled years of storylines. His trilogy with The Undertaker peaked in the "End of an Era" Hell in a Cell at WrestleMania XXVIII.

As NXT's architect he elevated a new generation before becoming WWE's head of creative — closing his in-ring career after health issues while overseeing the company's storytelling direction.

FAQ

What is Triple H's real name?
Paul Michael Levesque, known in the ring as "The Game" and now WWE's Chief Content Officer.
How many world titles did he win?
14 world titles combined (WWF/WWE Championship and World Heavyweight Championship), plus five Intercontinental reigns and the 2002 Royal Rumble.
Is he married to Stephanie McMahon?
Yes — he is married to Stephanie McMahon, daughter of Vince McMahon, and oversees WWE creative.

Ric Flair

Ric Flair (Richard Fliehr) is "The Nature Boy" — a 16-time world champion whose flamboyant, limousine-riding heel set the template for main-event wrestling. His fingerprints run through the modern era via Evolution proteges and his daughter Charlotte Flair.

  • Real name Richard Morgan Fliehr
  • From Charlotte, North Carolina (via Minnesota), USA
  • Debut 1972 (WWE run 2001–2008)
  • Promotions NWA, WCW, WWF / WWE, TNA
  • Nicknames The Nature Boy, Slick Ric, The Dirtiest Player in the Game
  • Finishers Figure-Four Leglock

Career

Debuting in 1972, Ric Flair became the consummate flamboyant heel and arguably the greatest all-around performer of his generation. His 16 recognized world titles across the NWA, WCW and WWE — a mark later matched by John Cena — and his "Wooo!" persona defined main-event wrestling for decades. He won the WWF Championship in the 1992 Royal Rumble.

His career-long rivalry with Sting, dating to their 1988 Clash of the Champions classic, is one of wrestling's greatest. In WWE he led Evolution as mentor to Triple H, Randy Orton and Batista, and had an emotional career-ending match against Shawn Michaels at WrestleMania 24 in 2008. His daughter Charlotte Flair carries on his legacy.

Bret Hart

Bret "Hitman" Hart is a five-time WWF Champion and two-time WCW World Champion, the technical master who carried WWF between the Hogan and Attitude eras. Known as "The Excellence of Execution," his Sharpshooter and his role in the 1997 Montreal Screwjob made him one of wrestling's most consequential figures.

  • Real name Bret Sergeant Hart
  • From Calgary, Alberta, Canada
  • Debut / era 1976 (Stampede) · WWF star 1984–1997 · WCW 1997–2000
  • Promotions Stampede, WWF/WWE, WCW
  • Nicknames The Hitman, The Excellence of Execution
  • Finisher Sharpshooter

Career

A son of the Hart wrestling dynasty out of Calgary's Stampede promotion, Bret Hart rose through the WWF first as one half of the Hart Foundation before establishing himself as the finest pure technician of his era. He carried the company as its ace through the mid-1990s, delivering clinics against Mr. Perfect, his brother Owen Hart and Shawn Michaels.

His 1996–97 program with Stone Cold Steve Austin produced the perfect double-turn at WrestleMania 13, an all-time classic that made Austin a star. Later in 1997, the Montreal Screwjob — losing the title to Michaels against his wishes at the direction of Vince McMahon — became the most infamous moment in wrestling history and sent him to WCW, where he won two more World Championships.

FAQ

What is Bret Hart's real name?
Bret Sergeant Hart, nicknamed "The Hitman" and "The Excellence of Execution."
What was the Montreal Screwjob?
At Survivor Series 1997 he lost the WWF Championship to Shawn Michaels via a controversial finish arranged by Vince McMahon without Hart's consent — the most infamous moment in wrestling, which preceded his WCW jump.
How many world titles did he win?
Five WWF Championships and two WCW World Heavyweight Championships, plus multiple Intercontinental and tag titles and the 1991 King of the Ring.

    Yota Tsuji

    IWGP Heavyweight Champion · NJPW

    Yota Tsuji restored the IWGP Heavyweight Championship in January 2026 and is recognized as its 87th champion, one of New Japan Pro-Wrestling's central figures.

    Biography

    Yota Tsuji stands at the center of New Japan Pro-Wrestling's 2026 title picture. His run reshaped NJPW's top championship at the start of the year.

    At Wrestle Kingdom 20 on January 4, 2026, Tsuji beat Konosuke Takeshita for the IWGP World Heavyweight Championship. He then split that belt back into its original components and restored the IWGP Heavyweight Championship, which had been retired since 2021.

    The IWGP Heavyweight Championship was reinstated on January 6, 2026, with Tsuji recognized as its 87th champion. His title also connects him to New Japan history alongside past IWGP figures such as Kazuchika Okada and Kenny Omega.

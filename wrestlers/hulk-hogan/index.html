Hulk Hogan

Hulk Hogan (Terry Bollea) is the biggest mainstream draw professional wrestling has ever produced — a five-time WWF and six-time WCW World Champion. He headlined the 1980s WWF boom with Hulkamania, then stunned the industry with his 1996 heel turn founding the nWo, the move that powered WCW past WWF in the Monday Night Wars.

  • Real name Terry Gene Bollea
  • From Georgia / Florida, USA
  • Debut / era 1977 · WWF icon 1983–1993 · WCW/nWo 1994–2000
  • Promotions WWF/WWE, WCW, TNA
  • Nicknames The Immortal, Hollywood Hogan, The Hulkster
  • Finisher Atomic Leg Drop

Career

Hogan was the face of the 1980s WWF boom, turning "Hulkamania" into a mainstream cultural force with red-and-yellow patriotism, the big boot and the Atomic Leg Drop. He headlined WrestleMania III against Andre the Giant and reigned as a five-time WWF Champion before jumping to WCW in 1994.

In 1996 he shocked the wrestling world at Bash at the Beach, turning heel to form the New World Order with Scott Hall and Kevin Nash — sending WCW's Nitro to 83 straight weeks atop the ratings. His long "Hollywood" Hogan run drove the defining feud with Sting, and he later dropped the WCW title to Goldberg on Nitro before a late-career WWE showdown with The Rock at WrestleMania X8 and TNA runs.

FAQ

What is Hulk Hogan's real name?
Terry Gene Bollea — "The Hulkster," and "Hollywood" Hogan during his heel/nWo era.
What is the nWo?
The New World Order, the faction Hogan founded with his 1996 heel turn alongside Scott Hall and Kevin Nash. It powered WCW's Nitro past WWF's Raw.
How many world titles did he win?
Five WWF Championships and six WCW World Heavyweight Championships, plus two Royal Rumble wins (1990, 1991).

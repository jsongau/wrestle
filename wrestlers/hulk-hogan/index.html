    Biography

    Hulk Hogan is the most commercially successful professional wrestler in history. His impact on the business — arriving at precisely the moment WWF expanded nationally and MTV brought wrestling to a new audience — is impossible to overstate. Hulkamania was a genuine cultural phenomenon, not a wrestling angle.

    Hogan's WWF tenure (1983–1993, then briefly 2002–2003) produced five world title reigns and a succession of WrestleMania main events that defined the annual spectacle for a decade. His formula was simple and devastating: take a beating, Hulk Up, big boot, leg drop, pin. The crowd went berserk every single time.

    The creative reinvention as Hollywood Hulk Hogan — villain, nWo leader, goatee, black-and-white — was one of the great character pivots in entertainment history. Audiences who had cheered him for 13 years couldn't fully boo him, creating a fascinating liminal figure: a heel who generated more heat as a hated legend than any other character on the WCW roster.

    No assessment of Hogan is complete without acknowledging the controversies: a recording leak in 2015 resulted in his release from WWE and a brief Hall of Fame removal. He was reinstated in 2018. His ring work was never technically exceptional, but his understanding of crowd psychology — when to sell, when to "Hulk Up," when to stall, when to pose — was unmatched in the territory era.

    Finishing Moves

    • Leg Drop — The Atomic Leg Drop — Hogan's running leg drop, the most famous finishing move in wrestling history by commercial reach if not technical artistry.
    • Big Boot — Running big boot that sets up the leg drop — the two-move combination was simple, effective, and the crowd never tired of it.

    Career Timeline

    1. 1977

      Pro debut

      Breaks into wrestling in Florida; is quickly identified as having unusual physical presence and crowd appeal.

    2. 1980

      AWA Champion

      Wins the AWA World title — already one of the biggest stars in wrestling before WWF.

    3. 1983

      WWF debut

      Returns to WWF (after a brief earlier run) and transforms the company's business model with national expansion.

    4. 1984

      First WWF Championship

      Defeats the Iron Sheik in MSG — Hulkamania is born. The moment that begins wrestling's pop-culture saturation.

    5. 1987

      WrestleMania III

      Body-slams André the Giant in front of a claimed 93,173 fans — the most watched wrestling moment of the decade.

    6. 1991

      WWF's biggest crisis

      Steroid trial fallout — Hogan's departure creates an enormous vacuum that the New Generation scrambles to fill.

    7. 1996

      nWo turn

      Turns heel at WCW Bash at the Beach — Hollywood Hulk Hogan is born in one of wrestling's greatest swerves.

    8. 2002

      WWE return

      Returns as part of the nWo with Nash and Hall; quickly turns face due to crowd reaction; wins the WWE title briefly.

    9. 2005

      Hall of Fame (first)

      Inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame by Sylvester Stallone — a first-ballot, no-debate induction.

    10. 2018

      Hall of Fame reinstatement

      Reinstated to the Hall of Fame after a three-year removal following the 2015 recording controversy.

    FAQ

    How many world titles did Hulk Hogan win?

    Hulk Hogan won 12 recognized world titles: 5 WWF Championships, 6 WCW World Heavyweight Championships, and 1 AWA World Heavyweight Championship.

    What is Hulkamania?

    Hulkamania is the pop-culture phenomenon built around Hulk Hogan's babyface character in the 1980s WWF. The phrase 'Hulkamania is running wild' became one of professional wrestling's most recognized catchphrases.

    Is Hulk Hogan in the WWE Hall of Fame?

    Yes — Hogan was originally inducted in 2005, removed in 2015 following a controversy, and reinstated in 2018. He remains in the Hall of Fame.

    Signature Matches

    ★★★★

    Hulk Hogan vs. André the Giant

    WrestleMania III — Mar 29, 1987

    The body slam heard round the world — not a great technical match but the most important moment in WWF's commercial history. 93,173 claimed attendance; the spectacle that proved wrestling was mainstream entertainment.

    ★★★½

    Hulk Hogan vs. The Ultimate Warrior

    WrestleMania VI — Apr 1, 1990

    Champion vs. Champion — Warrior wins clean in Toronto. The rare case of Hogan putting someone over completely. One of Hogan's most competitive losses.

    ★★★½

    Hollywood Hulk Hogan vs. The Rock

    WrestleMania X8 — Mar 17, 2002

    The crowd turns it inside out — Toronto cheers Hogan over The Rock, flipping the intended dynamic. A masterclass in crowd psychology and legacy.

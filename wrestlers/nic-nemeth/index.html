    01

    Overview

    Nic Nemeth is the man WWE audiences knew for fifteen years as Dolph Ziggler. He was a three-time Mid-American Conference champion at Kent State, debuted in Ohio Valley Wrestling in 2004, and spent 2006 in a cheerleader costume as Nicky of the Spirit Squad. Then came Dolph Ziggler. 6Intercontinental Championship reigns, 2010–2018The WWE record reads two World Heavyweight Championships, six Intercontinental titles, two United States titles, four tag team title reigns, the 2012 Money in the Bank briefcase, two Survivor Series sole survivals and a 27-day NXT Championship in 2022. WWE released him on September 21, 2023, after 19 years.

    Setting one thing straight

    The old version of this page said he won the TNA World Championship at Slammiversary on June 28, 2026, and still held it. Both halves need fixing. That night was his second TNA World title. The first came at Slammiversary on July 20, 2024, in a six-way elimination match in Montreal, and lasted 183 days until Joe Hendry beat him at Genesis. The second reign ended at 105 days, when Leon Slater beat him in the Bound for Glory main event on October 11, 2026. As of the November 5, 2026 Impact, the gold he holds is the TNA World Tag Team Championship, with his brother Ryan. One more counting rule: this page lists four world titles (two in WWE, two in TNA). The NXT Championship is not classed as a world title by WWE, and the AAA Mega and IWGP Global titles are counted separately below.

    The WWE peak came in two bursts. On July 15, 2012 he won the Money in the Bank ladder match in Phoenix. On April 8, 2013, the night after WrestleMania 29, he cashed it in on Alberto Del Rio in East Rutherford, New Jersey, and won the World Heavyweight Championship. April 8, 2013the Money in the Bank cash-in on Alberto Del Rio, the night after WrestleMania 29A concussion followed within weeks. He lost the title back to Del Rio at Payback on June 16, 2013, after 69 days. His first World Heavyweight reign, in February 2011, had lasted part of one SmackDown taping. The second burst was 2014: three Intercontinental titles and the Survivor Series main event in St. Louis, where Sting's WWE debut helped him pin Seth Rollins as the last man standing for Team Cena.

    After the release he worked everywhere at once. He beat David Finlay for NJPW's IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship on February 23, 2024 in Sapporo. He won the vacant AAA Mega Championship from Alberto El Patron on April 27, 2024 in Monterrey. He won the TNA World title on July 20, 2024. For a week in 2024 he held two of those belts at once, and for four weeks he held two others. 105days in his second TNA World Championship reign, June 28 to October 11, 2026In TNA he turned heel with Ryan in March 2025, won the tag titles twice, re-signed in January 2026, and cashed in a Call Your Shot trophy on Mike Santana at Slammiversary 2026. He made one WWE appearance in between, on November 17, 2025, billed again as Dolph Ziggler in John Cena's Last Time Is Now tournament. He lost to Solo Sikoa. He has said WWE asked him back several times and he turned the other offers down.

    05

    Factions

    Mostly a singles act, with five groups that marked the eras.

    WWE · 2006

    Spirit Squad

    Kenny, Johnny, Mitch, Nicky, Mikey

    Five male cheerleaders who debuted on Raw on January 23, 2006, won the World Tag Team titles on April 3, and feuded with the reformed D-Generation X. They lost the belts to Ric Flair and Roddy Piper at Cyber Sunday and disbanded on Raw on November 27, 2006. Nicky was Nemeth.

    WWE · 2010–2013

    With Vickie Guerrero and Big E

    Dolph Ziggler, Vickie Guerrero, Big E Langston

    Vickie Guerrero handed him the vacant World Heavyweight title in 2011 and was at ringside when he won Money in the Bank in 2012. Big E was his enforcer by the 2013 title reign and was ejected from ringside at Payback.

    WWE · 2018

    Dogs of War

    Dolph Ziggler, Drew McIntyre, Braun Strowman

    Formed on Raw on September 3, 2018, the night Ziggler and McIntyre won the Raw Tag Team titles from The B-Team. They lost them to Seth Rollins and Dean Ambrose on October 22.

    WWE · 2019–2022

    Ziggler and Roode

    Dolph Ziggler, Robert Roode

    Raw Tag Team Champions in 2019 and SmackDown Tag Team Champions in 2021. Roode was in his corner for the 2022 NXT Championship run and pulled Bron Breakker out of the ring at Roadblock.

    TNA · 2025–present

    The Nemeths

    Nic Nemeth, Ryan Nemeth

    The brothers turned heel together at Sacrifice on March 14, 2025, attacking Matt Hardy. They have held the TNA World Tag Team Championship twice, both times taking it from The Hardys.

    06

    Personas

    One career under two names. Dolph Ziggler and Nic Nemeth are the same wrestler.

    NNOVW · 2004–2005

    Nick Nemeth

    Debuted in Ohio Valley Wrestling on November 3, 2004, under his own name, and spent 2005 as the caddie for Chavo Guerrero's Kerwin White character until the angle ended after Eddie Guerrero's death.

    NYWWE · 2006

    Nicky

    The Spirit Squad cheerleader. A comedy gimmick, but it put him on Raw, made him a World Tag Team Champion under the Freebird Rule, and put him in the ring with D-Generation X.

    DZWWE · 2008–2023

    Dolph Ziggler

    Introduced on Raw on September 15, 2008. A bleached-blond, self-adoring heel who sold big and stole shows, later a fan favorite from 2013. Every WWE title on this page was won under this name.

    NNNJPW · AAA · TNA · 2024–present

    Nic Nemeth

    After the September 2023 release he went back to his legal name and won titles in NJPW, AAA and TNA within a year. WWE billed him as Dolph Ziggler again for one Raw match on November 17, 2025.

    07

    Career Timeline

    Kent State to OVW to nineteen years in WWE, then four promotions in two years.

    2003

    Kent State

    Finishes with 121 career wins, then the school record, and three Mid-American Conference titles at 165 lb. He had been accepted to Arizona State's law school.

    2004

    OVW debut

    First match in Ohio Valley Wrestling on November 3, as Nick Nemeth.

    2006

    Spirit Squad

    Debuts on Raw on January 23 and becomes a World Tag Team Champion on April 3. The group disbands on November 27 after the DX feud.

    2008

    Dolph Ziggler

    The new name debuts on Raw on September 15.

    2010

    First Intercontinental title

    Beats Kofi Kingston at a July 28 SmackDown taping and holds the title for 160 days.

    2011

    World champion for one taping

    Awarded the vacant World Heavyweight title on February 15 and loses it to Edge the same night. Wins the US title at Capitol Punishment on June 19.

    2012

    Money in the Bank

    Wins the World Heavyweight contract ladder match on July 15 in Phoenix, then survives alone for his team at Survivor Series on November 18.

    2013

    The cash-in

    Cashes in on Alberto Del Rio on Raw, April 8, the night after WrestleMania 29. A concussion follows, and Del Rio takes the title back at Payback on June 16.

    2014

    Sole survivor again

    Three Intercontinental title wins, and the last man standing for Team Cena at Survivor Series on November 23, with Sting's debut at the finish.

    2016

    Title vs. career

    Loses to Dean Ambrose for the WWE World Championship at SummerSlam, then puts his career on the line against The Miz at No Mercy on October 9 and wins the Intercontinental title.

    2018

    No. 30 and the sixth IC title

    Surprise No. 30 in the Royal Rumble, a sixth Intercontinental title from Seth Rollins on June 18, and Raw tag gold with Drew McIntyre on September 3.

    2019

    Rumble final three

    Lasts to the final three of the Rumble from No. 28, loses to Goldberg at SummerSlam, and wins the Raw tag titles with Robert Roode.

    2020

    WWE Championship challenge

    Loses to Otis at WrestleMania 36 and challenges Drew McIntyre for the WWE Championship at Extreme Rules on July 19.

    2022

    NXT Champion

    Wins the NXT Championship at Roadblock on March 8 and holds it 27 days, until Bron Breakker takes it back on Raw.

    2023

    Released

    Goes undrafted, then is released on September 21 after 19 years with WWE.

    2024

    Three promotions, three titles

    Frequently Asked

    Is Nic Nemeth the same wrestler as Dolph Ziggler?

    Yes. Dolph Ziggler was his WWE ring name from September 2008 until his release in September 2023. Nicholas Theodore Nemeth is his legal name, and he has wrestled as Nic Nemeth in NJPW, AAA and TNA since 2024. WWE billed him as Dolph Ziggler once more for a Raw match on November 17, 2025.

    Is Nic Nemeth still the TNA World Champion?

    No. He won the title for the second time at Slammiversary on June 28, 2026, and lost it to Leon Slater in the Bound for Glory main event on October 11, 2026, after 105 days. He is still a champion: he and his brother Ryan hold the TNA World Tag Team Championship, which they retained on the November 5, 2026 Impact.

    How many times has Nic Nemeth been TNA World Champion?

    Twice. The first reign began at Slammiversary on July 20, 2024 in Montreal, where he won a six-way elimination match, and ended at Genesis on January 19, 2025 against Joe Hendry (183 days). The second began on June 28, 2026 and ended on October 11, 2026 (105 days).

    How many world titles did Dolph Ziggler win in WWE?

    Two, both the World Heavyweight Championship. The first, in February 2011, lasted part of one SmackDown taping before Edge won it back. The second came from the Money in the Bank cash-in on April 8, 2013 and lasted 69 days. His 2022 NXT Championship is not classed as a world title by WWE. Counting his two TNA World titles, the total is four.

    What happened with Dolph Ziggler's 2013 Money in the Bank cash-in?

    He won the briefcase on July 15, 2012 in Phoenix and cashed it in on Alberto Del Rio on April 8, 2013, on the Raw after WrestleMania 29, to win the World Heavyweight Championship. He suffered a concussion soon after and lost the title back to Del Rio at Payback on June 16, 2013.

    How many times was Dolph Ziggler Intercontinental Champion?

    Six: 2010 (from Kofi Kingston), August 2014 (SummerSlam), September 2014 (Raw), December 2014 (TLC ladder match in Cleveland), October 2016 (No Mercy, title vs. career against The Miz) and June 2018 (from Seth Rollins).

    Was Dolph Ziggler in the Spirit Squad?

    Yes. He was Nicky, one of the five cheerleaders who debuted on Raw on January 23, 2006. The group won the World Tag Team Championship on April 3, 2006, with all five recognized as champions under the Freebird Rule, and disbanded in November 2006.

    Did Dolph Ziggler win the NXT Championship?

    Yes, at NXT Roadblock on March 8, 2022, in a triple threat with champion Bron Breakker and Tommaso Ciampa. He retained against Breakker at Stand & Deliver and lost to him on Raw on April 4, 2022, after 27 days.

    When did WWE release Dolph Ziggler?

    On September 21, 2023, after 19 years with the company. He had gone undrafted earlier that year. He then won titles in NJPW, AAA and TNA during 2024.

    Has Nic Nemeth gone back to WWE?

    Once. He returned as Dolph Ziggler on Raw on November 17, 2025, in John Cena's Last Time Is Now tournament, and lost to Solo Sikoa in the first round. He re-signed with TNA in January 2026 and told Slam Wrestling that WWE had asked him back several times and he had declined the other offers.

    Who is Ryan Nemeth?

    His younger brother, also a professional wrestler. Together they are The Nemeths, two-time TNA World Tag Team Champions: April 27 to July 20, 2025, and again since Lockdown on August 23, 2026.

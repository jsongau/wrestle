    Nic Nemeth

    TNA World Champion · Former WWE star

    Nic Nemeth is the reigning TNA World Champion, a title he won at Slammiversary 2026 on June 28, 2026. He spent years in WWE as Dolph Ziggler before his run in TNA.

    Biography

    Nic Nemeth spent years as a WWE main-roster fixture, where he competed as Dolph Ziggler, before moving to TNA and reaching the top of that promotion.

    At Slammiversary 2026 on June 28, 2026, he won the TNA World Championship, the reign he currently holds. The win puts him in a title lineage that runs through Kurt Angle, the inaugural TNA World Heavyweight Champion, along with AJ Styles, Samoa Joe, and Sting.

Trish Stratus

Trish Stratus (Patricia Stratigeas) is a seven-time WWE Women's Champion who elevated women's wrestling in the early 2000s. Her landmark rivalry with Lita produced the first women's match to main-event Monday Night Raw, making her a face of the era's women's division.

  • Real name Patricia Anne Stratigeas
  • From Toronto, Ontario, Canada
  • Debut 2000 (retired 2006; periodic returns)
  • Promotions WWF / WWE
  • Nicknames The Queen of Stratusfaction
  • Finishers Chick Kick, Stratusfaction

Career

A fitness model turned in-ring standout, Trish Stratus debuted in 2000 and rapidly developed into one of the most accomplished performers of the Attitude and Ruthless Aggression eras, winning seven WWE Women's Championships. She headlined feuds with Mickie James, Victoria, Molly Holly and Jazz.

Her defining program was with Lita: in December 2004 the two became the first women to main-event Monday Night Raw. Stratus retired in her hometown of Toronto after a farewell match against Lita at Unforgiven 2006, and has returned periodically as a legend.

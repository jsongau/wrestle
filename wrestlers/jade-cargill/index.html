    Jade Cargill

    Power athlete · Inaugural AEW TBS Champion

    Jade Cargill, the inaugural AEW TBS Champion, jumped to WWE in November 2023 and held the WWE Women's Championship in 2026 before losing it to Rhea Ripley.

    Biography

    Jade Cargill built her reputation as a power athlete in AEW, where she became the inaugural AEW TBS Champion and carried a long undefeated streak. That streak ended at Double or Nothing 2023, and later that year she signed with WWE, debuting in November 2023.

    In WWE she moved quickly into the main-event women's picture. She held the WWE Women's Championship in 2026 and lost it to Rhea Ripley at WrestleMania 42, the match that started Ripley's 2026 reign.

    She stayed at the top of the SmackDown division through the rest of 2026, facing Tiffany Stratton at Night of Champions 2026 in a match Charlotte Flair inserted herself into. Cargill and Stratton both qualified for the first-ever interim WWE Women's Championship ladder match at SummerSlam 2026, crowned while Ripley was out injured.

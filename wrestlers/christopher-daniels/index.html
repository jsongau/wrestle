Christopher Daniels

Christopher Daniels, "The Fallen Angel," is a former ROH World Champion and multi-time TNA X-Division and tag team champion who helped define TNA's golden era. A workhorse cult favorite, he formed the third pillar of TNA's greatest rivalry with AJ Styles and Samoa Joe before later taking a backstage role in AEW.

  • Real name Daniel Christopher Covell
  • Born March 24, 1970 · Kalamazoo, MI
  • Debut 1993
  • Promotions TNA, ROH, AEW
  • Nicknames The Fallen Angel, Curry Man
  • Finishers Angel's Wings, Best Moonsault Ever

Career

A tireless independent and international competitor from 1993, Daniels became a foundational star of TNA's X-Division, where his crisp technical style and the Angel's Wings finisher made him a fan favorite. He was central to the promotion's most acclaimed match — the three-way with AJ Styles and Samoa Joe at Unbreakable 2005.

His career-long rivalry and friendship with AJ Styles ran through TNA, and he collected multiple X-Division and TNA World Tag Team Championships, several alongside partner Frankie Kazarian as Bad Influence/The Addiction. His versatility even extended to the comedic "Curry Man" alter ego.

After years as a respected veteran, Daniels finally captured the ROH World Championship in 2017 in his mid-40s — an emotional milestone — before joining AEW both as a wrestler and in an executive/backstage capacity.

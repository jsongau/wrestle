Shinsuke Nakamura

Shinsuke Nakamura is "The King of Strong Style" — a three-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion and 2018 Royal Rumble winner. One of New Japan's biggest stars, he brought striking artistry and rock-star swagger to WWE, where he became a two-time NXT Champion.

  • Real name Shinsuke Nakamura
  • From Kyoto, Japan
  • Debut 2002 (WWE 2016)
  • Promotions NJPW, WWE / NXT
  • Nicknames King of Strong Style, The Artist, Swagsuke
  • Finishers Kinshasa, Landslide

Career

Debuting in 2002, Shinsuke Nakamura rose to become one of New Japan Pro-Wrestling's defining stars — a three-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion and the face of its Intercontinental division. His charismatic "King of Strong Style" persona made him a global attraction well before his 2016 move to WWE.

His WWE arrival began with an instant classic against Sami Zayn at NXT TakeOver: Dallas in 2016. He won the 2018 Royal Rumble and challenged fellow New Japan alum AJ Styles at WrestleMania 34, a headline crossover match. A two-time NXT Champion and two-time WWE Intercontinental Champion, he also feuded with Seth Rollins and Kevin Owens.

FAQ

Why is he called the King of Strong Style?
For his striking-based, hard-hitting Japanese wrestling blended with rock-star charisma, honed as a top star in New Japan.
Did Shinsuke Nakamura win the Royal Rumble?
Yes — the 2018 Royal Rumble, earning a WrestleMania 34 title match with AJ Styles.
What titles has he held?
Three-time IWGP Heavyweight Champion and multiple IWGP Intercontinental reigns in NJPW; two-time NXT Champion and two-time WWE Intercontinental Champion.

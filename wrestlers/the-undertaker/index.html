The Undertaker

The Undertaker (Mark Calaway) is a seven-time world champion and the author of the 21-match WrestleMania Streak — the most mythologized attraction in wrestling history. Across three decades he evolved from the supernatural "Deadman" to the biker "American Badass" and back, becoming the enduring conscience and locker-room leader of WWE.

  • Real name Mark William Calaway
  • Born March 24, 1965 · Houston, TX
  • Debut 1987 (WWF debut 1990)
  • Promotions WWF/WWE, WCW (early)
  • Nicknames The Deadman, The Phenom
  • Finishers Tombstone Piledriver, Chokeslam, Hell's Gate

Career

Debuting at Survivor Series 1990 as a silent, gloved mortician, The Undertaker became WWF's most durable character — a supernatural presence who could headline against any opponent in any era. He won his first WWF Championship from Hulk Hogan in 1991 and would hold world gold across four different decades, adapting the gimmick through the "Ministry of Darkness," the "American Badass" biker years, and a final return to the Deadman.

His defining legacy is the WrestleMania Streak: 21 straight victories on WWE's biggest stage from 1991 to 2013, a booking institution that turned every WrestleMania opponent into a potential legend. The Streak produced multiple all-time classics before Brock Lesnar ended it at WrestleMania XXX (2014), leaving the final tally at 25–2.

FAQ

What is The Undertaker's real name?
Mark William Calaway, born March 24, 1965, in Houston, Texas.
What was the WrestleMania Streak?
21 consecutive WrestleMania wins from 1991–2013. Brock Lesnar ended it at WrestleMania XXX (2014); the final record is 25–2.
How many world titles did he win?
Seven world championships — four WWF/WWE Championships and three World Heavyweight Championships — plus multiple tag titles.

The Undertaker

The Undertaker (Mark Calaway) is a seven-time world champion and the author of the 21–0 WrestleMania Streak — the most mythologized attraction in wrestling history, and a final WrestleMania record of 25–2. Across three decades he evolved from the supernatural "Deadman" to the biker "American Badass" and back, becoming the enduring conscience and locker-room leader of WWE.

Career timeline

Four gimmicks, one legend

  1. 1990–1993

    The Deadman arrives

    Debuts at Survivor Series 1990; wins his first WWF Title from Hulk Hogan in 1991 and starts the WrestleMania Streak.

  2. 1997–1999

    Lord of Darkness / The Ministry

    Originates Hell in a Cell vs Shawn Michaels; the Mankind Cell fall; the Ministry of Darkness and a third world title.

  3. 2000–2003

    American Badass

    Reinvents as a biker; Undisputed Championship in 2002; brawls with Triple H and the roster's best.

  4. 2004–2013

    The Deadman returns · Streak peak

    World titles over Batista, Edge and CM Punk; the WM25/26 classics; Streak reaches 21–0.

  5. 2014–2020

    The final chapters

    Brock Lesnar ends the Streak; the Boneyard Match with AJ Styles is his effective farewell. Hall of Fame 2022.

FAQ

Frequently asked

What is The Undertaker's WrestleMania record?
His final WrestleMania record is 25–2. He went 21–0 (the Streak) from WrestleMania VII (1991) through WrestleMania 29 (2013) before Brock Lesnar ended it at WrestleMania XXX (2014). His only WrestleMania losses are to Brock Lesnar and Roman Reigns.
How many world titles did he win?
Seven world championship reigns — four WWF/WWE Championships (1991, 1997, 1999, 2002) and three World Heavyweight Championships (2007, 2008, 2009) — plus multiple tag titles.
What is his real name and finisher?
Mark William Calaway, born March 24, 1965 in Houston, Texas. His signature finisher is the Tombstone Piledriver; he also used the Chokeslam, Last Ride and the Hell's Gate submission.
Is this his complete career record?
No. This is a curated ledger of his 28 most significant matches and moments. His full career spans thousands of matches over 30 years (1990–2020); wrestling outcomes are scripted (kayfabe).

Diamond Dallas Page

Diamond Dallas Page (DDP) is a three-time WCW World Champion and wrestling's greatest late bloomer — a self-made blue-collar underdog who debuted in his mid-30s and clawed his way to the top as the everyman foil to the nWo. His Diamond Cutter became one of the sport's most imitated finishers.

  • Real name Page Joseph Falkinburg Jr.
  • From New Jersey, USA
  • Debut / era in-ring c.1991 · WCW 1991–2001 · WWE 2001–2002
  • Promotions WCW, WWE, TNA
  • Nicknames DDP, "The People's Champion" (WCW), Self-High-Five
  • Finisher Diamond Cutter

Career

Starting his in-ring career remarkably late, in his mid-30s, DDP overcame the odds through sheer persistence, transforming from manager to WCW headliner. As a beloved self-made everyman, he became the perfect blue-collar foil to Hollywood Hogan and the nWo's Outsiders, winning three WCW World Championships.

His signature feuds included a classic WCW title program with Randy Savage and a title win over Goldberg, while his Diamond Cutter — a "self-high-five" preceding the strike — became one of the most copied finishes ever. He later jumped to WWE for a run that included a memorable stalking angle with The Undertaker.

Post-career, DDP built the DDP Yoga fitness empire, credited with helping revive the health of Jake Roberts and others.

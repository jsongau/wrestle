Brock Lesnar

Brock Lesnar (The Beast Incarnate) is a multiple-time WWE world champion, a UFC Heavyweight Champion and the man who ended The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak. A once-in-a-generation athlete, Lesnar became WWE's youngest champion in 2002, crossed over to win the UFC Heavyweight title, then returned as an unstoppable force whose long Universal Championship reign redefined part-time dominance.

  • Real name Brock Edward Lesnar
  • Born July 12, 1977 · Webster, SD
  • Debut WWE main roster 2002
  • Promotions WWE, UFC (MMA), NJPW
  • Nicknames The Beast Incarnate, The Next Big Thing
  • Finishers F-5, Kimura Lock

Career

A dominant NCAA amateur wrestling champion, Lesnar arrived on WWE television in 2002 and, guided by advocate Paul Heyman, became the youngest WWE Champion in company history at just 25. His freakish blend of size and speed made him an instant main-event attraction, feuding with Kurt Angle, The Rock and The Big Show before winning the 2003 Royal Rumble.

After a stint in the NFL and a legitimate run to the UFC Heavyweight Championship, Lesnar returned to WWE in 2012 as "The Beast Incarnate." He conquered John Cena at SummerSlam 2014 and, in the defining moment of his second run, ended The Undertaker's 21-0 WrestleMania Streak at WrestleMania XXX (2014).

Lesnar's later years were built around blockbuster attractions — a record-length Universal Championship reign, multiple WrestleMania collisions with Roman Reigns, and rematches with Goldberg — cementing his status as WWE's biggest "special attraction" of the modern era.

FAQ

What is Brock Lesnar's real name?
Brock Edward Lesnar, born July 12, 1977, in Webster, South Dakota.
Did Brock Lesnar end The Undertaker's WrestleMania Streak?
Yes — he ended the 21-match Streak at WrestleMania XXX in 2014, one of the most shocking results in WWE history.
Was Brock Lesnar a UFC champion?
Yes. He won the UFC Heavyweight Championship, a rare crossover who held world titles in both pro wrestling and MMA.

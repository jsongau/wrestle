Owen Hart

Owen Hart — the King of Harts — was one of the finest in-ring performers of the 1990s, whose career was cut tragically short on May 23, 1999. The younger Hart brother was technically gifted, deeply funny on the mic, and capable of working any style. His WrestleMania X opener against Bret Hart remains one of the finest openers in WrestleMania history, and his legacy endures as one of wrestling's great unfinished stories.

How they win

The enzuigiri and a dozen technical tricks

Owen Hart won with the Sharpshooter (a family trademark), the enzuigiri spinning heel kick, and pure technical wrestling. He was versatile enough to work a five-star opener or a comedic mid-card feud — his range was unmatched among his contemporaries.

WM X opener

The Hart brothers' WrestleMania X match was unannounced as an opener and still rated among the finest WM matches ever — it set the standard for modern opener psychology.

Owen Hart's life ended in a tragic accident at age 34 during WWF Over the Edge. His legacy as a performer and person endures.

Hart Dungeon

Trained by his father Stu Hart in the Hart Dungeon alongside Bret, Davey Boy, Chris Benoit and dozens of others — one of wrestling's greatest training institutions.

Career timeline

The arc of a legend

  1. 1986–1987

    Stampede & the Blue Blazer

    Trained in the Hart Dungeon; debuted the Blue Blazer masked superhero character in Stampede Wrestling and briefly in WWF before working the international circuit.

  2. 1988–1993

    Hart Foundation tag era

    Worked as part of the Hart Foundation tag team; multiple appearances in WWF working up the card as one of the company's most reliable performers.

  3. 1994

    WrestleMania X — the family feud

    Turned heel on his brother Bret; their WrestleMania X opener became an instant classic. Won the IC title at King of the Ring 1994.

  4. 1994–1997

    The King of Harts

    Built a singles career as the cocky younger Hart, the King of Harts — IC and European titles; tag reigns with Yokozuna, Davey Boy, and Jeff Jarrett.

  5. 1997–1999

    Hart Foundation & the Blue Blazer

    Core member of the reformed Hart Foundation; the piledriver incident with Austin at SummerSlam 1997; returned to the Blue Blazer persona in 1998.

  6. May 23, 1999

    A tragic end

    Owen Hart's life ended during WWF Over the Edge at Kemper Arena. He was 34 years old. The wrestling world lost one of its most gifted performers.

FAQ

How did Owen Hart die?
In a tragic accident during WWE Over the Edge on May 23, 1999. A harness lowering him as the Blue Blazer failed. He was 34.
What was Owen Hart's best match?
The WrestleMania X opener vs Bret Hart (1994) — unannounced, unexpected, and universally acclaimed as one of the finest WrestleMania matches ever.
Did Owen Hart ever win the WWF Championship?
No — and that remains one of the biggest booking mysteries in WWE history. He was world-title caliber throughout his career but never won the top prize.

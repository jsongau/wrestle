    Kenny Omega

    Ex-Bullet Club leader · AEW World Champion

    Kenny Omega led Bullet Club in NJPW, won IWGP Heavyweight gold and the G1 Climax, and is a two-time AEW World Champion set to defend against Will Ospreay at All In 2026.

    Biography

    Kenny Omega took control of Bullet Club in New Japan Pro-Wrestling in January 2016, turning on AJ Styles. Styles had succeeded founder Prince Devitt, better known now as Finn Balor, as Bullet Club leader in 2014.

    In NJPW, Omega became IWGP Heavyweight Champion and won the G1 Climax, the promotion's marquee summer tournament. That run made him one of the most prominent foreign stars in New Japan history.

    Omega is now a two-time AEW World Champion. He beat MJF at Beach Break 2026 for his second reign and is set to defend the title against Will Ospreay at All In 2026. He shares NJPW and AEW history with Jon Moxley, a former IWGP United States Champion.

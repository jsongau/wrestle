CM Punk

CM Punk, "The Best in the World," is a multiple-time WWE and AEW World Champion whose 434-day WWE title reign and 2011 "pipe bomb" reshaped modern wrestling. An anti-establishment icon from the independents and ROH, he became the voice of a disillusioned fanbase before sensational exits and an unlikely 2023 WWE return.

  • Real name Phillip Jack Brooks
  • Born October 26, 1978 · Chicago, IL
  • Debut 1999 (WWE 2005)
  • Promotions ROH, WWE, AEW (UFC crossover)
  • Nicknames Best in the World, Straight Edge Superstar
  • Finishers GTS (Go to Sleep), Anaconda Vise

Career

Punk built his reputation on the independent scene and in Ring of Honor, where his intense promos and technical skill made him a cult favorite — including a landmark 2004 feud with Samoa Joe. He signed with WWE in 2005, winning the ECW Championship and cashing in Money in the Bank as the straight-edge crusader.

His defining moment came in June 2011, when a shoot-style "pipe bomb" promo set up a hometown classic against John Cena at Money in the Bank in Chicago. Punk then held the WWE Championship for 434 days — the longest reign of the modern era — while feuding with Chris Jericho and The Undertaker.

After an acrimonious 2014 WWE departure and a UFC crossover, Punk resurfaced in AEW as a two-time World Champion before another explosive exit. He then stunned the industry by returning to WWE in 2023 and later capturing the World Heavyweight Championship.

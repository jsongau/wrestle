    01

    Overview

    CM Punk is the most important anti-establishment act in professional wrestling since Steve Austin. He broke in on the Chicago independents and IWA Mid-South in 2000, then made his name in Ring of Honor, where a straight edge gimmick, the Samoa Joe trilogy and the year-long “Summer of Punk” storyline turned him into ROH World Champion and the most quotable talker of his generation.

    JUNE 27, 2011the “pipe bomb” — the most-watched WWE promo of the YouTube eraHe arrived in WWE in 2006 and spent five years being pushed and misused in roughly equal measure. That changed on June 27, 2011. Sitting cross-legged on the stage with his contract set to expire, Punk aired real grievances at WWE management in the “pipe bomb” promo. It became the most-watched WWE promo of the YouTube era and the moment that reframed his entire career, casting him as the genuine alternative to corporate WWE.

    434days as WWE Champion — the longest reign of the modern eraThe reign that followed ran 434 days, the longest WWE Championship run of the modern era. It carried the strongest run of title defenses WWE had booked in fifteen years, with standout matches against John Cena, Chris Jericho and Daniel Bryan, and, at WrestleMania 29, a Paul Bearer tribute classic with The Undertaker that ranks among the Streak’s best nights.

    12world championships held across ROH, ECW, WWE and AEWPunk walked out of WWE the night after the 2014 Royal Rumble, fought twice in the UFC, and returned to wrestling through AEW in 2021 to a Chicago rapture. His 2023 WWE homecoming closed the biggest loop in modern wrestling. Across ROH, ECW, WWE and AEW he has held twelve world championships, and he currently holds the Undisputed WWE Championship at the top of the card.

    Frequently Asked

    What is CM Punk’s real name?

    Phillip Jack Brooks, born October 26, 1978. He is billed from Chicago, Illinois.

    How long was CM Punk’s WWE Championship reign?

    434 days, from November 20, 2011 to January 27, 2013, the longest WWE Championship reign of the modern era.

    What is the pipe bomb?

    His worked-shoot promo on the June 27, 2011 episode of Raw, sitting on the stage airing real grievances at WWE, widely seen as one of the most influential promos in wrestling history.

    Did CM Punk fight in the UFC?

    Yes. He lost to Mickey Gall at UFC 203 in 2016; his UFC 225 bout with Mike Jackson in 2018 was later ruled a No Contest.

    Why did CM Punk leave WWE in 2014?

    He walked out the night after the 2014 Royal Rumble over creative and medical grievances, and did not return to WWE until 2023.

    Where does CM Punk wrestle now?

    He returned to WWE in November 2023 and holds the Undisputed WWE Championship at the top of the current card.

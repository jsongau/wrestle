    Edge and Christian

    1998 to 2001 WWF / WWE Edge & Christian

    Edge and Christian are real-life best friends since childhood who ran as a regular WWF/WWE team from 1998 to 2001. As the comedic-heel foil in the Attitude Era tag trilogy, they turned smug charisma and ladder-match nerve into multiple tag title reigns before both broke out as singles main-eventers.

    Signature

    Their act ran on the five-second pose, the con-chair-to and a fearlessness on the ladder. Their wars with the Hardy Boyz and the Dudley Boyz built the TLC match into a WWE staple.

    The partnership was a launchpad. Edge became a multi-time world champion and a WWE Hall of Famer, and Christian won world titles of his own, both careers rooted in the timing they sharpened as a duo.

    FAQ

    Are Edge and Christian friends in real life?
    Yes. Edge and Christian have been real-life best friends since childhood, which they carried into their tag team run in the WWF/WWE from 1998 to 2001.
    What was Edge and Christian's signature bit?
    The five-second pose and the con-chair-to. They were ladder-match specialists who won the first Tag Team Ladder match and the first TLC match.

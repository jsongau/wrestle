    The New Day

    2014 to 2025 WWE Kingston, Woods & Big E

    Kofi Kingston, Xavier Woods and Big E debuted The New Day in November 2014 and ran as a trio through the 2010s and early 2020s. Unicorn horns, the Booty-O's cereal gimmick and relentless positivity turned them into one of the most merchandised acts of the decade.

    The New Day's all-time tag title count varies by source, so this page lists the verified 483-day reign rather than a single all-time number.

    Signature

    The act ran on crowd participation, Woods on the trombone and a merchandise machine built around Booty-O's. The group also fed Kingston's singles breakout: his 2019 WrestleMania 35 WWE Championship win, nicknamed KofiMania, grew directly out of the team's momentum.

    Kingston and Woods were released by WWE on May 3, 2026, and Big E had already stepped away from the ring after a 2022 neck injury. This page reads as a retrospective on a full-time run that has ended.

    FAQ

    Who are the members of The New Day?
    Kofi Kingston, Xavier Woods and Big E. The trio debuted in November 2014 and ran through the 2010s and early 2020s in WWE.
    What happened to The New Day?
    Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods were released by WWE on May 3, 2026. Big E had already stopped in-ring competition after a 2022 neck injury, so the full-time New Day run is over.

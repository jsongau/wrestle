    Legion of Doom

    Formed 1983 AWA, NWA/WCW, WWF, NJPW Hawk & Animal

    Road Warrior Hawk and Road Warrior Animal, managed by Paul Ellering, were the power template almost every hoss tag team that followed copied. Known as both the Road Warriors and the Legion of Doom, they drew across the AWA, the NWA and Jim Crockett territory, WCW, the WWF and Japan from 1983 into the late 1990s, with a WWF reunion in 2003.

    Signature

    The finish was the Doomsday Device: Animal carried an opponent on his shoulders while Hawk came off the top rope with a clothesline. The face paint, spikes and sheer intimidation made them one of the most influential power teams in the sport.

    Their standing as the first team to hold the AWA, NWA and WWF World Tag Team Championships marks them as one of the great cross-promotional acts of their era.

    FAQ

    Who were the Road Warriors?
    Road Warrior Hawk and Road Warrior Animal, managed by Paul Ellering. Also known as Legion of Doom, they were a face-paint power team formed in 1983 who worked the AWA, NWA/WCW, WWF and Japan.
    What was the Legion of Doom's finishing move?
    The Doomsday Device, in which Animal held an opponent on his shoulders for a flying clothesline from Hawk off the top rope.

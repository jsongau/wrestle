    The Usos

    Debuted 2010 WWE Jimmy Uso & Jey Uso

    Jimmy Uso and Jey Uso reached the main roster in 2010 and anchored the SmackDown and Raw tag divisions through 2023. Sons of Rikishi and part of the Anoa'i family, they became the most decorated modern WWE tag team and the muscle of The Bloodline.

    Signature

    The Usos wrestled a fast, hard-hitting tandem style capped by the double superkick and the Uso Splash from the top rope. Their 622-day reign, running from July 18, 2021 to WrestleMania 39 Night 1 on April 1, 2023, is the longest in WWE tag team title history.

    Their story fed the wider Bloodline saga. Jey Uso's 2023 to 2025 split from the group, alongside cousin Roman Reigns and Sami Zayn, launched a breakout singles run. Both brothers now work heavily as singles acts.

    FAQ

    How long were the Usos tag team champions?
    The Usos held the tag titles for 622 days, from July 18, 2021 to April 1, 2023 at WrestleMania 39 Night 1, the longest reign in WWE tag team title history.
    Are the Usos related to Roman Reigns?
    Yes. Jimmy and Jey Uso are sons of Rikishi, members of the Anoa'i family, and cousins of Roman Reigns. They were the core of The Bloodline.

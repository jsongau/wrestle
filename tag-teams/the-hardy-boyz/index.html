    The Hardy Boyz

    1998 to present WWE, TNA, ROH, AEW Matt Hardy & Jeff Hardy

    Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy are real-life brothers who turned high-risk ladder spots into a main-event draw. Debuting as a regular WWF team in 1998, they reunited on and off through the 2010s and 2020s across the WWF/WWE, TNA/IMPACT, Ring of Honor and AEW.

    Signature

    The team's calling card was Poetry in Motion, with one brother launching off the other into a cornered opponent, and Jeff's Swanton Bomb delivered from ladders and the tops of cages. Their ladder and TLC matches against Edge and Christian and the Dudley Boyz built the Tables, Ladders and Chairs concept that WWE still runs today.

    The Hardyz helped define an era where the tag division could headline. Both brothers went on to singles runs and world-title reigns, then kept returning to the team fans grew up on.

    FAQ

    Are the Hardy Boyz real brothers?
    Yes. Matt Hardy and Jeff Hardy are real-life brothers from Cameron, North Carolina, who broke through as a regular WWF team in 1998.
    What match are the Hardy Boyz known for?
    The Hardy Boyz are known for the ladder and TLC matches against Edge and Christian and the Dudley Boyz. The three teams built the Tables, Ladders and Chairs concept together.

    Tag Teams

    The duos that carried the tag division across the WWF, WCW, ECW, New Japan and AEW, from face-paint power teams to the ladder-match innovators of the Attitude Era.

    These eight teams anchor the Wrestle Lore tag division. The Hardy Boyz, Dudley Boyz and Edge and Christian built the TLC match between them. The New Day and the Usos rewrote the modern title-reign records. The Hart Foundation and Legion of Doom set the template decades earlier, and the Young Bucks carried the independent scene into AEW. For the larger groups, browse the WWE and AEW rosters.

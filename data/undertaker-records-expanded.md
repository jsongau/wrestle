> **Note on overall PPV standing:** Across a ~30-year career (1990–2020) The Undertaker competed on well over 150 pay-per-view / premium live events. There is no single "official" complete PPV win–loss record, and figures vary by source depending on how tag matches, multi-man matches, and no-contests are counted. He finished with a strongly positive overall PPV record. The PPV list below is a **curated selection of his most significant non-WrestleMania matches**, not a complete PPV record.

# The British Bulldog (Davey Boy Smith)

Status as of November 9, 2026: died May 18, 2002, aged 39. WWE Hall of Fame, Class of 2020. No activity is attributed to him beyond legacy. [S1][S2][S27]

## Canonical identity
- Birth name: David Smith. [S1]
- Born: November 27, 1962, Golborne, Lancashire, England. [S1]
- Died: May 18, 2002, aged 39, of a heart attack while on vacation in British Columbia, Canada. [S1][S30][S32]
  - ⚑ Place of death differs by source: Invermere, BC [S1][S32]; Fairmont Hot Springs / "Fairmont Springs", BC [S30][S31]; one UK outlet says "Redding, British Columbia" [S33]. Invermere and Fairmont Hot Springs are neighboring Columbia Valley communities. The page says "British Columbia" and flags the town.
  - Cause: heart attack [S1][S30][S32]. Wikipedia reports a forensic finding of natural causes linked to an enlarged heart [S1]. ESPN The Magazine (2002) reported investigations into underlying causes were ongoing and that WWE disputed steroid involvement [S30]. The page states "heart attack" only.
- Billed: 5 ft 11 in, 260 lb, from Manchester, England (WWE.com, Fox Sports) [S2][S3]. Wikipedia lists billings from both Leeds and Manchester [S1]. ⚑ The old Wrestle Lore page listed Leeds as hometown and 254 lb; neither matches the WWE.com billing.
- Ring names: Young David, Davey Boy Smith, The British Bulldog. [S1]
- Trained by: Ted Betley; then Stu Hart (with Roy Wood) in the Hart family Dungeon. [S1][S29]
- Debut: 1978 on British television as Young David [S1]. Wrestling Heritage dates his TV debut to September 1978, about age 15, a draw with Steve Wright [S29]. ⚑ Wikipedia names a different first opponent (Bernie Wright) [S1].
- Finisher: running powerslam. [S1][S2]
- Entrance theme: a "Rule, Britannia!" remix in his early WWF run; rock music with barking from 1999 [S1]. No verifiable track title, composer or streaming link was found, so the walk-out theme band is omitted.
- Family: married Diana Hart in October 1984, divorced January 2000; son Harry (born August 2, 1985, Calgary), daughter Georgia (born September 26, 1987, Calgary) [S1]. Cousin of Tom Billington, the Dynamite Kid [S1][S38]. Brother-in-law of Bret Hart and Owen Hart through Diana [S8].

## Nicknames
The British Bulldog · Davey Boy · Young David [S1]

## Championships and accolades
- WWF World Tag Team Championship, 2 reigns [S1][S5]
  - With Dynamite Kid: won April 7, 1986, WrestleMania 2, Rosemont, IL, from the Dream Team (Greg Valentine & Brutus Beefcake); 294 days; lost to the Hart Foundation (Bret Hart & Jim Neidhart) January 26, 1987, Superstars taping, Tampa [S5][S9][S4]
  - With Owen Hart: won September 22, 1996, In Your House: Mind Games, Philadelphia, from the Smoking Gunns; 246 days; lost May 26, 1997, Raw is War, Evansville, to Steve Austin & Shawn Michaels [S5][S17]
- WWF Intercontinental Championship, 1 reign: won August 29, 1992, SummerSlam, Wembley Stadium, from Bret Hart; lost to Shawn Michaels at Saturday Night's Main Event XXXI, taped October 27, 1992 in Terre Haute, aired November 14 (59 days; WWE recognizes 58) [S7][S8][S1]
  - ⚑ The old page claimed 2 IC reigns (1992, 1997). Only one exists [S1][S7].
- WWF European Championship, 2 reigns, inaugural champion [S1][S6]
  - Won the tournament final from Owen Hart, February 26, 1997, Berlin (aired March 3, 1997 on Raw); 206 days, the longest reign in the title's history; lost to Shawn Michaels September 20, 1997, One Night Only, Birmingham, England [S6][S43][S20]
  - Won from D'Lo Brown October 26, 1999, SmackDown taping, Springfield, MA (WWE recognizes October 28, the air date); 47 days; lost to Val Venis in a triple threat with D'Lo Brown at Armageddon, December 12, 1999 [S6][S25]
  - ⚑ The old page claimed 3 European reigns. There were 2 [S1][S6].
- WWF Hardcore Championship, 2 reigns: won September 9, 1999 from Big Boss Man and forfeited it the same night; won May 6, 2000 at Insurrextion, London, from Crash Holly, who regained it May 11, 2000 [S1][S26]
- Stampede Wrestling: British Commonwealth Mid-Heavyweight (1; won July 9, 1982 from Dynamite Kid), World Mid-Heavyweight (1), North American Heavyweight (2), International Tag Team (2 with Bruce Hart, 2 with Dynamite Kid); Stampede Wrestling Hall of Fame 1995 [S1]
- WWE Hall of Fame, Class of 2020; announced March 12, 2020; ceremony postponed by the pandemic and held with the Class of 2021, broadcast April 6, 2021; inducted by Harry Smith, Georgia Smith and Diana Hart-Smith [S27][S28]
- Pro Wrestling Illustrated Match of the Year 1992 (vs Bret Hart, SummerSlam) [S1][S8]
- Wrestling Observer: Tag Team of the Year 1985 (with Dynamite Kid); Feud of the Year 1997 (Hart Foundation vs Steve Austin) [S1]
- Canadian Pro-Wrestling Hall of Fame; Canadian Wrestling Hall of Fame [S1]
- ⚑ The old page claimed WCW World Tag Team Championship reigns with Sting and a WWF tag title with Lex Luger. No source supports either. He never held a WCW title; with Luger (the Allied Powers) he lost his only tag title shot at In Your House 2 [S1]. The site's /wrestlers/lex-luger/ page repeats the false Luger tag title claim.

## Character eras
- Young David (1978–81): teenage British TV wrestler, briefly a Big Daddy tag partner [S29]
- The British Bulldogs (1983–88/90): with Dynamite Kid in Stampede, New Japan, All Japan and the WWF; mascot Matilda the bulldog [S4]
- The British Bulldog, singles (1990–92): WWF return October 1990, Warlord feud, Wembley [S1]
- Return and the Hart family wars (1994–95): back at SummerSlam 1994, the Allied Powers with Lex Luger [S1][S39]
- Camp Cornette heel (1995–96): turns on Diesel in August 1995, joins Jim Cornette with Owen Hart and Yokozuna, WWF title shots [S1][S18]
- Hart Foundation (1997): reunited with Bret Hart, Owen Hart, Jim Neidhart and Brian Pillman; heroes in Canada and Europe, villains in the US [S18][S19]
- WCW (1993; 1997–98) [S1]
- 1999–2000 WWF return: jeans-clad heel, Triple H's ally at Unforgiven 1999 [S1][S23]

## Career timeline
- 1978: TV debut as Young David [S1][S29]
- 1979: televised win over British welterweight champion Jim Breaks; disallowed title win December 29 [S29]
- 1981: leaves for Calgary and Stampede Wrestling [S29][S30]
- 1982: wins the Stampede British Commonwealth Mid-Heavyweight title from Dynamite Kid, July 9 [S1]
- 1983–84: The British Bulldogs form; first Stampede International tag title March 1984; WWF after McMahon buys Stampede (1984) [S4]
- October 1984: marries Diana Hart [S1]
- 1986: WWF Tag Team Champions at WrestleMania 2, April 7 [S9]
- December 1986: Dynamite Kid's serious back injury in Hamilton, Ontario [S38]
- 1987: lose the titles to the Hart Foundation January 26; WrestleMania III loss March 29 [S5][S10]
- 1988: Survivor Series November 24, last PPV as a team; leave WWF after altercations with the Rougeaus [S4]
- 1989–90: team splits in Stampede May 5, 1989; last team match January 28, 1990 in All Japan [S4]
- 1990: WWF return, house show October 6 vs Haku; TV October 27 [S1]
- 1991: beats The Warlord at WrestleMania VII, March 24 [S11]
- 1992: Royal Rumble entrant No. 1, January 19, lasts 23:33; SummerSlam at Wembley August 29; drops IC title October 27 (aired November 14); released [S1][S7][S8]
- 1993: WCW debut at SuperBrawl III February 21; Slamboree vs Vader May 23; released December 1993 [S1]
- 1994: WWF return at SummerSlam August 29 [S1][S39]
- 1995: Royal Rumble runner-up from No. 2, January 22; WrestleMania XI win with Luger April 2; heel turn on Diesel August; In Your House 4 vs Diesel October 22; In Your House 5 vs Bret Hart December 17 [S12][S13][S1][S14]
- 1996: WrestleMania XII six-man win March 31; Shawn Michaels title matches May 26 and June 23; tag titles with Owen September 22 [S18][S15][S16][S17]
- 1997: first European Champion February 26; Hart Foundation reforms (March 31 Raw); Canadian Stampede July 6; loses European title at One Night Only September 20; sole survivor at Survivor Series in Montreal November 9; leaves for WCW [S6][S18][S19][S20][S21][S1]
- 1998: WCW knee injury April; Fall Brawl trapdoor back injury September 13; spinal infection, about six months hospitalized; WCW terminates contract [S1][S22]
- 1999: WWF return September; Unforgiven six-pack September 26; Rebellion win October 2; European title October 26; loses it December 12 [S1][S23][S24][S6][S25]
- 2000: divorce finalized January; Hardcore title May 6 in London; leaves WWF May 15 [S1][S26]
- May 11, 2002: six-man win in Winnipeg with son Harry and Zack Mercury, pinning an opponent with the powerslam [S33]
- May 18, 2002: dies of a heart attack in British Columbia, aged 39 [S1][S30][S32]
- 2020: WWE Hall of Fame announced March 12; ceremony broadcast April 6, 2021 [S27][S28]
- 2024: A&E Biography: WWE Legends episode, March 24 [S40]
- 2026: Harry Smith's gear bag stolen in Canada; his father's Hall of Fame ring recovered after an attempted pawn (PWInsider, February 26) [S35]

## Rivalries
- Bret Hart: brother-in-law; Wembley 1992, In Your House 5 (1995), then allies in 1997 [S8][S14][S18]
- Shawn Michaels: took the IC title (1992) and European title (1997) from him; three 1996 WWF title matches; beat him at the 1995 Rumble [S7][S12][S15][S16][S20]
- Owen Hart: partner, then the European final opponent, then partner again in the Hart Foundation [S17][S18]
- Dynamite Kid: cousin and partner; later estranged, including a dispute over the "British Bulldog" name [S38]

## Outside the ring
- Biography: WWE Legends, "British Bulldog," A&E, March 24, 2024 [S40]
- WWE 2K22: playable legend via the Clowning Around Pack DLC [S41]; also on WWE 2K16 and 2K19 rosters per The SmackDown Hotel
- "Davey Boy" feature documentary announced by The City Talking Studios, Leeds; in development, no release date found [S42]
- Son Harry Smith (Davey Boy Smith Jr.): WWE as DH Smith / David Hart Smith 2007–2011, Hart Dynasty with Tyson Kidd, WWE Tag Team and Unified Tag titles 2010; IWGP tag champion three times with Lance Archer; AJPW Triple Crown Champion November 4 – December 31, 2024; acquired the "British Bulldog" trademark in 2015 [S34]. Active in Canada in 2026 (CTV, February 23) [S37]; June 29, 2026 Fightful interviews on lessons from his father [S36].
- Niece Natalya is Harry's maternal cousin [S34]

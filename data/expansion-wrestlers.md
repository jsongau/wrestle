## 42. Jeff Hardy
- **Real name:** Jeffrey Nero Hardy
- **Nationality:** American (Cameron, North Carolina)
- **Debut / era active:** Debuted 1993; WWF main roster 1998; peak 1999–2009 (WWE) and 2010–2013 (TNA)
- **Primary promotions:** WWF/WWE, TNA/Impact, AEW
- **Major championships:** WWE Championship (1x, 2008–09); World Heavyweight (2x, 2009); WWE Intercontinental (4x); Light Heavyweight; record-tying WWE Tag Team reigns (Hardy Boyz); TNA World Heavyweight (2x, 2010–2013)
- **Finishing moves:** Swanton Bomb; Twist of Fate
- **Nicknames:** "The Charismatic Enigma," "The Enigmatic One"
- **Defining feuds:** CM Punk (2009); Edge; Triple H; Randy Orton; Undertaker; Sting (TNA); AJ Styles
- **Signature matches:** TLC and ladder wars vs Edge & Christian and the Dudleys (2000–01); Ladder Match vs Undertaker, Raw 2002; vs CM Punk, SummerSlam 2009
- **Real-life links:** Brother and tag partner of Matt Hardy (The Hardy Boyz); part of Team Xtreme with Lita; involved in the infamous 2011 TNA Victory Road main event with Sting.
- **Cross-promotion jumps:** WWE → TNA (2004, 2010) → WWE (2017) → AEW (2022, with Matt)
- **Bio:** A daredevil high-flyer beloved for ladder-match bumps and a face-painted rock-star persona, Jeff Hardy grew from tag specialist into a WWE and World Champion by the late 2000s. His TNA run made him a top singles draw despite well-documented personal struggles, and he later reunited with brother Matt in AEW.
- **SEO keywords:** Jeff Hardy, Charismatic Enigma, Swanton Bomb, Hardy Boyz, TNA World Champion, ladder match
- **slug:** jeff-hardy

## Notes on scope & accuracy
- This file extends `wrestlers.md`; the two together profile roughly 86 entries. Championship counts and reign lengths reflect widely recognized public records through mid-2026 and can vary slightly by source (title unifications, WWF→WWE renaming, WCW/NWA/TNA lineage changes).

- Tag teams (The Usos, Motor City Machine Guns) and pure managers (Paul Heyman, Bobby Heenan, Jim Cornette) are profiled where their significance to the era and the relationship graph warrants it.
- "Cross-promotion jumps" trace the most historically significant moves, not every appearance. Relationship edges emphasize connections between wrestlers across both files.

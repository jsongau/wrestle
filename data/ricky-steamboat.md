# Ricky Steamboat: The Dragon

Rules for this file: every claim carries a URL. ⚑ marks a disputed, conflicting or estimated figure. Harvested claims from the old page (wrestlers/ricky-steamboat/index.html, "X" variant) were checked one by one; the errors found are listed at the bottom.

## Canonical identity
- Real name: Richard Henry Blood. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Born: February 28, 1953, West Point, New York; Japanese American mother, white father. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Age on 2026-11-09: 73 (computed from birth date).
- High school: Boca Ciega High School, Gulfport, Florida (class of 1971); amateur wrestler, Florida state champion. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Billed from: Honolulu, Hawaii (WWE.com and Wikipedia); also billed from Charlotte, North Carolina (Wikipedia). https://www.wwe.com/superstars/rickysteamboat
- Height ⚑: 6 ft 0 in billed (Wikipedia infobox); WWE.com lists 5 ft 10 in. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat · https://www.wwe.com/superstars/rickysteamboat
- Weight: 235 lb (both sources). https://www.wwe.com/superstars/rickysteamboat
- Trained by: Verne Gagne and The Iron Sheik. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Debut ⚑: Wikipedia's infobox says February 15, 1976; the body says March 1976 in the AWA as "Rick Blood". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Ring names: Rick Blood, Sam Steamboat Jr., Ricky Steamboat, The Dragon. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Name origin: Eddie Graham gave him "Ricky Steamboat" for his resemblance to Sammy Steamboat and pitched him as Sammy's nephew. They are not related. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat · https://www.wrestlinginc.com/1043997/ricky-steamboats-first-pro-wrestling-championship-came-by-defeating-ric-flair
- Signature move: flying crossbody (WWE.com lists "Crossbody"). Other finishes documented below: small package, double chickenwing, double thrust off the top rope. https://www.wwe.com/superstars/rickysteamboat
- Family: older brother of Vic Steamboat; son Richard Jr. (born 1987) wrestled as Richie Steamboat. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- Entrance theme: NOT VERIFIED. WWE.com and Wikipedia name none. Walk-out band omitted.

## Career timeline
- 1976: debut in the AWA as Rick Blood; to Florida in May 1976. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- 1977: joins JCP; June 15 wins Mid-Atlantic TV title from Flair; first US title from Flair late 1977 (⚑ date). Sources above.
- 1978: World tag with Paul Jones (April 23); Jones turns on him at the Charlotte battle royal December 3; US title from Flair in the hair-vs-title cage match (⚑ Dec 17 Toronto / Dec 30 Greensboro). https://midatlanticwrestling.net/almanac/history/1978_12.htm
- 1979–83: five World tag reigns with Jay Youngblood; Starrcade '83 win over the Briscos; announces retirement, titles vacated December 25, 1983. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions
- 1984: back in action; third US title April 21, 1984. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_United_States_Heavyweight_Champions
- 1985: WWF; WrestleMania I win over Matt Borne (March 31, MSG, crossbody, 4:39). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_I
- 1986: WrestleMania 2 LA portion, beats Hercules Hernandez (April 7, 7:27). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_2 · Jake Roberts DDT on the concrete (tied to the cancelled May 3 SNME), Snake Pit win at The Big Event, Toronto, August 28 (10:17, 61,470 paid). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Big_Event · Savage crushes his larynx with the ring bell (late 1986). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_III
- 1987: WrestleMania III, March 29, Pontiac Silverdome: beats Savage for the IC title, small package reversing a scoop slam, 14:35, George Steele in his corner; first IC title change at a WrestleMania; PWI and Observer MOTY. Attendance ⚑ 93,173 announced, ~78,000 per retrospective analyses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_III · Drops the title to Honky Tonk Man (June 2 taping). Time off for his son's birth. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- 1988: loses to Greg Valentine in the WrestleMania IV tournament first round (March 27, 9:12); leaves WWF. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_IV
- 1989: returns to NWA January 21 as Eddie Gilbert's partner, pins Flair; NWA World title at Chi-Town Rumble (Feb 20, UIC Pavilion, 23:18, small package, Teddy Long replacement referee); keeps it in the 6-star Landover house show (March 18); Clash VI 2-of-3 falls (April 2, Superdome, falls at 19:33 Flair, 34:54 Steamboat by chickenwing submission, 55:49 Steamboat, Flair's foot under the rope on the final pin); loses at WrestleWar (May 7, Nashville, judges Terry Funk, Pat O'Connor, Lou Thesz; inside cradle after his knee gave out). Luger turns on him at Clash VII (June 14); Great American Bash US title loss to Luger by DQ (July 23, Baltimore). - 1990: foot surgery; NAWA/SAPW; NJPW tour September–October 1990. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat
- 1991: WWF return as The Dragon (vignettes February, TV from March 30); leaves before Survivor Series; WCW debut November 19 at Clash XVII as surprise partner, wins World tag with Dustin Rhodes. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricky_Steamboat · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions
- 1992: SuperBrawl II US title loss to Rick Rude (Feb 29, Milwaukee, Paul E. Dangerously as a ninja hits him with a phone). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SuperBrawl_II · WarGames win at WrestleWar (May 17, Jacksonville; Meltzer 5). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleWar_(1992) · Iron Man win over Rude at Beach Blast (June 20, Mobile, 4–3, last fall 29:30). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beach_Blast_(1992) · TV title from Austin (Sept 2); World tag with Shane Douglas (Nov 18).

## Rivalries
- Ric Flair: 1977 TV title, US title exchanges 1977–79, the 1989 series (Chi-Town, Landover, Clash VI, WrestleWar), 1994 Spring Stampede; Flair inducted him in 2009 and was in his corner at WM25.
- Randy Savage: bell-to-the-throat angle 1986, WrestleMania III.
- Jake Roberts: DDT on concrete; Snake Pit at The Big Event; SNME rematch Oct 4, 1986 (Wikipedia).
- Steve Austin: TV title 1992, Hollywood Blonds 1993, US title 1994 (Bash at the Beach, Clash XXVIII). Austin was "Stunning" Steve Austin in this period (persona page exists).
- Rick Rude & the Dangerous Alliance, 1992.
- Lex Luger, 1989.
- Paul Jones, 1978–79 (partner turned rival).
- Chris Jericho, 2009.
- FTR: partners 2022, attackers 2025.

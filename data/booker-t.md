# Booker T: the five-time WCW champion

Compiled 2026-11-09 for the Wrestle Lore dossier generator. Every line carries a source. ⚑ marks a dispute, a count that depends on who is counting, or a single-source claim.

## Canonical identity
- Real name: **Booker T. Huffman Jr.** (Wikipedia). He has said the "T." stands for nothing. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)
- Born: March 1, 1965, Plain Dealing, Louisiana. ⚑ Wikipedia notes the birthplace is often given wrongly as Houston; WWE bills him from Houston. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler) · https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
- Raised: youngest of eight children; father died before he was one, mother died when he was 13; moved in with brother Lash (Stevie Ray) at 17; drum major at Jack Yates High School, Houston. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)
- Billed: 6 ft 3 in, 256 lb, from Houston. https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
- Trained: Ivan Putski's school (Western Wrestling Alliance) under Scott Casey. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)
- Debut: 1990 as G.I. Bro (WWA), about eight weeks into training. ⚑ The old Wrestle Lore page said 1989; Wikipedia's infobox says 1990. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)
- Signature moves (WWE.com): Axe Kick (the Scissors Kick), Spinaroonie, Book End. Also the Harlem Hangover and Harlem Sidekick (event reports). https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WrestleMania_XIX · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Bash_(1998)
- Catchphrase: WWE credits him with popularizing "Sucka!" https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
- Married Sharmell (Sharmell Sullivan) in 2005. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)

## Before wrestling
- At 22 he pleaded guilty (December 1987) to two aggravated robbery counts tied to Wendy's restaurant holdups in Houston, was sentenced to five years, served 19 months, and was on parole until April 1992. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)
- Memoir: *Booker T: From Prison to Promise: Life Before the Squared Circle* (2012, Medallion Press, with Andrew William Wright). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler)

## Championships and accolades
- World titles: WWE.com calls him a **six-time world champion**. https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
  - ⚑ Counting: five WCW world title reigns (four in WCW, one more after the WWF purchase, when the belt was billed as the WCW Championship) plus one World Heavyweight Championship (2006). WWE.com lists "WCW Champion (4 times)" and separately "World Heavyweight Champion"; the list of WCW champions counts all five reigns. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Heavyweight_Champions · https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
  - He was **never WWE Champion**. ⚑ The old Wrestle Lore page claimed a 2006 WWE Championship as King Booker; the 2006 title was the World Heavyweight Championship. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Bash_(2006)
- WCW World Heavyweight Championship, 5 reigns, 253 days combined. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Heavyweight_Champions
  1. Jul 9, 2000, Bash at the Beach, Daytona Beach: beat Jeff Jarrett in an impromptu match after the Hogan/Russo incident (50 days). Lost to Kevin Nash, Nitro, Aug 28, 2000.
  2. Sep 17, 2000, Fall Brawl, Buffalo: beat Nash in a Caged Heat match (8 days). Lost to Vince Russo in a cage, Nitro, Sep 25, 2000.
  3. Oct 2, 2000, Nitro, Daly City: beat Jarrett in a San Francisco 49ers match for the vacant title (55 days). Lost to Scott Steiner at Mayhem, Nov 26, 2000.
  4. Mar 26, 2001, final Nitro, Panama City Beach: beat Steiner, winner take all with the US title (120 days). Lost to Kurt Angle, SmackDown taped Jul 24, 2001.
  5. Jul 30, 2001, Raw Is War, Philadelphia: beat Angle (20 days). Lost to The Rock at SummerSlam, Aug 19, 2001.
  - He was the WCW World Heavyweight Champion when WCW closed. https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t · https://www.wwe.com/classics/wcw/booker-t-recalls-final-nitro
- World Heavyweight Championship (WWE), 1 reign: won Jul 23, 2006 at The Great American Bash from Rey Mysterio; lost Nov 26, 2006 at Survivor Series to Batista. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_American_Bash_(2006) · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survivor_Series_(2006)
- WCW World Tag Team Championship: **11 reigns**, the individual record (10 with Stevie Ray as Harlem Heat, the team record, plus 1 with Test in 2001). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions · https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
  - Harlem Heat reigns: Dec 8, 1994 (Saturday Night, aired Jan 14, 1995); May 3, 1995; Sep 17, 1995 (Fall Brawl, 1 day); Sep 27, 1995; Jun 24, 1996 (Nitro); Jul 27, 1996 (house show, Dayton); Oct 1, 1996; Aug 14, 1999 (Road Wild); Sep 12, 1999 (Fall Brawl); Oct 24, 1999 (Halloween Havoc). 470 days combined. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions
  - Opponents named: Stars and Stripes (Dec 1994), Dick Slater & Bunkhouse Buck (Fall Brawl 1995), Lex Luger & Sting (Jun 24, 1996), Bam Bam Bigelow & Kanyon (Road Wild 1999), Konnan & Billy Kidman and Brian Knobbs & Hugh Morrus (Halloween Havoc 1999 three-way street fight). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_T_(wrestler) · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions
  - With Test: Sep 25, 2001, SmackDown, Dayton (aired Sep 27), 13 days. ⚑ Online World of Wrestling names The Undertaker and Kane as the team they beat; the Wikipedia list does not name the opponents. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Tag_Team_Champions · https://www.onlineworldofwrestling.com/?p=38309
- WCW World Television Championship: **6 reigns, the record**. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WCW_World_Television_Championship · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Television_Champions
  1. Dec 29, 1997, Nitro, Baltimore (from Disco Inferno), 49 days
  2. Feb 22, 1998, SuperBrawl VIII (from Rick Martel), 67 days
  3. May 1, 1998, house show, Greenville SC (from Chris Benoit), 1 day

  6. Mar 14, 1999, Uncensored, Louisville (from Scott Steiner), 56 days; lost to Rick Steiner May 9, 1999
  - Reign 5 ended July 13, 1998 when Stevie Ray claimed the title while holding Booker's "power of attorney" during an injury absence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_WCW_World_Television_Champions

## Character eras

- Kole of Harlem Heat (1993–94): renamed Kole (Lash became Kane) on WCW debut, managed by Col. Robert Parker, later Sister Sherri. ⚑ Debut date: Harlem Heat page says June 22, 1993; Booker T page says August 1993. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlem_Heat
- Booker T of Harlem Heat (1994–99): own name back in 1994. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harlem_Heat

- Booker T & Goldust (2002–03): comedy tag team. https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
- King Booker (2006–07): crowned on SmackDown by William Regal after winning King of the Ring; named Sharmell his Queen; faux British persona. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judgment_Day_(2006) · https://www.wwe.com/superstars/booker-t
- TNA / Main Event Mafia (2007–10). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Event_Mafia

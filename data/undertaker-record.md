## 1. Definitive WrestleMania Record

- **Final overall WrestleMania record: 25–2** (verified).
- **The Streak: 21–0** — undefeated at WrestleMania from WrestleMania VII (1991, def. Jimmy Snuka) through WrestleMania 29 (2013, def. CM Punk).
- **The Streak was ended by Brock Lesnar** at **WrestleMania XXX (2014)** via F5, dropping his run to 21–1.
- **After the Streak:** def. Bray Wyatt (WM31), def. Shane McMahon (WM32), **lost to Roman Reigns (WM33)**, def. John Cena (WM34), def. AJ Styles (WM36). He did not compete at WrestleMania 35 (2019).
- **Post-Streak WrestleMania record:** 4–1, giving the career total of **25 wins, 2 losses**.
- **Both WrestleMania losses:** Brock Lesnar (WM30) and Roman Reigns (WM33).

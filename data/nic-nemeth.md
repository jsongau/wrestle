# Nic Nemeth (Dolph Ziggler): The Showoff

One career, two names. "Dolph Ziggler" is the ring name he used in WWE from September 2008 until his release in September 2023. Since 2024 he has worked under his legal name, Nic Nemeth. WWE billed him as Dolph Ziggler again for his one-off Raw appearance on November 17, 2025.

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Nicholas Theodore Nemeth. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Born: July 27, 1980, Cleveland, Ohio. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Billed from: Hollywood, Florida (WWE billing). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Height / weight: 6 ft 0 in (183 cm) / 213 lb (97 kg), billed. ⚑ billed figures; WWE billings varied over 19 years. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- High school: St. Edward High School, Lakewood, Ohio; school record 82 career pins. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- College: Kent State University, political science major, pre-law minor; 121 career wins (2000–2003), the school record until 2006; three-time Mid-American Conference champion at 165 lb (2000, 2002, 2003). Accepted to Arizona State law school before his WWE tryout. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Trainers / systems: Ohio Valley Wrestling, Florida Championship Wrestling, Lance Storm, Steve Keirn, Tom Prichard. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Pro debut: November 3, 2004, OVW, as "Nick Nemeth". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Dolph Ziggler debut: September 15, 2008, Raw. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth
- Finishers: Zig Zag (jumping reverse bulldog; renamed Danger Zone after WWE), superkick, sleeper hold (early). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth ; Danger Zone finishes 2026: https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2203408/nic-nemeth-wins-2nd-tna-world-title-slammiversary-main-event/
- Entrance theme (WWE): "Here to Show the World" by Downstait, revealed November 2011, his eighth WWE theme. https://whatculture.com/wwe/dolph-zigglers-new-8th-wwe-theme-song-is-here-to-show-the-world-by-downstait . Earlier: "I Am Perfection". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth . No streaming URL verified, so no walk-out band.
- Family: younger brother Ryan Nemeth, his TNA tag partner. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nic_Nemeth

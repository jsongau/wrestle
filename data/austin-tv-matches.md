# "Stone Cold" Steve Austin — Notable Non-PPV Matches (Weekly TV & House Shows)

*A CURATED selection of Steve Austin's most notable matches OUTSIDE of WrestleMania and PPV/PLE main cards — i.e., weekly television (WWF Raw is War / SmackDown; WCW TV, Saturday Night, and the free-TV Clash of the Champions specials) plus signature angles. This is a highlight reel of genuinely famous TV moments, **NOT** a complete match log.*

*Sources cross-checked: Wikipedia, WWE.com (title histories & video archive), Online World of Wrestling, Cagematch, ProFightDB, and contemporaneous televised results/recaps. Compiled 2026-07-25. Any date or result that could not be fully verified is flagged in the notes and in the "Verification flags" section below.*

> **Scope note:** WrestleMania and pay-per-view matches (e.g., KOTR 1998, SummerSlam 2001, the WM trilogy vs. The Rock) are covered separately and are intentionally excluded here. Clash of the Champions was a **free TBS television special**, not a PPV, so those matches qualify for this list.

## Verification flags

- **Entry 1 (vs. Bobby Eaton, Jun 3, 1991):** Date is consistently cited (OWOW, Wikipedia) as June 3, 1991, but the exact TV program/taping it aired on is not uniformly documented — **flagged as approximate for venue/show only.**
- **Entry 4 (Hollywood Blonds tag-title win):** The recognized win date **varies by source** — Wikipedia narrative lists ~March 27, 1993 while Online World of Wrestling lists March 2, 1993 (the match was TV-taped in Feb 1993 and aired later). Listed as "early 1993." **Result (WIN, unifying NWA/WCW tag titles over Steamboat & Douglas) is verified; exact air date is approximate.**
- **Entry 12 (vs. Kurt Angle, SmackDown Mar 15, 2001, No DQ):** Genuinely notable/acclaimed match, but the **finish is muddled by The Rock's interference** (Rock's Rock Bottom set up the pin). Not a clean Austin win — **result flagged.**
- **Entry 17 (Booker T "supermarket brawl," Dec 13, 2001):** Date and event verified, but this was an **angle/segment, not a sanctioned match** — included for its fame and explicitly flagged.
- All other entries (2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16) have **date + result verified** against multiple sources.

*Reminder: This is a curated highlight selection of ~17 notable non-PPV matches/segments, not a complete televised-match record. Austin wrestled hundreds of additional TV and house-show matches not listed here.*

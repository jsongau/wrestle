## 2. Training / School

- **Killer Kowalski's Institute of Professional Wrestling** — Malden, Massachusetts.
- Trained **1990–1992** under **Walter "Killer" Kowalski**, who gave him the "Terra Ryzing" name.
- School-milieu classmates: **Chyna (Joanie Laurer)**, Perry Saturn.

| Years | Promo | Gimmick | Note |

## 4. In-ring style + influences

- **Killer Kowalski** — trainer; fundamentals + old-school psychology.
- **"Superstar" Billy Graham** — documented inspiration (physique/charisma/promos); HHH inducted him into the HOF in 2004. *(Strongest hard-sourced influence.)*
- **Harley Race** — HHH's own words (2019): praised Race's "presence" → the King of Kings persona.
- **Ric Flair** — most-cited idol/mentor; built into Evolution as HHH's on-screen mentor.
- **Arn Anderson** ⚑ — stylistic lineage (spinebuster; Evolution modeled on the Horsemen); not a firsthand quote.

Sources: [Billy Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superstar_Billy_Graham) · [SI — Harley Race](https://www.si.com/wrestling/2019/08/02/harley-race-tribute-terry-funk-triple-h-jim-ross-ric-flair)

## 5. Signature moves + nicknames

- **Finisher:** The Pedigree (double-underhook facebuster).
- **Signatures:** spinebuster, jumping/high-knee facebuster, the **sledgehammer** prop.
- **Nicknames:** HHH, The Game, The Cerebral Assassin, The King of Kings, The Connecticut/Greenwich Blueblood, "Hunter." The "H's" = Hunter Hearst Helmsley.

## 6. Championships & accomplishments

- **14× World Champion = 9× WWF/WWE Championship + 5× World Heavyweight Championship** (3rd-most world reigns in WWE history).
- **Intercontinental — 4×** ⚑ *(some sites say 5; Wikipedia raw list = 4).*
- WWF European — 2× · World Tag Team — 1× (w/ Steve Austin, 2001) · Unified WWE Tag Team — 1× (w/ Shawn Michaels/DX, 2009).
- **King of the Ring 1997** · **Royal Rumble winner 2002 & 2016** (2016: won Rumble for the WWE World Heavyweight Championship, last-eliminated Roman Reigns).
- Grand Slam (2nd ever) · Triple Crown (7th ever) · **7 WrestleMania main events** (WM 16, 18, 20, 21, 22, 25, 32).
- **WWE Hall of Fame ×2:** D-Generation X (2019) + **individually (Class of 2025)** — announced first inductee Jan 29 2025; inducted **April 18, 2025 at the Fontainebleau Las Vegas** the night before WrestleMania 41, **by Shawn Michaels**.

Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triple_H) · [WWE HOF 2025](https://www.wwe.com/article/paul-triple-h-levesque-to-be-inducted-into-the-wwe-hall-of-fame-class-of-2025) · [Wikipedia — WWE HOF (2025)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Hall_of_Fame_(2025))

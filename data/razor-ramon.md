# Razor Ramon (Scott Hall): The Bad Guy

Status: deceased. Died March 14, 2022, age 63. Legacy only; no activity implied. [S1][S2][S3]

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Scott Oliver Hall. [S1][S4]
- Born: October 20, 1958, St. Mary's County, Maryland. [S1] ⚑ the old Wrestle Lore page said Baltimore; Wikipedia gives St. Mary's County. Corrected.
- Died: March 14, 2022, WellStar Kennestone Hospital, Marietta, Georgia, age 63. [S1][S2]
- Cause as reported: complications after hip-replacement surgery (following a fall and broken hip in March 2022); a blood clot, three heart attacks on March 12, life support withdrawn March 14. [S1][S2] Kevin Nash announced the life-support decision on Instagram; WWE confirmed the death at the start of Raw: "WWE is saddened to learn that two-time WWE Hall of Famer Scott Hall has passed away." [S2][S3][S5]
- Buried April 8, 2022, Trinity Church Cemetery, St. Mary's, Maryland. [S1][S6]
- Billed: 6 ft 7 in (201 cm), 287 lb (130 kg), from Miami, Florida. [S1][S4] ⚑ billed figures.
- Trained by Hiro Matsuda (infobox); body text also credits Florida training under Dusty Rhodes with Mike Rotunda and Barry Windham. [S1]
- Debut: October 2, 1984 (Wikipedia infobox). [S1] ⚑ Dan Spivey's Wikipedia page dates the American Starship team to CWF in 1983. [S7]
- Ring names: American Starship Coyote, "Magnum" / "Big" Scott Hall, "Gator" Scott Hall, Texas Scott, The Diamond Studd, Razor Ramon, Scott Hall. [S1][S8]
- Children: Cody (born 1991, also a wrestler) and Cassidy (born 1995). [S1]
- Finisher: Razor's Edge (crucifix powerbomb), called the Outsider's Edge in WCW. [S1][S4][S9][S10] Toothpick flick; catchphrase "Say hello to the Bad Guy." [S1]
- Razor Ramon character modeled on Tony Montana of Scarface; catchphrase "Hey, yo." [S11]
- ⚑ Brief premise corrected: "Starship Coyote" was NOT an AWA name. American Starship (Coyote = Hall, Eagle = Dan Spivey) was a Championship Wrestling from Florida / Jim Crockett Promotions team. In the AWA (1985 on) he was "Magnum" and then "Big" Scott Hall. [S1][S7][S8]

## Championships
- AWA World Tag Team Championship x1, with Curt Hennig: won January 18, 1986, Albuquerque, over Jimmy Garvin & Steve Regal; lost May 17, 1986 to Buddy Rose & Doug Somers by countout. [S1][S8]
- WWC Caribbean Heavyweight Championship x1 (Puerto Rico): won March 3, 1991 over Miguel Perez Jr.; lost April 20 to Super Medic III. [S1] ⚑ year implied by Wikipedia's chronology.
- WWF Intercontinental Championship x4 (first four-time champion). [S1][S12][S13] WWE-recognized days in parentheses (sum 434):
  1. September 27, 1993, Raw, New Haven, battle royal final over Rick Martel for the vacant title (197) - to Diesel April 13, 1994, Superstars taping, Rochester. [S12][S13] ⚑ dates are WWE title-history (taping) dates; Wikipedia's Hall bio gives the airing dates October 11, 1993 and April 30, 1994. [S1]
  2. August 29, 1994, SummerSlam, Chicago, over Diesel (145) - to Jeff Jarrett January 22, 1995, Royal Rumble, Tampa. [S12][S13][S14][S15]
  3. May 19, 1995, house show, Montreal, ladder match over Jarrett (2) - WWE recognizes the end as May 22, 1995. [S12][S13] ⚑ Wikipedia's IC list dates Jarrett's next win to May 21 (Trois-Rivieres); WWE uses May 22.
  4. October 22, 1995, In Your House 4, Winnipeg, over Dean Douglas, who had won the title by forfeit from Shawn Michaels minutes earlier (90) - to Goldust January 21, 1996, Royal Rumble, Fresno. [S12][S13][S16][S17]
- WrestleMania X (March 20, 1994): retained and unified the IC title over Michaels in the ladder match (no separate reign). [S12][S18]
- WCW World Tag Team Championship x7 [S19] (Wikipedia bio prose is internally inconsistent on the numbering; the title-history list is used):
  1. Oct 27, 1996, Halloween Havoc, Las Vegas, with Kevin Nash over Harlem Heat (90 days) - lost Jan 25, 1997 Souled Out, Cedar Rapids, to The Steiner Brothers. [S19][S20]
  2. Jan 27, 1997, Nitro (Bischoff reversed Souled Out; outside referee) (27) - lost Feb 23, 1997 SuperBrawl VII to Lex Luger & The Giant. [S19]
  3. Feb 24, 1997, Nitro (Luger not cleared) (231) - Syxx defended in Nash's place under "Wolfpac Rules" from October 1997; lost Oct 13, 1997 Nitro, Tampa, to the Steiners. [S19][S21]
  4. Jan 12, 1998, Nitro, Jacksonville, over the Steiners (28) - lost Feb 9, 1998 Nitro, El Paso. [S19]
  5. Feb 22, 1998, SuperBrawl VIII, over the Steiners after Scott Steiner turned on Rick (84) - lost May 17, 1998 Slamboree, Worcester, to Sting & The Giant when Hall hit Nash with a belt. [S19][S22][S23]
  6. Jul 20, 1998, Nitro, Salt Lake City, with The Giant over Sting & Kevin Nash (97) - lost Oct 25, 1998 Halloween Havoc. [S19]
  7. Dec 13, 1999, Nitro, New Orleans, with Nash over Bret Hart & Goldberg (14) - vacated Dec 27, 1999 after Hall's injury. [S19][S1]
  - Six of seven with Nash; combined days by the list: 571 (arithmetic from [S19]).
  - ⚑ Wrestle Lore's /wrestlers/diesel/ page says "WCW Tag Team Championship 2x (with Scott Hall)". Wrong: six.
- WCW United States Heavyweight Championship x2: Feb 21, 1999 SuperBrawl IX, Oakland, over Roddy Piper (stripped March 18, 1999); Nov 8, 1999 Nitro, Indianapolis, four-way ladder over Goldberg, Bret Hart and Sid Vicious (ended Dec 19, 1999 Starrcade after a knee injury). [S24][S25][S1] ⚑ reason for first vacancy: title list says WCW President Ric Flair stripped him; Hall bio says a foot injury. Both stated as storyline/real.
- WCW World Television Championship x1: Nov 21, 1999, Mayhem, Toronto, after Rick Steiner forfeited; abandoned by Nov 29, 1999 when Nash threw the belt in a trash can. [S26][S1]
- TNA World Tag Team Championship x1: with Nash (Eric Young added under the Freebird rule), Feast or Fired cash-in over Matt Morgan, taped May 4, 2010 (aired May 13); stripped at the June 14, 2010 tapings. [S1][S21]
- WWC Universal Heavyweight Championship x1 (2007, over Carlito and Apollo). [S1]
- DDT Ironman Heavymetalweight Championship (won June 17, 2016 from Chuck Taylor in his final match). [S1]
- World War 3 1997 winner (60-man battle royal, Nov 23, 1997, Auburn Hills; last eliminated The Giant). Earned a WCW title shot, lost to Sting at Uncensored, Mar 15, 1998. [S27][S28][S4]
- Awards: PWI Most Improved 1992; PWI and Observer Match of the Year 1994 (WM X); Observer Best Gimmick 1996 (nWo); PWI Tag Team of the Year 1997 (with Nash). [S1][S18][S21]
- WWE Hall of Fame: 2014 solo, ceremony April 5, 2014, Smoothie King Center, New Orleans, inducted by Kevin Nash [S29] ⚑ WWE.com profile says April 6, 2014 [S4]; 2020 class with the nWo (Hogan, Nash, Hall, Sean Waltman), ceremony delayed by COVID-19, pretaped March 30 and April 1, aired April 6, 2021. [S29][S2]
- Never a world heavyweight champion.

## Character eras
- American Starship Coyote (CWF/JCP, c.1983-1985, with Dan Spivey as Eagle). [S7][S8]
- "Magnum" / "Big" Scott Hall, AWA 1985-c.1989. [S1][S8]
- WCW 1989 as "Gator" Scott Hall; NJPW 1987-1990; Texas Scott in Germany (CWA Catch Cup final loss Dec 22, 1990); WWC Puerto Rico 1991. [S1]
- The Diamond Studd, WCW 1991-1992, managed by Diamond Dallas Page in the Diamond Mine, teamed with Oz (Kevin Nash). [S1][S8] ⚑ Hall bio says he debuted as the Studd at SuperBrawl I (May 19, 1991) but the SuperBrawl I card on Wikipedia does not list him. Debut date not used.
- Razor Ramon, WWF 1992-1996 (first TV match Aug 8, 1992 Superstars over Paul Van Dale). [S1]
- Scott Hall / The Outsider / nWo, WCW 1996-2000. [S1][S4]
- WWF 2002 nWo return; TNA 2002, 2004-2005 (Kings of Wrestling), 2010 (The Band). [S1][S21]

## Career timeline
- 1983/1984 debut in Florida (American Starship). [S1][S7]
- 1986 AWA tag titles with Curt Hennig. [S1]
- 1989 WCW: TV debut July 9, 1989, pinned by The Great Muta. [S1][S8]
- 1991 Diamond Studd. [S1]
- 1992 WWF: dark match May 18; TV debut Aug 8. [S1]
- 1993 WrestleMania IX (Apr 4, Caesars Palace) pinned Bob Backlund with a small package, 3:45. [S30]
- 1993 IC title #1 (Sep 27). 1994 WM X ladder win (Mar 20, MSG, 18:47, 18,065). [S18]
- 1994 SummerSlam IC win with Walter Payton in his corner (Aug 29). [S14] Survivor Series (Nov 23, San Antonio) sole survivor for The Bad Guys over The Teamsters. [S31]
- 1995 Royal Rumble loss to Jarrett (restart after countout). [S15] WM XI (Apr 2, Hartford) beat Jarrett by DQ, no title change. [S32] Third and fourth IC reigns. SummerSlam ladder loss to Michaels (Aug 27, Pittsburgh, 25:01). [S33]
- 1996 Royal Rumble loss to Goldust (1-2-3 Kid attacked him per [S1]). Suspended earlier in 1996 [S1]. Curtain Call May 19, 1996 at MSG; he wrestled Hunter Hearst Helmsley that night [S34] (result not verified, no row). Signed with WCW in April 1996 [S35]. Nitro debut May 27, 1996 from the crowd [S1][S4]. Bash at the Beach July 7, 1996: no contest, Hogan revealed as third man. [S36]
- 1996-1999 tag reigns (above). 1997 World War 3 win. 1998 Uncensored loss to Sting; Slamboree turn on Nash. Starrcade Dec 27, 1998: stun-gunned Goldberg so Nash ended the streak (no match for Hall). [S37] Souled Out Jan 17, 1999: lost stun gun ladder match to Goldberg. [S38] SuperBrawl IX Feb 21, 1999: US title over Piper, and with Nash beat Konnan & Rey Mysterio in the hair vs mask match (Hall replaced the injured Luger). [S24][S39]
- 2000 last WCW match Feb 20, SuperBrawl 2000, three-way won by Sid Vicious (also Jeff Jarrett). [S40]
- 2000 ECW house show appearance Nov 10. 2001 NJPW (Team 2000; Sept match vs Keiji Muto). [S1]
- 2002 WWF: No Way Out Feb 17 nWo return; first WWF match since 1996 beating Spike Dudley on Raw Mar 4; WM X8 Mar 17 lost to Steve Austin (9:51, SkyDome) - his first WrestleMania loss; released May 2002. [S1][S41]
- 2002 TNA appearances; 2004 Turning Point (Dec 5) six-man loss with Jarrett & Nash to AJ Styles, Jeff Hardy & Randy Savage; 2005 Final Resolution (Jan 16) loss to Jeff Hardy, Roddy Piper refereeing. [S42][S43]
- 2010 TNA tag title; released June 15, 2010. [S1]
- 2014 Hall of Fame. "Hard work pays off, dreams come true, bad times don't last, but bad guys do." [S11][S29]
- 2015 appears in The Resurrection of Jake the Snake (Slamdance premiere Jan 23, 2015), living at DDP's Atlanta "Accountability Crib". [S44]
- 2016 final match June 17, Des Moines. [S1]
- 2021 nWo Hall of Fame (2020 class). 2022 death. [S29][S2]

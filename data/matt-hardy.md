# Matt Hardy: The Broken One

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Matthew Moore Hardy. [S1]
- Born: September 23, 1974, Cameron, North Carolina. Age 52 on 2026-11-09. [S1]
- Billed from: Cameron, North Carolina. [S1][S36]
- Billed height/weight: 6 ft 2 in (188 cm), 236 lb (107 kg). [S1] ⚑ billed figures; TNA's roster page lists no height or weight. [S36]
- Pro debut: October 15, 1992. [S1] ⚑ the old Wrestle Lore page said 1993; Wikipedia gives 1992.
- First WWF match: May 23, 1994, Raw, lost to Nikolai Volkoff. Full-time WWF contract 1998. [S1]
- Trainer: Dory Funk Jr. [S1]
- OMEGA (Organization of Modern Extreme Grappling Arts): founded 1997 with Jeff Hardy; folded October 1999 after both signed with the WWF. [S1]
- Family: brother Jeff Hardy. Married Rebecca Reyes (Reby Sky) on October 5, 2013; three sons and one daughter. [S1]
- Education: Union Pines High School; a year of engineering at UNC Charlotte; associate degree from Sandhills Community College. [S1]
- Finishers/signature: Twist of Fate, Side Effect, leg drop off the top of a cage. [S1]
- Catchphrase: "Delete." [S1]
- Socials: X and Instagram @matthardybrand. [S36]

## Ring names and nicknames
Version 1 (Matt Hardy V1), Broken Matt Hardy, Woken Matt Hardy, Big Money Matt, "Multifarious" Matt Hardy, Unkillable Matt Hardy; early names High Voltage, Surge, Ingus. [S1]

## Character eras
- Hardy Boyz / Team Xtreme (1998-2002): tag daredevils with Jeff, later with Lita. [S1][S2]
- Version 1 / Mattitude (2002-2003): heel turn August 12, 2002 Raw by attacking Jeff; SmackDown move; Mattitude Followers with Shannon Moore, disbanded November 2003. [S1]
- 2004-2005: Lita/Kane storyline; 2005 release and Edge feud. [S1]
- Broken Matt Hardy (TNA 2016-2017): debuted May 17, 2016; Final Deletion July 5, 2016; Delete or Decay September 8, 2016. [S1]
- Woken Matt Hardy (WWE 2018): Ultimate Deletion vs Bray Wyatt on Raw, March 19, 2018. [S1]
- AEW (2020-2024): debut March 18, 2020 Dynamite; Big Money Matt; Hardy Family Office (formed after Revolution 2021, first called the Matt Hardy Empire). [S1] Andrade bought a 51% stake in HFO. [S37] Hardy Party ended January 5, 2024 Rampage. [S38]
- Broken again, TNA (2024-present): returned April 20, 2024 at Rebellion, laid out Moose. [S35][S2]

## Status as of 2026-11-09
Active, TNA roster. Not a champion (Nemeths have held the TNA tag titles since August 23, 2026 [S3]). Jeff is out indefinitely in storyline ("lacerations and head trauma") after the October 29 Slaughterhouse match [S32]; Matt teams with Moose against syX at Destination X on November 15, 2026. [S31] Hardys re-signed with TNA (one-year deal announced November 27, 2024 [S33]; Matt said December 22, 2025 they had "been re-signed for a while" [S34], terms undisclosed ⚑).

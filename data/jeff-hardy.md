# Jeff Hardy: The Charismatic Enigma

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Jeffrey Nero Hardy. [S1]
- Born: August 31, 1977, Cameron, North Carolina. Age 49 on 2026-11-09. [S1]
- Billed from: Cameron, North Carolina. [S1]
- Billed height/weight: 6 ft 1 in (185 cm), 225 lb (102 kg). [S1] ⚑ billed figures.
- Pro debut: May 23, 1994, WWF, vs Razor Ramon in Youngstown, Ohio, at 16 (he claimed to be 18). [S1] ⚑ the previous Wrestle Lore module said 1993; Wikipedia's infobox gives May 23, 1994. Earlier backyard/trampoline matches (Trampoline Wrestling Federation) are undated. [S1]
- Trainers: Dory Funk Jr. and Michael Hayes. [S1] ⚑ the previous module said "largely self-taught"; replaced.
- OMEGA: the Trampoline Wrestling Federation grew into OMEGA as the brothers got older [S1]; OMEGA was founded in 1997 and folded in October 1999. [S2 via data/matt-hardy.md S1]
- Family: brother Matt Hardy. [S1]
- Ring names (infobox): Brother Nero, Itchweed, Jeff Harvey, Keith Davis, Wildo, Jynx, Willow, Wolverine. [S1]
- Signature moves: Swanton Bomb, Twist of Fate, Whisper in the Wind, Poetry in Motion (with Matt). [S1][S9]

## Championships (counts as of 2026-11-09)
- WWE Championship x1: Armageddon, December 14, 2008, triple threat with Edge and Triple H; lost to Edge at the Royal Rumble, January 25, 2009 (Matt's chair betrayal). [S1]
- World Heavyweight Championship x2 (both 2009): Extreme Rules, June 7, 2009, ladder match over Edge, lost minutes later to CM Punk's Money in the Bank cash-in; won back at Night of Champions, July 26, 2009; lost to Punk at SummerSlam TLC, August 23, 2009. [S1]
- TNA World Heavyweight Championship x3 [S1]:
  - Won October 10, 2010, Bound for Glory, three-way with Kurt Angle and Mr. Anderson for the vacant title (heel turn with Hogan and Bischoff; Immortal named on the next Impact). Lost January 9, 2011, Genesis, to Mr. Anderson.
  - Won February 13, 2011, Against All Odds, ladder match over Mr. Anderson. Lost February 24, 2011 to Sting.
  - Won October 14, 2012, Bound for Glory, over Austin Aries. Lost March 10, 2013, Lockdown, steel cage, to Bully Ray.
- WWF/WWE Intercontinental Championship x5 [S1]: 2001 over Triple H (SmackDown taped April 10, aired April 12, 2001 ⚑ [S10]); October 2, 2006 Raw over Johnny Nitro; November 13, 2006 Raw over Nitro; September 3, 2007 over Umaga; August 21, 2020 SmackDown over AJ Styles [S11].
- WWE United States Championship x1: April 16, 2018 Raw over Jinder Mahal. [S1] Wikipedia: this made him the second person to complete the modern Grand Slam, having completed the original format separately. [S1]
- WWE European Championship x1: July 8, 2002 Raw over William Regal. [S1]
- WWF/E Hardcore Championship x3; WWF Light Heavyweight Championship x1 (over Jerry Lynn, 2001). [S1]
- 18th WWE Triple Crown Champion; 9th WWE Grand Slam Champion. [S1]
- Tag championships with Matt [S1][S2]:
  - WWF/World Tag Team x6 (first won July 5, 1999 Raw over The Acolytes; last won April 2, 2007). [S2]
  - WCW Tag Team x1 (October 8, 2001). [S2]
  - Raw Tag Team x1 (WrestleMania 33, April 2, 2017; lost June 4, 2017 Extreme Rules cage to Cesaro & Sheamus). [S2]
  - SmackDown Tag Team x1 (April 9, 2019 over The Usos; vacated after Jeff's knee injury). [S2]
  - NXT Tag Team x1 (October 7, 2025 NXT vs. TNA Showdown over DarkState; lost October 25, 2025 Halloween Havoc, Broken Rules match). [S1]
  - ROH World Tag Team x1 (March 4, 2017 to April 1, 2017). [S2]
  - TNA World Tag Team x5 [S1 list; S3 via data/matt-hardy.md]:
    1. March 16, 2015, Ultimate X for the vacant title (taped; aired April 17); vacated May 8, 2015.
    2. October 2, 2016, Bound for Glory, "The Great War" over Decay, as the Broken Hardys (152 days).
    3. October 26, 2024, Bound for Glory, Detroit, Full Metal Mayhem over The System and ABC (183 days; lost April 27, 2025 Rebellion to the Nemeths). [S1]
    4. July 20, 2025, Slammiversary, four-way ladder (265 days; lost April 11, 2026 Rebellion, Cleveland, to The System [S4]).
    5. June 28, 2026, Slammiversary, Agganis Arena, Boston, four-way ladder over The System, The Righteous and The Great Hands, billed as The Broken Hardys (Broken Matt and Brother Nero) [S5][S6]; lost August 23, 2026, Lockdown, Chicago, steel cage, to Nic and Ryan Nemeth (56 days). [S7][S8]
  - ⚑ The previous Wrestle Lore module said 3 TNA tag reigns. Wikipedia's championships list and the TNA title history both count 5; 5 is used.
  - WWE tag total with Matt: 9 main-roster reigns (6 World, 1 WCW, 1 Raw, 1 SmackDown) plus 1 NXT reign = 10 inside WWE. [S1][S2] ⚑ the previous module said "10 WWE tag reigns" and also listed NXT separately, which double counted.
  - Wikipedia: the brothers have held twenty-one world tag team championships together (all promotions). [S1]
- Other: Terri Invitational Tournament (1999, with Matt); Tag Team Triple Crown (4th, with Matt); TNA World Heavyweight Championship Tournament (2010); TNA World Cup (2015, 2016). [S1]

## Career timeline
- 1994: first WWF match May 23 (vs Razor Ramon). [S1]
- 1999: first WWF tag titles July 5; No Mercy ladder match October 17. [S2]
- 2000: WrestleMania 2000 triangle ladder (April 2); SummerSlam TLC (August 27); Unforgiven cage title win (September 24). [S2]
- 2001: TLC II (April 1); IC title over Triple H (April 12 aired); Vengeance win over Matt (December 9). [S1][S2]
- 2002: Undisputed title ladder match vs The Undertaker, Raw, July 1 [S12]; European title July 8. [S1]
- 2003: released April 22. [S1]
- 2006-2007: three IC reigns. [S1]
- 2008: WWE Champion, Armageddon, December 14. [S1]
- 2009: Royal Rumble loss (January 25); WrestleMania 25 loss to Matt (April 5) [S2]; two World Heavyweight reigns; SummerSlam TLC loss to Punk (August 23). [S1]
- 2010-2013: TNA, three world titles; Victory Road (March 13, 2011) match with Sting stopped in about 90 seconds [S1 ⚑ duration from prior module and widely reported; not re-timed here]; Lockdown 2013 loss to Bully Ray. [S1]
- 2016: Final Deletion (July 5) as Brother Nero vs Matt; Great War tag win (October 2). [S2]
- 2017: WrestleMania 33 surprise return and Raw Tag win, April 2. [S2]
- 2018: US Champion, April 16. [S1]
- 2019: SmackDown Tag, April 9. [S2]
- 2020: fifth IC reign, August 21. [S11]
- 2022: AEW debut (March 9 per data/matt-hardy.md); Double or Nothing win over the Young Bucks May 29; DUI arrest June 2022 (Wikipedia states June 14, 2022, Volusia County) and indefinite suspension. [S1][S2]
- 2023: AEW return April 12. [S1]
- 2024: last AEW match February 14 Rampage (No DQ loss to Sammy Guevara); contract expiry reported June 14; TNA return June 14 (Against All Odds); TNA tag titles October 26. [S1]
- 2025: lost titles April 27; regained July 20; NXT titles October 7-25; Bound for Glory October 12 tables win over Team 3D in their retirement match. [S1]
- 2026: lost titles to The System April 11 [S4]; regained June 28 as the Broken Hardys [S5][S6]; lost at Lockdown August 23 (steel cage, Nic pinned Matt with the Danger Zone) [S7][S8]; found attacked backstage on the August 27 iMPACT in a mystery-attacker storyline [S13]; Joseph Sawyer and Samuel Shaw (syX, formerly Joe Gacy and Dexter Lumis of the Wyatt Sicks) unmasked standing over the Hardys on the September 24 iMPACT [S14][S15]; October 8 iMPACT title rematch lost when Nic Nemeth rolled up Jeff after a syX distraction [S16]; Bound for Glory October 11, Yuengling Center, Tampa, Falls Count Anywhere loss to syX after Erick Redbeard interfered (Shaw and Sawyer pinned Matt stacked on Jeff) [S17][S18]; October 29 iMPACT, Dothan, Alabama, Slaughterhouse syX match lost, Jeff struck with axes while wearing a trash can, storyline injury report "lacerations and head trauma," out indefinitely. [S19][S20][S21]

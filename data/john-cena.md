# John Cena — The Franchise

## Canonical identity
- Full name: John Felix Anthony Cena
- Born: April 23, 1977 — West Newbury, Massachusetts, USA
- Billed from: West Newbury, Massachusetts
- Height: 6 ft 1 in (billed); Weight: 251 lb (billed, varied by era)
- Pro wrestling debut: 1999 (Ultimate Pro Wrestling)
- Final match: December 13, 2025 (loss to Gunther by submission, Saturday Night's Main Event)
- Status: Retired from in-ring competition after a 26-year career
- Primary promotion: WWE
- Trainers/systems: Ultimate University / Ultimate Pro Wrestling; Ohio Valley Wrestling
- Ring names: John Cena, The Prototype, Juan Cena
- Finishers: Attitude Adjustment (formerly F-U); STF (formerly STFU)
- Signature gesture: "You Can't See Me"; Slogan: Hustle, Loyalty, Respect

## Nicknames
The Franchise · The Champ · The Face That Runs the Place · Doctor of Thuganomics · Chain Gang Soldier · Cenation Leader · Big Match John · The GOAT (farewell era)

## Championships and accolades
- 17-time world champion (record): 14 in the WWE Championship lineage + 3 World Heavyweight Championship reigns. Surpassed the long-standing 16-time tie by defeating Cody Rhodes at WrestleMania 41 (2025).
- WWE United States Championship — 5 times
- WWE Tag Team / World Tag Team Championship — 4 reigns (with Shawn Michaels, Batista, David Otunga, The Miz)
- WWE Intercontinental Championship — 1 time (won November 10, 2025 vs Dominik Mysterio), completing the Grand Slam
- Developmental: OVW Heavyweight (1), OVW Southern Tag (1, w/ Rico Constantino), UPW Heavyweight (1)
- Royal Rumble winner — 2008, 2013
- Money in the Bank winner — 2012
- Men's Elimination Chamber winner — 2025
- 100 WWE PLE/PPV victories reached in 2025 (100th: def. AJ Styles at Crown Jewel)
- Grand Slam and Triple Crown champion (completed 2025)
- 380-day WWE Championship reign (2006–2007)

## WrestleMania highlights
XX def. Big Show (first US title) · 21 def. JBL (first WWE title) · 22 def. Triple H · 23 def. Shawn Michaels · XXVI def. Batista · XXVIII lost to The Rock · 29 def. The Rock · 31 def. Rusev (US) · 41 def. Cody Rhodes (record 17th world title).

## Character eras
- The Prototype (1999–2001): semi-robotic UPW developmental character; UPW Heavyweight Champion.
- Ruthless Aggression prospect (2002): televised debut answering Kurt Angle's open challenge.
- Doctor of Thuganomics (2002–2003): trash-talking freestyle-rap heel; chains, throwback jerseys.
- Chain Gang Soldier → The Franchise / Cenation leader: bright merch, military salute, Hustle Loyalty Respect.
- "Super Cena": fan label for the comeback-heavy peak; both criticism and evidence of his centrality.
- United States Open Challenge workhorse (2015): weekly matches spotlighting new opponents.
- Free agent / part-time legend: dream matches and generational programs.
- Firefly Fun House subject (WM36): cinematic deconstruction by Bray Wyatt.
- Farewell-tour heel and final champion (2025): heel turn, 17th world title, Grand Slam, retirement.

## Career timeline
- 1999–2001 UPW/The Prototype; won UPW Heavyweight Championship.
- 2000–2002 OVW developmental; OVW Heavyweight and Southern Tag titles.
- 2002 Ruthless Aggression debut vs Kurt Angle (SmackDown, June 27).
- 2002–2003 Doctor of Thuganomics breakthrough.
- 2004 First WWE title (US) def. Big Show at WrestleMania XX; full babyface turn.
- 2005 First WWE Championship def. JBL at WrestleMania 21; drafted to Raw; spinner belt.
- 2006 Edge rivalry, first MITB cash-in loss, TLC win at Unforgiven; def. Triple H at WM22.
- 2007 380-day reign; def. Shawn Michaels at WM23 and the near-hour London Raw match; torn pec, vacates title.
- 2008 Surprise Royal Rumble return and win (No. 30); World Heavyweight title from Jericho at Survivor Series.
- 2009 Orton rivalry; 60-minute Iron Man match at Bragging Rights.
- 2010 def. Batista at WM XXVI; The Nexus angle.
- 2011 The Miz at WM27; CM Punk at Money in the Bank (Chicago).
- 2012 lost to The Rock at WM XXVIII; def. returning Brock Lesnar at Extreme Rules; MITB winner.
- 2013 second Royal Rumble win; def. The Rock at WM29 for the title; lost to Daniel Bryan at SummerSlam.
- 2014 WWE World Heavyweight at MITB; lost decisively to Brock Lesnar at SummerSlam.
- 2015 def. Rusev at WM31; the United States Open Challenge era.
- 2016 AJ Styles trilogy (lost MITB and SummerSlam).
- 2017 def. AJ Styles at Royal Rumble to tie 16 world titles; lost to Roman Reigns at No Mercy.
- 2018–2020 part-time legend; Firefly Fun House vs Bray Wyatt at WM36.
- 2021 challenged Roman Reigns at SummerSlam.
- 2022–2023 anniversary appearances; lost to Solo Sikoa at Crown Jewel 2023.
- 2024 announced 2025 as final in-ring year.
- 2025 farewell tour: Elimination Chamber winner, heel turn, def. Cody Rhodes at WM41 for record 17th world title, def. CM Punk at Night of Champions, lost title back to Cody at SummerSlam (Street Fight), 100th PLE win vs AJ Styles at Crown Jewel, IC title + Grand Slam vs Dominik Mysterio, final match loss to Gunther Dec 13.

## Before WWE — college and bodybuilding
- Springfield College, Class of 1999; degree in exercise physiology/kinesiology.
- Football center, No. 54; tri-captain of the 9–2 1998 team that reached Springfield's first NCAA Division III Tournament.
- Hewlett-Packard Division III First-Team All-American; ECAC New England/Northeast Division III All-Star; multiple all-conference and all-academic honors.
- Inducted into the Springfield College Athletic Hall of Fame (2015).
- Moved to California after college; pursued competitive bodybuilding, worked at Gold's Gym and as a limo driver before entering Ultimate Pro Wrestling.

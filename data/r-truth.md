# R-Truth (Ron Killings), The Truth

Compiled November 9, 2026. Every line carries a source. Disputed or estimated figures are flagged ⚑.
Harvested from the old /wrestlers/r-truth/ page (186 words): the March 20, 2026 tag title win with Damian Priest and the real name. Both verified below. The old page called him the current champion; that went stale on August 14, 2026 and is corrected here.

## Canonical identity
- Real name: Ronnie Aaron Killings. [W] https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R-Truth
- Born: January 19, 1972, Charlotte, North Carolina. [W]
  - ⚑ TheSmackDownHotel lists his birthplace as Williamsburg, South Carolina, with Charlotte as billed hometown. Wikipedia gives Charlotte as birthplace. Charlotte is used; the conflict is noted. https://thesmackdownhotel.com/wrestlers/r-truth
- Billed: 6 ft 2 in, 220 lb, from Charlotte, N.C. (WWE.com) https://www.wwe.com/superstars/r-truth ; 188 cm / 100 kg [W]
  - ⚑ TheSmackDownHotel records an earlier billed weight of 235 lb before October 2011.
- Trainers: Chris Hamrick, George South, Italian Stallion, Manny Fernandez. [W]
- Debut: ⚑ Wikipedia infobox gives October 30, 1999 as the in-ring debut; the body says he first appeared in 1997 for PWF as a manager. Page uses 1999 for in-ring work. [W]
- Ring names: K-Krush, K-Kwik, K. Malik Shabaz, Ron "The Truth" Killings, R-Truth, Ron Killings (2025), plus comedy aliases R-Ziggler and Ron Cena. [W]
- Nicknames: The Truth; R-Trizzle (TheSmackDownHotel). Catchphrase "What's up?" [SDH]
- Finishers: Lie Detector (WWE.com lists it as his signature move) https://www.wwe.com/superstars/r-truth . TheSmackDownHotel gives the move history: corkscrew scissors kick (2000–2011), corkscrew flying forearm as "Lie Detector" (2009–2011), leaping reverse STO billed as Lie Detector / What's Up? / Little Jimmy (2011–present), and the "Hat Rack Crack" sitout suplex slam (2000–2004). [SDH]
- In 2026 he pinned JC Mateo with John Cena's Attitude Adjustment and has used the STF. https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2128495/wwe-smackdown-damian-priest-r-truth-mfts-tag-team-championship
- X: @RonKillings (embedded in Fightful's October 19, 2026 report) https://www.fightful.com/?p=334695
- Family: married Pamela Killings April 7, 2011; five children per infobox. [W]
- Before wrestling: Harry P. Harding High School, class of 1991; turned down college scholarships for music; sold drugs in his early 20s, arrested four times, served 13 months. [W]

- Ron "The Truth" Killings (2002–2007): NWA-TNA world champion; 3 Live Kru from July 2003; Team Pacman 2007. [W]
- R-Truth (2008–2011): rapping babyface, "What's up?" entrance. [W]
- Heel R-Truth / Awesome Truth (2011): conspiracy-paranoid "Little Jimmy" heel, Capitol Punishment title shot, Awesome Truth with The Miz Aug 22–Nov 21, 2011. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Awesome_Truth
- Comedy R-Truth (2015–2025): Golden Truth with Goldust (2016–17), 24/7 Championship (2019–22), Judgment Day "member" storyline (Nov–Dec 2023). [W]

## Career timeline
- 1999: NWA Wildside as K-Krush; TV title from AJ Styles Nov 6. Signs WWF developmental deal. [W]
- 2000: MCW Southern Heavyweight champion (Apr 12); Raw debut Nov 13 as K-Kwik. [K-Kwik W]
- 2001: two Hardcore reigns Feb 3–4; Royal Rumble entry, eliminated by Big Show; released Aug 2001. [W]
- 2002: XPW as K. Malik Shabaz; signs with NWA-TNA June 2002; NWA World champion Aug 7. [W] [PWInsider]
- 2003: 3 Live Kru (July); NWA tag titles Nov 26. [K-Kwik W]
- 2004: second NWA World title May 19, lost June 2. [Garoon]
- 2007: TNA tag titles with Pacman Jones Sept 9, lost Oct 14; leaves TNA via release deal Dec 2007. [W]
- 2008: WWE return as R-Truth, SmackDown Aug 29, def. Kenny Dykstra. https://kbwrestlingreviews.com/2025/06/09/smackdown-august-29-2008-nice-to-meet-you-and-you
- 2009: Team SmackDown wins at Bragging Rights. [W]
- 2010: WrestleMania XXVI (with John Morrison, lost to ShoMiz); first US title May 24. [W]
- 2011: heel turn; WWE title match vs John Cena at Capitol Punishment, June 19, 2011 (lost) https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WWE_Capitol_Punishment ; Awesome Truth main-events Survivor Series Nov 20 vs Cena & The Rock (lost). [Awesome Truth W]
- 2012: tag champion with Kofi Kingston Apr 30–Sept 16. [W]
- 2015: WrestleMania 31 IC ladder match; 2015 Slammy. [W]
- 2016: Golden Truth def. Breezango at Money in the Bank, June 19. [W]
- 2018: Mixed Match Challenge win with Carmella, Dec 16. [W]
- 2019: US title Jan 29–Mar 5; first 24/7 title May 20. [W]
- 2022: last 24/7 reign June 20; title retired Nov 7. [24/7 list]
- 2023: Survivor Series return Nov 25; Judgment Day angle; def. JD McDonagh Dec 18 (Loser Leaves Judgment Day Miracle on 34th Street Fight). [W]
- 2024: Awesome Truth reunites Jan 1; WrestleMania XL tag title win Apr 6; lost June 24; Miz turns on him Sept 30. [Awesome Truth W]
- 2025: SmackDown brand debut Feb 14 (lost to Carmelo Hayes); Cena puts him through a table at the Backlash press conference; loses to Cena at Saturday Night's Main Event, May 24, Tampa (non-title) https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/wwe/wwe-saturday-night-s-main-event-5-24-25-live-blog-jey-uso-vs-logan-paul-john-cena-vs-r-truth ; June 1 posts "I just got released from WWE" (contract allowed to lapse, per Fightful via POST Wrestling) https://www.postwrestling.com/?p=116644 ; returns June 7 at Money in the Bank to cost Cena & Logan Paul against Cody Rhodes & Jey Uso [W] https://cultaholic.com/posts/r-truth-returns-at-wwe-money-in-the-bank-helps-cody-rhodes--jey-uso-defeat-john-cena--logan-paul ; haircut on Raw June 9; turns down Metro Boomin's offer of a new theme June 10 https://www.fightful.com/?p=232643 ; SmackDown June 20 vs Cena ends by DQ when Cena hits him with the title [W] (the john-cena dossier records this as a Cena loss); The White Album released Aug 7, No. 10 Billboard Country Albums https://wrestletalk.com/news/wwe-billboard-album-r-truth/ ; Raw Dec 29 loses to Gunther by sleeper two weeks after Gunther retired Cena https://lastwordonsports.com/prowrestling/?p=149572

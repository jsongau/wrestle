# Austin Theory: The A-Town Kid who beat John Cena

## Canonical identity
- Real name: Austin Tyler White. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory
- Born: August 2, 1997, McDonough, Georgia. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory ; https://www.espn.com/wwe/story/_/id/29202013/theory
- Billed from: Atlanta, Georgia (WWE.com, ESPN). McDonough is the real hometown (WWE.com's 2019 signing release names McDonough). ⚑ billed vs real. Sources: https://www.wwe.com/superstars/austin-theory ; https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/article/austin-theory-santana-garrett-headline-wwe-performance-center-class
- Height: 6 ft 1 in billed (ESPN, Wikipedia). WWE.com's current profile lists no height. Source: https://www.espn.com/wwe/story/_/id/29202013/theory
- Weight: 220 lb billed (WWE.com, ESPN). Source: https://www.wwe.com/superstars/austin-theory
- Trainers: AR Fox; WWA4 Wrestling School; WWE Performance Center. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory
- Pro debut: April 29, 2016, Glory Pro Wrestling (lost a three-way to Mike Cobb, with Poncho Arp). Single source. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory
- WWE signing: announced August 14, 2019 as part of a Performance Center class, age 22, as reigning Evolve and WWN Champion. Source: https://www.wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/article/austin-theory-santana-garrett-headline-wwe-performance-center-class
- Finisher: A-Town Down (WWE.com). ESPN's 2022 profile calls it "ATL, a spinning cutter". Sources: https://www.wwe.com/superstars/austin-theory ; https://www.espn.com/wwe/story/_/id/29202013/theory
- Brand / status (November 9, 2026): Raw; member of The Vision (Paul Heyman's group). Lost the World Tag Team Championship with Bron Breakker at Crown Jewel on November 7, 2026. Sources below.
- Socials (per WWE.com): X @_Theory1; Instagram austin_theory; Facebook TheoryWWE. Source: https://www.wwe.com/superstars/austin-theory
- Personal: straight edge, partly because of his father's alcoholism; NPC Georgia Teen Men's bodybuilding title in 2015 at 17. Single source. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin_Theory

## Career timeline
- 2016: Debut April 29 (Glory Pro). WWA4 Heavyweight title from AR Fox May 5. FIP/WWN debut August 12 (FIP Heatstroke). [Wikipedia]
- 2017: Evolve debut February 24 (Evolve 78) beating Darby Allin. FIP title December 10. [Wikipedia]
- 2018: WWN title April 6 from Keith Lee; Evolve Championship December 15. [Wikipedia; WWE.com]
- 2019: WWN title again July 13 (winner takes all vs JD Drake). Seen at NXT TakeOver: Toronto August 10; signing announced August 14. NXT TV debut December 25 losing to North American Champion Roderick Strong. [WWE.com https://wwe.com/shows/wwenxt/2019-12-25/article/nxt-north-american-champion-roderick-strong-def-austin-theory]
- 2020: Main roster debut March 24 (ESPN). Replaced Andrade alongside Angel Garza and lost to The Street Profits for the Raw Tag titles at WrestleMania 36 Night 2, April 5. [https://www.f4wonline.com/news/wwe/austin-theory-replacing-andrade-wwe-wrestlemania-36-307726 ; https://www.wwe.com/shows/wrestlemania/wrestlemania-36/the-street-profits-vs-austin-theory-angel-garza-results] Short alliance with Seth Rollins and Murphy until June 22, 2020 [Wikipedia]. Back in NXT; lost to Bronson Reed September 8. Helped Johnny Gargano win the North American title at TakeOver: WarGames December 6; revealed in The Way December 9 with Gargano, Candice LeRae and Indi Hartwell. [Wikipedia]
- 2021: Last NXT match July 20 (lost to Kyle O'Reilly). Drafted to Raw; returned October 4. Team Raw at Survivor Series November 21. Revealed as the thief of Vince McMahon's "Cleopatra's Egg" on Raw November 22, earned a WWE Championship match with Big E that night and lost; became McMahon's protege. [https://www.f4wonline.com/news/wwe/austin-theory-revealed-golden-egg-thief-wwe-raw-360471]
- 2022: Royal Rumble entrant No. 3 (eliminated by AJ Styles), Elimination Chamber WWE title match February 19 (last eliminated by Brock Lesnar) [Wikipedia]. Lost to Pat McAfee at WrestleMania 38 Night 2 April 3; Stone Cold Steve Austin stunned him, McMahon and McAfee afterward [https://foxsports.com/articles/wwe/pat-mcafee-def-austin-theory-then-lost-to-mr-mcmahon-afterward-stone-cold-steve-austin-stunned-all-three]. First US title April 18. Lost US title and won Money in the Bank July 2. Briefcase interjections at SummerSlam (July 30) and Clash at the Castle (September 3) never became official matches; the official cash-in was November 7 on Raw against US Champion Seth Rollins and failed after Bobby Lashley pulled him out [https://www.f4wonline.com/news/wwe/austin-theory-fails-in-wwe-raw-money-in-the-bank-cash-in]. Second US title November 26.
- 2023: Retained in the Elimination Chamber February 18 (Montreal) with help from Logan Paul [https://fightful.com/wrestling/austin-theory-retains-wwe-us-title-inside-elimination-chamber-logan-paul-stomps-seth-rollins]. Beat John Cena at WrestleMania 39 Night 1, April 1: Cena's STF tap-out came with the referee down; Theory hit a low blow and A-Town Down [https://www.wwe.com/index%2Ephp/shows/wrestlemania/austin-theory-vs-john-cena-results]. Retained at Backlash May 6 vs Lashley and Bronson Reed [Wikipedia]. Lost US title to Rey Mysterio August 11; lost rematch at Payback September 2 [https://www.wrestlinginc.com/1384110/rey-mysterio-retains-united-states-title-austin-theory-wwe-payback]. Drafted to SmackDown; A-Town Down Under debuted September 1; lost to Cody Rhodes and Jey Uso for the Undisputed Tag titles October 13 [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-Town_Down_Under].
- 2024: Won a tournament in March to qualify for the WrestleMania XL ladder match; won SmackDown Tag titles April 6 (Waller pulled them down). First defense May 3 vs Street Profits. Lost to #DIY July 5. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-Town_Down_Under]
- 2025: Moved to Raw January 24. Lost a three-way NXT Championship match to Oba Femi at NXT Vengeance Day February 15 (Femi pinned Waller after Waller accidentally hit Theory) [https://www.wrestlinginc.com/1791045/oba-femi-overcomes-main-roster-stars-wwe-nxt-vengeance-day-new-threat-appears]. Last match before injury: loss to El Grande Americano at the July 14 Main Event taping. Waller announced Theory injured and quietly split the team on Raw July 21. Reported removed from WWE's internal active roster (PWInsider, corroborated by Fightful Select), August 6 [https://www.si.com/fannation/wrestling/wwe/austin-theory-reportedly-removed-from-wwe-active-roster ; https://www.wrestlinginc.com/1918519/wwe-raw-grayson-waller-austin-theory-injured-quietly-breaks-up-tag-team]. As a hooded masked man, superkicked and stomped CM Punk in the men's WarGames match at Survivor Series, November 29, helping The Vision (Breakker, Reed, Lesnar, Logan Paul, McIntyre) win [https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2039103/wwe-survivor-series-war-games-2025-mystery-man-team-vision]. Unmasked on Raw December 15 with a shaved head [https://www.fightful.com/?p=272256]; formally inducted by Paul Heyman December 29 [Wikipedia].
- 2026: Royal Rumble January 31, entrant No. 15, 10:08, eliminated La Parka, eliminated by LA Knight [https://www.wwe.com/article/2026-royal-rumble-match-stats]. World Tag titles with Logan Paul March 30 (Street Fight over The Usos, after IShowSpeed hit LA Knight with brass knuckles) [Wrestling Inc]. Lost with Paul and IShowSpeed to LA Knight and The Usos at WrestleMania 42 Night 1, April 18 (Knight's BFT on Theory) [https://www.fightful.com/?p=300480]. Retained vs Street Profits at Saturday Night's Main Event May 23 (Heyman put Theory's foot on the rope) [https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25427927-logan-paul-theory-beat-street-profits-retain-tag-titles-wwe-saturday-nights-main-event]. Lost titles June 22 with Breakker (Seth Rollins hit Theory with the knuckles). Regained with Breakker July 6 (Maxxine Dupri low blow on Dawkins; Dupri kissed Theory after) [Wrestling Inc]. Lost to Penta and Je'Von Evans at Crown Jewel, November 7, Riyadh: Bronson Reed interfered, Oba Femi returned to put Reed through a table, Penta hit Theory with a Mexican Destroyer and Evans won with the OG Cutter on Theory [Khel Now; Sacnilk, 14:35].

## Character eras / personas
- Evolve/WWN young champion (2017–2019): the teenage-to-22 prodigy holding Evolve and WWN gold, wrestling NXT names (Adam Cole, Johnny Gargano) through the WWE–Evolve relationship [WWE.com 2019 release].
- The Way's apprentice (2020–2021): Johnny Gargano's protege in NXT's sitcom-heel family.
- McMahon's protege / selfie heel (late 2021–2022): stole Cleopatra's Egg, took selfies with opponents [Wikipedia], one-name "Theory".
- Cena's heir-heel (2022–2023): US Champion, the WrestleMania 39 win. Theory cites Cena as an influence [Wikipedia].
- A-Town Down Under (2023–2025): the Aussie-Atlanta braggart tag duo with Grayson Waller.
- The Vision's masked man, then shaved-head enforcer (2025–present).

## Factions
- The Way (NXT, 2020–21): Johnny Gargano, Candice LeRae, Indi Hartwell, Austin Theory.
- A-Town Down Under (SmackDown/Raw, 2023–25): with Grayson Waller.
- The Vision (Raw, 2025–present): founded by Seth Rollins with Paul Heyman after WrestleMania 41 (April 2025); Bron Breakker and the group turned on Rollins in October 2025, before Theory joined [https://wrestletalk.com/news/wwe-seth-rollins-bron-breakkes-betrayal-october/ ; https://www.f4wonline.com/news/wwe/huge-betrayal-takes-place-on-wwe-raw]. Current: Bron Breakker, Bronson Reed, Logan Paul; Maxxine Dupri allied in 2026.

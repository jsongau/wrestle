# FTR: Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler

## Canonical identity
- Team names: The Mechanics (NXT house shows), Dash and Dawson, The Revival (WWE, from February 2016), FTR (AEW, 2020 to present; WWE kept "The Revival" name). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTR_(professional_wrestling)
- Dax Harwood: real name David Michael Harwood; born June 30, 1984, Whiteville, North Carolina; debut August 13, 2004; WWE ring name Scott Dawson. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dax_Harwood
- Cash Wheeler: real name Daniel Marshall Wheeler; born May 17, 1987, Asheville, North Carolina; debut 2005; WWE ring name Dash Wilder. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash_Wheeler
- Managers: Tully Blanchard (2020 to 2021); Stokely Hathaway ("Big Stoke") since the April 23, 2025 Dynamite. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTR_(professional_wrestling)
- ⚑ Team formation credited to Dusty Rhodes in 2014 (first match listed July 17, 2014). Single source (Wikipedia); not independently confirmed.

## Young Bucks rivalry
- Full Gear, November 7, 2020: Young Bucks won the AEW tag titles from FTR. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTR_(professional_wrestling)
- April 6, 2022 Dynamite: FTR evened a series with the Bucks while retaining the ROH and AAA titles. Source: https://www.fightful.com/?p=99783
- All In, Wembley, 2023: FTR retained the AEW tag titles over the Bucks. Source: https://bleacherreport.com/articles/10085734-ftr-beat-young-bucks-retain-aew-tag-team-titles-at-all-in-2023-at-wembley
- Dynasty, April 2024: Bucks beat FTR in a ladder match for the vacant titles (tournament final), with Jack Perry interfering. Source: https://bleacherreport.com/articles/10116474-young-bucks-beat-ftr-with-jack-perrys-help-to-win-aew-tag-team-titles-at-dynasty
- Revolution, March 15, 2026: FTR retained over the Bucks ("FTR vs. Young Bucks V"). Sources: https://www.f4wonline.com/news/aew/ftr-vs-young-bucks-aew-revolution-2026 ; https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25400927-cope-and-christian-return-after-ftr-beat-young-bucks-retain-aew-tag-team-titles-revolution
- All Out, September 26, 2026: Bucks won a three-way ladder match over Copeland and Cage (champions) and FTR. Source: https://wrestlinginc.com/2269980/aew-all-out-2026-young-bucks-ladder-match-four-time-tag-team-champions/

## 2025 to 2026 timeline
- Worlds End, December 28, 2024: teamed with Adam Copeland as Rated FTR. April 2025: turned on Copeland after losing a trios title match at Dynasty. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTR_(professional_wrestling)
- June 21, 2026: Dax Harwood announced a break from wrestling, the team's first in 12 years, citing exhaustion and injuries (bicep tear, hip labral tear, shoulder). Source: https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2198441/aew-ftr-announces-hiatus-wrestling/

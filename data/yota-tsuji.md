# Yota Tsuji (辻陽太)

- Ring name: Yota Tsuji. Legal name: Yota Tsuji (辻陽太). Source: https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/辻陽太
- Born: September 8, 1993, Yokohama, Kanagawa, Japan. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yota_Tsuji ; https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/辻陽太
- Age 33 at the October 12, 2026 defense (Daily Sports): https://www.daily.co.jp/ring/2026/10/13/0020921775.shtml

- Young Lion (2018-2021): black trunks, Boston crab. Debut April 10, 2018; first singles win over Yuya Uemura April 13, 2019 (⚑ Wikipedia). Send-off match: lost to Tetsuya Naito, Summer Struggle, Korakuen Hall, August 1, 2021. https://www.fightful.com/?p=84497
- Excursion (2021-2023): about 18 months mainly with RevPro in the UK, then CMLL from late 2022 as "Yota", plus RevPro and wXw dates. https://cultaholic.com/posts/yota-tsuji-returns-to-njpw-at-wrestling-dontaku
- Avoided the LA Dojo on purpose: he gave NJPW an excuse about his US visa because dojo graduates wrestled like "clones" of Katsuyori Shibata. https://www.postwrestling.com/?p=92535
- L.I.J. prodigy (2023-2025): "the leader of the new era" after the NJC win. https://www.postwrestling.com/2024/03/20/new-japan-cup-2024-winner-crowned/
- Unbound champion (2026-): title revival, NJPW-first stance, wants "an equal relationship with AEW". https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2265333/aew-njpw-yota-tsuji-equalize-relationship/

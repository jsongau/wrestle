## ⚑ flags

1. Crown Jewel champion vs. champion matches. Reigns beat Zayn and Green beat Morgan for the honorary Crown Jewel Championships. These were not brand title matches. POST (S3) confirms Zayn kept the WWE Championship and Reigns kept the World Heavyweight title. For Liv Morgan, no source says outright that the Women's World title was off the line. She lost by pinfall. Treat her as champion on the same format, and recheck after Raw on Nov 9.
2. Leads that were wrong or incomplete. The World Tag lead (Penta & Evans, Nov 7) checked out. TNA (Slater, Oct 11), Trick Williams (Sep 6, 2nd reign) and Tsuji (Jun 14, 2nd reign) checked out. Zayn does hold the Undisputed title. Oba Femi is not NXT champion; Grayson Waller is. Stratton holds the WWE Women's title and Morgan holds the Women's World title. Not in the leads: Ethan Page won the IC title on Nov 2. Josh Bishop won the MLW title on Nov 7. Atticus Cogar won back the GCW title on Nov 7.
3. Wikipedia's "List of current champions in WWE" (as of Nov 1) is stale for IC, World Tag and WWE Women's. The Crown Jewel 2026 Wikipedia page still shows no results. Do not use either as the only source.
4. Women's World Championship lineage. Vaquer won the title from Morgan at a non-televised live event in Santiago, Chile (28-day reign per S18). Morgan won it back at Money in the Bank on 2026-10-10. Wrestling Inc guessed Oct 11. Fightful's results page and Wikipedia both use Oct 10, a Saturday.
5. MFT lineup. Wikipedia lists the champions as Tama & Talla Tonga. Wrestling Inc names "Tama, Erik, and Talla" as MFT members and does not say which two wrestled. Check WWE.com's title page before printing names.
6. Zayn venue. Mexico City comes from Fightful's live-results URL slug ("cdmx"). No fetched article states the arena. The WWE.com recap URL is dated 2026-09-18, but every outlet gives the title change as the 2026-09-11 live SmackDown.
7. GCW. Only one fetched source (Fightful) states the Crossroads result in full. 411Mania confirms "new World Champion crowned" in its headline, but the page would not load. "2nd reign" is inferred from Cogar's first reign (2025-11-22 to 2026-08-15, S44). The stipulation (billed as no rules on Oct 11) is not confirmed in results.
8. NWA. Taped vs. aired: won 2025-08-16 at NWA 77, aired 2025-11-18. NWA and Wikipedia use the taping date. The latest confirmed defense is Hard Times 6 on 2026-06-06 (aired 2026-08-22). No NWA 78 (2026-07-25) result was found in a reliable source; a pending reader comment says Mason retained. NWA's own site still calls him reigning. This is the weakest row for recency.
9. ROH World. The last confirmed date with Bandido as champion is Oct 13-16, 2026 (S34, S35). ROH tapes weekly HonorClub shows. No change was found through Nov 8.
10. TNA tapes iMPACT ahead. No taping spoiler showing a change after Bound for Glory was found. Venue for Bound for Glory not stated in the sources fetched.
11. Reign counts for Stratton, Penta & Evans, Page and Waller are left off. No fetched source stated them.

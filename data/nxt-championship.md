# NXT Championship

## Canonical identity
- Name: NXT Championship (WWE NXT brand's top men's title). Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship
- Introduced: 2012. ⚑ Wikipedia gives July 1, 2012 in the infobox and August 1, 2012 in its history section. Publish only "2012". Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship
- First champion: Seth Rollins, winner of the eight-man Gold Rush tournament (four NXT and four main-roster wrestlers). Beat Jinder Mahal in the final. Taped July 26, 2012; aired August 29, 2012. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship
- WWE.com dates Rollins' reign from August 29, 2012 (air date), 133 days. Source: https://www.wwe.com/titlehistory/nxt-championship

## Place in WWE
- Top title of NXT, WWE's developmental brand. Was billed as a world championship while NXT ran as WWE's third brand (September 2019 to September 2021); that label was dropped when NXT returned to a developmental role in September 2021. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship
- During that period it was an option for the Royal Rumble winner at WrestleMania 37. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NXT_Championship
- Not classed today as one of WWE's world titles (WWE Championship, World Heavyweight Championship). ⚑ Wikipedia also notes WWE's official history once described McIntyre's 2017 win as a world championship.

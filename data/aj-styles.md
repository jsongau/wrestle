## 2. Career Overview

AJ Styles is one of the most decorated and acclaimed wrestlers of his generation, a genuine grand-slam-level champion in multiple major promotions. After breaking in on Georgia independents in 1998, he became the original franchise player of NWA-TNA (2002–2013), where he defined the high-flying X-Division, won its first Triple Crown and Grand Slam, and reigned as both NWA and TNA World Champion.

A 2014–2016 run in New Japan Pro-Wrestling reinvented him as a cocky heel and de facto figurehead of the Bullet Club, producing two IWGP Heavyweight Championship reigns and a cluster of classic matches that cemented his reputation as the best wrestler outside WWE. That reputation drove a high-profile 2016 WWE debut.

In WWE (2016–2026) he became a two-time WWE Champion — his second reign lasting 371 days, among the longest of the modern era — plus a multi-time United States Champion, Intercontinental Champion, and Raw Tag Team Champion. He is widely credited with proving that an international/independent star with no WWE developmental pedigree could headline at the top, helping legitimize the globalized, workrate-driven main-event style.

## 3. Era: NWA / TNA (2002–2013)

Styles signed with NWA-TNA in May 2002 and appeared on its very first weekly PPV, becoming the homegrown star the company built its identity around. He first made his name in the fast-paced X-Division ("not about weight limits, it's about no limits") before graduating to the main event, becoming TNA's first Triple Crown and first Grand Slam Champion. In 2010 he was mentored by Ric Flair, who dubbed him "The New Nature Boy," and headlined the Four Horsemen-homage stable Fortune. His defining rivalries came against Christopher Daniels and Samoa Joe (the X-Division trio), Kurt Angle (2008, built around the Karen Angle storyline), plus Sting, Jeff Jarrett, and the Main Event Mafia. He won the 2013 Bound for Glory Series and departed in December 2013 after contract talks broke down.

**Title reigns (TNA/NWA):**
- **NWA World Heavyweight Championship (3x):**
  - Reign 1: won 2003 in a three-way involving Jeff Jarrett (first TNA Triple Crown champion); lost to Jarrett Oct 22, 2003
  - Reign 2: won steel cage vs. Jarrett Apr 21, 2004; lost to Ron "The Truth" Killings May 19, 2004
  - Reign 3: won from Jarrett at Hard Justice May 15, 2005; lost to Raven at Slammiversary (King of the Mountain) June 19, 2005
- **TNA World Heavyweight Championship (2x):**
  - Reign 1: won a five-way at No Surrender Sept 20, 2009 (over Angle, Sting, Morgan, Hernandez), ~211 days; lost to Rob Van Dam at Lockdown (Apr 2010) *(one agent dated this reign to Sept 2008–Apr 2009; the year is unresolved — see Open Questions)*
  - Reign 2: won at Bound for Glory Oct 2013 (def. Bully Ray); vacated/unified late Oct 2013 as he left TNA
- **TNA X-Division Championship (6x):** inaugural champion 2002; further reigns Aug 28, 2002; Oct 23, 2002 (def. Syxx-Pac); June 9, 2004 (def. Frankie Kazarian); Sept 11, 2005 at Unbreakable (def. Daniels & Joe); Nov 2, 2006 (def. Chris Sabin)
- **TNA Legends/Global/Television Championship (2x):** same continuously-renamed title, c. 2009–2010
- **NWA World Tag Team Championship (4x):** with Jerry Lynn (2002), Abyss, and Christopher Daniels (x2, 2006 vs. LAX)
- **TNA World Tag Team Championship (2x):** with Travis Tomko (won BFG Oct 2007, ~184 days) and Kurt Angle (2012)

## 4. Era: NJPW / Bullet Club (2014–2016)

Styles signed with NJPW in early 2014 (Wikipedia lists Mar 27, 2014) and debuted at Invasion Attack on April 6, 2014, attacking IWGP champion Kazuchika Okada and revealing himself as Bullet Club's newest member — stepping in as figurehead the same night founder Prince Devitt (later Finn Bálor) departed. The run produced a string of acclaimed matches against Okada, Tanahashi, Nakamura, Ibushi, and Suzuki, and is frequently cited as his finest work. His final NJPW appearance was at Wrestle Kingdom 10 (Jan 4, 2016), a lauded IWGP Intercontinental title challenge lost to Shinsuke Nakamura; days later the group storyline-ejected him and installed Kenny Omega as leader as Styles left for WWE.

**Title reigns (NJPW):**
- **IWGP Heavyweight Championship (2x):**
  - Reign 1: won May 3, 2014 at Wrestling Dontaku (def. Okada), ~163 days; lost Oct 13, 2014 at King of Pro-Wrestling to Hiroshi Tanahashi
  - Reign 2: won Feb 11, 2015 at The New Beginning (def. Tanahashi), ~145 days; successfully defended vs. Kota Ibushi at Invasion Attack (Apr 5, 2015); lost July 5, 2015 at Dominion 7.5 to Okada

> Styles was the on-screen figurehead of Bullet Club, though he has publicly disputed being its literal "leader." He did **not** hold IWGP Tag Team gold, and — contrary to a common misconception — never held the ROH **World** Championship.

## 5. Era: WWE (2016–2025)

Styles debuted as a surprise #3 entrant at the 2016 Royal Rumble (Jan 24, 2016), was assigned to SmackDown, and quickly reached the main event. His career-validating 2016 feud with John Cena — including a clean SummerSlam win — established him at the top. He held the WWE Championship twice (the second reign a 371-day run), headlined WrestleMania 34 retaining against Nakamura, led The Club / The O.C. with Luke Gallows and Karl Anderson, and later feuded with Edge, Judgment Day, Seth Rollins, and the Bloodline. Brand moves: SmackDown (2016), Raw (Apr 2019 Shake-up), SmackDown (2020), Raw (2021, w/ Omos), SmackDown (2023).

**Title reigns (WWE):**
- **WWE Championship (2x):**
  - Reign 1: won from Dean Ambrose at Backlash Sept 11, 2016 (~140 days); lost to John Cena at Royal Rumble Jan 29, 2017
  - Reign 2: won from Jinder Mahal on SmackDown (Manchester, England) Nov 7, 2017 — first WWE title change on English soil — held 371 days; lost to Daniel Bryan on SmackDown Nov 13, 2018
- **United States Championship (3x):**
  - Reign 1: won from Kevin Owens at an MSG live event July 7, 2017 (16 days); lost to Owens at Battleground July 23, 2017
  - Reign 2: won triple threat (Owens & Jericho) on SmackDown July 25, 2017 (~75 days); lost to Baron Corbin at Hell in a Cell Oct 8, 2017
  - Reign 3: won from Ricochet at Extreme Rules July 14, 2019 (~134 days); lost to Rey Mysterio on Raw Nov 25, 2019
- **Intercontinental Championship (1x):** won tournament final vs. Daniel Bryan on SmackDown June 12, 2020 (~74 days); lost to Jeff Hardy Aug 2020
- **Raw Tag Team Championship, w/ Omos (1x):** won from The New Day at WrestleMania 37 Apr 10, 2021 (133 days); lost to RK-Bro at SummerSlam Aug 21, 2021

## 8. Personas & In-Ring Style

**Persona eras:** (1) TNA babyface franchise / X-Division ace (2002–2013), origin of "The Phenomenal One," anointed by Sting to "carry the torch"; (2) NJPW Bullet Club heel (2014–2016), a cocky, dominant reinvention that "changed everything" and elevated his global stock; (3) WWE main-event ace (2016–2026), two-time WWE Champion and later O.C. figurehead. His 2013 look change (beard, unkempt hair, all-black) marked a heel/loner turn.

**Style:** A hybrid of aerial/high-flying offense and technical mat wrestling, hardened by strong-style influence absorbed in Japan. The Styles Clash sits at the center of a "safest vs. most dangerous move" debate — Styles argues it is safe with the chin tucked and hands flat, but opponents who lift their heads (reportedly Lionheart/Adam Pearce Mar 1, 2014, and Yoshitatsu Nov 8, 2014) suffered neck injuries, and WWE has periodically restricted the move. The Calf Crusher (submission), added on his 2013 TNA return, became a primary WWE finish alongside the Phenomenal Forearm.

## 9. Factions & Tag Teams

- **Bullet Club** (NJPW/ROH, 2014–2016): on-screen figurehead after Prince Devitt's departure; ejected Jan 2016.
- **The Club → The O.C.** (WWE): "The Club" formed May 2016 with Luke Gallows & Karl Anderson; reunited and rebranded "The O.C." (Original Club) April 2019 on Raw; disbanded April 2020 (Gallows/Anderson's release), reformed October 2022, quietly disbanded September 2024. Michin (Mia Yim) later joined.
- **Fortune / Fourtune** (TNA, 2010–2012): Ric Flair-associated Four Horsemen homage with Kazarian, Robert Roode, James Storm and others; initially aligned with Immortal, then turned face against it in 2011.
- **Good Brothers** (Gallows & Anderson): Styles' most enduring alliance, spanning Bullet Club, The Club, and The O.C. (2014–2024).
- **Other:** TNA Front Line with Samoa Joe vs. Main Event Mafia (2008–2009); Christian's Coalition (TNA, 2007–2008); tag pairings with Christopher Daniels; short-lived "Y2AJ" with Chris Jericho (WWE, early 2016).

## 10. Rivalries & Relationships

- **John Cena** (WWE, 2016) — self-cited greatest WWE feud; clean SummerSlam 2016 win, plus MITB 2016; Cena won their Royal Rumble 2017 title match.
- **Samoa Joe & Christopher Daniels** (TNA/ROH) — the foundational X-Division trio; Daniels feud spanned Iron Man, Ultimate X, and "I Quit" matches while the two also teamed for tag gold; longtime real-life friends.
- **Kazuchika Okada & Hiroshi Tanahashi & Shinsuke Nakamura** (NJPW) — his acclaimed IWGP-era opponents; the Nakamura rivalry carried into WWE (WM34).
- **Roman Reigns** (WWE, 2016), **Kurt Angle** (TNA, 2008–2013; Angle has called him the best talent he faced), **Jeff Jarrett** (first Triple Crown), **Randy Orton** (WWE, 2017–2019), **Chris Jericho** and **Dean Ambrose** (WWE, 2016), and **Gunther** (final/retirement match).
- **Mentors:** Ric Flair (TNA, "The New Nature Boy," Jan 21, 2010). Styles mentored Jimmy Rave in ROH (2003–2004).
- **Finn Bálor:** treated more as a parallel/comparison (both led Bullet Club) than a defined marquee feud.

## 11. Media, Gaming & Off-Ring

- **Video games:** Cover athlete of **WWE 2K19** (announced June 18, 2018; released Oct 9, 2018; "Million Dollar Challenge"). Playable across the WWE 2K series continuously — appearing from 2K16 through 2K26, including Retro/alternate versions in recent entries.

# The Steiner Brothers

## Canonical identity
- Members: Rick Steiner (Robert Rechsteiner, born March 9, 1961, Bay City, Michigan) and Scott Steiner (Scott Rechsteiner, born July 29, 1962, Bay City, Michigan). [W-Rick] [W-Scott]
- Formed: 1989 (Scott accompanied Rick at Starrcade '88 and Chi-Town Rumble; teaming by early 1989). [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- Disbanded as a regular team: 1998 (Scott's SuperBrawl VIII turn). Sporadic reunions through 2020. [W-Team] [MGO]
- Early valets/managers: Missy Hyatt, Robin Green (later Woman); Ted DiBiase introduced as manager on Nitro, August 4, 1997. [W-Team]
- Nicknames: Rick "The Dog-Faced Gremlin"; Scott "Big Poppa Pump" (1998 onward). [WWE-HOF-CER] [W-Scott]
- Team status as of 2026-11-09: inactive as a regular team. No current championship. Both members are WWE Hall of Famers (Class of 2022). [W-Team] [WWE-HOF-CER]

## Amateur backgrounds (University of Michigan)
- Scott: NCAA All-American, placed sixth as a senior in 1986; three-time Big Ten finalist; career record 125-51-2. [MGO]
- ⚑ Scott's weight class: Michigan Athletics says 190 lb; Wikipedia says he wrestled at 156 lb. Page uses no weight class. [MGO] [W-Scott]
- Rick (listed by Michigan as Rob Rechsteiner): Big Ten finalist at heavyweight, 90 career wins, program record for fastest fall (0:15); injury cut short his 1984 senior season. [MGO] Wikipedia: second at the 1983 Big Ten Championships, NCAA qualifier, education degree. [W-Rick]
- Both earned education degrees per Wikipedia (Scott explicitly; Rick bachelor's in education). [W-Scott] [W-Rick]
- WWF run used Michigan's "The Victors" as entrance music. [MGO] [W-Team]

## Championships and accolades
- NWA/WCW World Tag Team Championship: 7 reigns as a team. First: November 1, 1989, Atlanta, def. Fabulous Freebirds; lost to Doom at Capital Combat, May 19, 1990. [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- ⚑ One reign (July 24, 1996, def. Harlem Heat) lasted three days and was never mentioned on TV; the "7" count follows WWE and standard histories, but Wikipedia's narrative does not date every reign. [W-Team]
- NWA/WCW United States Tag Team Championship: 1 reign, won August 24, 1990, East Rutherford, NJ, def. Midnight Express; vacated February 20, 1991 after winning the World Tag titles. [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- IWGP Tag Team Championship: 2 reigns. Won March 21, 1991 (Tokyo) def. Hiroshi Hase and Kensuke Sasaki, lost November 5, 1991 to Hase and Keiji Muto; won June 26, 1992 def. Vader and Bam Bam Bigelow, lost November 22, 1992 to Scott Norton and Tony Halme. [W-Scott] [W-Team]
- WWF Tag Team Championship: 2 reigns. Won June 14, 1993 (Columbus, OH) from Money Inc.; lost June 16 (Rockford, IL house show); regained June 19 (St. Louis house show); lost to the Quebecers on Raw, September 13, 1993. [W-Team] [MGO] [WWE-HOF]
- ⚑ "11 world tag team titles" (Michigan Athletics) is a cross-promotion sum: 7 WCW + 2 IWGP + 2 WWF. [MGO]
- WCW World Television Championship: Rick 3 reigns (first from Mike Rotunda at Starrcade '88), Scott 2. [W-Rick] [W-Team]
- WCW United States Heavyweight Championship: Scott 2 reigns; Rick 1 (won February 5, 2001 def. Shane Douglas, lost to Booker T at Greed, March 18, 2001). [W-Scott] [W-Rick]
- WCW World Heavyweight Championship (Scott): won November 26, 2000 at Mayhem def. Booker T; lost to Booker T on the final Nitro, March 26, 2001. [W-Scott]
- PWI Tag Team of the Year 1990 and 1993; Wrestling Observer Tag Team of the Year 1990; Observer Match of the Year 1991 (vs Hase and Sasaki, March 21, 1991, Tokyo); PWI Match of the Year 1991 (vs Lex Luger and Sting, SuperBrawl I). [W-Team]
- PWI ranked them No. 2 of the 100 best tag teams of the PWI Years (2003). [W-Team]
- George Tragos/Lou Thesz Hall of Fame, Class of 2014. [W-Team]
- WWE Hall of Fame, Class of 2022: inducted April 1, 2022, American Airlines Center, Dallas; inducted by Bron Breakker; opened the ceremony. [WWE-HOF] [WWE-HOF-CER]
- ⚑ The announcement article on WWE.com links a Ringer report dated March 28, 2022; Michigan Athletics' story is also dated March 28, 2022. Treat March 28, 2022 as the announcement date. [WWE-HOF] [MGO] [RINGER]

## Signature offense
- Frankensteiner: Scott's huracanrana variant where the opponent lands on the head. [W-Scott]
- Steiner Screwdriver: Scott's vertical suplex into a reverse piledriver. [W-Scott]
- Steiner Recliner: Scott's camel clutch submission (later used by nephew Bron Breakker). [W-Scott] [site: /wrestlers/bron-breakker/]
- Rick: signature diving bulldog. [W-Rick]

## Career timeline
- 1983: Rick debuts (introduced by George Steele; trained by Brad Rheingans and Eddie Sharkey). Later charter member of the Varsity Club with Mike Rotunda and Kevin Sullivan. [W-Rick]
- 1989: team forms in NWA/WCW; first World Tag reign November 1, 1989. [W-Team]
- 1990-91: US Tag titles (Aug 24, 1990); World Tag titles again (Feb 18, 1991); IWGP Tag titles March 21, 1991, making them the first team to hold WWF, WCW and IWGP tag gold (per Wikipedia, counted after their WWF win). [W-Team]
- 1992: second IWGP reign (June 26). WWF debut interview on Prime Time Wrestling, December 21, 1992. [W-Team] [W-Scott]
- January 11, 1993: on the first episode of Monday Night Raw. January 24, 1993: Royal Rumble debut, def. Beverly Brothers. April 4, 1993: def. the Headshrinkers at WrestleMania IX. [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- June-September 1993: two WWF Tag reigns; defended vs Heavenly Bodies at SummerSlam (Aug 30). [W-Team]
- November 24, 1993: Survivor Series "All-Americans" with Lex Luger and The Undertaker def. Foreign Fanatics. [W-Team]
- January 22, 1994: both in the Royal Rumble. Left WWF mid-1994. [W-Team]
- July 28 to October 28, 1995: ECW run (debut Middletown, NY; WrestlePalooza 1995 six-man with Eddie Guerrero vs 2 Cold Scorpio, Dean Malenko and Cactus Jack; tags vs Dudleys, Eliminators, Raven and Stevie Richards). [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- 1996: WCW return; feuds with Road Warriors, Sting and Lex Luger, Harlem Heat, The Outsiders. [W-Team] [WWE-HOF]
- August 4, 1997: Ted DiBiase introduced as manager. [W-Team]
- February 22, 1998: SuperBrawl VIII, Scott attacks Rick, hands the tag titles to Scott Hall and Kevin Nash, joins the nWo. [W-Team] [W-Scott]
- 2000-01: Scott's Big Poppa Pump singles peak; WCW World title. Last WCW team match March 5, 2001 (loss to Booker T and Diamond Dallas Page). [W-Team] [W-Scott]
- 2002: NJPW reunion tags. 2007: TNA reunion (beat Team 3D at Hard Justice and Bound for Glory). Jan 4, 2008 NJPW loss to IWGP champs Giant Bernard and Tomko. [W-Team]
- February 29, 2020: six-man with Dory Funk Jr. [W-Team]
- April 1, 2022: WWE Hall of Fame. [WWE-HOF-CER]

## Scott Steiner singles (WWE/TNA)
- Royal Rumble January 19, 2003: Scott def. Triple H by DQ (World Heavyweight Championship match). No Way Out February 23, 2003: lost. [W-Scott]
- Test and Stacy Keibler storyline 2003: Bad Blood June 15 Scott def. Test for Keibler's services; Test won her back on Raw August 18; Unforgiven September 21 Scott lost to Test. [W-Scott] [site: /wrestlers/stacy-keibler/]
- TNA: debut March 12, 2006 (Destination X); final TNA match August 2, 2019 (Unbreakable). [W-Scott]
- March 6, 2020: cardiac emergency at an Impact taping. [W-Scott]

## Outside the ring
- Rick: Cherokee County (GA) School District board, District 4, since 2006; unopposed in the May 19, 2026 Republican primary. [W-Rick] [BRANCH]
- Rick's son Bronson Rechsteiner wrestles in WWE as Bron Breakker and inducted the team into the Hall of Fame. [W-Rick] [WWE-HOF-CER]

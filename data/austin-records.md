# "Stone Cold" Steve Austin — Career Match Records (Split by Promotion)

**Real name:** Steven James Anderson (birth) → Steven James Williams (after adoption by stepfather) → legally changed to **Steve Austin** in December 2007.
**Ring names by era:** "Stunning" Steve Austin (WCW) → "Superstar"/"Stunning" Steve Austin (ECW) → "The Ringmaster" then **"Stone Cold" Steve Austin** (WWF/WWE).
**Signature finish:** **Stone Cold Stunner** (also the Million Dollar Dream / cobra clutch and the Lou Thesz Press + elbow in early WWF; the Stungun in WCW).

*Sources cross-checked against Wikipedia, Cagematch, WWE.com, Online World of Wrestling, and ProFightDB. Items where sources conflict or exact dates are hard to pin down are flagged with ⚠️.*

## 1. WCW Record (1991–1995) — as "Stunning" Steve Austin

### Championships
- **WCW World Television Championship ×2**
  - Won June 3, 1991 def. **Bobby Eaton** (weeks after debuting)
  - Lost April 27, 1992 to **Barry Windham**; **regained May 23, 1992** def. Windham; lost Sept 2, 1992 to **Ricky Steamboat** (Clash of the Champions XX)
- **WCW United States Heavyweight Championship ×2**
  - Won Dec 27, 1993 (**Starrcade '93**) def. **Dustin Rhodes** (2-of-3 falls); lost Aug 24, 1994 (Clash XXVIII) to Ricky Steamboat
  - Awarded 2nd reign by forfeit Sept 18, 1994 (Fall Brawl '94) after Steamboat's back injury — then **lost it ~35 seconds later to "Hacksaw" Jim Duggan** the same night
- **WCW World Tag Team Championship (with Brian Pillman — "The Hollywood Blonds")**
  - Won **March 2, 1993** (aired ~March 27) def. **Ricky Steamboat & Shane Douglas**; held ~5½ months, lost **Aug 18, 1993** to **Arn Anderson & Paul Roma**
  - ⚠️ **On the "×2" note:** Sources record this as **one reign** that was recognized simultaneously as the **NWA World Tag Team** *and* **WCW World Tag Team** Championship (the belts were unified at the time). Counting it as "×2" reflects the two lineages of that single reign — it was not two separate tag-title runs.

## 2. ECW Record (1995–1996)

**Title status: ⚠️ CONFIRMED — Austin held NO ECW championship.** His ECW run was short (roughly Sept 1995 – late 1995/early 1996) and pivotal for **character reinvention, not title accomplishment.** Paul Heyman paid him ~$500/night largely to **cut promos** — the anti-establishment, anti-WCW, anti-Bischoff/Hulk Hogan tirades that rebuilt his persona and directly led to WWF interest.

## 3. WWF/WWE Record

### Championships & Accolades
- **WWF/WWE Championship ×6**
- **WWF Intercontinental Championship ×2**
- **WWF/World Tag Team Championship ×4** (partners incl. Shawn Michaels, Dude Love, Undertaker, Triple H)
- **Million Dollar Championship ×1** (awarded as "The Ringmaster")
- **1996 King of the Ring** (birth of "Austin 3:16")
- **Royal Rumble winner ×3 — 1997, 1998, 2001** (record, tied)
- **WWE Triple Crown Champion**; **WWE Hall of Fame 2009**

### Debut / gimmick evolution
- Dec 1995 / Jan 1996: debuts as **"The Ringmaster,"** managed by Ted DiBiase, awarded the Million Dollar Championship.
- Adopts **"Stone Cold"** ring name (early 1996); by **WrestleMania XII (Mar 1996)** wrestling as Stone Cold.

## 5. Quick Accuracy Flags

- ⚠️ **WCW tag-title "×2":** one reign, dual (NWA + WCW) recognition — not two separate runs.
- ⚠️ **Second U.S. Title reign (Sept 1994):** won by forfeit and lost to Jim Duggan within ~35 seconds the same night; some databases list it, some minimize it.
- ⚠️ **Exact early WCW TV dates / some 1993–94 house-show and Clash finishes** (e.g., Clash XXIII fall/DQ specifics, Fall Brawl '93 result) vary between sources — verify against Cagematch/ProFightDB before publishing individual match finishes.
- ⚠️ **ECW dates** are approximate; ECW record-keeping is spotty. The key verified fact: **no ECW title**, run was promo-driven.
- ⚠️ **Royal Rumble 2001** occurred **January 21, 2001** (WrestleMania X-Seven was April 1, 2001 — do not confuse the two events, which fell in the same "road to WrestleMania" storyline).

# Stacy Keibler: The Legs of WWE

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Stacy Ann-Marie Keibler. [W]
- Born: October 14, 1979, Rosedale, Maryland, USA. [W] [CBS]
  - ⚑ Cagematch gives the birthplace as Baltimore. Rosedale is in Baltimore County; WWE billed her from Baltimore. [CM-P] [WWE-P]
- Billed from: Baltimore, Maryland. [W] [WWE-P]
- Height: 5 ft 11 in (180 cm). [W] [CM-P] [WWE-P]
- Weight: ⚑ 136 lb (62 kg) billed per Wikipedia; Cagematch lists 134 lb (61 kg). [W] [CM-P]
- Education: Catholic High School of Baltimore; Towson University, mass communication. [W]
  - ⚑ Wikipedia says she attended Towson; CBS Baltimore calls her a Towson graduate. [W] [CBS]
- Before wrestling: dance from age three; Baltimore Ravens cheerleader at 18. [W] [WWE-HOF]
- Trained: WCW Power Plant (Wikipedia). Cagematch names Diamond Doll, Paul Orndorff and Madusa as trainers. [W] [CM-P]
- Ring names: Skye (Nitro Girl), Miss Hancock, Stacy Keibler, Super Stacy. [W] [CM-P]
  - ⚑ Spelled "Miss Hancock" by Cagematch and "Ms. Hancock" by WWE.com and the Bash at the Beach 2000 article. Same character. [CM] [WWE-HOF] [BATB]
- Signature move: roundhouse kick (WWE.com); spinning heel kick (Cagematch). [WWE-P] [CM-P]
- Nicknames: The Legs of WCW, The Legs of WWE, Duchess of Dudleyville, Super Stacy, "Weapon of Mass Seduction" (Bruno Tonioli, DWTS). [W] [CM-P] [WWE-P]
- Entrance theme: "Legs," a Kid Rock cover of ZZ Top, track 2 on WWF Forceable Entry (Columbia / SmackDown! Records, March 26, 2002, 4:53). It returned in WWE 2K26. [FE] [NMA]
- Status (as of 2026-11-09): retired from wrestling since 2006. WWE Hall of Famer (2023). Playable in WWE 2K26. No WWE in-ring or TV return found in searches run November 9, 2026. [W] [NMA]

## Championships and accolades
- Championships: none in WCW or WWE. Her Women's Championship challenges all lost (see record). [W] [CM]
- WCW Nitro Girl Search winner, announced on Nitro November 8, 1999 (300 entrants, eight finalists, $10,000 prize). [W] [NG]
  - ⚑ WWE.com's Hall of Fame announcement says she won the contest "in 2000." The Nitro broadcast date is November 8, 1999. [WWE-HOF] [NG]
- WWE Babe of the Year: ⚑ WWE.com's profile says 2005; Wikipedia's 2023 Hall of Fame article says 2004. Year unresolved. [WWE-P] [HOF23]
- WWE Hall of Fame, Class of 2023. Announced March 27, 2023; inducted March 31, 2023 at Crypto.com Arena, Los Angeles, by Mick Foley and Torrie Wilson. Classmates: Rey Mysterio, The Great Muta, Andy Kaufman. [WWE-HOF] [WWE-HOF2] [HOF23] [WI23]
- Dancing with the Stars season 2 (2006): third place with Tony Dovolani, behind Drew Lachey and Jerry Rice. Finale February 26, 2006. A perfect 30 for the week 5 samba; WWE.com calls it the season's first perfect score. [DWTS] [WWE-P]
- Maxim Hot 100: No. 5 in 2006, ranked every year 2006–2012. [W]

## Character eras
- Skye, Nitro Girl (late 1999 – early 2000): WCW dance troupe member after winning the fan-vote search. [NG] [W]
- Miss Hancock (2000): business-suit heel valet, first with the tag team Standards and Practices, known for table dancing; then David Flair's girlfriend, which put her in a feud with Daffney. [W] [NG] [WWE-HOF] [DAF]
- Stacy Keibler, WCW (2001): returned under her own name managing Shawn Stasiak in a feud with Bam Bam Bigelow. [W]
- Alliance mean girl (2001): WWF debut on SmackDown! June 14, 2001, then heel member of The Alliance paired with Torrie Wilson. [W] [TW]
- Duchess of Dudleyville (October 2001 – March 2002): manager of the Dudley Boyz. [W] [WWE-P]
  - ⚑ Wikipedia dates her Dudleys debut to Raw October 1, 2001; the earliest Raw results entry showing her as their manager is October 8, 2001. [W] [H01]
- Mr. McMahon's assistant (2002, SmackDown!): won the role with a desk dance. [W] [WWE-P] [CBS]
- Test's manager, then Scott Steiner's (August 2002 – December 2003): moved to Raw August 12, 2002 as Eric Bischoff's acquisition. [H02] [W]
- Randy Orton's girlfriend (early 2005): ended when Orton RKO'd her on Raw, March 21, 2005, Birmingham. [H05] [SPT]
- Super Stacy (2005): superhero sidekick to The Hurricane and Rosey. [W] [CBS] [H05]

## Career timeline
- 1999 Nov 8: wins the Nitro Girl Search on Nitro. [NG] [W]
- 2000: becomes Miss Hancock. June 5: first logged match, a mixed tag win with Diamond Dallas Page over Kimberly and Mike Awesome on Nitro (Cagematch). [CM] [W]
  - ⚑ Wikipedia calls Bash at the Beach 2000 her in-ring debut. Cagematch logs the June 5, 2000 Nitro mixed tag first. [W] [CM]
- 2000 Jul 9: loses WCW's first Wedding Gown match to Daffney at Bash at the Beach, Ocean Center, Daytona Beach. [BATB] [CM] [DAF]
- 2000 Aug 13: loses a mud match to Major Gunns at New Blood Rising, Vancouver; the implied-miscarriage finish led to the pregnancy angle being exposed as false and her removal from TV. [NBR] [W]
- 2001: returns as Stacy Keibler with Shawn Stasiak; WWF buys WCW and her contract. [W] [WI23]
- 2001 Jun 14: WWF TV debut, SmackDown!. [W]
- 2001 Jul 22: Invasion, Gund Arena, Cleveland. Loses the first tag team bra and panties match with Torrie Wilson to Lita and Trish Stratus; Mick Foley referees. [INV] [W] [TW] [CM]
- 2001 Oct 21: No Mercy, St. Louis. Loses the first lingerie match to Torrie Wilson. [NM01] [W] [TW]
- 2001 Nov 18: Survivor Series, Greensboro. At ringside as the Dudleys beat the Hardys in a steel cage to unify the WWF and WCW tag titles; she steals the cage key and passes in a table. [SS01] [WWE-P]
- 2002 Mar 17: WrestleMania X8 debut, at ringside for the Dudleys in the four-corners tag title match. [WMX8] [W]
- 2002 Mar 18: the Dudleys powerbomb her through a table on Raw, Montreal. [H02]
- 2002 May 19: Judgment Day, Nashville. Loses a WWE Women's Championship challenge to Trish Stratus. [JD02] [CM]
- 2002: Mr. McMahon's assistant on SmackDown!; Aug 12 moves to Raw; pairs with Test; Nov 2002 the "Testicles" act turns them babyface. [W] [H02]
- 2003 Jun 15: Bad Blood, Houston. Scott Steiner beats Test for her managerial services. [BB03]
- 2003 Sep 21: Unforgiven, Hershey. Test beats Steiner and wins control of both Keibler and Steiner. [UNF03]
- 2003 Dec 1: co-GM Mick Foley fires Test and Steiner on Raw; Wikipedia says this freed her from both contracts. [H03] [W]
- 2004 Mar 14: WrestleMania XX, Madison Square Garden. Loses the Playboy Evening Gown tag with Miss Jackie to Sable and Torrie Wilson. [WMXX] [CM]
- 2004 Oct 11: Raw, Manchester, England. Loses a WWE Women's Championship match to Trish Stratus. [H04] [CM]
- 2004 Oct 19: Taboo Tuesday, Milwaukee. In the Fulfill Your Fantasy battle royal for the Women's title; eliminated by Molly Holly, second-to-last out; Trish Stratus retains. [TT04] [CM]
- 2005 Mar 21: Randy Orton RKOs her on Raw. [H05] [SPT]
- 2005: Super Stacy with Hurricane and Rosey; Aug 8 loses a mixed six-person tag to the Heart Throbs and Victoria on Raw, Pittsburgh. [H05] [CM]
- 2005 Aug: moves to SmackDown! with Christy Hemme in a trade for Torrie Wilson and Candice Michelle (Wikipedia gives August 25). [W]
- 2005 Oct 25: last match, a loss to Jillian Hall on Velocity (Cagematch date). [CM] [W]
  - ⚑ Cagematch's profile field gives "end of in-ring career: 28.09.2005," but its own match list has the Velocity match on October 25, 2005. Cagematch lists SmackDown and Velocity matches by taping date. [CM-P] [CM]
- 2006 Feb 26: finishes third on Dancing with the Stars season 2. [DWTS]
- 2006 Jul 21: announces on her website that her WWE contract "has come to an end" and that she has a development deal with Touchstone TV. [WI06]
- 2007–2014: TV acting (see Outside the ring).
- 2011: appears on WWE Tough Enough. [W]
- 2014 Mar 8: marries Jared Pobre in Mexico. [W]
- 2019 Apr 6: inducts Torrie Wilson into the WWE Hall of Fame. [TW] [W] [CIN]
- 2023 Mar 31: inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame by Mick Foley and Torrie Wilson. [HOF23] [WWE-HOF2] [TW]
- 2026 Mar: playable in WWE 2K26 with "Legs" restored as her entrance music. [NMA] [W]

## Rivalries and pairings
- Torrie Wilson: Alliance partner (2001), then rival after Wilson's Tajiri romance turned her face (No Mercy 2001 lingerie match; 2002 bra and panties series). Close friends and one-time Los Angeles roommates; each inducted the other into the Hall of Fame (2019, 2023). [TW] [W] [CM]
- Trish Stratus: opponent in every Women's Championship match she had: Nov 2001, Judgment Day 2002, Oct 11 2004, Taboo Tuesday 2004. Never beat her for the title. [W] [CM] [JD02] [TT04]
- Lita: Invasion 2001 opponent and repeated tag and singles opponent 2001–2002. [INV] [CM] [H01]
- Daffney: WCW 2000 rivalry over David Flair; Bash at the Beach Wedding Gown match. [DAF] [BATB]
- Major Gunns: WCW 2000; Nitro hardcore win, New Blood Rising mud loss. [CM] [NBR]
- Molly Holly: 2004 Raw tag opponent; Stacy's singles win Oct 4, 2004. [H04] [CM]

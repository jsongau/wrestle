## 1. Stone Cold Steve Austin
- **Real name:** Steven James Anderson (later Williams)
- **Nationality:** American (Texas)
- **Debut / era active:** Debuted 1989; peak WWF run 1996–2003 (Attitude Era)
- **Primary promotions:** WCW, ECW, WWF/WWE
- **Major championships:** WWF Championship (6x, 1998–2001); WWF Intercontinental (2x); WWF Tag Team (4x); Royal Rumble winner (1997, 1998, 2001)
- **Finishing moves:** Stone Cold Stunner; (early) Stun Gun
- **Nicknames:** "Stone Cold," "The Texas Rattlesnake," "The Bionic Redneck"
- **Defining feuds:** Mr. McMahon (defining anti-authority rivalry); Bret Hart (1996–97, elevated Austin); The Rock (WrestleMania trilogy); Dude Love/Mick Foley; Triple H (2001)
- **Signature matches:** vs Bret Hart, submission match, WrestleMania 13 (1997, the double-turn); vs The Rock, WrestleMania X-Seven (2001); vs The Rock, WrestleMania XV (1999)
- **Real-life links:** Feud with Bret Hart made him a star; part of Attitude Era core with The Rock, Foley, Undertaker.
- **Cross-promotion jumps:** WCW → ECW → WWF (1995–96)
- **Bio:** The face of WWF's Attitude Era, Austin's beer-swilling, boss-defying antihero drove the company past WCW in the Monday Night Wars. His 1997 King of the Ring "Austin 3:16" promo launched a cultural phenomenon. A neck injury forced early retirement from full-time competition after WrestleMania X-Seven's peak.
- **SEO keywords:** Stone Cold Steve Austin, Austin 3:16, Attitude Era, Stone Cold Stunner, WWF Championship, Texas Rattlesnake

# The Hurricane: Gregory Shane Helms

Harvested from the old /wrestlers/the-hurricane/ page (variant X, ~2,064 words) and re-verified claim by claim. Corrections to the old page are listed at the end.

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Gregory Shane Helms. [W]
- Born: July 12, 1974, Smithfield, North Carolina. Raised in Wooster, Ohio. [W]
  - ⚑ Cagematch gives the birth year as 1972 (12.07.1972). Wikipedia gives 1974. Printed as 1974 with the dispute noted. [CM-P] [W]
- Billed from: Smithfield, Raleigh, or "Joco" (Johnston County), North Carolina. [W]
- Height: 6 ft 0 in (183 cm) per WWE.com and Wikipedia. ⚑ Cagematch lists 5 ft 11 in (180 cm). [WWE-P] [W] [CM-P]
- Weight: 215 lb (98 kg) per WWE.com and Wikipedia. ⚑ Cagematch lists 216 lb; The SmackDown Hotel and the old page list 200 lb. [WWE-P] [W] [CM-P] [TSH]
- Debut: ⚑ July 6, 1990 per the Wikipedia infobox and Cagematch; the Wikipedia body text says he first wrestled in 1991 at 17; The SmackDown Hotel says July 6, 1991. Printed as 1990 with the dispute noted. [W] [CM-P] [TSH]
- Ring names: "The Show" Shane Helms, Shane Helms, Sugar Shane, "Hollywood" Gregory Helms, The Hurricane, Gregory Helms, Hurricane Helms, Gregory Shane Helms. [W] [CM-P] [TSH]
- Nicknames (Cagematch): IBM (Insane Bump Machine), Serial Thriller, Sugar, The Show. [CM-P]
- Finishers: Vertebreaker (back-to-back double underhook piledriver, 1991–2003 per TSH); Eye of the Hurricane, renamed Nightmare on Helms Street (spinning headlock elbow drop); Shining Wizard; Hurri-chokeslam (chokeslam); avalanche swinging neckbreaker (2005–07). [CM-P] [TSH]
- Entrance theme (WWE, as The Hurricane): "Eye of the Hurricane," written and performed by Jim Johnston, disc 3 track 10 of WWE Anthology (Koch Records / SmackDown! Records, November 12, 2002), 2:51. TSH dates its use to 2001–2005 and 2009–2010. [ANTH] [TSH]
  - Other themes per TSH: "Can't Get You Out of My Heart" and "Do the Three Count" (3 Count, 1999–2001), "Vertebreaker" (Jimmy Hart and Howard Helm, 2001), "Fire Storm A" (2005–07), "It's Time" (Jim Johnston, 2007–09), "Dynasty" (Dale Oliver, TNA 2016–17). [TSH]
- X: @ShaneHelmsCom. [X] [W]
- Status (as of 2026-11-09): WWE backstage producer. Fightful called him "a WWE producer" on June 4, July 1 and July 21, 2026; the July 21 story describes him as the first person to comfort AJ Styles after Styles' retirement match loss to GUNTHER at the 2026 Royal Rumble. Cagematch lists his current role as Road Agent, WWE. Signed a WWE Legends deal March 24, 2022. Last match on WWE TV: 2021 Royal Rumble. No later release or departure found in searches run November 9, 2026. [FTF-LK] [FTF-AJ] [FTF-TAG] [CM-P] [FTF-LEG]
  - ⚑ The research brief said he "has worked for AEW and TNA in recent years." Not supported. His AEW work is one appearance, in the Elite Deletion match at Full Gear on November 7, 2020, while furloughed by WWE. His TNA run was 2015–2017. He has been a WWE producer since January 2019, apart from the April–November 2020 furlough. [PW-2020] [FTF-HARDY] [W] [WI-2017]

## Championships and accolades
Major-promotion reigns: 8. [TSH] [W]
- WCW / WWE Cruiserweight Championship (the WCW-origin lineage, 1991–2007): 3 reigns. [W] [WCW-CW]
  1. As Shane Helms: won at WCW Greed, March 18, 2001, Jacksonville, pinning Chavo Guerrero Jr. (13:57 per the Wikipedia title list footnote). Carried into the WWF after the sale. Lost to Billy Kidman on SmackDown; WWE recognizes the change as of the July 5, 2001 air date. [W] [WCW-CW] [GREED]
  2. As The Hurricane: won a triple threat over Tajiri and Billy Kidman on SmackDown, May 16, 2002. Lost to Jamie Noble at King of the Ring, June 23, 2002, Columbus. [W] [BR] [KOTR02]
  3. As Gregory Helms: won the Cruiserweight Open at the Royal Rumble, January 29, 2006, Miami, pinning Funaki after a Shining Wizard. Held until the Cruiserweight Open at No Way Out, February 18, 2007, Los Angeles, where Chavo Guerrero won by pinning Jimmy Wang Yang. 385 days; WWE recognizes 384. [RR06] [NWO07] [WCW-CW]
  - The 385-day reign is the longest in the history of that title and the only reign of a year or more. Wikipedia's Helms article adds that it was, at the time, the longest reign of any champion in SmackDown history. [WCW-CW] [W]
  - ⚑ "Longest-reigning Cruiserweight Champion" is true only for the WCW-origin title (retired 2007). The separate 2016–2022 WWE/NXT Cruiserweight Championship, which Wikipedia says is "distinct from the previous" title, had a longer reign: Jordan Devlin, 439 days (438 recognized by WWE). [NXT-CW]
- World Tag Team Championship (Raw): 2 reigns. [W]
  1. With Kane: beat Christian and Lance Storm on Raw, September 23, 2002. Lost to Chris Jericho and Christian on Raw, October 14, 2002. [W] [UNAM]
  2. With Rosey: won the Tag Team Turmoil match at Backlash, May 1, 2005, Manchester, New Hampshire, last eliminating La Résistance (13:43). Lost to Lance Cade and Trevor Murdoch at Unforgiven, September 18, 2005, Oklahoma City (7:40). [BL05] [UNF05] [ROSEY]
  - ⚑ The old page and some sources call the 2002 belts the "WWE Tag Team Championship." In September 2002 the Raw belts were the World Tag Team Championship; the old page and Wikipedia's Un-Americans article use different names for the same reign. Printed here as World Tag Team Championship. [UNAM] [W]
- WWF European Championship: 1 reign. Won from Matt Hardy on Raw is War, August 27, 2001, with help from Ivory, the night The Hurricane debuted. Lost to Bradshaw on October 22, 2001. [W]
- WWF Hardcore Championship: 1 reign. WrestleMania X8, March 17, 2002, Toronto: swung in on a rope, kicked Spike Dudley and pinned him backstage under the 24/7 rule. Lost the same night to Mighty Molly (Molly Holly), who hit him with a frying pan. [WMX8] [MOLLY]
- WCW Hardcore Championship: 1 reign, as 3 Count with Shannon Moore and Evan Karagias. Won by pinning Brian Knobbs on Nitro, February 28, 2000, Minneapolis; held under the Freebird Rule. Lost to Knobbs in a hardcore gauntlet at Uncensored, March 19, 2000, Miami (6:51). 20 days. [WCW-HC] [UNC00] [3C]
- Independent titles (Wikipedia list, not individually re-verified): SWA Light Heavyweight (first title, beating T.C. Cruise), OMEGA Tag Team (2x, with Mike Maverick as The Serial Thrillaz), SCW Heavyweight, SCW Tag Team, NWA Wildside Tag Team (with Shannon Moore, one day), PWI International Heavyweight (CWF Mid-Atlantic, vacant title, April 2011), and others. [W] [3C]
- Wrestling Observer Newsletter Best Gimmick, 2001. [W] [TSH]
- PWI 500: No. 21 in 2003. ⚑ Single secondary source (TSH); Wikipedia cites the PWI 2003 list without the rank in the text read. [TSH] [W]

## Character eras
- "The Show" Shane Helms, Carolina indies (1990/91–1999): The Serial Thrillaz with Mike Maverick in OMEGA, the Cameron, NC promotion run by Matt and Jeff Hardy; Bad Street Boys in NWA Worldwide with Shannon Moore, Christian York and Joey Matthews. [W]
- 3 Count (WCW, December 1999–2000): Jimmy Hart's boy-band parody with Shannon Moore and Evan Karagias. Debuted on Thunder, December 23, 1999. Songs "Can't Get You Outta My Heart," later "Dance With 3 Count." Tank Abbott as self-appointed enforcer. Karagias was kicked out; Helms and Moore split at SuperBrawl Revenge, February 18, 2001. [W] [3C]
- Sugar Shane (WCW, 2001): solo cruiserweight, Vertebreaker finisher, WCW Cruiserweight Champion at the close. [W] [3C]
- "Hollywood" Gregory Helms (WWF, July 2001): debuted on SmackDown July 5, 2001 as part of The Alliance and lost the WCW Cruiserweight title to Billy Kidman. [W] [INV01]
- The Hurricane (WWF/WWE, August 2001–October 2005): green-masked comic-book superhero; the Hurri-Cycle, sidekicks Mighty Molly (from September 2001) and Rosey, the "Super Hero in Training" (from 2003); Stacy Keibler as Super Stacy in 2005. [W] [MOLLY] [ROSEY] [BR]
- Gregory Helms (WWE, October 2005–2009): heel turn after a Kurt Angle assault on October 17, 2005; no-showed the next week's title match, dropped the mask, beat Rosey on November 7, 2005. Record Cruiserweight reign 2006–07. [W] [ROSEY]
- ECW reporter / The Hurricane (2009): drafted to ECW April 15, 2009 as a backstage interviewer, revived The Hurricane in August 2009, beat Paul Burchill in a mask vs career match November 17, 2009. Released February 26, 2010. [W] [BR]
- Hurricane Helms / Gregory Shane Helms (2010–2019): independents, TNA (as Gregory Shane Helms, leader of The Helms Dynasty), ROH. [W] [WNS-2016]

## Career timeline
- 1990/91: Debuts in North Carolina (⚑ date disputed, see identity). Wins the SWA Light Heavyweight title early. [W] [CM-P]
- 1997–1998: OMEGA Tag Team Champion twice with Mike Maverick (The Serial Thrillaz). [W]
- May 1999: Signs with WCW alongside Shannon Moore. [W] [3C]
- December 23, 1999: 3 Count debuts on Thunder. [W]
- February 28, 2000: 3 Count wins the WCW Hardcore title from Brian Knobbs on Nitro, Minneapolis. [WCW-HC]
- March 19, 2000: Loses it to Knobbs at Uncensored. [UNC00]
- August 13, 2000: Wins the Gold Record ladder match over the Jung Dragons at New Blood Rising, Vancouver. [NBR]
- December 17, 2000: Wins the ladder match for a Cruiserweight title shot at Starrcade, Washington, D.C. [SC00]
- January 14, 2001: Loses to Chavo Guerrero Jr. in a Cruiserweight title match at Sin, Indianapolis. [SIN01]
- February 18, 2001: Wins the six-way elimination at SuperBrawl Revenge after splitting with Moore. [SBR] [3C]
- March 18, 2001: Beats Chavo Guerrero Jr. for the WCW Cruiserweight title at Greed, WCW's last pay-per-view. [GREED] [WCW-CW]
- March 2001: His contract is one of 25 included in the WWF purchase of WCW. [W]
- July 5, 2001: WWF debut on SmackDown as "Hollywood" Gregory Helms; loses the WCW Cruiserweight title to Billy Kidman. [W] [WCW-CW]
- August 27, 2001: The Hurricane debuts on Raw is War and wins the European title from Matt Hardy. [W]
- March 17, 2002: Wins and loses the Hardcore title at WrestleMania X8. [WMX8]
- May 16, 2002: Wins the Cruiserweight title on SmackDown. [W] [BR]
- September 23, 2002: Wins the World Tag Team title with Kane. [W] [UNAM]
- March 10, 2003: Pins The Rock on Raw at Gund Arena, Cleveland, rolling him up after Steve Austin's arrival distracted him. [W] [KB-RAW] [BR]
- 2003: Rosey becomes his Super Hero in Training. [ROSEY]
- May 1, 2005: Wins the World Tag Team title with Rosey at Backlash; Stacy Keibler joins as Super Stacy. [BL05] [W] [BR]
- September 18, 2005: Loses the tag titles at Unforgiven. [UNF05]
- October–November 2005: Kurt Angle assault, heel turn, becomes Gregory Helms. [W] [ROSEY]
- January 29, 2006: Wins the Cruiserweight Open at the Royal Rumble, Miami. [RR06]
- October 8, 2006: Loses a non-title match to Matt Hardy at No Mercy in Raleigh, North Carolina. [NM06]
- December 17, 2006: Retains against Jimmy Wang Yang at Armageddon, Richmond. [ARM06]
- February 18, 2007: Reign ends after 385 days at No Way Out. [NWO07] [WCW-CW]
- May 18, 2007: WWE reports two broken vertebrae in his neck; surgery about a week later. [W]
- September 19, 2008: Returns in a non-wrestling "Hurrapops" role. [W]
- December 5, 2008: In-ring return, beats MVP on SmackDown. [W]
- April 15, 2009: Drafted to ECW. [W]
- November 17, 2009: Beats Paul Burchill, mask vs career, on ECW. [W] [BR]
- February 26, 2010: Released by WWE. [W] [BR]
- 2010–2014: Independents (Jersey All Pro debut October 23, 2010; Lucha Libre USA 2011; PWG debut January 29, 2011; CWF Mid-Atlantic PWI International title April 2011; OMEGA, lost to Chris Hero November 16, 2013). [W]
- March 2015: Hired by TNA as an agent; on-screen debut at Bound for Glory 2015 as Gregory Shane Helms. [W]
- February 2, 2016: Helps Trevor Lee win the X Division title and forms The Helms Dynasty with Lee and Andrew Everett. February 19, 2016: TNA announces a multi-year deal. [W] [WNS-2016]
- June 19, 2017: Announces his TNA departure. [W] [WI-2017]
- January 28, 2018: Surprise Royal Rumble entrant, No. 21, eliminated by John Cena after 45 seconds. [WWE-RR18] [RR18]
- June 2018: Signs with ROH as wrestler and producer. [W]
- January 28, 2019: Reported heading back to WWE as a backstage producer. [FTF-2019] [W]
- April 27, 2019: Billed as possibly his last match ever, an eight-person tag at CHIKARA Fright Knight, Pocono Summit, Pennsylvania (result not verified). [WT-2019] [IWTV]
- April 15, 2020: Furloughed in WWE's pandemic cuts. [W] [411]
- November 7, 2020: Appears in Matt Hardy and Sammy Guevara's Elite Deletion match at AEW Full Gear. [PW-2020] [FTF-HARDY]
- November 22–23, 2020: Back at WWE as a producer (Survivor Series, then Raw). [PW-2020] [W]
- January 31, 2021: Royal Rumble entrant No. 23 at the ThunderDome, eliminated by Big E and Bobby Lashley after 30 seconds. His last WWE match. [WI-RR21] [RR21] [FTF-LEG]
- March 24, 2022: Signs a WWE Legends deal while still producing. [FTF-LEG]
- January 6, 2025: Fightful lists him as producer of Braun Strowman vs. Austin Theory on the December 27, 2024 SmackDown. [FTF-PROD]
- 2026: Still a WWE producer per Fightful (June 4, July 1, July 21). [FTF-LK] [FTF-AJ] [FTF-TAG]

## Greatest rivalries
- The Rock: the March 10, 2003 Raw upset, Helms later called the win "a debt I can never repay" and added "So much respect for The Rock." [W] [KB-RAW] [ITR]
- Chavo Guerrero Jr.: lost to him at Sin 2001, beat him at Greed 2001; Chavo also ended the 385-day reign in 2007. [SIN01] [GREED] [NWO07]
- Matt Hardy: OMEGA roots, the European title win in 2001, the No Mercy 2006 loss in Raleigh. [W] [NM06]
- Rosey: partner from 2003, tag champions in 2005, then the opponent Gregory Helms beat on November 7, 2005. [ROSEY]
- Shannon Moore: 3 Count partner from the Carolina indies to WCW; Moore turned on him and Helms won the six-way at SuperBrawl Revenge. [3C] [SBR]

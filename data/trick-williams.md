## "Whoop That Trick": the chant and the theme
- The chant: crowd-driven, from Booker T's ad-libs on NXT commentary; the phrase comes from Al Kapone's song in the 2005 film Hustle & Flow. Williams: "It never gets old." He calls the chant "totally organic" (Post Wrestling). Kapone later found him on Instagram and sent him merchandise. https://www.postwrestling.com/?p=103373 https://www.wrestlinginc.com/2236226/wwe-whoop-that-trick-williams-theme-song/ · https://www.fightful.com/wrestling/trick-williams-the-original-songwriter-of-whoop-that-trick-wants-to-work-with-wwe-to-get-some-stuff-going/
- The theme: an instrumental WWE produced after he pitched "Family Ties" (Baby Keem and Kendrick Lamar) as a reference. https://www.bodyslam.net/2026/03/06/trick-williams-recalls-pitching-family-ties-by-baby-keem-and-kendrick-lamar-to-inspire-his-wwe-theme
- ⚑ Track title and artist are NOT solid. Sportskeeda names "Locked In" by Def Rebel; no WWE or streaming source confirmed it, and the 2026 interview implies the main-roster theme may be newer. The walk-out band is therefore omitted.

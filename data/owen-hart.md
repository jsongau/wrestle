# Owen Hart: The King of Harts

Memorial file. Owen Hart died May 23, 1999. Nothing here describes present activity. Legacy items are dated.

## Canonical identity
- Full name: Owen James Hart. [S1]
- Born: May 7, 1965, Calgary, Alberta, Canada. [S1]
- Died: May 23, 1999, Kansas City, Missouri, at 34, after a fall during a stunt entrance at the WWF Over the Edge pay-per-view at Kemper Arena. [S1] [S16]
- Billed from: Calgary, Alberta. [S1]
- Height: 5 ft 10 in (178 cm), billed. ⚑ The old Wrestle Lore page said 5 ft 11 in (180 cm); Wikipedia's billed figure is used. [S1]
- Weight: 227 lb (103 kg), billed. [S1]
- Trained by: Stu Hart, in the Hart family Dungeon. [S1]
- Debut: May 30, 1986 (Stampede Wrestling). [S1]
- Family: youngest of the 12 children of Stu and Helen Hart; brother of Bret Hart. Married Martha Patterson on July 1, 1989; two children. [S1]
- Finisher: Sharpshooter (adopted late 1993); won King of the Ring 1994 with an elbow drop. [S1] [S6]

## Nicknames and ring names
The Blue Blazer (first billed as The Blue Angel) · The Rocket · The King of Harts · The Black Hart / The Lone Hart (post-Montreal) · "Nugget" (fan chant, 1998). [S1] [S25]

## Championships and accolades
- IWGP Junior Heavyweight Championship, 1 reign: def. Hiroshi Hase May 27, 1988 in Sendai; lost to Shiro Koshinaka June 24, 1988. [S1] [S18] ⚑ Wikipedia calls him the first non-Japanese holder; F4W does not state it. Published with attribution to Wikipedia only.
- Stampede International Tag Team Championship (with Ben Bassarab), 1986. [S1]
- USWA Unified World Heavyweight Championship, 1 reign (from Papa Shango, 1993; date not verified). [S1]
- WWF World Tag Team Championship, 4 reigns. [S4]
  - with Yokozuna: April 2, 1995 WrestleMania XI (Hartford) def. the Smoking Gunns, 175 days; titles changed to Diesel & Shawn Michaels at In Your House: Triple Header on September 24, 1995 (Owen did not appear; Bulldog substituted), then were restored to Owen & Yokozuna on Raw September 25, 1995 and lost to the Smoking Gunns. [S4] ⚑ The source dates both the restoration and the Gunns' win September 25.
  - with the British Bulldog: September 22, 1996, In Your House: Mind Games (Philadelphia) def. the Smoking Gunns; 246 days; lost to Stone Cold Steve Austin & Shawn Michaels May 26, 1997. [S4]
  - with Jeff Jarrett: January 25, 1999, Raw (Phoenix) def. Ken Shamrock & the Big Boss Man; lost to Kane & X-Pac. [S4] ⚑ The title list dates the loss March 30, 1999 (taping); the Owen Hart article says the pre-taped Raw of April 5, 1999.
- WWF Intercontinental Championship, 2 reigns. [S2]
  - April 28, 1997, Raw (Omaha) def. Rocky Maivia; lost to Austin at SummerSlam August 3, 1997.
  - October 5, 1997, Badd Blood (St. Louis) def. Faarooq in the tournament final for the vacant title; lost to Austin at Survivor Series November 9, 1997 (Montreal).
- WWF European Championship, 1 reign: def. The Artist Formerly Known As Goldust (disguised as Triple H) on Raw taped in Davis, California January 20, 1998; lost to Triple H March 16, 1998. [S3] ⚑ WWE recognizes the reign from January 22, 1998, the air date.
- Lost the inaugural European Championship tournament final to the British Bulldog, February 26, 1997, Raw, Berlin. [S3]
- King of the Ring 1994 (June 19, Baltimore): def. Tatanka, the 1-2-3 Kid, Razor Ramon. [S6]
- Slammy Awards: ⚑ Wikipedia's Owen Hart article lists three; the Slammy Award article credits him with one (Biggest Rat, December 31, 1994 special) and records that at the March 21, 1997 ceremony he "stole" the Best Bow Tie award, which had no official winner. [S1] [S24] Published as the stolen 1997 award only.
- PWI Rookie of the Year 1987; PWI Feud of the Year 1994 (vs Bret Hart); PWI Editor's Award 1999. [S1]
- Wrestling Observer Best Flying Wrestler 1987, 1988; Observer Feud of the Year 1997 (Hart Foundation vs Austin). [S1]
- Halls of fame: Pro Wrestling Hall of Fame (2019); Tragos/Thesz (2018); Prairie Wrestling Alliance (2010); Legends Pro Wrestling (May 28, 2011); Canadian Wrestling Hall of Fame. Not in the WWE Hall of Fame (see Legacy). [S1]

## Character eras
- The Blue Blazer (WWF 1988–1989; mask lost to El Canek May 29, 1991, Naucalpan). [S1]
- WCW, 1991: debuted on WCW TV March 16, 1991; never signed full-time because he would not move to Atlanta. [S1]
- The Rocket (1991–1994): New Foundation with Jim Neidhart (beat the Orient Express at Royal Rumble January 1992); High Energy with Koko B. Ware (1992). [S1]
- King of Harts (1994–1997): jealous younger brother; pink-and-black tights and the Sharpshooter after Survivor Series 1993. [S1] [S8]
- The Hart Foundation (1997): anti-American stable with Bret, Bulldog, Neidhart, Brian Pillman. [S10]
- Black Hart / Lone Hart (late 1997–1998), then Nation of Domination member alongside The Rock (1998). [S1] [S13] [S25]
- Partnership with Jeff Jarrett and the Blue Blazer revival (1998–1999). [S1] [S25]

## Career timeline
- 1986: debuts May 30 in Stampede Wrestling; Stampede International Tag Team titles with Ben Bassarab. [S1]
- 1987: PWI Rookie of the Year; Observer Best Flying Wrestler (also 1988). [S1]
- 1988: wins IWGP Junior Heavyweight title May 27 in Sendai; signs with WWF that summer as the Blue Blazer; PPV debut at Survivor Series 1988. [S1] [S18]
- 1989: loses to Mr. Perfect at WrestleMania V (April 2). [S1]
- 1991: WCW TV debut March 16; loses the Blazer mask to El Canek May 29; returns to WWF. [S1]
- 1992: beats Skinner at WrestleMania VIII; High Energy with Koko B. Ware. [S1]
- 1993: Survivor Series, November 24, Boston: eliminated after colliding with Bret; confrontation follows. [S8]
- 1994: Royal Rumble January 22: kicks Bret's injured leg after the tag title loss to the Quebecers. [S9] WrestleMania X March 20 at MSG: pins Bret clean in the opener (20:21). [S5] King of the Ring June 19. [S6] SummerSlam August 29: loses the 32:22 steel cage match for the WWF title. [S7] Survivor Series: tricks Helen Hart into throwing the towel for Bret. [S1]
- 1995: WrestleMania XI April 2: tag titles with Yokozuna. [S4]
- 1996: tag titles with the British Bulldog September 22. [S4]
- 1997: European final loss Feb 26; IC title April 28; tag titles lost May 26; Hart Foundation; Canadian Stampede July 6 (Owen scores the winning pin). [S2] [S3] [S4] [S10] SummerSlam August 3: botched piledriver injures Austin's neck; Austin wins the title. [S11] Wins vacant IC title October 5; loses it to Austin November 9 at Survivor Series, the night of the Montreal Screwjob. [S1] [S2]
- 1998: European title January; loses to Triple H at WrestleMania XIV March 29 (Chyna handcuffed to Sgt. Slaughter). [S3] [S12] Turns on Ken Shamrock, joins the Nation; pins Triple H with a Pedigree at Over the Edge May 31. [S1] [S13] Loses the Lion's Den match to Shamrock at SummerSlam August 30. [S14] Teams with Jeff Jarrett; Blue Blazer returns. [S1]
- 1999: tag titles with Jarrett January 25; retains at WrestleMania XV March 28 (vs Test & D'Lo Brown). [S4] [S15] Dies May 23 at Over the Edge, Kansas City, where he was scheduled to face The Godfather for the Intercontinental title as the Blue Blazer. [S16] Raw Is Owen airs May 24 from St. Louis. [S1]
- 2000: wrongful-death suit settled November 2 for US$18 million. [S1]
- 2015: Owen: Hart of Gold DVD (December 7 UK, December 8 US). [S1]
- 2020: Dark Side of the Ring, "The Final Days of Owen Hart," Vice, May 19. [S26]
- 2021: AEW announces the Owen Hart Cup with the Owen Hart Foundation, September 20. [S1]
- 2022–2026: Owen Hart Cup editions (see Legacy). [S17]

## Death (handled with care)
- May 23, 1999, Kemper Arena, Kansas City, Missouri, Over the Edge pay-per-view. He fell from the rafters during a planned Blue Blazer entrance in which he was to be lowered into the ring. He was taken to a hospital and pronounced dead. He was 34. [S1] [S16]
- TV viewers did not see the fall. Jim Ross later announced his death on the broadcast. The event resumed after about 15 minutes. [S16]
- ⚑ Fall height differs between sources (78 ft in [S1], about 90 ft in [S16]); not published.
- The family's lawsuit was settled for US$18 million on November 2, 2000. [S1]

## Rivalries
- Bret Hart: 1993–1995 brother feud; WrestleMania X (Owen wins), SummerSlam 1994 cage (Bret wins). Reunited in the 1997 Hart Foundation. [S5] [S7] [S8]
- Stone Cold Steve Austin: 1997; Canadian Stampede, SummerSlam injury, Survivor Series IC loss. [S10] [S11] [S2]
- Triple H: 1998 European title and Nation vs DX. [S3] [S12] [S13]
- Ken Shamrock: 1998, ending in the SummerSlam Lion's Den. [S1] [S14]
- British Bulldog: tag partner 1996–97 and opponent in the first European title final. [S3] [S4]

- Owen Hart Foundation: set up by Martha Hart with part of the settlement. [S1] [S21] Programs: Owen Hart Scholarship Fund (ten $4,000 Calgary high-school scholarships a year), Owen Hart Home Owners Program run by Momentum (100+ homes purchased per the foundation), partnership program. [S29] [S30]
- AEW Owen Hart Cup (Owen Hart Foundation Tournament), announced September 20, 2021. [S1] [S17]

  - 2023: Ricky Starks / Willow Nightingale (Collision, July 15, 2023, Calgary)
  - 2024: Bryan Danielson / Mariah May (Dynamite, July 10, 2024)
  - 2025: "Hangman" Adam Page / Mercedes Moné (Double or Nothing, May 25, 2025)
